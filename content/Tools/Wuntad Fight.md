> [!warning] Unpublished Material
This is the perfect way to execute a mid-campaign cliffhanger. A scripted or mathematically overwhelming defeat (a "curb-stomp" encounter) can be incredibly memorable if handled correctly. Since Wuntad is Level 10, Ibard is Level 8, and Saavia is Level 6, your Level 5 party mathematically stands almost zero chance—which is exactly what you want.

> [!note]
Here is a step-by-step GM guide on how to run this encounter, ensuring it feels like a desperate, terrifying struggle before transitioning into the cinematic, lore-heavy cliffhanger.

### Phase 1: The Brutal Reality (Rounds 1-2)

Let the players roll initiative and use their new Level 5 powers. Let them feel the horrific realization of the power gap organically.

- **Saavia's Skirmish:** Let Saavia (Level 6) engage Gona or Wilgard. She is roughly their equal, so they might actually land a hit on her, giving them a false sense of hope.
    
- **Ibard's Bell:** On Ibard's turn, she uses her **Bell of Sleep** (DC 26 Fortitude). At Level 5, most of your party will critically fail or fail this save, instantly knocking 1 or 2 of them unconscious.
    
- **Wuntad's Arrival:** Wuntad doesn't even need to rage. He casts _Divine Wrath_ (DC 29) or swoops in with his shadow wings to strike whoever is still standing. With a +23 to hit, he will critically hit a Level 5 character on a roll of 8 or 9, dealing massive damage and instantly dropping them to 0 HP.
    

### Phase 2: The Transition (Round 3)

Once two or three party members are on the floor bleeding out, **stop the initiative order**. Transition from tactical combat into narrative time to prevent actual character death (and failed recovery saves).

- **Action:** Saavia moves to execute a downed player (like Kino or Gona), her jaws opening wide.
    
- **The Intervention:** Wuntad casually swats Saavia back with a wing of crackling shadow. _"Leave them,"_ he commands. _"They are not meat. They are marked."_
    

### Phase 3: The Theft and The Key

Wuntad and Ibard begin casually stepping over the groaning, paralyzed, or bleeding party members, violently ripping the bags from their shoulders to take the Chaotic Artifacts (the Skull of Chaos, the Anarchic Longsword, the Dust, etc.).

**Calebrimbor’s Moment:** As Ibard approaches Cal, tell Cal's player: _"You have exactly three seconds before she searches you. Give me a Thievery or Stealth check."_ * Even if he rolls poorly, tell him he manages to desperately shove the **Chaos Spirit Key** up his _Sleeves of Holding_ just a microsecond before Ibard boots him in the ribs and takes the rest of his gear.

### Phase 4: The Monologue (Read-Aloud Text)

As the cultists gather the stolen artifacts, Wuntad approaches the center of the broken party. He kneels, his sallow green face and malevolent white eyes coming uncomfortably close to one of the conscious players (perhaps Gona or Willie). As he gets close, the **Runic Birthmarks** on the players' skin begin to painfully burn and glow with chaotic light.

> **Read or paraphrase:**
> 
> Wuntad chuckles, a wet, rasping sound that crackles with static electricity. He reaches out a clawed, dawnsilver-gauntleted hand and violently grabs your jaw, forcing you to look at him.
> 
> _"You thought yourselves heroes, didn't you? Delvers. Meddlers. You unlock a fraction of your potential in the Crucible, you find a few baubles in the dirt, and you think you can stand before the Vested of the Galchutt?"_
> 
> He traces a claw over the glowing runic birthmark on your skin. The pain is blinding.
> 
> _"You don't even know what you are. You think these marks are blessings? Gifts of the earth and arcane? You fools. They are beacons. They are tethers. You belong to the Deep Chaos, just as I do. You are not the saviors of Ptolus... you are the very vessels our Lords will use to break it."_
> 
> He stands up, his massive shadow-wings expanding to fill the room, crackling with dark lightning. He looks down at your broken, bleeding bodies with absolute contempt.
> 
> _"Let them live, Ibard. Let them heal. Let them grow stronger. When the Night of Dissolution comes, the Natharl'nacna will gladly harvest the power they have cultivated for us. Thank you for gathering our relics, little vessels. We will see you soon."_

### Phase 5: The Blackout

Wuntad, Ibard, and Saavia turn and walk into the shadows, the darkness physically swallowing them as they leave the ruined room.

Tell the players: _"The sound of Wuntad's laughter fades. The burning in your runes subsides into a dull, throbbing ache. The cold stone floor presses against your cheeks, and as the blood loss takes hold, your vision finally fades to black."_

**"And that is where we will pause our campaign."**

### Why this works:

1. **It preserves the Key:** Calebrimbor saving the Chaos Spirit Key means their entire run through Pythoness House wasn't a waste; they still have the vital clue to progress to Chapter 2.
    
2. **It integrates their backstory:** Tying the Runic Birthmarks (and subtly hinting at the Crucible suppression) to the Galchutt makes the overarching plot deeply, uncomfortably personal.
    
3. **It explains why they survive:** Wuntad isn't sparing them out of mercy; he is sparing them because they are "livestock" being fattened up for a cosmic slaughter. This fuels the party with a terrifying motivation to get stronger and prove him wrong.
