## 🛠️Overview
**Hero Lab Online** is a cloud-based character management and tactical campaign suite engineered by Lone Wolf Development. Built primarily to handle highly complex, rule-dense RPG engines, it acts as a digital ledger that dynamically tracks math, modifiers, gear bulk, and character progression natively within the cloud architecture. For a multi-timeline sandbox vault running Pathfinder 2e Remaster, it ensures that math validation remains exact across all shifting encounters.
