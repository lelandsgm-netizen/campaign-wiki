## 🛠️ Tool Overview
 **Quest Portal** is a modern, streamlined virtual tabletop (VTT) and campaign hub explicitly designed to lower the barrier to entry for running tabletop roleplaying games. Unlike traditionally heavy, map-and-token-dense desktop VTT software, Quest Portal focuses on slick theater-of-the-mind narrative styling, built-in mobile accessibility, and deep integration of ambient generative assistance tools to speed up live scene-setting during sandbox multi-versal travel.
