> [!warning] Unpublished Material
> This is the classic tabletop moment every GM dreams of—the party zigged when they should have zagged, ignored the warnings, and opened the boss door early!
> Do they stand a chance? **Yes, but this is a Severe-to-Extreme encounter (Level 8 Boss vs. Level 5 Party).** It is going to be a brutal, high-lethality slugfest. The math is stacked against them, but they have a few specific aces up their sleeves based on their recent Level 5 power-ups.

Here is a tactical breakdown of how the party survives, and how you should run the Chaosomaton to make it a memorable, terrifying fight without an unfair TPK.

### The Threat: Why the Chaosomaton is Terrifying

- **The "Crit" Math:** With a **+20 to hit**, it will hit most of your Level 5 party members (who likely have an AC of 20-22) on a roll of 2. It will **Critically Hit** on a roll of 10-12.

- **The Damage:** Its _Mechanical Jaws_ deal `2d12+8` piercing damage, with the _Fatal d10_ trait. A critical hit will do `(2d10+8)*2 + 1d10`, averaging around **43 damage**. This will instantly drop squishy characters like Calebrimbor, and heavily bloody Gona or Willie.

- **The Reach:** It has a **15-foot reach** and _Reactive Strike_. If the party tries to run past it or cast manipulate spells within 3 squares of it, they are going to get bitten.

### The Silver Lining: How the Party Wins

Despite the terrifying math, your party's specific composition is perfectly suited to counter this machine.

**1. Calebrimbor is the MVP (The Electricity Weakness)** At Level 5, Cal unlocked 3rd-Rank spells, specifically **Lightning Bolt**. The Chaosomaton has a Weakness to Electricity 10, and electricity damage completely shuts off its **Regeneration 10**.

- Even if the Hydra succeeds on its Reflex save (+13 vs Cal's DC ~21) and takes half damage, it _still_ triggers the Weakness 10 and shuts down the healing. Cal just needs to zap it every round or two to keep it vulnerable.

**2. Gona's Master Proficiency** Hitting an **AC 28** is usually a nightmare for a Level 5 party, but Gona is a Fighter with Master proficiency in her Maul. She is likely swinging with a +16 to hit. If Willie casts _Heroism_ (+1) on her, or if she flanks the Hydra to make it Off-Guard (-2 AC), she will be hitting it on a 9 or 10 on the die. She is the only one who can reliably shatter its armor.

**3. Willie’s Massive Heals** Willie’s 3rd-Rank _Heal_ spells output `3d8+24` (average 37 HP) using the 2-action version. He will need to stand far back (outside the 15-foot reach) and dedicate all his slots to keeping Gona and Kino on their feet after they take critical hits.

**4. Kino's Ranged Pressure** As long as Kino stays outside of that 15-foot threat radius, they can constantly pepper the Hydra with 2d8 Elemental Blasts. Since the Hydra is Mindless and Huge, it is going to get stuck on Gona, giving Kino free rein to act as artillery.

### GM Advice for Running the Encounter

#### To make this feel like a chaotic scramble for survival rather than a mathematical execution:

> [!example] **The Squeeze (Give them a round):**
>
> - ** The construct has been immobile for years and is Huge (15x15). When the door opens, spend its first turn having it violently rip itself from its moorings and squeeze through the fortified doorway. This gives the party **one free round** to realize their mistake, draw weapons, cast _Haste_ or _Heroism_, and position themselves.
> - **Telegraph the Reactive Strike:** Have it snap its jaws wildly at the air. Warn the players: _"Its six heads are darting around on long, metallic necks, watching every twitch you make. Moving close to this thing looks incredibly dangerous."_
> - **Play it Mindless:** The Hydra has an Intelligence of -5. It does not use advanced tactics. It will not bypass Gona's high AC to dive the Wizard. It will lash out at whatever is closest and dealing the most damage to it. Let Gona hold the line.

> [!faq] **Narrate the Weakness:**
> The first time it takes electricity damage, don't just subtract the HP. Tell them: _"The lightning arcs through its metallic frame! Several of its chaositech glowing runes violently short-circuit and explode, and the whirring sound of its internal repair gears completely grinds to a halt!"_ If they manage to take down a 240 HP Level 8 boss at Level 5, it will be the most legendary combat of the campaign so far!
