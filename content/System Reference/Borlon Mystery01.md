## Episode 1: Ashes of the West Camp Episode 01

### The Setup: An Urgent Invitation

> [!info] 2026 New Session 01
> The session opens in the tense, crowded streets of Borlon during the evening hours . The party receives a hurried, handwritten note smuggled into their hands from **Elspeth Geddes**, the red-headed seamstress they previously encountered.
> Elspeth is a survivor of the tragic sacking of Oakhaven to the west . When the party last spoke with her, she shared a terrifying piece of information: she witnessed **Elias Thorne** conspiring directly with the Orcs on the coast while her home town was burned to the ground. Now, her message is frantic—she has discovered something new and begs to meet the party immediately at her temporary tent within the refugee encampment just outside Borlon's west wall .

### The Scene: Chaos at the West Wall

As the party arrives at the sprawling, claustrophobic sea of tents, the atmosphere shifts from miserable to lethal . Before a proper greeting can take place, the crisp evening air is shattered by screams .

> [!warning] Scenario Note
> Earlier in the day, Elspeth witnessed a cart delivery at the gate. Deliveries were not unusual, but this one seemed to initiate a response from the guards like it was not scheduled. It was pulled aside for an inspection. The driver seemed enraged by the delay and stalked back an forth refusing to cooperate and dropping names. After a messenger dispatch and return, the guards backed off and let the cart in. This cart was moved to a back alley along the fence. Over the course of the afternoon, zombies escaped the cart and invaded several encampment structures killing refugees and growing their numbers. Soon the camp was overwhelmed!

```

* **Once the issue is discovered determine initiative. The battle kicks off at a breakneck pace . Elias’s hand-delivered horror erupts within the camp .
* **The Tactical Situation:** A wave of aggressive, plague-burst zombies moves rapidly through the dry, highly flammable canvas tents . The encounter is a multi-layered challenge: the party must cut through the undead while simultaneously managing the spreading fires threatening to consume the camp.
* **The Primary Objective:** This isn’t a standard "clear the room" monster fight—it is a brutal race against time . The zombies are actively killing all of the refugees. Elias knows she is a liability, and his explicit goal is to permanently silence her before she can pass on what she knows.
* **The Complication:** The local wall guards ignore the initial sounds of slaughter . Thanks to Anya's deliberate scheduling interferences, the local watch is completely absent from the sector . The party is entirely on their own to protect the refugees and save Elspeth for the first crucial rounds of the engagement .

### The Aftermath & The Clues

> [!info] Potential results
> Once the final undead threat is put down and the flames are suppressed, the investigation truly begins based on Elara's fate.

#### Path A: Elara Survives

> [!success] Elspeth Lives
> If the party successfully shields her from the zombies, a trembling but determined Elara drops a massive logistical bombshell that ties the entire conspiracy together:

* She confirms the alledged unexpected delivery earlier in the day.
* She feels like the entire encampment was purposefully targeted to silence them and her.
* Elspeth is now deatly afraid for the encampment. She is willing to discuaa ideas on how to protect them.

#### Path B: Elara Perishes

> [!failure] Elspeth Perishes
> If the undead manage to kill her before the party can intervene, her silence isn't absolute . A search of her body or her scorched text reveals a blood-stained, frantic journal entry :

* The notes detail of her witnessing the interaction between the delivery driver and the guards.

### The Late Shield Arrives

> [!IMPORTANT] **The Confrontation with Anya**
> Just as the dust begins to settle and the party is collecting evidence or comforting a surviving Elspeth, the heavy boots of the Local City Guard echo through the smoke .

Anya’s patrol finally arrives on the scene, feigning shock and outrage at the "sudden, unpredictable tragedy" . Acting with aggressive authority, Anya immediately orders her guardsmen to seal off the area, push the party away from the witnesses, and seize the remnants of the covered wagon under the guise of "cleaning up the crime scene" and collecting evidence.

* **The Social Danger:** If the characters openly lose their tempers and accuse Anya or her watchmen of being intentionally late, she won't arrest them immediately in front of a rioting camp . Instead, she coldly marks them as political agitators and subversives. This political targeting ensures that in **Episode 2**, the city's official channels will be locked down tight against them, forcing them to operate from the shadows .
