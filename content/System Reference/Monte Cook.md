## 🛠️ Publisher & Studio Overview

**Monte Cook Games (MCG)** is an award-winning tabletop roleplaying game publisher founded in 2012 by legendary designers Monte Cook and Shanna Germain. Renowned for pushing the boundaries of surreal world-building, high-concept fantasy, and experimental player-narrative focus, the studio is famous for creating the Cypher System (powering _Numenera_ and _The Strange_) and publishing the massive, legendary city-sourcebook **Ptolus: City by the Spire**. For your sandbox realm of **Eraedal**, their publications provide the complex urban structures, hidden cult networks, and cosmic horrors needed to anchor massive, layered city adventures.

## Inspiration

I am a newcomer to Monte Cook and his works, but I had to find room in my worlds for his immortal city of Ptolus. If I add NPCs from his works, I may include modified stat blocks to match the PF2E world that I am blending his works into.
