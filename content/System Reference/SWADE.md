## 🎲 System Overview

The **Savage Worlds Adventure Edition (SWADE)** is a fast-paced, cinematic multi-genre engine built around the core philosophy of being "Fast, Furious, and Fun!" Rather than using massive static math tracking, characters are entirely defined by Trait Dice (ranging from a humble $d4$ up to an elite $d12$), allowing the Game Master to resolve complex combat scenarios, high-octane chases, and dramatic tasks with extreme speed.
