## Episode 2: The Paper Trail & The Late Shield

> [!info] Follow the Crumbs
> With the undead assault thwarted, the campaign shifts away from open combat and plunges into a high-stakes phase of urban stealth, social engineering, and espionage within the walled city of Borlon.

### The Core Objective

> [!warning] Where the Cards Land
> The party knows the local guard’s absence wasn't an accident. To prove a coordinated conspiracy before their meeting with Ornric, they must obtain the **Official Patrol Logs** for the night of the attack to expose the intentional delay.
>
> - **The Leveraged Evidence:** If Elspeth survived, she is now a highly targeted asset hidden away in a party safehouse. Her eyewitness account of the cart exchange. If she perished, her blood-stained journal and half-written formal testimony to the Head Sheriff serve as the primary motivation to dig up matching administrative proof.
> - **The Location:** The objective rests within the Local Guard barracks—a sub-sector of the city's civil authority under the native Head Sheriff.

---

### The Tactical Approaches

> [!info] How Does He Know?
> You can present your players with two distinct strategic routes to acquire the logs, allowing them to play to their specific character strengths:

#### Route A: The Diplomatic / Social Approach

The party chooses to navigate the complex social web of Borlon's disgruntled rank-and-file guardsmen.

- **The Method:** The characters track down and corner the specific watchmen who were actually assigned to the west perimeter on the night of the attack. These local guards already harbor deep-seated resentment toward the political state of the city.
- **The Clue:** With a well-placed bribe or a high social check, a frustrated guardsman cracks. He admits that **Sergeant Anya** personally reassigned their route at the eleventh hour. Her justification? A fabricated tip about a "suspected rebel assembly" taking place all the way across the city at the high-security Northeast Shipyards.

#### Route B: The Stealth / Heist Approach

The party opts for a classic, dark-of-night infiltration into the watch station itself.

- **The Method:** This requires mapping guard rotations, bypassing locked grates, or utilizing stealth to break into the administrative office where the official daily ledgers are securely filed.
- **The Clue:** Successfully locating and opening the ledger reveals the smoking gun. The written orders for that evening feature a forged or hastily scribbled tactical diversion explicitly signed by Anya, leaving the refugee camp completely unprotected.

---

### Elias's Countermove: Turning the Screws

Elias Thorne is far from passive; he is acutely aware that the party is pulling on the threads of his conspiracy. The moment the characters start asking questions or infiltrating guard property, he pushes Anya to retaliate using the full weight of her legal authority.

> [!CAUTION] **The City Closes In**
> Anya issues an active watch-restriction or places a localized bounty on the party, painting them as dangerous subversives or political agitators. For the rest of the episode, the characters are forced to operate out of the shadows of Borlon's alleys, raising the tension to a fever pitch just as they pinpoint Elias's hidden base.
