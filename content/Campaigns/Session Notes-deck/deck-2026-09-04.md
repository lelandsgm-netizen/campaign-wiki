## Session Meta-Information

> [!info] Campaign & Session Overview
>
> - The fight with zombies completed with the realization that every surviving refugee from Oakhavan had died except Elspeth Gedde. When the party found her perched on a nail high up on a 12in x 12in wooden column, she passed out from horrer and strain. Wuf-Jaw caught her before she hit the ground. He was unable to yell for help though. His mouth was full with all of the zombie ears he had bitton off in the fight. Her face and hands had an etheral glow. He rushed her to the rest of the group to stabilize her after initially trating her wounds with sawdust. After securing the area and doors, Grigorey climbed up one of the towers to survey the surrounding area for any other signs of the zombie brutes.
> - He discovered a wagon that seemed out of place just inside the walls by some stacks of hay. Before leaving to investigate, they managed to wake Elara and informed her of the plan. She was to secure the door behind them and remain in the makeshift fort. On approaching the wagon, they were nearly overcome by a rotting stench centered on the wagon. Inside was have of a butchered cow carcass that looked like most of it's flesh had been chewed off by what looked like human sized mouths. Prahs searched the wagon. He and Wuf-jaw seemed mostly unaffacted by the stench. He discovered a small 💰 with 10 platinum and a torn receipt for a 500 gold payout. There was a partially torn stamped logo near the top of the receipt that could be a match for the Thorne Mercantile logo they saw on the sail of his shit and on the accounting notebook they returned. Grigorey Trapped the area with some alarms and deterrents to secure the evidence and they went to alert Anya Volkov. Before they went to the fort, they took Elspeth to their apartment and left her under Lightfoots protection.
> - Anya sent her sargent and a small group to check on the guard tower that should have been watching over to encampment as she suited up and went with the characters to the site. It was discovered that a "scheduling mistake" was responsible for the tower being unmanned that night. The soldiers began cleaning up the site and securing the area. After gathering the dead, Anya counted the bodies and discovered that 1 of the refugees was missing.
> - The plan for the following evening is to infiltrate the Thorne Mercantile warehouses.
