# The Occupied Juggernaut

## Overview
**Borlon** is a sprawling, heavily fortified port city anchoring the bustling northern coast of [[Oen]] Island. Originally constructed over a century ago as a vital stronghold for the [[Western Empire]], it was left to its own devices when the empire abandoned the archipelago. For eighty years, the city thrived as an independent entity, developing its own domestic shipbuilding industries and unique local royalty. 

Everything changed twenty years ago when the **[[Timro]] Royal Navy** arrived in overwhelming force. Executing a strategic, systemic annexation, Timro seized control of the city’s vital infrastructure to secure a permanent military and economic gateway into the [[Floenry Isles]]. Today, it is a bustling, tense colonial hub where ancient imperial stone structures house a massive population of sailors, merchants, indigenous laborers, and occupying soldiers.

## Current Power Structure

### The Occupying Authority: Constable [[Anya Volkov]]
Absolute military and administrative rule over Borlon is held by **Constable Anya Volkov** ([[Anya Volkov]]). A severe, imposing Northern native and a seasoned Fighter with twelve years of distinguished service in the [[Timro]] Navy, she governs the city with iron-clad discipline and an unmistakable northern(Russian) accent. From her seat of power, she manages the heavy military garrisons, oversees regional taxation, and directs navy patrols under the auspices of "maintaining [[Timro]] security interests" across the northern waters.

### The Civil Authority: Head Sheriff & The Local Guard
To prevent total civilian revolt, [[Timro]] preserved Borlon’s domestic civic infrastructure. Everyday municipal laws, civil crimes, and domestic policing among the civilian populace are handled by the native **Local Guard**, led by the city's **Head Sheriff**. While the Sheriff maintains peace in the streets, they ultimately report directly to Constable Volkov, creating a strained relationship between the local watchmen and the occupying [[Timro]] soldiers.

### The Captive Royalty
The descendants of the original nobility who ruled Borlon during its eighty years of golden independence still reside within the central keep. However, their sovereignty is gone. They exist in a state of polite exile and gilded confinement within their own ancestral castle. While Timro permits them to retain limited, ceremonial diplomatic functions and civic duties to appease the populace, their every move is heavily monitored by colonial eyes.

## Key Tactical Sectors & Locations

### [[Northeast Shipyards]]
The economic lifeblood of Borlon. Once the pride of the city's independent merchant class, these massive dry docks and timber yards have been completely requisitioned by the [[Timro]] Crown. It is currently operating under maximum security, dedicated to repairing [[Timro]] warships and refitting colonial supply vessels.

### The [[Island Fort]] & The [[Lighthouse]]
Sitting as an iron wedge at the mouth of the bay, the Island Fort completely commands the harbor with heavy naval artillery. The connected Lighthouse functions as a crucial navigational beacon and a heavily fortified outpost. It currently serves as the strategic base of operations for **Captain Marcus Renault** ([[Marcus Renault]]), a highly disciplined, buttoned-up Timro Special Investigator sent from the mainland to audit the garrison.

### The [[South Forts]] & [[Central Keep]]
The defensive fortifications anchoring the landward perimeter of the city. These positions are entirely manned by elite [[Timro]] infantry to guard against any sudden northern push from the insurgent [[Order of the Indomitable Shield]] hiding in the island's interior.

## Active Operational Intelligence

### Current Objectives & Investigation
The Black Regiment is currently operating deep within the urban sprawl of Borlon, investigating a critical supply chain failure: a massive cache of **missing Ironwood supplies** that were officially designated for the Northeast Shipyards. 

### Key Figures of Interest
* **Ornric Broadman ([[Ornric Broadman]]):** A cantankerous, grizzled Dwarf Investigator and native [[Timro]] spymaster. Speaking with a thick southern drawl, he acts as the handler for the Black Regiment. He is currently waiting in the wings and expects a full operational report on the missing ironwood investigation from the squad in **three days**.
* **Elias Thorn ([[Elias Thorn]]):** A prominent and highly influential Half-Elf merchant prince in the city. The squad is actively investigating Thorn, looking for any definitive connections between his extensive trading network and the missing Ironwood. They are also probing connections to Constable Volkov.
* **Captain Marcus Renault ([[Marcus Renault]]):** The squad has established contact with this rigid [[Timro]] investigator at the [[Lighthouse]]. Sensing a potential ally within a compromised system, the operators have confided a small amount of sensitive, classified information to him regarding their current findings.
