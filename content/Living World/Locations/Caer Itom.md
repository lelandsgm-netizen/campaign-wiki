## 📍 Overview
*Caer Itom, once the glittering seat of the Imperial [[House Itomas]], has been utterly transformed into a grim, hyper-industrialized nightmare. Drawn into the chaotic crossfire of the regional three-way civil war, the metropolis was violently seized by a terrifying alliance: the **[[Cyclopean Vanguard]]** and the militant **[[Free Slave Coalition]]**. What was once a majestic center of human supremacy is now an occupied war zone where the former human, elven, and dwarven populations face systemic executions or are forced into brutal chains.

The sprawling stone grid-iron streets are now choked with the black soot of non-stop, repurposed manufacturing factories. The ingenious waterwheel pump systems and elemental warlock engineering that once created pristine plazas are now entirely utilized to churn out siege weapons, grind wartime rations, and forge heavy plate armor to supply the rebel armies. Perched critically near the heart of the empire, this heavily fortified fortress-city stands as a terrifying stronghold of absolute vengeance, completely cutting off the old aristocratic bloodlines and turning the "Forest of Stone" into a production hub for an escalating planar war.*

## 🗺️ Points of Interest
### 1. The [[Captured Imperial Plaza]]

- **Immersive Description:** Once the grandest and most heavily guarded section of town, this plaza is a beautiful network of water-washed stone, marble fountains, and majestic groves of trees. The soaring 10-foot-tall marble statues of the _Plaza of Emperors_—historically called the "Forest of Stone"—still stand, now cast in the dark shadows of rebel banners.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** It has been stripped of its aristocratic luxury and converted into the **High Command and Military Nexus** for the [[Free Slave Coalition]]. The pristine training yards and barracks of the fallen [[Imperial Janissaries]] are now used to drill rebel soldiers, while the warded vaults of the adjacent Imperial Mint are heavily locked down to store plundered bullion to fund the global uprising.
    

### 2. [[The Foundry Grid]] (Industrial Row)

- **Immersive Description:** A massive, mile-long stone complex of dense manufacturing plants, mills, and processing foundries that sit adjacent to the city's rushing rivers and automated aqueduct channels.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** This sector operates as a **Hyper-Industrial War Machine and Logistics Engine**. The massive waterwheels constructed by warlock magic are forced to spin 24 hours a day to power twelve specialized spice-grinding factories, high-output weapons foundries, and leatherworking tanneries. These facilities are used to mass-produce arms, tactical armor, and processed field rations exclusively for the [[Cyclopean Vanguard]]'s front-line battalions.
    

### 3. The [[Iron Hill Slums]] (Eastern Commercial Plaza)

- **Immersive Description:** A bustling, overcrowded lower-class sector dominated by ancient, multi-story stone apartment blocks and interconnected basements.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** It serves as a **Strategic Staging Ground and Underground Smuggling Hub**. The deep, subterranean catacombs beneath the textile factories—secretly connected to the city's main sewer lines—are heavily populated by displaced, militant tribes of Goblins, Hobgoblins, and Ratlings. These subterranean denizens use the neighborhood as a hidden base to coordinate sudden, brutal nocturnal raids against any remaining surface loyalists.
    

### 4. The [[Sovereign Dock Ward]] (Royal Docks & Shipyards)

- **Immersive Description:** A massive, fortified deep-water marina and public shipyard lining the rocky coastline, completely isolated by towering cliffside fortifications.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** It has been repurposed into an active **Naval Construction Yard and Blockade Fleet Base**. The private, [[Bizantium]]-style corsair fleets of [[House Itomas]] have been fully commandeered. The shipyards are used to construct heavy vanguard war vessels and repair captured blockade runners, giving the Coalition total control over the local maritime shipping lanes.
    

### 5. The [[Rebel Colosseum]] (The Imperial Arena)

- **Immersive Description:** A mammoth, stadium-style stone arena featuring a massive network of warded underground containment cells and an advanced aqueduct tap capable of completely flooding the arena floor.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** It is now utilized as a **Brutal Retribution Arena and Vanguard Training Ground**. The dark underground cells that once held exotic monsters and condemned criminals now overflow with enslaved human nobles, corrupt merchants, and captured knights. In a twist of poetic cruelty, the Coalition uses the flooded arena floor to conduct live-fire naval combat drills, forcing the captured aristocracy to fight for their survival against the arena's undefeated champions to entertain the garrisoned troops.
