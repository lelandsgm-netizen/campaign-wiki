# 🌍 Welcome to Eraedal: The Tri-System Synthesis
![Eraedal](eraedalmap.jpg)

**Eraedal** is a world of ancient, cyclical trauma, rigid geographic boundaries, and highly volatile, modern political upheaval. It represents a reality where mortal bloodlines build towering empires over the sealed horrors of a previous cosmic age, oblivious (or indifferent) to the fact that the very ground they walk on is a cage.

## 🏛️ The Three Pillars of Reality

To understand Eraedal, one must understand the three distinct cosmic forces that forged its current state:

![[eraedalsetting.png]]

### 1. The Physical Shell (Palladium Geography)

The world’s layout, regions, and regional politics are drawn explicitly from the Palladium Fantasy map. The **Western Empire** remains the decaying, blood-soaked crown of civilization, with **[[West Kighfalton]]** serving as its fully developed, concrete-paved heartland. Cities like **[[Caer Itom]]** are massive, urban sprawls built around rigid class divides, sprawling sewer networks plagued by Ratlings, and competitive regional noble houses (**Itomas, Paaslaan, Milaszc**). The rivers, mountain ranges (Koerdian), and sea lanes are physically identical to Palladium lore, dictating travel times, trade routes, and military bottlenecks.

### 2. The Ancient Bones (Praemal Lore)

While the surface politics look like Palladium, the _metaphysical history_ belongs to Monte Cook’s Praemal.

- **The "Old Ones" of Palladium are structurally fused with the Galchutt**—the cosmic lords of ultimate entropy. They do not merely sleep; they are imprisoned deep within the planet's core, and their foul, dreaming corruption leaks upward into the underbelly of the world's cities.
    
- Historical events like the **Great Cataclysm** and the creation of reality-warping magical anchors mirror the lore of Praemal. The world itself is a prison or a crucible, where high-level magical orders (like the **Inverted Pyramid** or the **Eraedal Wizards' Guild**) wield reality-bending spells (like rifting entire city districts into the sky) to keep themselves separate from the rot of the world below.
    

### 3. The Active Pulse (Paizo/Pathfinder Mechanics & Entities)

When a mortal swings a sword, casts a spell, or kneels to pray, they are using the rule laws and divine network of Paizo.

- **The Gods:** The traditional Western Empire pantheon is replaced or syncretized with the Golarion Pantheon. Clerics in Caer Itom pray to **Iomedae** for justice, **Abadar** to protect the Imperial Mint, **Asmodeus** to maintain the rigid slave contracts, or **Lamashtu** down in the dark, monster-infested sewers.
    
- **The Rules:** All classes, ancestries, feats, and monsters use Pathfinder rules. A Wolfen is modeled with custom ancestry rules or as an optimized Beastkin/Gnoll variant; the Free Slave Coalition fights using Pathfinder tactical rules, utilizing martial maneuvers, occult summoners, and divine champions.
