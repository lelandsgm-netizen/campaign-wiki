## 📍 Overview
*The **Imperial Province**, encompassing the grand capital of [[Caer Itom]], stands as the most shocking conquest of the **[[Free Slave Coalition]]** and the **[[Cyclopean Vanguard]]**. For over 6,000 years, this territory served as the unassailable seat of the Imperial [[House Itomas]] and the absolute geopolitical, military, and financial heart of the [[Western Empire]]. In all the centuries of bloody civil wars and inner-realm devastation, this specific province was never overrun, sacked, or defiled—until the recent slave uprising completely tore the crown away from the old aristocracy.

Geographically, the Imperial Province is completely flat and artificially developed. Every single acre of land not consumed by urban concrete is filled with highly systematized farms and massive livestock yards that historically kept the massive empire fed. Crisscrossed by a flawless network of concrete highways and interlocking brick roads, the countryside is heavily populated, featuring large farmhouses, fortified rural villages, and booming trade towns.

Now, the legendary golden towers and pristine brick plazas have been violently repurposed. What was once a symbol of luxurious human supremacy is now a massive, heavily fortified rebel bastion. The ancestral home of the Emperors has been completely upended, transforming a territory of two million citizens into a militant, industrial garrison for the liberation armies.

### ⚠️ Critical Demographic Variables

- **The Liberated Ranks:** The statistics above do _not_ include the historic slave population, which originally equaled approximately 50% of the entire free population. This massive, long-oppressed workforce now serves as the fanatical standing army of the [[Free Slave Coalition]].
    
- **The Transient Population:** Visitors, traveling merchants, and seasonal adventurers historically made up roughly 20% of the overall population at any given time, leaving the province highly volatile to corporate espionage and partisan activity.
    
- **The Sewer Plague:** Beneath the cobblestones, the sprawling sewer systems and ancient catacombs are choked with a massive civilization of native Ratlings, roughly equal to 30% of the entire free surface population.*

## 🗺️ Points of Interest
### 1. The Concrete Intersections (The Gridiron Blockades)

- **Immersive Description:** A flawless, vast network of interconnected roads paved with dense concrete and interlocking bricks that tightly links every major town, agricultural facility, and sea port across the flat plains.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** Built by the empire to facilitate lightning-fast deployment of the Imperial Legions, this infrastructure has been seized as a **Strategic Logistical Highway Network**. The Coalition has constructed heavy stone blockades, defensive palisades, and watchtowers at every key intersection, turning the ancient trade routes into highly regulated checkpoints to choke out incoming imperial counter-offensives.
    

### 2. The Commandeered [[Agrarian Rings]] (The Slave Granaries)

- **Immersive Description:** Thousands of acres of highly structured, flat mega-farms and geometric livestock production fields dotting the countryside outside the major city limits.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** Once the private luxury estates of [[House Itomas]] and their favorite lords, these lands now function as the **Primary Food Engine for the Revolution**. The liberated slave population manages the fields collectively, stockpiling immense mountains of grain, preserved meat, and crop yields to supply the frontline battlefields of neighboring provinces like [[Rallian]] and [[Iaendria]].
    

### 3. The [[Catacomb Vaults]] (The Ratling Deep-Passages)

- **Immersive Description:** A colossal, multi-tiered underground network of stone-lined sewer arteries, water aqueducts, and ancient burial chambers threading beneath the entirety of the province.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** Historically an unsavory hiding spot for outlaws, this dark underbelly operates as a **Lawless Subterranean Frontier**. Following the collapse of the surface government, the native population of hundreds of thousands of Ratlings and Goblins have claimed total dominion over the depths. They use these hidden passages to conduct brutal nocturnal raids against both remaining human loyalists and overextended Coalition patrol squads alike.
    

### 4. The Rebuilt [[Outer Camps]] (The Vanguard Outposts)

- **Immersive Description:** Sprawling, heavily fortified military compounds consisting of deep trenches, timber battlements, and heavy siege foundries built directly over the ruins of old imperial toll stations.
    
- **Current Functional Utility:** These bases serve as the **Primary Shock Battalion Staging Grounds** for the [[Cyclopean Vanguard]]. Utilizing the immense stockpiles of food, construction materials, and refined metal tools left behind by [[House Itomas]]'s historical reconstruction industry, the Vanguard continuously manufactures heavy armaments and trains non-human conscripts to reinforce the global war effort.
