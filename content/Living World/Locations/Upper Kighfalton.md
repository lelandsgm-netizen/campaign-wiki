## 📍 Overview
*Upper Kighfalton** is a booming shoreline district on the peninsula's north shore whose ruling famil, [[House Ne'Klosh]], has aggressively capitalized on the region's prosperity to declare himself Emperor—establishing it as a primary front in the fractured realm's [[Three-way Civil War]]. Economically, the region acts as a powerhouse export coast, buying produce wholesale from the vast [[Kighfalton Plains]] to process and export fine wines, Western Champagne, grain alcohol, blown glass, and stone masonry, while additionally serving as a lucrative supply junction and toll gate for renewed colonization efforts into the [[Northern frontiers]]. Though its noble houses remain rock-solid in their unified allegiance to their self-proclaimed sovereign, this glittering veneer of wealth casts a long, dark shadow. The rebuilt cities are plagued by rampant cultism, predatory assassins' guilds, and displaced hordes of vengeful subterranean non-humans—particularly Goblins and Ratlings—who strike from the dark underbelly, turning the region into a dangerous, war-torn landscape where nightly disappearances and brutal ritual mutilations occur in open defiance of imperial military extermination campaigns.*

## 🗺️ Points of Interest
* **[[The Northern Sea]] / [[Sea of Dread]]** 
* The treacherous seafront that frames Upper Kighfalton’s northern coast. While the region boasts beautiful, well-reconstructed ports, northern maritime trade is exceptionally scarce because few independent merchants dare to navigate the monster-infested, storm-tossed waters of the Sea of Dread. Instead, Upper Kighfalton operates heavily as a unilateral export shoreline.
    
- **[[The Northern Staging Ports]]** _A lucrative network of military and colonial deep-water harbors across the district's coastline. Because the adjacent [[Vequerrel Woodlands]] lack natural ports, Upper Kighfalton has become the primary military gate and staging junction for the Empire's massive overseas colonization push into [[Ophid's Grasslands]], pulling in substantial wealth by charging hefty tolls on every crown vanguard and supply fleet.
