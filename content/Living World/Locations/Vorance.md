## 📍 Overview
***[[Vorance]] Province** is a highly lucrative but geographically isolated northern territory characterized by a jagged, treacherous shoreline completely unsuited for maritime trade, yet immensely valuable for the vast salt deposits locking its cliffs and beaches. Governed by **[[House Csusk]]**—an ancient, immensely wealthy, and prestigious lineage that takes the mockery of rival houses in stride—the province generates unfathomable profits by processing and exporting salt across the Empire, relying heavily on a brutal underworld economy of condemned criminals and slave labor to keep the mines operational. At the heart of this economic powerhouse lies the provincial capital of **[[Vorance]]**, a pristine, spacious metropolis of 30,000 residents that was entirely rebuilt during the Reconstruction. Thanks to aggressive, gold-funded renewal programs, [[Vorance]] boasts flawless sewers, aqueducts, a thriving cultural center, and beautifully maintained formal gardens and greenhouses so magnificent that even emperors make occasional pilgrimages to see them. Despite effectively suppressing crime, political unrest, and racial strife, this glittering paradise exists on borrowed time; widely gossiped about as a "Utopia held together with gold thread," the entire social order faces total structural collapse the moment the salt mines bottom out or the ruling house pauses its massive municipal subsidies.*
