##### 🌾 Overview & Current Status

The **Hrodan Province** dominates the final, isolated stretch of the [[Tarldet Plains]]. It is a territory defined by a stark, desolate beauty, characterized by endless rolling grasslands, wide-open skies, and sprawling cattle ranches. In contrast to more densely populated imperial regions, Hrodan suffers from a very low, remarkably uneven population distribution. A staggering **95%** of the province's citizens live directly within the walls of the provincial capital, leaving the remaining vast wilderness a lonely haven for hermits, wanderers, independent farmers, desperate squatters, and the occasional raiding group of bandits or monsters.

The ruling lineage of **[[House Bereggia]]** operates with a detached, duty-bound compliance. Historically quiet in high imperial court politics, they reliably pay their regional and Imperial tributes dutifully and contribute to regional troop conscription without fail.

##### 🤝 Inter-House & Faction Relations

- **The Consignment of Steel:** [[House Bereggia]] wants little part in the political theater of the grander houses, but they are fiercely protective of their borders. They fulfill their conscription quotas perfectly, filtering raw human recruits into the broader imperial infantry lines while keeping their specialized local forces strictly independent.
    
- **Tactical Isolationism:** While neighboring provinces boast massive armies that rely heavily on sheer numbers and crushing infantry blocks, [[House Bereggia]] completely rejects these messy tactics. They rely entirely on a small, hyper-specialized cavalry force to maintain their boundaries, choosing to master the open terrain rather than build massive fortifications.
    

##### 🏹 The [[Longriders]] & The Whistler Signals

Because [[House Bereggia]] rules an unfortified, wide-open grassland, they do not maintain massive standing infantry legions. Instead, their entire military backbone consists of an elite force of crack horsemen known across the realm as the **[[Longriders]]**.

- **The Elite Vanguard:** The [[Longriders]] number only about **800 strong**, but they are famous for being incredibly fierce warriors and expert shots with their longbows (operating at a formidable **3rd to 7th level**). Their battlefield tactics are so vastly superior to raw infantry that they have repeatedly held off invading forces ten times their size through speedy hit-and-run attacks and perfectly synchronized ambushes.
    
- **The Multi-Blade Heads:** For heavily armored targets or monstrous threats, the Longriders unleash distinct, specialized arrows fitted with brutal, multi-blade heads engineered to tear through flesh and armor alike, dealing devastating **$2\text{D}6+4$** damage.
    
- **The Acoustic Network:** To coordinate maneuvers across miles of flat, featureless plains, the [[Longriders]] utilize the famous **"Whistler shaft"**—an arrow fitted with a specially engineered whistle on the end instead of a pointed head. When launched, the arrow emits a loud, piercing sound that cuts through the flat wind for miles. For centuries, the [[Longriders]] have used five distinct pitches of these whistler arrows to relay complex military intelligence instantly across the province, mapped to explicit tactical directives:
    
    1. _Pitch One:_ **"Use Caution"**
        
    2. _Pitch Two:_ **"Enemy Approaching"**
        
    3. _Pitch Three:_ **"Charge the Enemy"**
        
    4. _Pitch Four:_ **"Monsters"**
        
    5. _Pitch Five:_ **"Retreat"**
        

##### 👑 Settlement Profile: Hrodan

- **Type:** Settlement (Regional Capital)
    
- **Population:** 22,000 people
    
- **Description:** By all imperial accounts, Hrodan is a thoroughly ordinary, unremarkable urban center compared to other grand regional hubs in the Western Empire. It features functional stone architecture, standard market squares, and basic defensive walls that control a quiet network of surrounding smaller towns, villages, farms, and ranches.
    
- **Current Status:** While the capital remains stable and continues to dutifully process its administrative tasks and regional tributes, the stark divide between the safe city center and the dangerous, desolate plains continues to widen.
