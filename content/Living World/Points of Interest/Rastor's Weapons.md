## Overview

Rastor's Weapons is the premier shop in [[Delver's Square]] for traditional arms, blade refitting, and specialized martial gear. The shop features a heavy stone facade lined with racks of meticulously polished steel swords, massive greataxes, halberds, and exotic tactical weapons favored by frontline delvers. Unlike standard blacksmiths, [[Cornellius Rastor | Rastor]] caters explicitly to high-risk dungeon-crawlers, stocking masterwork pieces, cold iron armaments, and used magical combat equipment.

## The Iron Alliance Partnership

[[Cornellius Rastor | Rastor]] maintains an exclusive, highly lucrative trade alliance with **The [[Bull and Bear]]** across the square. Because [[Cornellius Rastor | Rastor]] completely lacks the forge facilities to handle heavy armor plate casting and scale-fitting, he operates under a strict referral and supply pact:

- **The Bundle Discount:** Any customer who purchases a primary weapon from [[Cornellius Rastor | Rastor]] receives an iron-stamped token granting a **5% discount on custom armor fittings** over at The [[Bull and Bear]].
    
- **The Trade-In Pipeline:** [[Cornellius Rastor | Rastor]] manages a joint "Used Gear Liquidation" fund. When an adventuring party sells off a mixed haul of battlefield loot, [[Cornellius Rastor | Rastor]] buys the weapons and immediately sends the armor crates over to the [[Bull and Bear]] for melting, refitting, and resale.
