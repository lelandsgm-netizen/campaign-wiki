title: Docks
location: "[[Ptolus]]"

Built on sinking sand, mud, and waterlogged timber at the absolute bottom of the eastern cliffs, **The Docks** is the rough-and-tumble maritime heart of [[Ptolus]]. Shrouded in sea fog and the heavy stench of salt, rotting fish, and cheap ale, this district is home to sailors, smugglers, wharf laborers, and those trying desperately to keep a low profile.

The geography is dominated by a sheer cliffside that separates the harbor from the elevated city proper. Because of this massive drop-end elevation, goods are transported to the higher districts via a complex network of massive mechanical lifts, cranes, and switchback stairs managed by the various merchant cartels. While the [[City Watch]] patrols the main wharves during daylight hours to secure Imperial shipping, their presence vanishes after dark, leaving the district entirely governed by its own rough code of the sea and underworld syndicates.

### Prominent Locations

- **[[Savage Shark | The Savage Shark]]:** A notorious, blood-soaked tavern built right on the water. It is famous across the city for its customary evening entertainment: unstructured, bare-knuckle tavern brawls. Adventurers frequently come here to scout for cutthroats, muscle-for-hire, or rumors of shipwrecks out in the bay.
    
- **[[Sailor's Rest | The Sailor's Rest]]:** The premier dockside inn for travelers. While it is considerably rougher than [[Midtown]]'s establishments, it is widely recommended by locals as the cleanest and safest place to get a bed, a hot meal, and a lockable door near the waterfront.
    
- **[[Shipyards | The Shipyards]]:** A sprawling, heavily industrial stretch of docks and dry docks dominated by independent shipwrights and naval carpenters. **[[House Rau]]** heavily influences these yards, ensuring their merchant galleons and privateering fleets receive elite alchemical hulls and nautical wards.
    
- **[[Lifts | The Lifts]]:** A marvel of early mechanical engineering (originally designed in part by **[[House Shever]]**). These towering, steam-and-pulley powered platforms transport massive cargo crates, horses, and wealthy merchants up the sheer cliffside directly into the markets above.
    

### Prominent People

- **[[Linech Cran | Linech Cran Deceased]]:** A prominent, highly dangerous, and deeply entrenched dockside crime boss. Operating out of a lavishly eccentric parlor near the wharves, Linech manages an extensive network of smuggling rings, deep-sea salvage scams, and black-market acquisitions. Mechanically, he fits as a high-level **Rogue** (Mastermind or Underworld Kingpin) who uses a halfling fixer named **Seanus** to scout marks and recruit disposable adventuring parties for high-risk maritime operations.
    
- **[[Sardell McManus | Old Sard]]:** A weathered, salt-crusted local ferryman and harbor guide. He knows every hidden cove, treacherous reef, and dynamic tide across the [[Bay of Ptolus]]. For a handful of silver coins, he will rent out rowboats or personally pilot parties out into the deeper waters of the [[Inland Sea]]. He serves as an excellent source of information regarding recent shipwrecks or strange aquatic monster sightings.
    

### PF2e Mechanical Identity for Vault Tagging

- **Common Ancestries:** Mostly Human, but heavily populated by **Iruxi (Lizardfolk)** and **Undine** versatile heritages working as specialized divers, salvage scouts, and elite underwater muscle.
    
- **Prevalent Classes:** **Swashbucklers** (Braggart or Wit styles), **Rogues** (Thief or Scoundrel), and **Fighters** with a focus on brawling or agile aquatic weapons.
    
- **Threat Focus:** Encounters here frequently involve **Sahuagin** raiders lurking around coastal shipwrecks, aggressive shoreline sharks, corrupt harbor thugs utilizing pocket poisons, and environmental hazards like thick, vision-blocking sea fog.
