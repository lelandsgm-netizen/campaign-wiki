The **Temple District** is the spiritual heart and religious epicenter of [[Ptolus]]. It is a district of sweeping architectural contrast, where towering, immaculate marble cathedrals sit shoulder-to-shoulder with humble stone shrines and hidden, secretive chapels.

The geography of the district is entirely defined by the **[[Street of a Million Gods]]**, a massive, cobblestone boulevard that bisects the neighborhood. In your _[[Eraedal]]_ setting, the district serves as a theological melting pot where the orthodox, state-sanctioned [[Church of Light]] constantly battles for spiritual and political dominance against a rising tide of traditional Pathfinder 2e faiths. The air here is perpetually thick with the scent of burning incense, the chiming of holy bells, and the chanting of prayers. Because it houses the city's divine power, the district is heavily populated by clerics, champions, and religious pilgrims from all across the [[Western Empire]].

### Prominent Locations

- **The [[Street of a Million Gods]]:** A magnificent, crowded avenue lined on both sides with shrines, temples, and alters dedicated to virtually every deity imaginable. Here, the grand, militaristic cathedrals of **[[Iomedae]]** and the gilded banking-temples of **[[Abadar]]** dominate the skyline, standing alongside prominent holy sites dedicated to **[[Sarenrae]]**, **[[Nethys]]**, **[[Pharasma]]**, and hundreds of minor local gods.
    
- **The [[Priory of Introspection]]:** A vast, austere monastic compound built from pale stone that serves as the absolute headquarters for the **[[Sisterhood of Silence]]**. This quiet sanctuary features serene meditation gardens and sprawling training courtyards where the silent female monks hone their formidable martial arts.
    
- **The [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]:** A colossal, imposing fortress-cathedral representing the official, imperial religion of the [[Western Empire]]. Its stained-glass windows depict the torment and triumph of the martyr Lothian, and its heavily armored paladins guard the grand archives within.
    
- **The Sanctuary of the Sun:** A beautiful, open-air marble temple dedicated to **Sarenrae**. It features a massive, polished brass sun-disk that catches the morning light, serving as a prominent center for healing, charity, and public welfare in the lower half of the district.
    

### Prominent People

- **Prince Kirian Ylestos:** The ambitious, politically ruthless leader of the Church of Lothian in Ptolus. Sitting as a permanent member of the Council of Coin, Kirian views the rapid rise of independent Pathfinder faiths with open hostility. He uses his considerable political influence and religious authority to enforce imperial orthodoxy, levy religious taxes on non-human shrines, and suppress any heretical factions. Mechanically, he is a high-level **Cleric** (Cloistered) who treats faith as a political weapon.
    
- **Mother Superior Solenna (or Orelia Kael):** The silent, deeply revered leader of the **Sisterhood of Silence** (operating out of the Priory of Introspection). She holds a permanent seat on the city's ruling council, though she rarely attends in person, choosing instead to send her votes via a high-ranking sister and a dedicated male eunuch translator. Mechanically, she is a high-level **Monk** (utilizing defensive and non-lethal subduing stances) who approaches city security with absolute, unwavering discipline.
    

### PF2e Mechanical Identity for Vault Tagging

Behind the GM screen, use these Pathfinder 2e baselines to flavor Temple District encounters, divine spellcasting, and holy factions:

- **Common Ancestries:** Human (predominantly traditional Prustan heritages), alongside highly devout **Dwarves** (worshiping Torag) and **Elves** or **Half-Elves** serving as keepers of the arcane/divine balance. **Nephilim (Aasimar)** are highly visible here, viewed as living blessings by the local temples.
    
- **Prevalent Classes:** **Clerics** (both Warpriests and Cloistered), **Champions** (enforcing holy causes), and **Monks** operating within the silent priories.
    
- **Signature Magic:** Divine spellcasters heavily utilize the **Vitality (Positive)**, **Holy**, and **Spirit** traits. Spells like _heal_, _divine wrath_, _sanctuary_, and _dispel magic_ define the defensive and cleansing nature of the district.
    
- **Common Associations:** Strongly aligned with **The Commissar (Igor Urnst)** and the orthodox traditionalists. They maintain a warm, charitable alliance with **House Nagel**, look upon the shadow-wielding **House Sadar** with extreme suspicion, and actively hunt the dark cults of the Necropolis.
