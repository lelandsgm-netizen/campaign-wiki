The Temple District Hub in [[Ptolus]]: [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]

In your Ptolus wiki, the **[[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]** replaces St. Valien's Cathedral. It stands as the grand, white-stone spiritual heart of the Temple District. Rather than honoring a single Imperial saint, its towering spires and stained-glass dome celebrate a unified coalition of lawful/benevolent deities known as the **[[Pantheon of Light]]**.

## The Pantheon of Light (Official PF2e Remaster Mechanics)

Characters who dedicate themselves to the Church of Light as a whole can follow this combined pantheon profile. Alternatively, clerics and champions can select any individual deity listed below as their primary patron.

- **Areas of Concern:** Light, justice, healing, civil order, defense against undead, and the pursuit of wisdom.
    
- **Pantheon Alignment/Tendency:** Lawful Good / Holy
    
- **Divine Font:** _heal_
    
- **Divine Ability:** Strength, Wisdom, or Charisma
    
- **Divine Skill:** Religion
    
- **Favored Weapon:** Longsword or Spear
    
- **Domains:** Sun, Protection, Truth, Healing
    
- **Edicts:** Root out corruption, protect the defenseless, restore order to chaos, destroy undead and fiends on sight.
    
- **Anathema:** Create or knowingly harbor undead, abuse a position of lawful authority, permit a major injustice to pass unchallenged out of cowardice.
