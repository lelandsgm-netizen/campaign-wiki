## House Vladaam: The Scions of Wickedness

House Vladaam is one of the oldest, most ancient noble houses in [[Ptolus]]. While the family has technically lost a fair amount of its official political influence on the Council of Coin in recent centuries, it remains a terrifyingly wealthy and deeply entrenched power.

Publicly, the Vladaams maintain a veneer of eccentric aristocrats, but the common folk of [[Ptolus]] whisper that the family is profoundly corrupt and that literal demonic blood runs through their veins. They are currently locked in a bitter high-society feud with the altruistic [[House Nagel]], and local broadsheets frequently gossip about half-mad Vladaam schemes to ruin the Nagels permanently.

- **Estate Location:** The Vladaam Estate is a sprawling, heavily fortified compound located in the **[[Nobles' Quarter]]**, climbing the city's western cliffs directly abutting the base of the massive [[Spire]]. The estate is rumored to be protected by advanced clockwork marvels and hidden arcane defenses.
    

### Prominent Family Members (Public Knowledge)

#### Lord [[Iristul Vladaam]]

- **Status:** Patriarch & Master of the House (Currently Absent)
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 15+ Nephilim (Tiefling) Fighter or Evil Champion
    
- **Description:** [[Iristul Vladaam | Iristul]] is the official head of the house, though he has not been physically seen in [[Ptolus]] for several years. Rumors suggest he is traveling the wider [[Western Empire]] or [[Old Kingdom]]  on a dark pilgrimage. In his absence, his children handle the family's extensive business holdings and estate.

### [[Navrus Vladaam]]

- **Status:** Eldest Son / Acting Estate Manager
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 10 Nephilim (Tiefling) Rogue (Mastermind or Poisoner)
    
- **Description:** Navrus manages the family's day-to-day legal and mercantile operations in the city. He is a brooding, deeply unpleasant man who rarely smiles. He is often seen accompanied by tight-lipped mercenaries and handles the family's aggressive financial investments.
    

#### [[Alisanna Vladaam]]

- **Status:** Public Face & Socialite
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 9 Nephilim (Tiefling) Witch (Grim or Curse Patron)
    
- **Description:** Glamorous, cruel, and fiercely intelligent, Alisanna represents House Vladaam at grand balls, high-society galas, and occasional diplomatic functions in the Nobles' Quarter. She uses her cutting wit and extensive network of high-society contacts to maintain the family's shield of political immunity.
    

#### [[Godrick Vladaam

- **Status:** The Enforcer
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 8 Nephilim (Tiefling) Alchemist (Mutagenist) or Magus
    
- **Description:** The youngest brother, Godrick is known for a volatile temper and a obsessive fascination with volatile chemicals and rare poisons. He spends his time lingering around the family's shipping warehouses and is whispered to handle the "messier" aspects of protecting Vladaam assets.
    

### PF2e Mechanical Identity for Vault Tagging

To smoothly run House Vladaam encounters or flavor text behind the scenes, use these mechanical baselines:

- **Ancestry:** Humans with the **Nephilim** versatile heritage. Focus heavily on fiendish ancestry feats that manifest dark aspects, such as _Fiendish Resistance_, _Darkvision_, and traits reminiscent of traditional tieflings (horns, sulfurous scent, or cast shadows that seem to move independently).
    
- **Favored Skills:** Deception (Trained/Expert), Intimidation (Expert), Society (Trained), and Poisoning Lore.
    
- **Common Associations:** They are known to patronize independent alchemists, aggressive mercenary bands, and shadowy criminal fronts throughout the Guildsman District and the Docks to do their bidding without dirtying the family name.
