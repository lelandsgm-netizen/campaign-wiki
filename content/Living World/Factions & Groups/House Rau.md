## The Privateers of the Bay

House Rau is universally regarded as the "black sheep" of the traditional [[Ptolus]] aristocracy. While other noble houses trace their lineages back to ancient kings, scholars, or high wizards, House Rau built its ancestral fortune on the high seas through piracy, privateering, and aggressive maritime trade across the [[Whitewind Sea]].

Though they have successfully transitioned into legitimate shipping magnates with a legal hereditary seat on the [[Council of Coin]], the family has never truly shed its rough, salt-crusted edges. They are fierce, loud, and unyielding political wildcards. On the council, they aggressively oppose any measures that introduce harbor tariffs, strict cargo inspections, or expanded [[City Watch]] authority over the waterfront. This stance puts them in a permanent, often bloody street-level feud with the ultra-martial [[House Khatru]].

- **Estate Location:** The Rau Estate breaks the mold of traditional Nobles' Quarter design. It is located on the lower eastern cliffs overlooking the harbor, directly abutting **The Docks** district. The compound resembles a heavily fortified maritime fortress, complete with defensive ballistae pointing out to sea, private sea-caves carved into the cliffside, and a bustling private wharf where the family's finest merchant galleons anchor.
    

### Prominent Family Members (Public Knowledge)

#### Lady [[Verrana Rau]]

- **Status:** Matriarch & Admiral of the Rau Fleet
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 12 Human **Swashbuckler** (Braggart or Wit style) with the _Pirate_ archetype.
    
- **Description:** A fierce, weather-beaten woman who prefers the deck of a flagship to the velvet chairs of the [[Council of Coin]]. [[Verrana Rau | Verrana]] is a commanding presence who wears her family's aristocratic finery over practical leather armor. She commands absolute loyalty from her captains and treats city politics like a naval engagement—using blunt intimidation, sharp wit, and sudden alliances to protect her shipping lanes from Imperial oversight.
    

#### Captain [[Torin Rau]]

- **Status:** Eldest Son & Harbor Master Liaison
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 8 Human **Rogue** (Scoundrel racket)
    
- **Description:** Charming, silver-tongued, and dangerous, Torin handles the family’s shore-side business operations and coordinates with the independent ship captains in the Docks. He is a fixture in the waterfront taverns and high-end [[Midtown]] gambling dens alike. Torin is the master of finding legal loopholes in maritime law, ensuring that Rau cargo vessels are unloaded long before the city inspectors can clear their paperwork.
    

#### "Salt-Eye" [[Martha Rau]]

- **Status:** Family Eldest & Mistress of the Tides
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 9 Human **Witch** (Sea/Wave themed or Wild patron)
    
- **Description:** [[Verrana Rau | Verrana]]’s elderly aunt and the family's spiritual anchor. [[Martha Rau | Martha]] spent decades at sea and is rumored to have bartered with ancient spirits of the [[Whitewind Sea]]. She manages the family’s private shipyards and ensures that every Rau vessel receives specialized alchemical treatments and nautical wards before sailing, keeping them safe from both natural storms and sea monsters.
    

### PF2e Mechanical Identity for Vault Tagging

Behind the scenes, use these Pathfinder 2e baselines to flavor House Rau sailors, bodyguards, and family agents:

- **Ancestry:** Predominantly Human, but they frequently employ **Iruxi (Lizardfolk)** and **Undine** versatile heritages as specialized divers, marine scouts, and elite ship guards due to their natural affinity for water.
    
- **Class Focus:** **Swashbucklers** (focusing on Athletics and Acrobatics), **Rogues** (Scoundrel or Thief), and **Rangers** (with a focus on maritime terrain and crossbows/firearms).
    
- **Signature Archetypes:** _Pirate_, _Horizon Walker_, and _Dual-Weapon Warrior_.
    
- **Common Associations:** Incredibly deep connections throughout **[[The Docks]]** and the independent merchant coalitions. They frequently clash with the **[[City Watch]]** and **[[House Khatru]]**, while maintaining a tense, neutral trade relationship with **[[House Abanar]]** regarding foreign import rights.
