## `Deity - Trudd the Mighty`

> "The mountain does not weep when the storm rages; it stands. Stand with it."

---

### Overview

**Trudd the Mighty** is an ancient dwarven deity of strength, physical endurance, honest labor, and steadfast protection. Unlike more localized ancestral spirits, Trudd represents the unyielding fortitude of the dwarven people—making him a deeply revered figure among the Stonelost exiles who have had to endure the heartbreak of losing Dwarvenhearth.

In the current era of Ptolus, a catastrophic deployment of reality-warping **chaostech** didn't just physically demolish his primary place of worship in northern Midtown; it completely severed his divine connection to the local population, systematically erasing the very *concept* and memory of his name from the minds of the Stonelost enclave.

* **Pantheon:** Dwarven Pantheon / Eraedal Active Deities
* **Areas of Concern:** Strength, Fortitude, Physical Labor, Protection of the Vulnerable, Reclamation
* **Edicts:** Test your physical limits, protect your community from structural decay, stand firm against impossible odds, honor the works of the mason and smith.
* **Anathema:** Abandon your kin in times of hardship, allow fear to dictate your architectural or tactical defense, use chaostech or madness-inducing magic.

### 🌀 The Chaostech Memory Fog & Campaign Milestones

Because chaostech inflicts deep scars upon reality, the memory of Trudd cannot be restored with a simple sermon. It requires localized, heroic acts of spiritual defiance by Willie and his fellow Runebearers.

As the party achieves these campaign milestones, the memory fog lifts for **Dalithor "D.J." Kaled** and his Midtown artisans step-by-step:

#### Milestone 1: Clearing the Foundation (Current State)

* **Action Required:** Willie must fully clear the physical debris and lingering alchemical background radiation from the ruined shrine location in northern Midtown.
* **The Awakening:** The dwarves of the enclave begin feeling a phantom, nostalgic ache when walking past the bridge. They cannot remember the name "Trudd," but they stop using foul language near the ruins out of an instinctual, unexplainable respect.

#### Milestone 2: Re-Consecrating the Altar

* **Action Required:** A significant holy relic or an item blessed by Trudd's portfolio must be returned to the altar, accompanied by a public show of divine might (e.g., Willie performing a miracle or using his Might domain to aid the community).
* **The Awakening:** The names return. Dalithor Kaled suddenly remembers the old battle cries of his youth. The older generation realizes that the "mysterious stories" Willie has been telling their children around the hearth at the *Hammer & Hog* were actually the lost holy parables of their people.

#### Milestone 3: Purging the Source (The Pythoness Connection)

* **Action Required:** Defeating a major source of localized chaotic energy—such as cleansing the immediate threats within **Pythoness House** or disabling a piece of active chaostech.
* **The Awakening:** Full restoration. The enclave remembers their forgotten oaths. The *Hammer & Hog* formally seals its partnership with the party to distribute **"Trudd's Reserve"** bourbon, transforming the drink from a creative moonshine project into a holy communion spirit that celebrates the true return of the Mighty Protector.
