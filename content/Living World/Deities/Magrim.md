# Magrim: The Taskmaster

> [!QUOTE]
> "The mortal forge must eventually grow cold, and the hammer must fall silent. Present your ledger cleanly, honor the paths of those who dug before you, and prepare for the final inspection."
> — *The Overseer's Ledger, Funerary Liturgy of Magrim*

Magrim is the stern, unyielding god of the dead, transitions, ancestral paths, and the dwarven underworld. As the elder brother of Torag, he is the oldest member of the divine family, acting as the solemn custodian who waits at the end of every dwarf's mortal life. Magrim has no interest in battlefield glory or the creative fires of construction; his focus is trained entirely on the spiritual accounting of souls, the preservation of ancestral memory, and the protection of ancient crypts. He ensures that those who labored honestly in life receive their hard-earned rest, while strictly managing the transitions of spirits from the material clay to the eternal vaults of the Outer Planes.

---

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Taskmaster, The Overseer, Lord of the Underworld, Elder Brother
* **Areas of Concern:** The dead, transitions, ancestral paths, final destinies, and the underworld.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal* or *Harm*
* **Divine Attribute:** Constitution or Wisdom
* **Divine Skill:** Religion
* **Favored Weapon:** Warhammer
* **Divine Sanctification:** Can choose Holy (or remain uncommitted)
* **Domains:** Darkness, Death, Earth, Repose

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Grim tendrils*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Talking corpse* (formerly *Speak with dead*)
* **7th-Level Spells:** *Finger of death*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Inter corpses with traditional clan rituals; guard and structurally fortify tombs, barrows, and crypts; complete your daily tasks with absolute discipline; record and honor your family's genealogy and ancestral history.
* **Anathema:** Disturb or rob a grave or ancestral resting place; animate mindless undead creatures or practice predatory necromancy; willfully break a generational family transition or tradition out of laziness; shirk your assigned communal labor obligations.

## Appearance & Iconography

Magrim is traditionally depicted as a burly, weathered dwarf whose skin is composed of dark, polished obsidian or porous volcanic stone. His massive beard is slate-grey, woven with heavy granite rings and carved runic tablets detailing the names of deceased heroes. His eyes are entirely featureless spheres of pale, chalky quartz, representing his absolute, blind impartiality when judging souls. He wears functional, unpolished grey plate armor that carries the scent of fresh grave-dirt and damp subterranean air, holding a massive stone warhammer in one hand and a giant iron ledger in the other.

His holy symbol is a stylized iron ledger stamped with a dark runic symbol for "Finality," or a stone warhammer pointing directly downward into a stylized mountain silhouette. Sacred animals include cave-dwelling scavengers and blind burrowers, particularly moles, cave crickets, and bats.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Guardian of the Deep Gates
Before the monumental **Quest for Sky** split the dwarven ancestry between the surface Citadels and the subterranean depths, Magrim was the primary divine anchor of the Darklands. While Torag managed the physical safety of the early clans, Magrim established the **Deep Vaults of the Rested**—localized planar pockets hidden within the solid stone where the souls of early dwarven miners could commune with their descendants. When the prophecy of the sky arrived, Magrim stepped back to guard the rear lines, protecting the ancestral tombs from being desecrated by pursuing orc hordes or abyssal entities during the centuries-long upward migration.

### The Spiritual Accountant
Central to Kuthite and Pharasmin records is Magrim’s role as the de facto estate manager for dwarven spirits. When a dwarf perishes anywhere in the universe, their soul does not instantly enter the standard line at Pharasma’s crystal throne. Instead, the spirit travels down through the rock to Magrim’s subterranean court in the Boneyard. There, Magrim reviews the soul's mortal ledger, ensuring they fulfilled their oaths, honored their ancestors, and completed their assigned craftsmanship tasks. Once approved, he personally guides them across the border to **Forgeheart**, allowing them to join Torag's eternal divine workforce or rest peacefully within Folgrit's celestial hearth chambers.

## Divine Relationships

Magrim maintains a cold, professional distance from the chaotic squabbles of the multiverse, operating as a pillar of absolute order within his family house.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Torag** | **The Patriarch's Counsel.** As the elder brother, Magrim respects Torag's position as leader of the pantheon. Torag frequently consults him before entering large conflicts, relying on Magrim’s absolute, unblinking pragmatism to predict strategic losses. |
| **Pharasma** | **Professional Alliance.** The Lady of Graves treats Magrim with exceptional courtesy. She permits him to run his auxiliary court within her Boneyard realm, trusting his flawless ledgers completely and allowing his psychopomps to manage dwarven necropolises without interference. |
| **Urgathoa** | **Absolute Enmity.** Urgathoa represents the ultimate rot that perverts life's natural transitions. Her necromancers, ghouls, and vampires directly violate Magrim's edicts, stealing souls from his ledger and leaving ancestral clay to rot in state, making her church a target for his inquisitors. |
| **Droskar** | **The Dark Smith's Shame.** Magrim harbors a profound, burning hatred for Droskar. While Magrim values purposeful, disciplined labor that leads to an honorable rest, Droskar perverts work into endless, soul-crushing toil and slavery, stripping away the spiritual value of a mortal's ledger. |
| **Trudd & Cayden Cailean** | **Severe Disapproval.** Magrim views Trudd’s reckless front-line bravado and his boisterous, casual carousing with Cayden Cailean as incredibly immature and disruptive. He continuously warns Trudd that an unprepared entry into his ledger is a massive dishonor to the family line. |
