# Angradd: The Forge Fire

> [!QUOTE]
> "A fortress wall is only half a victory. When the defile crawls with the enemy, stoke the furnace inside your chest, swing the brand, and consume them before they can touch the hearth."
> — *The Iron Line, Holy Strategy of the Forge Fire*

Angradd is the aggressive, hot-blooded god of war, fire, and offensive military tactics within the dwarven pantheon. As the younger brother of Torag, he stands as the dynamic tactical counterweight to his brother’s stoic, defensive pragmatism. While Torag builds permanent stone battlements and calculates bottleneck holding patterns, Angradd rules the roaring spark of the battlefield—advancing lines, crushing siege weapons, and using defensive aggression to ruthlessly clean out immediate threats. He is the patron of dwarven berserkers, vanguard captains, siege engineers, and any warrior who views fire as a sacred tool of cleansing purification.

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Forge Fire, The King of the Fire Lance, The Rising Vanguard, Younger Brother
* **Areas of Concern:** War, offensive military strategy, tactical fire, and siege craftsmanship.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal* or *Harm*
* **Divine Attribute:** Strength or Constitution
* **Divine Skill:** Athletics
* **Favored Weapon:** Greataxe
* **Divine Sanctification:** Can choose Holy
* **Domains:** Destruction, Fire, Might, Zeal

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Fire ray*
* **3rd-Level Spells:** *Blazing dive*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Fire shield*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Maintain absolute military readiness and peak battlefield discipline; eliminate imminent threats to your community using decisive offensive actions; master the tactical deployment of flame and black powder weaponry; craft lasting weapons of war and siege engines.
* **Anathema:** Flee from a battlefield out of cowardice or leave an ally behind to save yourself; allow an enemy force to encircle or breach your home territory without putting up a proactive fight; intentionally slaughter non-combatants or clean targets out of unguided sadism.

## Appearance & Iconography

Angradd is depicted as a burly, barrel-chested dwarven vanguard completely encased in heavy slate-gray plate mail that continuously shimmers with internal, molten-red radiant energy. His massive, wild beard is composed of crackling, living orange and crimson flames that cast long, dramatic shadow patterns across the stone floor wherever he stands. Unlike his solemn siblings, his expression is one of fierce, hyper-focused intensity. In his calloused hands, he wields *Flametongue*, a colossal, twin-bladed greataxe whose jags constantly leak white-hot liquid metal and volcanic ash sparks.

His holy symbol is a stylized iron greataxe silhouetted directly against a roaring, three-pointed flame crest. Sacred animals include fierce subterranean and apex territorial entities, particularly fire-salamanders, magma-drakes, and aggressive mountain badgers.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Clean Vanguard
During the primordial eons when the deities were carving reality out of raw planar energy, the subterranean deep was packed with nightmarish aberrations, primeval trolls, and ancestral horrors. While Torag sat at the master anvil drafting blueprints for the first fortress layouts, Angradd acted as his elder brother’s absolute martial vanguard. He led the sweeping, cleansing operations that drove these ancient entities out of the upper crust, burning out subterranean nests to clear the literal pocket cavities that would later be forged into the grand Sky Citadels.

### The Breach Catalyst
In traditional dwarven military doctrine, Angradd is invoked whenever a defensive line has suffered a critical collapse. While his nephew Trudd represents the mobile wall that plugs a breach, Angradd is the kinetic payload sent *through* the breach to break the enemy's momentum. His church teaches that static defense is a slow death if an army allows itself to be permanently surrounded. Angradd’s strategy centers on lightning-fast saps, overwhelming counter-charges, and deploying weaponized fire arrays to force a besieging force to panic and break their lines.

## Divine Relationships

Angradd operates as the fiery martial fist of Forgeheart, treating his family with deep loyalty while frequently clashing with their conservative pace.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Torag** | **The Elder's Blade.** Angradd respects Torag’s position as patriarch. While they frequently argue over tactical parameters—Angradd favoring rapid offenses and Torag demanding absolute defensive hold lines—their mutual operational trust is unbreakable. |
| **Trudd** | **The Strategic Vanguard.** Angradd treats his nephew Trudd with high military favor. When Trudd holds the front shield-wall line, Angradd effortlessly coordinates the black-powder sappers and heavy shock troopers to hit the enemy's exposed flank. |
| **Dranngvit** | **Bitter Alliance.** Angradd shares a dark, pragmatic understanding with his half-sister Dranngvit. While Angradd handles the open kinetic movement of war, he relies on Dranngvit’s unyielding scorecards to identify exactly which foreign empires or betrayers require systematic military execution. |
| **Iomedae** | **Martial Discipline Alignment.** The Inheritor and the Forge Fire share an ironclad respect for tactical discipline, honor, and battlefield bravery. While Iomedae approaches war through the lens of chivalric justice, Angradd treats it as a grim, structural engineering necessity to keep clans alive. |
| **Droskar** | **Utter Eradication.** Droskar perverts the purpose of labor into joyless slavery and copies designs out of laziness. Angradd views his deep duergar sweatshops as a total dishonor to the martial spirit, hunting down Droskar's slave-drivers without a shred of quarter. |
| **Cayden Cailean** | **Tavern Friction.** Angradd respects Cayden's raw courage on the battlefield but heavily disapproves of his complete lack of military rank, chaotic fighting style, and tendency to prioritize alcohol over strict strategic planning. |
