# Ketephys: The Bow

> [!QUOTE]
> "The forest does not offer clean choices; it offers paths. Breathe with the wind, draw until the wood groans, and let your arrow speak for the silent wild before the city can cut it away."
> — *The Tracker's Cadence, Liturgy of the Silent Hunt*

Ketephys is the ancient, laconic god of hunting, tracking, woodcraft, and wild predatory beasts within the Bright Elven Court. Representing the untamed frontier of the elven identity, he is the divine warden of deep, old-growth forests and the patron of all who venture into the wild spaces to live sustainably by the bow. Ketephys has no interest in grand municipal codes or metropolitan banking houses. Instead, he governs the unyielding equations of survival—teaching his followers to stalk silently, hit with surgical precision, and honor the natural cycle of the predator and the prey.

---

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Bow, The Hunter, Lord of the Primal Canopy, Sovereign of the Silent Wilderness
* **Areas of Concern:** Hunting, tracking, woodcraft, predatory beasts, and forestry.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal*
* **Divine Attribute:** Dexterity or Wisdom
* **Divine Skill:** Survival
* **Favored Weapon:** Longbow
* **Divine Sanctification:** Can choose Holy (or remain uncommitted)
* **Domains:** Cold, Moon, Nature, Precision

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Sure strike*
* **3rd-Level Spells:** *Wall of thorns*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Freedom of movement*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Hunt explicitly for sustenance, population management, or local community defense; master the art of the bow and woodcraft tracking; respect and mimic the clean lethality of apex predators; preserve pristine old-growth forests from reckless urban over-expansion.
* **Anathema:** Overhunt an ecosystem or leave the carcass of a kill to waste out of laziness; trap, cage, or display a wild creature for idle domestic amusement or spectacle; cause a forest fire through pure negligence; torture or leave a wounded beast to suffer needlessly.

## Appearance & Iconography

Ketephys is depicted as a exceptionally athletic, sun-bronzed elven hunter dressed in form-fitting leathers and camouflage cloaks woven from fresh moss and duskwood bark. His features are hard-edged and weathered by the outdoor elements, and his eyes glow with the intense, reflective green day-glow of a nocturnal hunting cat. He moves through visions with an absolute, ghost-like silence, carrying *Ashta*—a magnificent, longbow crafted out of polished white ironwood that never errors or loses its string tension. He is almost always accompanied by *Meyon*, a majestic, ink-black divine elven panther that stalks silently at his heels.

His holy symbol is a single silver arrow splitting a pristine green leaf vertically, or a crossed bow and hunting knife positioned beneath a silver crescent moon. Sacred animals include all nocturnal apex trackers and hunters, particularly panthers, hawks, lynxes, and silver-tipped wolves.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The First Guardian of the Canopy
In the prehistoric history of the elven ancestry, before cities of glass and telescoping magic bridges were elevated, the elves lived as nomadic tribes wandering through primeval forests. During this era of dangerous giants and giant dinosaurs, Ketephys walked the Material Plane directly as their supreme hunting guide. He taught early elves how to read the hidden language of the soil, track massive prey across regional continents, and weaponize the longbow to defend their camps from subterranean and monstrous intrusions, cementing the longbow as a central pillar of elven cultural defense.

### The Culling of the Rabid Packs
During the ancient cosmic war when **Lamashtu** consumed the beast-god Curchanus, a wave of chaotic, abyssal madness rippled across the natural world. Thousands of species of apex predators transformed into rabid, mutated abominations that hunted for the sake of torture rather than survival. Ketephys launched a world-spanning counter-offensive known as the **Great Culling**. Moving from canopy to canopy, he systematically tracked and put down these corrupted packs, preserving clean biological baselines and protecting elven enclaves from being utterly consumed by the early monstrous tides.

## Divine Relationships

Ketephys treats planar politics with practical survivalism, preferring quiet individual pacts with wild spirits over elaborate diplomatic treaties.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Desna** | **The Night Stalker.** Desna paints the very stars and crescent moon lines that Ketephys uses to navigate during nocturnal hunts. They share a deep, quiet mutual understanding within the `pantheon/stars`, with Ketephys guarding her traveling pilgrims whenever their roads cross into dense wilderness canopies. |
| **Yuelral** | **Crafting Synergy.** The Queen of Crystals rejects the smoke-choked smelters of industry, a stance Ketephys fiercely supports. Ketephys relies on her master crystal-weavers and alchemists to cultivate the organic ironwood bows and specialized stealth oils his rangers deploy on the front lines. |
| **Erastil** | **Primal Tense affinity.** Old Deadeye and The Bow share an ironclad respect for hunting, woodcraft, and archery. However, Erastil seeks to subtly domesticate the wild—clearing timber to plant arable wheat fields and establish small farming villages. Ketephys rejects this, demanding that the wilderness remain raw, untamed, and free of municipal fences. |
| **Lamashtu** | **Absolute Eradication.** The Mother of Monsters mutates natural beasts into nightmarish abominations. Ketephys views her creations as a direct corruption of the natural predatory hierarchy, commanding his hunters to execute her cultists and track down her monsters without quarter. |
| **Gozreh** | **Reverence for the Wild.** Ketephys views Gozreh as the literal terrain of his eternal hunt. He bows to Gozreh’s shifting weather patterns and tides, treating the Wind and the Waves with deep, survivalist humility. |
