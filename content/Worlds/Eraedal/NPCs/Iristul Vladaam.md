## 😈 Overview & Personality

**Lord Iristul Vladaam** is the dread, tyrannical patriarch of **[[House Vladaam]]**, the most feared noble dynasty in **[[Ptolus]]**. While other patrician houses play games of trade tariffs on the [[Council of Coin]] or host glittering masquerades in the [[Nobles' Quarter]], Iristul operates with ancient, multi-generational malice.

Iristul is aristocratic, cold, and possessed of immense personal power and tactical brilliance. He views ordinary mortals—and even fellow nobles like [[Kirstol Dallimothan]]—as fleeting, fragile cattle. Born of an ancient lineage steeped in pacts with abyssal lords, Iristul believes that House Vladaam’s divine right is absolute sovereignty over the [[Western Empire]].

For the past several years, Iristul has been physically absent from his grim estate on the Spire cliffs. He has been scouring ancient ruins across the **[[Old Kingdom]]** and forgotten tombs in the **[[Baalgor Wastelands]]**, hunting down dark primordial relics—the legendary *Banes*—to assemble the artifacts needed to breach the deep vaults of the **[[Banewarrens]]**. In his absence, his children—[[Navrus Vladaam]], [[Alisanna Vladaam]], and [[Godrick Vladaam]]—maintain the family’s iron grip over illicit trade, assassination contracts, and alchemy laboratories in Ptolus.

## 🗡️ Physical Appearance

When present, Iristul cuts an imposing, terrifying figure. Standing six feet, four inches tall, he has broad, heavily armored shoulders and a posture of effortless aristocratic command. His heritage manifests unmistakably in his demonic features: his skin is a pale ash-gray, his eyes burn like glowing coals with no visible pupils, and two swept-back, obsidian horns curl above his temples through his mane of raven-black hair.

He wears ornate, masterwork full plate forged from midnight-blue adamantine inlaid with weeping crimson runes that glow faintly in darkness. At his hip hangs *Nathil-Vond*, a sentient, jagged bastard sword that drinks the blood of his victims. Even when conversing politely, the faint scent of ozone and brimstone lingers in the air around him.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Courier from the Wastelands:** A ragged messenger arrives at the Vladaam estate bearing a lead canister sealed with Iristul’s private glyph, recovered from the [[Baalgor Wastelands]]. The party intercepts the courier and discovers that Iristul has located the *Black Grail* and is returning to Ptolus to crack the **[[Banewarrens]]**.
2. **The Estate Infiltration:** With Iristul away, Lord Zavere of **[[Castle Shard]]** hires the party to infiltrate the subterranean vaults beneath the Vladaam compound to steal a fragmented parchment detailing the Banewarren iris-gates before Navrus and Godrick can decode it.
3. **The Shadow Over the Council:** Iristul sends a magical astral projection into the [[Council of Coin]] chamber, delivering an ultimatum: the noble houses must surrender all excavated relics from [[Dwarvenhearth]] to House Vladaam, or his dark legions will march upon the city.
