## 🐉 Overview & Personality

**Lord Kirstol Dallimothan** is the proud, honorable patriarch of **[[House Dallimothan]]**, the famed "House of the Dragon." While other noble houses scheme in shadows or hoard gold coins in counting houses, Kirstol conducts himself according to an ancient, unyielding code of chivalry and martial strength.

Kirstol is charismatic, booming of voice, and fiercely dedicated to the defense and nobility of **[[Ptolus]]**. He believes that true aristocracy is defined not by birthright, but by the burden of protection. His family’s ancestral bond with true dragons—dating back over a thousand years to the founding epochs of the empire—has infused his lineage with draconic majesty and vitality.

Despite his noble station, Kirstol is a frontline warrior who frequently straps on his polished dawnsilver armor to lead aerial patrols over the [[Bay of Ptolus]] atop his bonded silver dragon-drake, *Caelith*. He is a staunch ally of Commissar **[[Igor Urnst]]**, supporting secular law and order, and holds an intense, open enmity toward the decadent tieflings of **[[House Vladaam]]**.

---

## 🛡️ Physical Appearance

Lord Kirstol is a striking, broad-chested man in his early forties, radiating health, power, and regal dignity. His shoulder-length golden-blonde hair frames a square, sun-tanned jaw and sharp, piercing amber eyes that faintly resemble dragon slashes. Subtle draconic scales of pale gold shimmer faintly along his cheekbones and temples in bright sunlight.

He wears ornate full plate forged from polished steel and white gold, embossed with stylized dragons coiling around the breastplate. A heavy crimson cape clasped with a carved dragon-tooth rests over his left pauldron. At his side rests *Dragonfang*, an ancestral +2 greater striking greatsword whose blade crackles with pale lightning whenever drawn.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Poisoned Drake:** Kirstol’s favored drake, *Caelith*, has fallen deathly ill from an unknown alchemical neurotoxin. Kirstol suspects an assassin from [[House Vladaam]] infiltrated the aerial rookeries, and he hires the party to find the antidote in the undercity before the beast dies.
2. **The Aerial Escort:** Kirstol contracts the party to secure a subterranean landing platform below the cliffs of [[Nobles' Quarter]], allowing Dallimothan knights to airlift stranded survivors during a subterranean breach from [[Dwarvenhearth]].
3. **The Banewarren Warning:** Kirstol receives an omen from an ancient brass dragon nesting in the Koerdian mountains warning that the awakening of the **[[Banewarrens]]** will attract chromatic terrors to Ptolus. He requests the party’s scouting report from the broken threshold.
