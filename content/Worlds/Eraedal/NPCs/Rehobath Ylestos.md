## 👑 Overview: The Holy Emperor in Exile

**Holy Emperor Rehobath Ylestos** is the spiritual titan and political lightning rod of the eastern [[Western Empire]]. As the supreme pontiff of the Imperial Church of Aroden and the self-proclaimed rightful sovereign of the Lion-Guarded Throne, Rehobath embodies the supreme convergence of divine dogma and imperial ambition.

When the imperial capital of Caer Itom fell to the Cyclopean Vanguard and barbarian hordes in **702 IA**, Rehobath refused to view the calamity as mere military defeat. Instead, he declared the sack of Caer Itom to be divine chastisement against centuries of aristocratic decadence and imperial impiety. Gathering his most loyal prelates, paladins, and treasuries, Rehobath journeyed east to the coastal metropolis of **[[Ptolus]]**, where his son, Prince Kirian, had maintained a permanent high ecclesiastical seat since 682 IA.

Upon his arrival, Rehobath took up residence within the newly consecrated Holy Palace in the [[Temple District]] and proclaimed **[[Ptolus]] as the Holy Capital of the Western Empire**. He crowned himself Holy Emperor, demanding absolute fealty from all imperial provinces and declaring all other claimants—including Empress Lasha Itomas in Caer Glaverius and Emperor Skluud Ne'Klosh in Caer Kurgas—to be heretics and traitors to the realm.

## ⚖️ Political Maneuvers in Ptolus

Though Rehobath commands immense spiritual authority and the fanatical devotion of thousands across the [[Tarldet Plains]] and [[Oceanside]], his supremacy within the city walls is far from absolute:

- **The Standoff with Dalenguard:** Secular rule within Ptolus is fiercely maintained by Imperial Commissar **[[Igor Urnst]]**, who commands the eight-hundred-strong [[City Watch]] and imperial legionnaires. Urnst treats Rehobath with icy diplomatic decorum, acknowledging his holy rank while firmly rejecting any church decree that would seize civic authority, violate the city's anti-slavery laws, or conscript citizens into foreign crusades.
- **The Financial Chokehold:** The merchant oligarchs of the **[[Council of Coin]]** view Rehobath's ambitions with deep suspicion. While they gladly accept the influx of wealthy church pilgrims pouring into the city through the [[Bay of Ptolus]], they resist paying direct imperial tithes into his holy war-chest.
- **The Agrarian Shield:** Rehobath's most reliable territorial backing comes from the neighboring province of [[Oceanside]]. The agrarian lords of House Haishe revere the Holy Emperor and willingly tithe food shipments to sustain the Holy Palace, hoping that the Holy Emperor's presence will shield them from the ravages of the [[Three-way Civil War]].

## 🎭 Persona & Roleplaying Guide

- **Voice & Tone:** Deep, resonant, and effortlessly commanding. Rehobath speaks not as a supplicant to power, but as the living conduit of divine right. His words are measured, elegant, and laced with absolute certainty.
- **Core Motivation:** To unify the fractured [[Western Empire]] under the benevolent, disciplined rule of the Imperial Church, restoring moral purity and driving out demonic and monstrous incursions.
- **Deepest Fear:** The insidious corruption of the [[Galchutt]] and the Old Ones slumbering beneath Ptolus. Though he publicizes the city as a sacred sanctuary, his private prayers are filled with dread over the dark vibrations radiating from the deep subterranean chambers beneath the [[Spire]].

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Commissar's Leverage:** Commissar Urnst hires the party to discreetly investigate a shipment of ancient church relics recovered from [[Dwarvenhearth]] destined for the Holy Palace, suspecting that the artifacts are tainted with chaos magic from the sleeping Galchutt.
2. **The Pilgrimage Escort:** Agents of the Holy Seat recruit the party to safeguard a convoy of high-ranking church envoys travelling from the [[Sea of Scarlet Waters]] through the monster-haunted [[Scarlet Mountains]] to Ptolus.
3. **The Inverted Pyramid Parley:** Rehobath requires a neutral, deniable intermediary to deliver an ultimatum to the arcane masters of the [[Inverted Pyramid]] regarding their clandestine experiments in the undercity.
