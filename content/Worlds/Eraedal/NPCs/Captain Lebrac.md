## 📝 Overview

**Captain Lebrac** is one of the most celebrated and enigmatic figures sailing the southern waters of Eraedal. Unlike the bloodthirsty cutthroats of the **[[Tortugo|Pirate Federation]]** or the ruthless privateers serving the **[[Western Empire]]**, Lebrac is governed by a strict, unyielding personal code of honor. He commands a flotilla of three swift raiding vessels and wages a relentless personal vendetta exclusively against the slave galleons and military cruisers of the Western Empire and the **[[Land of the South Winds]]**.

---

## 👥 Public Reputation & Corsair Code

Across the taverns of [[Tortugo]] and the docks of [[Vernazza]], Lebrac is regarded with a mixture of reverence and wariness:
- **The Slaver's Bane**: Lebrac refuses to trade in human cargo, ransom helpless passengers, or sell captives. When his ships seize imperial galleons, he breaks the chains of enslaved Orcs, Wolfen, and human galley slaves, offering them freedom or passage to safe harbors.
- **The Code of Iron & Salt**: He engages only warships, military convoys, and slaver vessels that pursue or threaten his fleet. He considers taking a life without just cause to be an unforgivable stain upon a sailor's honor.
- **Crew Loyalty**: His crews are fanatically devoted to him. His inner circle includes his second-in-command, an Elven primal air-shaper named **Sig**, and his personal aide and quartermaster, **Karvor**—a remarkably erudite, towering Ogre of immense physical strength and tactical intellect.

### Signature Equipment & Relics
- **Invisible Broadsword**: An ancient enchanted blade permanently veiled from sight, striking with lethal precision before an opponent can anticipate its guard.
- **Leather of Iron**: Supple enchanted sea-leather imbued with the defensive resilience of tempered steel plate.
- **Medallion of the Mystic Eye**: A talisman granting the power of levitation and shielding the bearer's mind from psychic intrusion.
- **Gem of Direction**: A flawless lodestone relic that always points toward true magnetic north across uncharted waters.

## 🕵️‍♂️ GM Secrets: The Hidden Sanctuary & The Ancient Wisp

> [!danger] DM Hidden Lore
> ### The Secret Hideaway
> Hidden within the uncharted interior of an isolated, jungle-choked atoll in the Floenry chain lies Lebrac's fortified haven—a fourteen-structure complex hidden entirely beneath the rainforest canopy:
> - **The Compound**: Features a ropehouse, carpentry docks stocked with ship-repair lumber and pitch, bunkhouses, animal pens, and a two-story main hall with a concealed subterranean storm cellar.
> - **The Inner Vault**: A double-hidden compartment in the storm cellar holds iron lockboxes containing over 3,800 gp in gems, emergency healing draughts, and masterwork armaments known only to Lebrac and his captains.
>
> ### The Colossal Tree & The Will-O'-The-Wisp
> Near the island's center stands an awe-inspiring, primeval sentinel: an 800-foot-tall ironwood tree with a 30-foot trunk and a 200-foot sprawl of gnarl-twisted roots that has shaded the forest floor for over a thousand years.
> 
> Within this ancient giant dwells a benevolent woodland spirit—a primal **Will-O'-The-Wisp** of ancient age, served by courts of tree sprites, faeries, and bogies.
> 
> - **The Pact of Blood & Bark**: Lebrac earned the spirit's eternal trust decades ago when he personally executed twelve mutinous crewmen caught attempting to torment faerie folk outside the tree's shadow. Later, Lebrac and his men risked their lives to defend the sacred grove from an incursory abomination from the outer seas.
> - **The Spirit's Vault**: In recognition of his valor, the Will-O'-The-Wisp allows Lebrac to bury his primary hoard deep beneath its colossal roots. When Lebrac approaches, the roots willingly untangle and part:
>   - Over 22,400 silver and gold coins, and 20,000 gp in uncut gems.
>   - A ceremonial suit of solid gold plate armor once forged for imperial royalty.
>   - Two ounces of powdered dragon bone, 44 rare lotus petals, and iron boxes of alchemical truth serums, water-breathing draughts, and superior restorative elixirs.
>   - Any intruder attempting to breach the roots without Lebrac's blessing is instantly entangled and crushed by 20,000 hit points of sentient ancient ironwood.
