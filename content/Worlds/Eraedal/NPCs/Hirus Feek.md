## Overview & Personality

Hirus Feek is the elder of the two brothers who own and operate _The [[Bull and Bear]]_. While his brother [[Sholum Feek | Sholum]] handles the roaring heat of the forge, Hirus is the pragmatic, business-minded public face of the shop. He is a pragmatic, no-nonsense man who understands that in a city surrounded by a lethal underground dungeon, good armor isn't a luxury—it's a basic requirement to see tomorrow's sunrise.

Hirus speaks in a calm, gravelly baritone and treats adventurers with a dry, professional respect. He handles the math, negotiates material costs with traveling caravans, and meticulously inspects every plate-seam and leather strap before it leaves the blockhouse. While he can appear tightly wound or overly bureaucratic, his care stems from a deep burden of responsibility; he genuinely hates seeing young, enthusiastic delvers march down the [[Delver's Stair]] only to return a week later in a pine box destined for the [[Necropolis]].

## Physical Appearance

Hirus is a broad, barrel-chested human, though his frame is leaner and more weathered than his massive brother's. He has short, receding steel-gray hair and a thick, neatly trimmed beard. He typically wears heavy wool shirts under a pristine, stiff leather apron that features countless specialized pockets holding charcoal pencils, measuring tapes, and calculating ledgers. A massive ring of heavy iron vault keys hangs permanently from his belt, clanging rhythmically against his hip as he walks.
