## 🛡️ Overview: The Iron Pillar of Ptolus

**Commissar Igor Urnst** is the supreme civil and military authority of **[[Ptolus]]**. A seasoned veteran of the Western Empire’s imperial legionnaire officer corps, Urnst was dispatched to Ptolus years before the empire fractured, tasked with securing the distant eastern port and maintaining the fortified imperial presence at **[[Dalenguard]]**.

When the Western Empire descended into the chaos of the **[[Three-way Civil War]]** in 702 IA, Urnst made the fateful decision to place the preservation of Ptolus and the rule of secular imperial law above regional factions. While he formally acknowledges the historical prestige of the Imperial Church and permits Holy Emperor **[[Rehobath Ylestos]]** to hold court in the Holy Palace, Urnst has steadfastly refused to surrender civic, judicial, or military command of Ptolus to church authorities.

Under Urnst’s iron-fisted yet pragmatic administration, Ptolus has remained a beacon of relative order amidst a crumbling continent:
- He vigorously enforces the city's ban on slavery, despite immense pressure from southern imperial houses.
- He maintains armed neutrality in the Three-way Civil War, refusing to march his eight-hundred-strong garrison into foreign meat-grinders.
- He holds the line against the encroaching madness bubbling up from subterranean vaults and the ancient prison beneath the **[[Spire]]**.

1. **The Eight Hundred:** The [[City Watch]] of Ptolus is not a ragtag town militia, but a disciplined, heavily equipped paramilitary force composed of heavy crossbowmen, pikemen, war-clerics, and arcanists trained to dispel unauthorized evocations.
2. **The Dungeon Gate Quarantine:** Urnst personally oversees the security protocol for the subterranean Dungeon Gate beneath Dalenguard, ensuring that subterranean horrors, necromancers, and chaos beasts crawling up from [[Dwarvenhearth]] are put to the sword before reaching the surface streets.
3. **Naval Patrols:** Working alongside harbor marshals, Urnst coordinates coastal patrol cutters across the [[Bay of Ptolus]], keeping naval traffic safe from Koerdian corsairs and deep-sea predators sweeping in from the [[Inland Sea]].

## 🎭 Persona & Roleplaying Guide

- **Voice & Tone:** Gravelly, clipped, and unvarnished. Urnst speaks like an officer who has spent forty years sleeping on campaign cots and reviewing casualty reports. He has zero patience for theatrical flattery, political posturing, or high-minded philosophical debates.
- **Core Motivation:** Preserving civic stability, protecting ordinary citizens from being pulverized between competing imperial factions, and preventing ancient horrors under the city from extinguishing mortal life.
- **Attitude Toward Delvers:** Urnst views adventurers with weary skepticism. While he acknowledges that their excavations bring immense wealth into Ptolus and unearth critical defensive lore, he considers them reckless mercenaries who frequently poke holes in dangerous subterranean dams and unleash eldritch plagues upon his streets.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Smuggler's Wharf:** Urnst contacts the party to intercept an illegal shipment of alchemical fire and black powder docking at the [[Docks]] along the [[Bay of Ptolus]], suspected to be sponsored by [[House Balacazar]] to incite gang riots in the [[Warrens]].
2. **Subterranean Breach:** Alarms toll from the deepest vaults of Dalenguard—a section of the ancient catacombs below the citadel has caved in, opening a passage directly into the upper reaches of [[Dwarvenhearth]]. Urnst needs an elite squad of delvers to descend and seal the breach before horrors break into the garrison.
3. **The Diplomatic Razor:** When a delegation of inquisitors dispatched by Holy Emperor [[Rehobath Ylestos]] attempts to arrest a visiting elven scholar on the steps of the Guildsman District, Urnst employs the party to act as neutral peacekeepers and extract the scholar to neutral ground without starting an armed street war between the City Watch and the Holy Guard.
