## ⚖️ Overview & Personality

**Brother Fabien Vance** is the grim, iron-jawed warden who holds the line between the living citizens of **[[Ptolus]]** and the restless dead of the **[[Necropolis]]**. Stationed in the fortified barbican of the **[[Gates of Lead]]**, Fabien has spent twenty-eight brutal years putting skeletons, ghouls, and wights back into the cold earth.

Fabien is a man forged from steel, prayer, and incense. He speaks in short, clipped sentences, completely devoid of theatrical piety or high-court hypocrisy. Having witnessed dozens of young crusaders torn to pieces by undead ambushes in the misty hills, he has no patience for reckless delvers who treat grave-robbing as a romantic adventure. If a party approaches the gates ill-prepared or carrying stolen consecrated relics, Fabien will turn them away with blistering reprimands.

Despite his severe demeanor, Fabien possesses a profound sense of duty and compassion for the common people. He personally escorts weeping widows to family mausoleums during daylight hours, shields common gravediggers with his own tower shield, and ensures that the poor receive consecrated burial rites without extortionate temple tithes.

## 🛡️ Physical Appearance

Brother Fabien is a broad-shouldered, weathered man in his late fifties, his face carved with deep fatigue lines and a prominent, puckered scar stretching from his right ear to his collarbone—a lingering memento from a vampire spawn's claw. His iron-gray hair is cropped in a severe military buzz, and his steel-colored eyes scan the graveyard horizon with perpetual, unblinking vigilance.

He is rarely seen outside his heavy, sanctified suit of full plate armor, forged from blackened steel inlaid with silver script detailing Pharasmin litanies and glowing sunburst emblems of the [[Order of Light]]. Across his broad back rests a massive, masterwork silvered warhammer (*The Final Rest*), and his belt brims with vials of holy water, consecrated oils, and silver stakes.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Midnight Rescue:** Two teenage scions from [[House Dallimothan]] slipped into the Necropolis on a dare before sundown and failed to return before the gates locked. Fabien cannot abandon his post on the battlements, so he hires the party to breach the foggy hills and rescue the nobles before the Forsaken find them.
2. **The Necrotic Bounty:** Fabien offers a 400 gp church bounty and a cache of holy water to any company that eliminates a monstrous, four-armed ghast lord lurking in the catacombs of the **[[Ossuary]]**.
3. **The Corrupted Relic:** Fabien discovers that a wagon of consecrated burial shrouds sent from the [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]] was swapped en route with shrouds soaked in ghoul bile. He suspects an inside traitor in the **[[City Watch]]** working for the **[[Fallen]]**, and enlists the party to find the mole.
