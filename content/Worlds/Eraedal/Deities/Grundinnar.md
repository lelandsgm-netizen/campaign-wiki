# Grundinnar: The Peacemaker

> [!QUOTE]
> "A wall can repel a siege, and a forge can shape a blade, but only an open hand and an honest word can truly hold a kingdom together. Break bread with your neighbor before you draw your steel."
> — *The Accord of the Hearth, Holy Scripture of the Peacemaker*

Grundinnar is the warm, diplomatic god of friendship, community cohesion, hospitality, and open negotiation. As a prominent son of Torag and Folgrit, his divine portfolio serves as the primary external face and internal glue of the dwarven people. While his father constructs defensive strongholds and his brother Kols monitors the absolute letter of legal contracts, Grundinnar works within the fluid space of personal trust and interpersonal bonds. He champions the idea that individual allies are a clan’s most valuable defensive barriers, dedicating his light to resolving generational blood feuds and bridging alliances with foreign ancestries.

> [!WARNING] Modern Planar Status
> Following the devastating, world-shaking events of the **Godsrain**, Grundinnar has been marked as **catastrophically missing**. His planar throne sits empty, and his quiet disappearance has left the dwarven people spiritually fractured and politically isolated across many regional frontiers.

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Peacemaker, The Emissary of the Forge, The Guest-Lord, The Shield of Friendship
* **Areas of Concern:** Friendship, diplomacy, hospitality, conflict resolution, and clan loyalty.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal*
* **Divine Attribute:** Charisma or Wisdom
* **Divine Skill:** Diplomacy
* **Favored Weapon:** Warhammer
* **Divine Sanctification:** Can choose Holy
* **Domains:** Community, Diplomacy, Family, Protection

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Charm*
* **2nd-Level Spells:** *Calm*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Suggestion*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Actively mediate disputes before they erupt into bloodshed; extend warm, unconditional hospitality to travelers and guests; forge cultural and economic ties with non-dwarven ancestries; forgive old slights to end family feuds.
* **Anathema:** Intentionally violate a formal truce, armistice, or peace treaty; abuse, poison, or exploit a guest who has accepted shelter or broken bread under your roof; intentionally stoke a blood feud to advance your own house's wealth or political standing.

## Appearance & Iconography

Before his modern disappearance, Grundinnar was traditionally depicted as a handsome, wide-shouldered dwarven diplomat with a bright, welcoming face and an exceptionally well-groomed, chestnut-colored beard braided with smooth silver rings and olive branches. He wears decorative, non-restrictive chain mail covered by rich, flowing velvet tabards of emerald green and gold filigree. He is never shown with a raised weapon; instead, he holds out a pair of open, unarmored hands, or is depicted extending a heavy golden chalice filled with sweet mead to toast a newly signed alliance.

His holy symbol is a stylized image of two dwarven hands firmly clasping a single, unadorned stone ring, or a stone archway flanked by twin blooming olive boughs. Sacred animals include those known for cooperative group survival and high social alignment, such as wolves that protect the pack, hunting hounds, and migrating swallows.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Great Emissary
In ancient history, as the dwarves successfully emerged from the Darklands onto Eraedal's surface world to establish their massive **Sky Citadels**, they found themselves entirely surrounded by foreign, suspicious surface empires. While Torag’s vanguard prepared for total, defensive warfare, it was Grundinnar who stepped forward to secure their geopolitical survival. 

He personally traveled to the ancient elven courts and early human kingdoms, breaking bread, matching trade commodities, and drafting the fundamental treaties of mutual defense that allowed the Sky Citadels to integrate into global commerce. He proved to his stubborn family that a wall built with a neighbor's goodwill is a barrier that never needs to be defended.

### The Quenching of Iron
Within the internal politics of dwarven society, Grundinnar’s church operates as the supreme arbiters of **Clan Law**. Dwarves are canonically a deeply honorable but incredibly stubborn ancestry; a single slight or minor border dispute can easily spiral into a multigenerational, bloody blood feud that drains a fortress's resources for centuries. 

Grundinnar’s priests are highly trained psychological counselors, professional negotiators, and legal cross-examiners. They step between warring houses, using sacred brewing rituals, public historical readings, and mandatory asset-sharing contracts to quench the fires of hatred, forcing rival houses to settle their grievances through commerce and marriages rather than the edge of an axe.

## Divine Relationships

Grundinnar was universally beloved as the master diplomat of Forgeheart, using his interpersonal grace to maintain soft open channels between rival cosmic groups.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Torag & Folgrit** | **The Trusted Voice.** Torag heavily relied on Grundinnar to act as his official emissary to the surface world. Folgrit works in perfect harmony with him, knowing that internal community peace is the single greatest shield a mother can give her children. |
| **Kols** | **The Strategic Partnership.** They form the twin pillars of dwarven governance. Kols monitors the cold, absolute text of a treaty, while Grundinnar cultivates the warm, personal friendships and shared trust that ensure both sides actually *want* to honor the document. |
| **Trudd** | **The Sibling Protector.** Trudd find his brother's endless diplomatic monologues completely exhausting, but loves him fiercely. Following Grundinnar's disappearance after the Godsrain, Trudd has taken his brother's loss incredibly hard, aggressively searching the lower planes for any trace of his capture. |
| **Abadar** | **Natural Trade Ally.** The Master of the Vault highly values Grundinnar's work. Peaceful external relations and community stability are the absolute baselines required for international merchant guilds and banking networks to flourish. |
| **Asmodeus** | **The Diplomatic Block.** Asmodeus utilizes legal loop-holes to turn treaties into enslavement webs. Grundinnar aggressively counters this, teaching mortals that a treaty must benefit *both* communities equally to have true structural integrity, blocking Hell's contract traps. |
| **Droskar** | **Absolute Outcast.** Droskar’s philosophy of endless, unrewarding slave labor destroys the very concept of community fellowship and hospitality. Grundinnar considers him a complete, soul-rotted perversion of dwarven culture. |
