## Deities of the Pantheon

These four core Pathfinder 2e Remaster deities form the foundation of the [[Church of Light]] within [[Eraedal]] and is the Imperial Religion of The [[Western Empire]], mapping perfectly to the leadership, healing, wisdom, and judicial traits of the original setting.

### [[Iomedae]] (The Inheritor)

_Matches the leadership, righteous warfare, and protective crusade aspects of the Church._

- **Role in the Church:** The Shield of the Radiance. She is the patron of the city's paladins, the [[Igor Urnst | Commissar]]’s elite martial forces , and those defending the city from the horrors of the [[N]ecropolis]].
    
- **Sanctification:** Can choose Holy
    
- **Divine Skill:** Society
    
- **Favored Weapon:** Longsword
    
- **Domains:** Confidence, Justice, Might, Zeal
    
- **Spells Granted:** 1st: _weapon surge_, 2nd: _enlarge_, 4th: _fire shield_
    

### [[Sarenrae]] (The Dawnflower)

_Matches the sun god/goddess archetype, bringing literal light, healing, and redemption._

- **Role in the Church:** The Sovereign Sun. Her presence dominates the high altars of the [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]. She inspires the _[[Brotherhood of Redemption]]_ in their efforts to salvage lost souls.
    
- **Sanctification:** Can choose Holy
    
- **Divine Skill:** Medicine
    
- **Favored Weapon:** Scimitar
    
- **Domains:** Fire, Healing, Sun, Truth
    
- **Spells Granted:** 1st: _burning hands_, 3rd: _fireball_ (Note: Strictly regulated by city watch ), 4th: _rejuvenating flames_
    

### [[Erastil]] (Old Deadeye)

_Matches the foundational, community-driven, law-and-order aspect of a decaying empire's traditional society._

- **Role in the Church:** The Pillars of Law. [[Erastil]] represents the preservation of civil society, family safety, and the preservation of [[Eraedal]]'s historical infrastructure against the encroaching chaos.
    
- **Sanctification:** Can choose Holy
    
- **Divine Skill:** Survival
    
- **Favored Weapon:** Longbow
    
- **Domains:** Earth, Family, Nature, Wealth
    
- **Spells Granted:** 1st: _heal_, 2nd: _animal messenger_, 3rd: _wall of thorns_
    

### [[Abadar]] (The Master of the First Vault)

_Matches the strict legal, bureaucratic, and city-building aspects of the traditional setting._

- **Role in the Church:** The Golden Scale. [[Abadar]] ensures that the city's laws, identification paper mandates , commercial trade in markets, and architectural order are maintained alongside the faith.
    
- **Sanctification:** None or Holy
    
- **Divine Skill:** Society
    
- **Favored Weapon:** Crossbow
    
- **Domains:** Cities, Earth, Travel, Wealth
    
- **Spells Granted:** 1st: _alarm_, 4th: _transmute rock to mud_, 7th: _magnificent mansion_
