# Gozreh: The Wind and the Waves

> [!QUOTE]
> "The sky does not weep for your empires, nor do the waves halt their march for your kings. Bow to the storm, take only what the soil offers, and look upon the horizon with absolute humility."
> — _The Whispering Leaf, Holy Text of the Wild_

Gozreh is Eraedal’s ancient, completely immutable deity of nature, weather, the sky, and the sea. Standing completely apart from the moral philosophies, legal frameworks, and political machinations of the civilized world, Gozreh represents the raw, uncaged forces of the cosmos. Shifting seamlessly between genders, forms, and temperaments, the Wind and the Waves can be a nurturing morning mist that coaxes crops from the dirt, or a catastrophic hurricane that snaps massive armada masts like dry twigs. Gozreh is the ultimate neutral anchor of reality, caring only for the preservation of the natural cycles and the unyielding survival of the wild spaces.

---

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics

- **Titles:** The Wind and the Waves, The Skyfather, The Stormmother, Him Who Is the Wind, Her Who Is the Sea
- **Areas of Concern:** Nature, weather, oceans, sky, and untamed wilderness.
- **Divine Font:** _Heal_ or _Harm_
- **Divine Attribute:** Constitution or Wisdom
- **Divine Skill:** Survival
- **Favored Weapon:** Trident
- **Divine Sanctification:** None (Gozreh is completely neutral and cannot be sanctified Holy or Unholy)
- **Domains:** Air, Nature, Water, Weather

### Devotee Benefits

- **1st-Level Spells:** _Hydraulic push_
- **3rd-Level Spells:** _Lightning bolt_
- **6th-Level Spells:** _Chain lightning_

### Edicts & Anathema

- **Edicts:** Protect untamed wilderness and old-growth habitats; read and follow the guidance of local weather patterns and tides; live sustainably off the native bounty of the land or sea; show absolute reverence to the open sky and deep water.
- **Anathema:** Introduce artificial chemical pollutions, toxic runoffs, or permanent arcane blights to a pristine natural area; construct giant dams, walls, or industrial irrigation grids that permanently alter or choke natural waterways; slaughter living creatures purely for sport, luxury trade, or cruel amusement.

## Appearance & Iconography

Gozreh is a dual-natured entity possessing no singular fixed form, dynamically shifting between gender expressions based on which aspect of nature is dominant.

When manifesting as the sky, Gozreh is **The Skyfather**—a towering, serene old man with a massive beard composed of shifting storm-clouds and eyes crackling with distant blue lightning. When manifesting as the ocean, Gozreh is **The Stormmother**—a breathtakingly beautiful, wildly volatile woman with long emerald hair that leaves trails of seafoam and skin that shimmers like fish scales beneath the moonlight. In localized visions, Gozreh often appears simply as a colossal elemental gale or a massive rogue wave breaking over the horizon.

Their holy symbol is a stylized blue leaf silhouette integrated into the shape of a breaking ocean wave, or a single white droplet split by a lightning bolt. Sacred animals include all apex migratory creatures, particularly sea hawks, giant gulls, albatrosses, whales, and electric eels.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Dawn of the Crucible

Gozreh is a primeval entity whose consciousness emerged the exact moment the first atmosphere condensed over Eraedal’s roiling, volatile volcanic oceans. Along with primal earth entities, Gozreh spent eons watching the slow, rhythmic crawl of tectonic lines and the birth of early biological life. Because they existed long before the concepts of good, evil, law, or chaos were defined by mortal minds, Gozreh views the entirety of civilized history—from the rise of ancient elven kingdoms to human imperial expansions—as a minor, temporary skin irritation upon the surface of the world.

### The Raging Veil

During the prehistoric war to seal **Rovagug, the Rough Beast**, Gozreh operated as the vital atmospheric specialist of the early pantheon. While Sarenrae and Torag engaged the beast directly in melee combat, Gozreh summoned a planet-wide canopy of category-five superstorms, churning tempests, and impenetrable marine fogs. This elemental veil effectively blinded Rovagug’s massive spawn, threw their kinetic charges off-course, and allowed the gods of order to construct the localized trap at the world's core without drawing the attention of the wider outer dark.

### The Custodian of the Floenry Archipelago

Within the regional geography of your campaign, Gozreh is arguably the most culturally dominant divine presence across the vast **Floenry Chain**. In a region defined by dense tropical jungles, rain forests that create oppressive steam baths of heat, and unpredictable open-ocean navigation, survival relies entirely on Gozreh’s favor. Sailors, independent merchant traders, and native fishermen treat the faith with pragmatic survivalism. Shrines to Gozreh are built on every rocky reef and jungle beachhead, where travelers leave offerings of fresh water and clean sea salt to buy safe passage through the unpredictable tropical depressions.

## Divine Relationships

Gozreh treats the rest of the cosmic pantheon with clinical, territorial detachment, judging other deities entirely by their impact on the global ecosystem.
