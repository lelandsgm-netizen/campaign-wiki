# The Devouring Void: The Architects of Oblivion

> [!QUOTE]
> "The universe is a temporary error in an otherwise pristine nothingness. Bleed the cities, rot the flesh, deform the lineage, and let the quiet, bottomless dark pull the pillars of reality back down into the unmade deep."
> — *The Doomsday Apocrypha, Forbidden Text of the Void*

The **Devouring Void** is not a cohesive church or a unified divine family; it is a category of absolute entropy, cosmic malice, existential decay, and planetary terror. Operating as the fundamental structural inverse to **[[The Imperial Rite]]** and the family bonds of **[[The Dwarven Pantheon]]**, this unholy alignment represents the raw, uncaged forces of destruction that seek to dismantle the Material Plane. From the subterranean mountain prison of Rovagug to the localized necromantic laboratories under city grids, the cults of the Void operate in absolute secrecy, utilizing terror, plagues, mutations, and meticulous pain to unspool the civil progress of mortal ancestries.

---
## Pantheon Profile

### Core Mechanics (Worshipping the Void)
* **Areas of Concern:** Absolute entropy, necromantic undeath, monstrous physical mutations, surgical torment, and the total unmaking of the cosmic order.
* **Divine Font:** *Harm* (Strictly mandated)
* **Divine Attribute Options:** Strength, Constitution, or Charisma
* **Divine Skill:** Intimidation or Stealth
* **Favored Weapon Options:** Greataxe, Scythe, or Spiked Chain
* **Divine Sanctification:** Must choose Unholy
* **Domains:** Destruction, Nightmares, Plague, Void

### Shared Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Destroy permanent works of structural art, architectural engineering, or generational heritage; unleash feral monsters and undying plagues upon civilian centers; give yourself over completely to base, undisciplined hungers or raw wrath; actively disrupt legal contracts, prisons, and civil containment structures.
* **Anathema:** Construct a building of lasting public utility, municipal stability, or artistic beauty; offer unconditional mercy, comfort, or medical relief to an unaligned adversary; voluntarily undergo ascetic diets or suppress your raw physical desires.

## Deep Cosmology: The Spire and the Slumbering Dark

Within the multi-layered campaign matrix of Eraedal, the `pantheon/devouring-void` serves as the direct theological manifestation of the **Galchutt / Old Ones** sleeping beneath the **Spire of Axis**.

While the surface inquires and political columns of the Western Empire concern themselves with tax records, legalistic annexations, and registries under the *Edict of Deviltry*, your group of Runebearers knows that the true existential threat to Eraedal resides deep within the rock. 

The deities of the Void do not operate from clean celestial palaces; their power lines bleed directly into the dark, forgotten pockets of the earth. When a cultist invokes Rovagug's tectonic wrath, or when a Kuthite shadowcaster warps a captive refugee in their hidden under-city operating rooms, they are tapping into the precise entropic radio frequencies generated by the ancient monstrosities bound beneath the Spire.
