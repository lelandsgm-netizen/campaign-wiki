# Pazuzu: The King of the Wind Demons

> [!QUOTE]
> "Purity is merely a glass vase waiting for the stones of temptation. Pray to me to escape the beast at your door, and I will gladly slide the bolt—for the price of your soul’s absolute descent."
> — *The Skyvane Gospel, Taboo Text of the Dark Angel*

Pazuzu is Eraedal’s master of the dark skies, a primordial Demon Lord who rules as the absolute king of wind fiends, predatory winged beasts, and the insidious corruption of the innocent. Sitting upon his shifting throne in Torment, the 503rd layer of the Abyss, he represents the subtle, malicious wind that coaxes mortals into moral ruin. Unlike reckless destroyers within the `pantheon/devouring-void`, Pazuzu thrives on deception; he famously answers the desperate prayers of mortals seeking to escape other monsters, only to trap them in a far more elegant, generational web of spiritual damnation.

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The King of the Wind Demons, The Dark Angel, Lord of the Skyvane, Prince of the Lower Aerial Kingdoms
* **Areas of Concern:** Winged creatures, harmful winds, corruption of the innocent, and sky dominance.
* **Divine Font:** *Harm*
* **Divine Attribute:** Dexterity or Charisma
* **Divine Skill:** Acrobatics
* **Favored Weapon:** Longsword
* **Divine Sanctification:** Must choose Unholy
* **Domains:** Air, Destruction, Travel, Trickery

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Tailwind*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Fly*
* **7th-Level Spells:** *Warp mind*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Master the currents of the air; corrupt pristine, innocent, or holy souls through subtle temptation; foster the growth of winged monsters and harpies; present yourself as a savior to the desperate to secure their hidden debt.
* **Anathema:** Cooperate with or aid the clergy of Lamashtu; allow an apex winged hunter to be permanently caged or clipped without a malicious purpose; display unprovoked, sloppy violence that drives an innocent soul away before they are fully corrupted.

## Appearance & Iconography

Pazuzu manifests as a terrifyingly majestic, statuesque humanoid figure blending canine, avian, and fiendish anatomy. He possesses a physically powerful, lean human torso covered in fine, oily black feathers, terminating in the lethal, razor-sharp talons of a giant predatory bird. His head combines a fierce, regal countenance with the snarling jaw of a jackal, complete with rows of serrated, needle-thin teeth. Shimmering from his back are four massive, independent bird wings capable of whipping up localized hurricanes, and a heavy scorpion’s tail curls aggressively behind him, dripping with a highly caustic, unholy vitriol.

His holy symbol is a stylized image of a shifting, wind-blown compass rose where the northern marker points directly downward, or a profile image of a four-winged jackal silhouette. Sacred animals include all predatory aerial scavengers, locust swarms, mantises, hawks, and corrupted eagles.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Inversion of the Savior
Pazuzu’s most insidious theological mechanical design centers on his status as an **Apotropaic Protector**. In the ancient history of Eraedal, particularly across tribal frontiers, pregnant mortals and desperate parents lived in absolute terror of **Lamashtu**, who systematically targeted infants for monstrous mutations. Pazuzu exploited this fear. He whispered a taboo equation into the mortal realm: *if you carve my image into an amulet and slide it over your threshold, my wind fiends will tear Lamashtu's monsters to pieces before they can touch your children.*

For millennia, millions of innocent mortals crafted icons of Pazuzu, genuinely viewing him as a protective savior deity. However, this was a massive psychological trap. The mere act of placing his image on a household slowly, generationally shifted the local alignment toward unholy compliance. By the time the family realized the danger, their children weren't monsters of the flesh (Lamashtu), but monsters of the soul—completely corrupted by greed, cruelty, and allegiance to the King of the Wind Demons.

### The Abyssal Marriage
Before Lamashtu ascended to full godhood by slaying the beast-god Curchanus, she and Pazuzu were linked in a toxic, extraordinarily violent planar partnership. They ruled neighboring sectors of the Abyss, combining their military forces to raid outer planes and baseline worlds. However, their mutual pride and absolute malice made a stable alliance impossible. 

When Lamashtu successfully achieved major divinity, she attempted to subjugate Pazuzu into her monstrous hierarchy. Pazuzu responded with absolute defiance, launching a surprise vanguard attack that permanently scarred her abyssal realm. Their bitter divorce initiated an eternal shadow war that splits the lower planes, and to this day, their cultists treat each other with zero-survival eradication parameters on sight.

## Divine Relationships

Pazuzu uses his aerial mobility and cunning deceptions to manipulate planar politics, remaining a constant thorn in the side of both holy crusaders and rival fiends.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Lamashtu** | **The Bitter Ex-Spouse.** The ultimate blood feud within the `pantheon/devouring-void`. Pazuzu despises her success and uses his mortal church primarily to intercept her worshippers, offering them false safety from her mutations to secure their souls for his own sky kingdoms. |
| **Desna** | **The Sky War.** Desna governs the beauty of the stars, dreams, and safe cosmic travel. Pazuzu represents the corruptive gale, the superstorm, and the nightmare-parasite that ambushes travelers under the open sky. Their celestial alignments make them immediate battlefield combatants. |
| **Gozreh** | **Elemental Intrusion.** Gozreh claims absolute neutrality over weather networks and wind paths. Pazuzu's attempt to weaponize tropical depressions and command wind fiends to disrupt maritime trade acts as a direct violation of Gozreh's natural territory. |
| **Asmodeus** | **Bureaucratic Disdain.** The Lord of Hell demands perfect, rigid contract structures and permanent legalistic prisons. Pazuzu represents chaotic Abyssal freedom and deceptive promises that look like liberation. Asmodeus views him merely as an unpredictable, dangerous aerial pirate. |
| **Sarenrae** | **The Solar Brand.** The Dawnflower treats Pazuzu's cults with zero diplomatic options. Because Pazuzu targets the pure and innocent for corruption, her angels explicitly target his high priests for absolute incineration before his poison can set in the community vein. |
