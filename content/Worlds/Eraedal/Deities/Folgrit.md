# Folgrit: The Watchful Mother

> [!QUOTE]
> "Torag builds the walls to keep the wind and the enemy at bay, but it is Folgrit who kindles the fire inside that makes those walls worth defending."
> — *Proverb of the Hearth-Keepers*

Folgrit is the patient, deeply compassionate matriarch of the dwarven pantheon. While her husband, Torag, focuses on the external defense of the fortress and the roaring fires of the structural forge, Folgrit turns her gaze inward to the preservation and emotional endurance of the dwarven people themselves. She is the goddess of motherhood, children, the hearth, safety, and community stability. She acts as the ultimate comforter for the grieving and serves as the emotional anchor for a species that frequently undergoes immense geopolitical hardship and generational transitions.

---

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Watchful Mother, The Hearth-Mother, Goddess of Casualties, Maternal Guardian
* **Areas of Concern:** Motherhood, children, safety, the hearth, internal defense, and comforting the grieving.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal*
* **Divine Attribute:** Constitution or Wisdom
* **Divine Skill:** Society
* **Favored Weapon:** Dagger
* **Divine Sanctification:** Can choose Holy
* **Domains:** Community, Family, Healing, Protection

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Sanctuary*
* **2nd-Level Spells:** *Clear mind*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Creation*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Provide sanctuary to refugees and those in need; nurture, educate, and protect children; maintain a warm, welcoming hearth fire; comfort those who have suffered catastrophic loss.
* **Anathema:** Abandon a child or a person explicitly placed under your protection; poison, spoil, or taint a community's food or water supplies; refuse shelter to a family fleeing unjust violence; stoke internal divisions that split a clan or household apart.

## Appearance & Iconography

Folgrit is traditionally depicted as a stout, serene dwarven woman with exceptionally kind eyes and a welcoming expression. Her long, luxurious hair and beard are braided in complex, interlocking patterns that tell the oral history of her ancestral family lines. Unlike the rest of her heavily armored divine family, she wears practical, comfortable robes stained with soot from the hearth fire or flour from baking. However, she is never entirely defenseless; a heavy stiletto or utility dagger is always tucked into her belt, symbolizing her unyielding readiness to fiercely defend her household.

Her holy symbol is a stylized hearth fire burning brightly inside a protective stone archway, or a simple, open pair of maternal hands offering shelter. Sacred animals include maternal beasts of burden, sheep, and small birds that nest safely within the deep cracks of fortress walls.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Mother of the Migration
During the monumental **Quest for Sky**, when the dwarven ancestry migrated from the terrifying depths of the Darklands to the surface world, Folgrit played a role just as vital as Torag’s strategic vanguard leadership. While Torag’s armies cleared the path ahead through orc hordes and subterranean horrors, Folgrit managed the grueling logistics of the civilian evacuation. She kept the encampments organized, ensured the wounded were healed, and safely moved generations of children upward through the dark. Because of this, she is often invoked as the "Goddess of Casualties," acting as the principal comforter for the thousands of widows, orphans, and grieving parents who lost loved ones during the centuries-long upward climb.

### The Guardian of the Under-Siege
In dwarven military doctrine, Folgrit is the absolute deity of the interior lines. If an enemy breaches a Sky Citadel’s outer gates, the conflict shifts directly from Torag’s domain to Folgrit's. She represents the fierce, desperate defense of the nursery, the kitchens, and the residential vaults. Her clerics are trained not just in spiritual healing, but in emergency logistics, rationing, and organizing civilian civilian militias to protect tactical choke points within a besieged city.

## Divine Relationships

Folgrit acts as the glue that keeps the divine family unified, offering a soft counter-balance to Torag's stubborn, rigid authoritarianism.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Torag** | **The Patriarch.** Her husband. Their marriage is an eternal partnership of deep mutual respect. Torag trusts her counsel above all others, and she is one of the few beings who can convince him to change his mind once his legendary stubbornness sets in. |
| **Trudd** | **The Reckless Son.** She is fiercely proud of Trudd's bravery but quietly worries about his front-line placement. She heavily disapproves of his casual, non-traditional flings with Cayden Cailean, wishing he would settle into a traditional clan lineage. |
| **Bolka** | **The Cherished Daughter.** Folgrit works hand-in-hand with her, balancing the protection of the home with Bolka's focus on matchmaking, romantic attraction, and marital joy. She often aids Bolka's schemes to get Trudd to commit. |
| **Cayden Cailean** | **Maternal Disapproval.** Folgrit respects Cayden’s focus on liberation, but she regards his chaotic lifestyle, public intoxication, and non-committal romantic interactions as a direct threat to the stability of a proper household. |
| **Calistria** | **Deep Enmity.** Folgrit views Calistria's endorsement of fleeting lust and personal independence without family strings as social poison. Calistria's active sabotage of Bolka's traditional matchmaking loops earns Folgrit's quiet, protective wrath. |
| **Asmodeus** | **Absolute Rejection.** While her husband legalistically coordinates with Hell when cosmic threats arise, Folgrit despises Asmodeus. Tyranny and institutional slavery destroy the fundamental sanctuary of the family unit she is sworn to protect. |
