# Kols: The Oath-Keeper

> [!QUOTE]
> "Flesh may wither and empires may fracture into gravel, but a sworn promise must remain as immutable as the deepest mountain root. Break your word, and you break the stone beneath your feet."
> — *The Ledger of Obligations, Holy Text of the Witness*

Kols is the stern, unblinking god of duty, solemn promises, honorable honor, and immutable legal contracts. As a prominent son of Torag and Folgrit, he serves as the absolute divine witness and chronicler of all oaths sworn under the mountain. Kols has no interest in individual battlefield glory, aggressive vanguard expansion, or wild artistic expression. Instead, he represents the structural framework of societal trust—monitoring legal transactions, auditing clan alliances, and tracking personal obligations to ensure that dwarven culture remains fundamentally anchored in absolute integrity.

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Fulfill every sworn promise, legal contract, and verbal treaty to the exact letter; act as a completely impartial, transparent witness to civilian and military agreements; respect and enforce lawful judicial authority; audit and document debts accurately.
* **Anathema:** Break a legally binding contract, sworn oath, or word of honor; forge an agreement containing intentionally deceptive or misleading language; hide or alter a legal document or ancestral transaction ledger to escape personal accountability.

## Appearance & Iconography

Kols is traditionally depicted as a stoic, impeccably groomed dwarf with a beard made of tightly braided, dark iron rings that never rust. His expression is completely carved from stone—showing neither anger nor joy—and his eyes flash with the precise, illuminating coloration of polished blue sapphire or lapis lazuli. He wears clean, polished steel breastplate armor overlaid with heavy judicial robes of dark navy and slate-grey, and he always carries a heavy iron stylus in one hand and a giant, rune-carved granite tablet in the other, ready to permanently etch a mortal contract into historical memory.

His holy symbol is a heavy stone tablet split down the center by a pristine vertical rune representing "Truth," or a pair of iron scales balancing a heavy stone ring. Sacred animals include creatures defined by unyielding, methodical paths and defensive duty, such as industrious badgers, steady work oxen, and guard hounds.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Witness of the Citadel Compacts
During the cataclysmic multi-generational migration known as the **Quest for Sky**, the dwarven ancestry faced continuous structural collapses, deep-subterranean betrayals, and resource wars. While Torag commanded the vanguard and Folgrit managed the civilian refugees, Kols was the master architect of internal cohesion. He instituted the **Citadel Compacts**—strict, sacred treaties drafted between rival clans to ensure that food, iron, and tunnel rights were distributed with absolute transparency. Kols taught his people that the orc hordes outside their walls could never destroy them, but the rot of internal dishonesty and broken treaties easily could.

### The Divine Auditor
Within the divine halls of **Forgeheart**, Kols occupies a critical, occasionally frustrating role as the de facto administrative manager of the pantheon. He sits at the right hand of Torag’s anvil, actively documenting every divine pact made between his father and the outer cosmic forces. He does not take sides in political disputes, operating strictly as a blind auditor. If a mortal king signs a treaty with a foreign nation, Kols watches from the stone; if that king seeks to break the pact due to political convenience, Kols immediately revokes his divine blessings, marking the line as "Oath-Breakers" until proper kinetic restitution is paid.
