# Iomedae: The Inheritor

> [!QUOTE]
> "Evil thinks itself iron, but honor is the forge that shatters it. Guard your shield-brother, give your enemy no cause to question your virtue, and strike with the clean edge of truth."
> — *The Acts of Iomedae, Holy Scripture of the Faith*

Iomedae is the fierce, radiant goddess of righteous war, justice, honor, valor, and compassionate leadership. Uniquely among the core pantheon, she began her existence as a mortal human warrior, rising through sheer righteousness and tactical brilliance to become a living legend. Following the sudden and apocalyptic death of her patron deity, Aroden, she took up his broken blade and assumed his mantle. Now known globally as "The Inheritor," she serves as the central vanguard of the Pantheon of Light, guiding holy crusades, inspiring paladins, and shielding the vulnerable from the predatory forces of the dark.

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Inheritor, Light of the Sword, The Sword of Justice, The First Maid
* **Areas of Concern:** Righteous war, justice, honor, valor, and rulership.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal*
* **Divine Attribute:** Strength or Charisma
* **Divine Skill:** Athletics
* **Favored Weapon:** Longsword
* **Divine Sanctification:** Must choose Holy
* **Domains:** Confidence, Duty, Justice, Zeal

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Sure strike*
* **2nd-Level Spells:** *Enlarge*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Fire shield*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Act with honor, valor, and temperance; fight injustice and predatory evil whenever you encounter it; protect the weak, the innocent, and refugees; demand truth from yourself and your allies.
* **Anathema:** Commit a cowardly or dishonorable act; slay a defenseless or surrendered foe; abandon a companion, ally, or community in their hour of need; use deceptive or underhanded poisons.

## Appearance & Iconography

Iomedae is traditionally depicted as a fierce, statuesque human woman with piercing eyes, clad from head to toe in gleaming, radiant white-gold plate armor. She carries a masterfully forged longsword that pulses with clean daylight and holds a heavy kited shield stamped with her holy icon. Despite her immense celestial power, her expression is rarely one of detached divinity; she carries the weathered, focused gravity of a general who has walked the muddy frontlines alongside her troops.

Her holy symbol is the "Sword of Justice"—a pristine, upright longsword wreathed in a sunburst or a halo of golden light. Sacred animals include noble beasts of war and guardians, such as warhorses, lions, and griffins that dive swiftly into battle.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The General of the Shining Crusade
Long before her ascension, Iomedae was a mortal paladin of Arazni (the herald of Aroden) in the ancient human kingdom of Taldor. When the lich-king Tar-Baphon, the Whispering Tyrant, rose to conquer the continent with armies of the undead, Iomedae assumed command of the Knights of Ozem. Throughout the grueling centuries of the **Shining Crusade**, her tactical genius and unyielding morale turned the tide of the war. Her legendary exploits during this era were chronicled into the **Eleven Acts of Iomedae**, which remain the central text of her martial doctrine today.

### The Test of the Starstone
Following the successful imprisonment of the Whispering Tyrant, Iomedae traveled to the city of Absalom to face the ultimate spiritual challenge: the **Test of the Starstone**. Navigating a labyrinth of shifting lethal traps, psychological horrors, and cosmic riddles designed by the old gods, her absolute clarity of purpose and pure heart allowed her to succeed. She emerged from the cathedral as a demigod, instantly chosen by Aroden to serve as his new divine Herald.

### Inheriting the Light
The defining turning point of her divinity occurred at the dawn of the **Age of Lost Omens**. Aroden, the ancient god of humanity, died mysteriously and catastrophically, shattering prophecies and throwing the mortal world into geopolitical chaos. Rather than letting his vast, leaderless flocks fall into despair or be consumed by the schemes of Hell, Iomedae stepped forward. She rallied the fractured faithful, repaired the holy networks, and inherited the primary duty of guarding civilization against the outer dark. Because of this, she stands as the bedrock of the modern **Pantheon of Light**.

## Divine Relationships

As a military commander and absolute legalist, Iomedae maintains highly structured, transparent relationships with her fellow deities.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Torag** | **Deep Military Alliance.** The Inheritor and the Father of Creation share an ironclad bond. While Torag prefers an unyielding bottleneck defense and stone fortifications, he highly respects Iomedae's battlefield discipline and honor. Their churches frequently launch joint military operations. |
| **Abadar** | **Strategic Partner.** Iomedae relies on Abadar’s legal frameworks and wealthy infrastructure to sustain her massive holy crusades, while Abadar relies on her paladins to keep trade routes clear of bandits and monsters. |
| **Erastil** | **Respectful but Tense.** Old Deadeye deeply admires Iomedae’s protective nature and honor. However, he quietly laments that her grand, sweeping crusades pull young farmers away from their families and villages, leaving ancestral lands untended. |
| **Asmodeus** | **Absolute Loathing.** As the master of Hell, Asmodeus represents everything Iomedae hates: tyranny, corruption, and contracts designed to entrap the weak. Though they both value order, she views his legal systems as a malignant perversion of true justice. |
| **Sarenrae** | **The Blade and the Sun.** Sarenrae represents the warm, restorative light of redemption, while Iomedae represents the sharp, defensive steel of justice. They complement each other perfectly within the Pantheon of Light, though Iomedae is far quicker to draw her sword when an evil entity refuses to reform. |
