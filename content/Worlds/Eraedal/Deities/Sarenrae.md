# Sarenrae: The Dawnflower

> [!QUOTE]
> "The sun rises not to expose the scars of the world, but to warm the hands that heal them. Offer the hand of redemption first, but if it is bitten, let the scimitar strike with the unyielding fire of the noon day."
> — *The Birth of Light and Truth, Holy Scripture of Sarenrae*

Sarenrae is the radiant, fiercely compassionate goddess of the sun, redemption, healing, and honesty. Standing as one of the oldest and most beloved pillars of the Pantheon of Light, she represents the dual nature of cleansing flame: it can soothe, warm, and restore life to the broken, or it can incinerate unrepentant corruption, fiends, and the undead until nothing but clean ash remains. She is a deity of patience and profound mercy, teaching that no mortal soul is truly beyond saving, though she commands a martial host of angels ready to execute absolute divine wrath if her kindness is weaponized by evil.

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Dawnflower, The Everlight, The Healing Flame, Angel of the Sun
* **Areas of Concern:** The sun, redemption, healing, honesty, and cleansing fire.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal*
* **Divine Attribute:** Strength or Charisma
* **Divine Skill:** Medicine
* **Favored Weapon:** Scimitar
* **Divine Sanctification:** Must choose Holy
* **Domains:** Fire, Healing, Sun, Truth

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Breathe fire* (formerly *Burning hands*)
* **3rd-Level Spells:** *Fireball*
* **7th-Level Spells:** *Sunburst*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Offer redemption to those who genuinely seek it; heal the sick, wounded, and plagued; speak the truth with clarity and kindness; use fire to cleanse corruption and destroy the undead.
* **Anathema:** Create undead or artificially prolong unholy life; lie or willfully deceive your community; refuse shelter or the path of redemption to a genuinely repentant foe; permit structural evil or systemic cruelty to fester without confronting it.

## Appearance & Iconography

Sarenrae is universally depicted as a magnificent, angelic humanoid woman with radiant bronze skin, eyes composed of molten gold, and multi-colored wings that burn with the shimmering spectrum of a morning sunrise. She wears elegant, flowing robes of pure alabaster and gold that never catch dust, and she floats slightly above the ground, surrounded by a crown of solar fire. In her right hand, she wields a beautifully curved scimitar that crackles with heat, capable of igniting into a white-hot solar brand when swung in battle.

Her holy symbol is the Dawnflower icon—a stylized, soaring angel silhouette with outstretched wings integrated into the shape of a blazing sunburst. Sacred animals include those tied to dawn, heat, and high altitude, such as hawks, eagles, lions, and brass dragons.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Warden of the Pit
Sarenrae's ancient history is defined by her central role in trapping the ultimate cosmic threat: **Rovagug, the Rough Beast**. Before the Material Plane was fully populated, Rovagug sought to consume the multiverse. Sarenrae led the vanguard of the early gods, drawing the entity's attention away from vulnerable worlds. She used her solar blade to slice open the tectonic foundations of Eraedal, creating a localized spatial tear that allowed the other deities to bind the beast inside the world's core. 

### The Tragedy of Ninshabur
Despite her vast wisdom, Sarenrae’s historical record contains a dark, sobering lesson on the dangers of absolute righteous pride. In the ancient human empire of Ninshabur, a corrupt cult of Rovagug took over a grand city, actively mocking her faith and murdering her clergy. Overcome with holy fury, Sarenrae smote the city directly with a pillar of raw, unmitigated solar fire. 

The strike was so powerful it tore an unhealing scar into the fabric of Eraedal, creating the **Pit of Gormuz**. Far from destroying the cult, the sheer kinetic shockwave loosened the seals of Rovagug's prison, allowing terrifying monsters known as the Spawn of Rovagug to crawl out onto the surface world for millennia. Horrified by her own destructive overreach, Sarenrae swore an oath never to deploy such devastating, unguided orbital power upon the mortal world again, shifting her core focus heavily toward patience, education, and redemption.

### The Beacon of the Pantheon
Within the **Pantheon of Light**, Sarenrae acts as the moral compass and primary warmth of the alliance. Her temples serve as public hospitals, orphanages, and community centers. Her order teaches that a sword should only be drawn when every diplomatic line, medical intervention, and offer of peaceful reform has been completely exhausted. However, once the scimitar *is* drawn, her paladins fight with absolute, terrifying kinetic speed, knowing that an enemy who refuses her light is an immediate existential threat to the innocent.

## Divine Relationships

Sarenrae seeks to build bridges with all who value life, though her absolute honesty occasionally causes friction with more deceitful or transactional gods.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Iomedae** | **The Blade and the Shield.** They enjoy a perfect, seamless tactical partnership. Sarenrae provides the moral clarity and restorative grace, while Iomedae provides the ironclad battlefield discipline and military infrastructure. They stand shoulder-to-shoulder against the dark. |
| **Asmodeus** | **Absolute, Visceral Loathing.** Sarenrae views the Archfiend as a cancer. He thrives on technical fine print, contracts designed to damn souls, and institutional slavery—concepts that completely mock her desire for genuine spiritual redemption and freedom. |
| **Pharasma** | **Professional Distance.** The Lady of Graves respects Sarenrae's efforts to heal the living but takes a firm stance if Sarenrae's clerics attempt to completely cheat fate. Sarenrae is a fierce opponent of Urgathoa, aligning her with Pharasma's anti-undead crusade. |
| **Shelyn** | **Intimate Harmony.** Sarenrae shares a beautiful, multi-planar romantic partnership with Shelyn (and Desna). Shelyn’s emphasis on inner beauty and artistic creation matches perfectly with Sarenrae's belief in the hidden goodness residing within flawed mortal souls. |
| **Rovagug** | **The Tectonic Nemesis.** Sarenrae maintains an eternal watch over the Rough Beast. Her clergy are explicitly trained to hunt down cults of the beast before they can weaken the anchors of the world's core. |
