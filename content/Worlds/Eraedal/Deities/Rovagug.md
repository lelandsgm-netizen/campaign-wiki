# Rovagug: The Rough Beast

> [!QUOTE]
> "The universe is a cage, and the gods are its fragile jailers. Tear down the pillars, shatter the foundations, and let the quiet dark of absolute nothingness consume what remains."
> — *The Chaos Redaction, Doomsday Scripture of the Void*

Rovagug is the ultimate, primordial titan of absolute destruction, cosmic wrath, slaughter, and oblivion. He is not a god who seeks to rule, enslave, or reshape the universe—he exists solely to tear it down, chew its planar foundations into dust, and return all of creation to a state of absolute, unmade nothingness. Trapped deep within the core of the Material Plane inside a localized spatial prison known as the Dead Vault, his mere slumber causes earthquakes and eruptions. He is the undisputed sovereign of the `pantheon/devouring-void`, serving as an existential nightmare that forces the gods of order and chaos alike to look upon the horizon with terrifying humility.

* **1st-Level Spells:** *Sure strike*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Weapon storm*
* **7th-Level Spells:** *Earthquake*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Destroy objects of lasting beauty, art, or structural engineering; unleash feral monsters and predatory terrors upon civilized settlements; give yourself over completely to raw, unguided rage; break cages, bonds, and prisons.
* **Anathema:** Create an object of permanent utility, architectural stability, or aesthetic beauty; show genuine mercy or quarter to a defeated foe; repair or structurally reinforce a damaged building or wall; willingly submit to chains, containment, or legal confinement.

## Appearance & Iconography

Rovagug possesses no static humanoid form, manifesting as an unfathomably colossal, multi-limbed monstrosity that defies natural anatomy. Traditional descriptions depict him as an amorphous mountain of chitinous, insectoid carapaces, writhing abyssal tentacles, and countless blinking, predatory eyes. At his center sits a localized, star-devouring maw lined with concentric rows of serrated teeth capable of grinding raw divine energy. Where he walks in visions, the stone melts into vitriol, the air bursts into toxic ash, and the very concept of time begins to unspool.

His holy symbol is a stylized, multi-legged arachnid or monster silhouette breaking out of a cracked vertical eye, or a heavy claw splitting a stone sphere. Sacred animals include all subterranean apex horrors, scorpions, rabid vermin, and the terrifying, apocalyptic monstrosities known as the Spawn of Rovagug.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The War of Creation
In the deepest pre-civilization history of the multiverse, Rovagug crawled out from the outer dark, drawn to the raw, vibrant magical energies of the newly forged Material Plane. He immediately began a planetary blitz, chewing through early outer planes and slaughtering minor gods. 

Realizing that Rovagug represented a total, zero-sum threat to existence itself, an unprecedented, cosmic alliance was forged. Deities who spent eons trying to assassinate each other stood shoulder-to-shoulder. Sarenrae led the tactical vanguard, blinding the beast with solar fire, while Torag and the ancient earth gods fashioned unbreakable, unholy chains. Together, they drove Rovagug deep into the molten tectonic core of Eraedal, locking him away inside the **Dead Vault**.

### The Pit of Gormuz
Though bound by divine equations and cosmic locks, Rovagug's hunger cannot be entirely suppressed. In ancient history, when Sarenrae smote a corrupt city in her pride, the sheer kinetic shockwave fractured the upper layers of his prison. This created the **Pit of Gormuz**, a miles-wide, bottomless chasm from which Rovagug's monstrous children—the **Spawn of Rovagug** (such as the tarrasque, Xotani, and Chemnosit)—crawl out every few centuries to lay waste to entire continents. His cultists do not build churches; they gather in secret deep subterranean catacombs or ruined wilderness warrens, conducting frantic, bloody rituals designed to slowly chip away at the anchors of his vault.

## Divine Relationships

Rovagug holds no alliances, viewing all entities in the cosmos merely as meat to be consumed or structures to be dismantled.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Sarenrae** | **The Tectonic Nemesis.** Sarenrae is the object of Rovagug's most burning, volatile hatred. Her solar blade carved open the earth to seal him away, and her church stands as the front-line shield hunting down his cults. |
| **Asmodeus** | **The Jailer's Leverage.** Asmodeus drafted the legalistic boundaries of the Dead Vault and holds the physical **Key to Rovagug's Prison**. The Archfiend uses this possession as ultimate diplomatic leverage over the celestial planes, knowing that if Hell falls, he can turn the key and let the beast consume the universe. |
| **Torag** | **Ironclad Defiance.** Torag engineered the structural vaults and divine anchors that keep Rovagug bound beneath the stone. The dwarven pantheon treats cults of the Rough Beast with absolute, merciless eradication. |
| **Gozreh** | **Elemental Fury.** Gozreh deploys the full weight of nature's superstorms, category-five typhoons, and oceanic pressures to drown and suppress the toxic volcanic exhaust and ash plumes that escape from Rovagug's prison vents. |
| **Lamashtu** | **Maternal Theft.** While Lamashtu sits in the `pantheon/devouring-void` due to her association with physical corruption, she treats Rovagug with extreme caution. She seeks to steal his raw, destructive energies to fuel her monster-breeding nurseries, while Rovagug views her creations merely as more things to chew into ash. |
