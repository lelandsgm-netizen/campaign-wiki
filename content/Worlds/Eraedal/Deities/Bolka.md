# Bolka: The Golden Gift

> [!QUOTE]
> "Stone may be rigid, but the heart must remain fluid. An anvil gives a blade its form, but love gives a clan its purpose. Let your devotion burn brighter than any forge fire."
> — *The Woven Ribbon, Sacred Text of the Golden Gift*

Bolka is the vibrant, affectionate goddess of love, matchmaking, physical beauty, and happy marriages. As the daughter of Torag and Folgrit, she serves a unique role within the traditionally stoic, pragmatic dwarven pantheon. While her family members focus heavily on defense, law, and structural defense, Bolka ensures that dwarven communities do not lose their joy, emotional warmth, and capacity for passion. She is the patron of romantic love, artistic aesthetics beneath the stone, and the divine spark that turns diplomatic, arranged clan alliances into deeply affectionate, lifelong partnerships.

## Divine Profile

### Core Mechanics
* **Titles:** The Golden Gift, The Heart-Warmer, The Matchmaker
* **Areas of Concern:** Love, beauty, attraction, matchmaking, and marriage.
* **Divine Font:** *Heal*
* **Divine Attribute:** Charisma or Wisdom
* **Divine Skill:** Diplomacy
* **Favored Weapon:** Mace
* **Divine Sanctification:** Can choose Holy
* **Domains:** Community, Family, Healing, Passion

### Devotee Benefits
* **1st-Level Spells:** *Charm*
* **2nd-Level Spells:** *Calm*
* **4th-Level Spells:** *Suggestion*

### Edicts & Anathema
* **Edicts:** Cultivate love and genuine affection in romantic partnerships; assist others in finding compatible, joyful companions; create art and jewelry that celebrates beauty; mend broken hearts and family relationships.
* **Anathema:** Force individuals into completely loveless, predatory, or abusive marriages; intentionally destroy a happy relationship out of jealousy or spite; judge or mock consensual expressions of romantic affection.

## Appearance & Iconography

Bolka is traditionally depicted as a radiant, exceptionally graceful dwarven woman with sparkling hazel or gold eyes and a warm, luminous smile. Her long, luxurious blonde beard and hair are intricately interwoven with threads of pure spun gold, glittering gems, and fresh blossoms—a shocking sight in the deep subterranean vaults. Rather than heavy armor, she wears elegant, comfortable silk gowns colored in rose-pinks, rich crimsons, and metallic golds. Unlike her brothers, she carries no weapons of war, holding instead a beautifully carved golden mace used primarily as a ceremonial scepter to bless newlyweds.

Her holy symbol is a single golden wedding ring or a pair of intertwined silk ribbons wreathed in gold filigree. Sacred animals include creatures that mate for life, particularly doves, swans, and burrowing cave-parrots.

## Canonical Eraedal History & Mythos

### The Blossom in the Stone
In ancient dwarven myth, after Torag had forged the first massive Sky Citadels and anchored the rigid codes of clan law, the dwarven people grew incredibly efficient but emotionally cold. Seeing her people drifting into a state of joyless, transactional survival, Folgrit requested that Torag help her spark a force of pure, emotional rejuvenation. 

From their shared devotion, Bolka was born. Her arrival brought music, fine jewelry designed for pure aesthetics rather than functional utility, and the concept of courtship festivals to the deep subterranean vaults. Her primary theological mandate is ensuring that the dwarven species continues to flourish not just through political obligation, but through genuine, biological, and emotional passion.

### The Transmutation of Duty
Because traditional dwarven culture relies heavily on arranged marriages to secure military alliances, trade monopolies, and ancestral lineages between powerful houses, Bolka’s clergy occupy a critical societal niche. They do not fight against the system of arranged marriages; instead, they serve as specialized psychological and spiritual advisors. 

A priest of Bolka is brought in to work with the betrothed couple, using empathy, counseling, and festive rituals to help compatibility grow. Dwarven lore is filled with romantic epics of cold, strictly political betrothals that blossomed into legendary, deeply passionate loves under Bolka's subtle guidance.

## Divine Relationships

Bolka is a relentless busybody in the halls of Forgeheart, constantly meddling in the personal lives of mortals and divinities alike to the sheer exhaustion of her siblings.

| Deity / Faction | Dynamic & Lore |
| --- | --- |
| **Torag & Folgrit** | **The Cherished Daughter.** Torag finds her lack of military discipline slightly baffling but recognizes her necessity for clan survival. Folgrit works hand-in-hand with her, balancing the protection of the home with the joy of romantic attraction. |
| **Trudd** | **The Sibling Nag.** Bolka is utterly fascinated—and deeply annoyed—by her younger brother Trudd's casual, non-traditional flings with the human god Cayden Cailean.
