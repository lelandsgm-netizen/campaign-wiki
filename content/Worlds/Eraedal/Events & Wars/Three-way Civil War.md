## ⚔️ Overview: The Shattered Lion-Guarded Throne

For over seven centuries, the **[[Western Empire]]** reigned as the undisputed colossus of human civilization on [[Eraedal]]. Governed from the gilded spires of Caer Itom, the **Lion-Guarded Throne** enforced imperial order, subjugated non-human populations through institutionalized slavery, and maintained continental dominance through vast legions and naval supremacy.

That millennium of unchallenged hegemony shattered catastrophically in **702 IA**. A sudden, devastating invasion spearheaded by the **Cyclopean Vanguard**—a terrifying coalition of Cyclopes, Coyle hordes, and barbarian raiders sweeping down from the [[Ophid Grasslands Colonies]]—overran and sacked Caer Itom. 

In the horrifying power vacuum that followed, the centralized imperial bureaucracy fractured into bloody chaos. Three distinct claimants declared themselves the true inheritor of the Lion-Guarded Throne, sparking the **Three-way Civil War** that has engulfed the continent for nineteen years.

### 1. The Holy Seat — [[Rehobath Ylestos|Holy Emperor Rehobath Ylestos]]
- **Claim & Ideology:** The Prince of the Church of Aroden, Kirian, had maintained a holy see in the coastal city of **[[Ptolus]]** since 682 IA. When Caer Itom fell, his father, High Prelate Rehobath, fled east to join him. Rehobath declared that the sack of the ancient capital was the divine wrath of Aroden against a corrupt, blasphemous nobility. Consecrating himself as **Holy Emperor**, Rehobath proclaimed [[Ptolus]] to be the **Holy Capital of the Western Empire**.
- **Power Base:** The fervent faithful of the Church of Aroden, the devout agrarian nobility of [[Oceanside]], and pious knights across the realm. 
- **Strategic Reality:** Rehobath holds tremendous religious authority, but his political control within Ptolus is severely contested by secular Commissar [[Igor Urnst]] at [[Dalenguard]] and the pragmatic merchant patricians of the [[Council of Coin]].

### 2. The Iron Sentinel — Emperor Skluud Ne'Klosh
- **Claim & Ideology:** Entrenched within the formidable mountain fortress of Caer Kurgas in [[Upper Kighfalton]], General Skluud Ne'Klosh declared himself Emperor in 704 IA on the grounds of military necessity. He argues that imperial legitimacy belongs to whoever has the martial might to defend the realm's borders from monstrous destruction.
- **Power Base:** The veteran northern border legions, garrison commanders, and provincial warlords.
- **Strategic Reality:** Ne'Klosh stands as the military bulwark actively stalling the advance of the Cyclopean Vanguard and Coyle marauders. To sustain his defensive war, he reopened financial and military subsidies to the [[Vequerrel Woodlands]] and what remains of the northern frontier outposts.

### 3. The Dynastic Heiress — Empress Lasha Itomas
- **Claim & Ideology:** Operating from Caer Glaverius on the [[Yin-Sloth Periphery]], Empress Lasha Itomas is the direct dynastic scion of the imperial house that held Caer Itom for generations. She denounces Rehobath as a treacherous zealot and Ne'Klosh as an illiterate military usurper.
- **Power Base:** The ancient aristocratic families, the southern slave-holding nobility, and naval fleets patrolling the southern trade lanes.
- **Strategic Reality:** Deeply entrenched in the opulent, slave-reliant plantations bordering the [[Yin-Sloth Jungles]], Lasha's forces possess immense wealth and mercenary backing, but are geographically distant from the heartland and constantly threatened by southern jungle horrors.

1. **The Anarchy of the [[Middle Kingdoms]]:** The fertile, populous central breadbasket of the Empire has collapsed into complete insurrection. Local barons and petty warlords have renounced all three claimants, fortifying their manors and plundering passing trade caravans.
2. **The [[Tarldet Plains]] Emancipation Front:** Severed from western grain and supply routes by the Middle Kingdoms rebellion, **[[House Wenglid]]** took the radical step of abolishing slavery across their domain. By granting instant freedom to any enslaved Orc or Goblin who signs a 5-year military contract, Wenglid created a massive shock army to protect the eastern frontier and push back against border incursions.
3. **The Monster Shield of the [[Old Kingdom Frontier]]:** With imperial funding and reinforcements diverted to the civil war, the legendary stone garrisons guarding the mountains against the monstrous horrors of the fallen [[Old Kingdom]] are reduced to starving skeleton crews, terrified that the dormant fiends and orc hordes will breach the passes.
4. **The [[Faerie War]] in the [[Vequerrel Woodlands]]:** Isolated from central authority, imperial lumber syndicates in Vequerrel face an intensifying guerrilla war against vengeful fey, treants, and ancient forest spirits determined to wipe humanity from the woods.

## ⚓ Maritime Repercussions: Inland Sea & Scarlet Waters

The civil war has profoundly reshaped the naval corridors of Eraedal:

- **The [[Bay of Ptolus]]:** Serving as the maritime lifeline for Holy Emperor Rehobath's faction, the bay swarms with privateers, supply barques from [[Oceanside]], and merchant galleys from Bizantium and the Eastern Territory. Commissar Urnst's naval patrol keeps the harbor secure from opportunistic pirate flotillas.
- **The [[Sea of Scarlet Waters]]:** Southern shipping routes connecting the Western Empire to the [[Baalgor Wastelands]], the Timiro Kingdom, and the Floenry Isles are plagued by rogue corsairs, mercenary blockade-runners, and the treacherous alchemical hazards of the Scarlet Waters. Empress Lasha's privateers actively contest these waters against northern blockade vessels.
- **The [[Pirates of Koerdian]]:** Exploiting the collapse of unified imperial naval policing, the raiders of the [[Koerdian Mountains]] have expanded their raiding radius, striking coastal settlements and grain barges across the western littoral.

## 📜 The Current State (721 IA)

Nineteen years after the fall of Caer Itom, the Western Empire remains frozen in a brutal war of attrition:
- None of the three claimants possesses the decisive strength to crush the other two.
- Meanwhile, the true existential perils of Eraedal—the rising chaos cults serving the imprisoned [[Galchutt]] / Old Ones beneath [[Ptolus]], the monster hordes along the [[Old Kingdom Frontier]], and the expanding giant kingdoms of [[Mount Nimro]]—gather strength in the shadows of humanity's folly.
