The Temple District Hub in [[Ptolus]]: [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]

In your Ptolus wiki, the **[[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]** replaces St. Valien's Cathedral. It stands as the grand, white-stone spiritual heart of the Temple District. Rather than honoring a single Imperial saint, its towering spires and stained-glass dome celebrate a unified coalition of lawful/benevolent deities known as the **[[Imperial Rite]]**.
