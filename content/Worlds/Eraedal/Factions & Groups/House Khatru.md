## House Khatru: The Iron Bastion

If [[Ptolus]] has a dedicated shield and sword, it belongs to **House Khatru**. Arrogant, deeply traditional, and intensely martial, the Khatrus are the physical enforcers of the old aristocracy. While other houses squander their focus on artistic galas, complex mercantile trade, or shadowy politics, House Khatru pours its immense resources into a single pursuit: military absolute dominance.

They pride themselves on being the ancestral backbone of the city's defense forces, consistently producing many of the highest-ranking officers within the [[Igor Urnst | Commissar]]'s [[City Watch]] and [[Igor Urnst]]'s elite circle of [[Twelve Commanders]]. On the [[Council of Coin]], Lord [[Dorant Khatru]] is a vocal, unyielding nationalist who consistently votes for heavy public taxation to fund city walls, martial infrastructure, and aggressive policing. This rigid authoritarian stance puts them in a permanent, violent street-level feud with the maritime smugglers and privateers of **[[House Rau]]**.

- **Estate Location:** The Khatru Estate is a towering, heavily fortified castle situated on the highest bluffs of the **Nobles' Quarter**. Less a luxury manor and more an active military citadel, it features sprawling cobblestone courtyards where plate-armored guards train daily. The ring of clashing steel, barked drill commands, and the blare of military horns constantly echo from its stone ramparts.
    

### Prominent Family Members (Public Knowledge)

#### Lord [[Dorant Khatru]]

- **Status:** Patriarch & Warlord of the House
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 13 Human **Fighter** (Marshal Archetype)
    
- **Description:** An imposing, battle-scarred man who commands the room by his sheer presence. Dorant approaches city governance with the inflexible mindset of a battlefield general. He has zero patience for political nuance or high-society games, viewing the more subtle houses as weak. He is fierce allies with the Imperial traditionalists and advocates for crushing city crime through absolute, overwhelming martial force.
    

#### Commander [[Krista Khatru]]

- **Status:** Eldest Daughter & [[City Watch]] Captain
    
- **Pathfinder 2e Concept:** Level 9 Human **Champion** (Paladin of [[Iomedae]] / Stalwart Defender Archetype)
    
- **Description:** Incredibly stern, dedicated, and strictly honorable, Krista is a high-ranking officer within the [[City Watch]] who oversees patrols in the volatile [[Midtown]] district. Unlike her haughty peers in the [[Nobles' Quarter]], she takes her duty to protect the city's borders seriously. She despises the lawless nature of the [[Delver's Guild]], viewing adventuring groups as reckless mercenaries who bring chaos and subterranean horrors to the city streets.
    

### PF2e Mechanical Identity for Vault Tagging

Behind the GM screen, use these Pathfinder 2e baselines to flavor House Khatru officers, elite household knights, and armored guards:

- **Ancestry:** Human, though they hold immense respect for, and frequently marry into, old **Dwarven** families who share their strict devotion to martial discipline, heavy armor, and strategic fortification.
    
- **Class Focus:** **Fighters** (specializing in heavy armor, shields, and tactical control), **Champions** (typically dedicated to lawful or martial deities like [[Iomedae]]), and aggressive **Rangers** who serve as specialized scouts.
    
- **Signature Archetypes:** _Marshal_ (for tactical auras), _Mauler_, _Sentinel_, and _Stalwart Defender_.
    
- **Tactical Focus:** In combat, Khatru forces operate as highly organized tactical units. They lean heavily on defensive formations, using feats like _Shield Wall_, _Reactive Strike_, and _Coordinated Slam_ to physically bully and lock down opponents.
    
- **Common Associations:** Strongly aligned with **[[Igor Urnst | The Commissar (Igor Urnst)]]** and the Orthodox faction of the **[[Church of Light]]**. They are the bitter, open enemies of **[[House Rau]]**, and they maintain a highly strained, mutually distrustful relationship with the independent mercenaries of the **[[Delver's Guild]]**.
