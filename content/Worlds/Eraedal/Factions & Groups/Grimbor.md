> ## Overview

The Grimbor are a formidable tribal faction within The Floenry Isles's jungle territories, characterized by a distinct caste system that leverages the unique strengths of two primary components: the Charau-ka and the Kech. They are intelligent and independent humanoids, capable of both diplomacy and fierce territorial defense. Their presence creates a challenging and memorable encounter for any who trespass into their domain.

## Membership

- **Leader:** Tribal Elders (Charau-ka)
- **Notable members:** None specified
- **Membership type:** Tribal, Monstrous, Caste System

### The Grimbor Caste System

#### 1. The Charau-ka Layer: The Tribal Core & Canopy Culture

The Charau-ka form the societal backbone and spiritual leadership of the Grimbor. They are responsible for constructing intricate treehouse settlements within the jungle canopy and managing the tribe's internal hierarchy. They represent the intelligent, independent humanoid aspect of the Grimbor, capable of negotiation, trade, and staunch defense against external threats and slavers.

**Tactics:** In combat, Charau-ka serve as scouts and ranged artillery. They utilize their natural climbing prowess and imprecise scent tracking to detect intruders early, raining down a terrifying barrage of thrown stones, debris, and clubs from the safety of the trees, keeping adversaries off-balance.

#### 2. The Kech Layer: The Elite Vanguards & Ruin Guards

The Kech are the heavy-hitting, physically imposing martial caste and specialized hunting packs of the Grimbor. While the Charau-ka inhabit the trees, the Kech claim the forest floor and ancient, overgrown jungle ruins as their domain. They are the dedicated warriors and apex predators of the territory.

**Visuals:** To maintain uniformity with the tribe, the Kech possess thick, classic Grimbor fur, rather than hairless camouflage skin.

**Tactics:** Kech do not engage in ranged attacks. Instead, they operate in highly coordinated ground packs, employing flanking maneuvers, ambushes, and overwhelming force to brutally engage trespassers in melee combat.

## Goals & Influence

The Grimbor's primary goals revolve around territorial defense, the preservation of their tribal way of life, and the protection of their hunting grounds and settlements. Their influence is localized but absolute within their jungle territories, where they act as a dominant predatory force, deterring most external incursions through their combined combat prowess and intimidating presence.

## Locations

- **Headquarters:** Complex treehouse settlements in the canopy (Charau-ka)
- **Strongholds:** Ancient, overgrown jungle ruins (Kech)
- **Associated locations:** Jungle hunting grounds, resource-rich areas within their territory.

## Plot Hooks

- **Territorial Incursion:** An adventuring party, while exploring the jungle, inadvertently stumbles into Grimbor territory. This triggers a terrifying two-pronged tactical encounter: a coordinated pack of Medium-sized Kech warriors emerges from the undergrowth or ruins, cutting off escape routes and engaging in brutal melee (The Anvil), while smaller, agile Charau-ka leap through the branches, tracking the party by scent and providing continuous ranged support (The Hammer) with clubs and heavy debris.
- **Missing Expedition:** A previous expedition into the jungle has gone missing. Traces lead to Grimbor territory, suggesting they may have been captured, sacrificed, or are being held within a Grimbor settlement or ruin.
- **Resource Dispute:** A neighboring settlement or faction requires a rare resource found exclusively in Grimbor-controlled jungle, necessitating a dangerous negotiation or infiltration mission.
- **Slave Trade Intervention:** Reports surface of slavers attempting to capture Grimbor (particularly Charau-ka) from their territories, potentially leading to a conflict where the players must choose sides or rescue captives.
