## ⚔️ Overview: The Contested Sword of the Faith

The **Order of the Dawn** is the premier, most visible, and militarily formidable holy knightly order stationed within **[[Ptolus]]**. Quartered within the brooding basalt fortress of **[[The Iron Tower]]** in the heart of the [[Temple District]], the Dawn knights are celebrated across the city as the glittering, plate-armored champions of justice who patrol the upper walls, lead strike forces into the monster-haunted [[Necropolis]], and stand as the living bulwark against the darkness rising from the subterranean undercity.

However, behind their polished gold-trimmed shields and fluttering heraldic sunburst banners, the Order of the Dawn is a house violently divided against itself.

Historically founded as the grand militant arm of the Western Empire’s Church in **[[Caer Itom]]**, the Order underwent a catastrophic identity crisis following the fall of the capital in **702 IA**. Today, the Order is fractured into two distinct ideological camps living under the same vaulted roof:

## 🏛️ The Two Factions of the Iron Tower

### 1. The Old Blood (Western Imperial Loyalists)
*   **The Cadre:** Composed of battle-scarred paladins, high officers, and noble scions who fled [[Caer Itom]] alongside Holy Emperor [[Rehobath Ylestos]]. 
*   **The Ideology:** Devout adherents of the **[[Imperial Rite - Pantheon | Imperial Rite]]**. They do not view [[Iomedae]] in isolation, but strictly as the *First Sword of [[Aroden]]*. To them, the survival of human civilization is inextricably bound to the restoration of the Lion-Guarded Throne.
*   **The Mission:** Commanded by the stern, uncompromising **Knight-Commander Darien Tallhart**, the Old Blood views Ptolus merely as a frontier staging garrison. They actively assist Grand Inquisitor [[Frederick Vane]] in hunting down ancient Azlanti superweapons in the **[[Banewarrens]]**, preparing for the day Rehobath sounds the holy crusade to march west.

### 2. The New Dawn (Knights of the Radiance)
*   **The Cadre:** Composed of locally born Ptolus knights, seasoned delvers who took holy vows, and young crusaders who have served under Prince [[Kirian Ylestos]] since before the imperial civil war began.
*   **The Ideology:** Sworn champions of the **global [[Order of Light - Pantheon | Order of Light]]**. Their devotion belongs to living gods: the mercy, healing, and holy fire of [[Sarenrae]] and the compassionate justice of [[Iomedae]]. They reject the Western Empire's authoritarian human supremacy.
*   **The Mission:** Guided by **Dame Seneschal Shannon**, the New Dawn believes their sacred duty begins and ends with the protection of Ptolus's mortal citizens. They refuse to spill blood in foreign imperial civil wars and look upon the snooping inquisitors of the Imperial Rite with deep disgust, warning that tampering with the Banewarrens will release doom upon the city.

## 🧭 Campaign Significance & Tensions

*   **The Banewarren Rifts:** Inside the barracks of **[[The Iron Tower]]**, rumors of the breached Banewarrens have driven the two factions close to open blows. When Knight-Commander Tallhart orders squads to escort Inquisitor [[Frederick Vane]] into the undercity, New Dawn knights often leak patrol routes to the [[Knights of the Golden Cross]] or Castle Shard to prevent disaster.
*   **The Adventurers' Stance:** Because the adventurers hold the **Chaos Spirit Key** and operate under the patronage of [[Lord Zavere]], Old Blood inquisitors treat them as dangerous rogue heretics, while New Dawn paladins may offer them emergency medical aid, sanctuary, and discreet warnings.
