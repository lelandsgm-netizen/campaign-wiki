## 🖐️ Overview: The Cult of Bodily Deformation

The **Cult of the Ebon Hand** is the most pervasive, grotesque, and actively feared chaos cult operating within **[[Ptolus]]**. While standard apocalyptic cabals preach destruction from a distance, the Ebon Hand preaches intimate, physiological heresy: they believe that the natural mortal form is an evolutionary cage, a temporary lie imposed by the rigid order of the gods. True perfection, they argue, is found only in the primordial chaos of the **[[Galchutt]]**—a sacred state achieved through intentional physical deformity, surgical mutation, and the grafting of aberrant flesh.

Operating through an intricate cell-structure that honeycombs the Ptolus sewers, abandoned guild houses, and forgotten sub-basements, the cult draws recruits from every echelon of society:
- Disfigured outcasts and desperate paupers from the [[Warrens]] seeking physical power.
- Wealthy, thrill-seeking nobles from the [[Nobles' Quarter]] bored with mortal decadence.
- Renegade alchemists and fleshcrafters obsessed with forbidden biotechnical augmentation.

Presided over by **[[Shigmaa Wuntad]]**, the Cult of the Ebon Hand has transitioned from a loose collection of subterranean fanatics into a coordinated paramilitary vanguard actively working to dismantle the city’s defenses.

1. **The Rite of the Black Hand:** Initiates undergo a horrific ritual wherein their right hand is severed and plunged into boiling, chaotically charged bitumen. When withdrawn, the hand regrows as a blackened, chitinous claw possessing extra joints, serrated talons, or a functioning, lidless eye on the palm.
2. **Chaositech Integration:** Unlike traditional mages who reject profane machinery, the Ebon Hand readily embraces **chaositech**—weapons, sensory implants, and combat appendages forged from a nightmarish fusion of living tissue, pulsing tubing, and corrupted brass salvaged from ancient undercity caches.
3. **The Banewarrens Excavations:** The cult's primary directive under Wuntad is to break into the **[[Banewarrens]]**. Cultists believe that Danar's ancient vaults contain the *Hand of Danar* and primeval chaos mutagen that can accelerate their flesh-warping rites across the entire surface population.

## 🧭 Active Campaign Clashes

*   **The Hunt for the Key:** Following the ambush at **[[Pythoness House]]**, Ebon Hand scouts are tearing through the Oldtown underground, frantically searching for the **Chaos Spirit Key** that [[Calebrimbor Algarvran | Calebrimbor]] concealed from Wuntad.
*   **The Infiltration of Dalenguard:** Lord Zavere warns that Ebon Hand cultists have begun using flesh-shaping cosmetics and mental conditioning to infiltrate the lower ranks of the [[City Watch]], feeding patrol schedules to Wuntad’s strike teams.
