## ⚰️ Overview: The Aristocracy of the Grave

**The Forsaken** are the clandestine, aristocratic syndicate of intelligent undead and allied mortal necromancers who claim absolute sovereign dominion over the **[[Necropolis]]** of **[[Ptolus]]**. 

Unlike mindless zombies or feral ghouls driven by base hunger, the Forsaken are composed of ancient beings who retain their memories, intellect, and aristocratic culture: noble vampires who were interred during the founding of the Western Empire, preserved mummy-lords from forgotten desert dynasties, cunning wights who commanded imperial legions, and powerful living spellcasters led by master necromancer **[[Vamal Shard]]**.

The Forsaken do not view themselves as monsters, but as the natural and immortal evolution of mortal civilization. They hold court in lavishly appointed subterranean crypts beneath the **[[Dark Reliquary]]**, draped in centuries-old brocades and illuminated by cold corpse-light. Here, they conduct delicate diplomatic summits, trade ancient scrolls looted from the deep levels of [[Dwarvenhearth]], and debate necromantic metaphysics.

## 🏛️ The Cold Truce of the Lead Gates

The Forsaken maintain a delicate, cold-blooded equilibrium with the surface city:
1. **The Daylight Compact:** The Forsaken strictly honor the daytime curfew of the **[[Gates of Lead]]**. They remain sealed in their subterranean sarcophagi while the living visit family graves, only emerging when the lead bells toll sundown.
2. **Discreet Feeding:** The vampire houses of the Forsaken avoid open massacres on the streets of Ptolus. Instead, they procure blood through discreet smuggling networks operating through the [[Docks]], using proxies to buy terminally ill paupers, condemned criminals, or purchasing blood-flasks from corrupt apothecaries.
3. **The Buffer of the Undead:** The Forsaken actively police rogue, feral undead within the cemetery walls. They recognize that if mindless ghouls or shrieking wraiths breach the city walls and slaughter commoners, Commissar **[[Igor Urnst]]** and Prince **[[Kirian Ylestos]]** will have no political choice but to unleash an army of holy paladins and war-clerics to raze the Necropolis to the bedrock.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Poisoned Vintage:** Someone is supplying the Forsaken's vampire covens with blood infected by an alchemical chaositech taint, driving young vampires into psychotic, uncontrolled blood-rages that terrorize the [[Rivergate District]]. Lord Malathir secretly hires the party to find and execute the poisoner before the City Watch retaliates.
2. **The Necrotic Vault Key:** [[Vamal Shard]] contacts the party through an underworld contact in [[Delver's Square]], seeking an ancient obsidian key discovered in the **[[Banewarrens]]**. In exchange, he offers to share classified notes regarding the **[[Night of Dissolution]]** and the locations of Galchutt sleeper-nodes.
3. **The Breach of the Ossuary:** A faction of younger wights rebels against the Council of the Tomb, attempting to tunnel out of the [[Ossuary]] directly into the city sewers beneath Midtown. The party is contracted to enter the tunnels from both sides and crush the mutineers before they surface.
