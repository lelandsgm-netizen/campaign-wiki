## 🧭 Overview: The Lifeline of the Subterranean Frontier

The **Delver's Guild** is the beating commercial, logistical, and operational spine of adventuring culture in **[[Ptolus]]**. Founded shortly after the breach of [[Dwarvenhearth]] in **713 IA** sparked the great subterranean gold rush, the Guild was established to bring order, mutual protection, and standardized contracts to what was previously a chaotic, bloody free-for-all among freelance mercenaries.

Operating out of a sprawling, reinforced stone guildhall fronting **[[Delver's Square]]**, the Guild provides essential infrastructure for anyone brave—or foolish—enough to descend through the **Delver’s Stair** into the sewers, Ghulvorn labyrinths, and ancient vaults beneath the city.

For a modest annual tithe (the standard Guild Token), the Delver’s Guild transforms reckless tomb-raiding into a professional enterprise: offering access to verified dungeon maps, renting enchanted excavation gear, appraising salvaged relics, and—most importantly—operating elite **Retrieval Teams** contracted to recover fallen or paralyzed members from the depths before the subterranean horrors can devour them.

1. **The Great Map Room:** The most secure archive in Midtown. Staffed by guild cartographers, the Map Room catalogs hundreds of parchment surveys detailing sewer junctions, water levels, known monster lairs, and breached subterranean vaults. Members can buy copies or submit new surveys for substantial bounties.
2. **The Retrieval Unit (Rescue Insurance):** The Guild’s most famous service. Guild members wear an attuned copper-and-dawnsilver guild medallion. If a member fails to return by a contracted time, or if their life-thread snaps, the Guild mobilizes heavily armed, high-level rescue squads equipped with tracking crystals and *teleport* scrolls to extract them or recover their remains for resurrection at the [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]].
3. **The Assay Offices:** Operating alongside merchant appraisers from the [[Council of Coin]], Guild arcanists identify magical properties, detect ancient curses, and ensure that dangerous relics do not detonate in open market stalls.
4. **Resurrection Covenants:** The Guild maintains bulk contracts with both the [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]] and the [[Order of Light - Pantheon | Order of Light]], securing discounted diamond components and resurrection priority for members who carry prepaid funerary bonds.

## ⚖️ Pathfinder 2e Remaster Mechanics

Members of the Delver's Guild in good standing gain access to unique gear, operational support, and specialized contacts:

*   **Guild Membership Token:** Carrying a registered Delver's Token grants a +1 item bonus to Society and Diplomacy checks when interacting with merchants in Delver's Square and underworld informants.
*   **Emergency Extraction Contract:** An active membership guarantees that if a party member is left behind in the upper dungeon levels (above deep Dwarvenhearth), a Retrieval Squad will deploy within 2d4 hours (DC 25 flat check to recover the body safely without gear loss).

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Ghost Map of the Banewarrens:** A dying explorer stumbles out of the Delver's Stair into Delver's Square, clutching a blood-stained survey parchment that depicts an uncharted entry into the **[[Banewarrens]]**. Guildmaster Brigan needs a trusted party to verify the route before [[House Balacazar]]’s agents murder the cartographers.
2. **The Retrieval Ambush:** A high-ranking Retrieval Squad sent into the Oldtown sewers to rescue a noble scion has gone dark. The Guild hires the party to track the rescue team, discovering they were ambushed by the **[[Cult of the Ebon Hand]]** and mutated into fleshwarped thralls.
3. **The Synod Tax Audit:** Grand Inquisitor [[Frederick Vane]] of the [[The Inquisitorial Synod | Inquisitorial Synod]] arrives at the guildhall with a company of Old Blood knights, demanding the immediate confiscation of all subterranean map records under imperial forfeiture edicts. Brigan looks to the party to secretly smuggle the sensitive archive vaults to safety.
