## ⚔️ Overview: The Ancient Guardians of the Seal

The **Knights of the Golden Cross** is the oldest, most secretive, and historically tragic knightly order on [[Eraedal]]. Founded thousands of years ago during the mythic antiquity preceding the [[Western Empire]], the order was originally established by **Danar the Fair** to serve as the holy sentinels guarding the subterranean perimeter of the **[[Banewarrens]]**.

When Danar fell into madness and became the dreaded Dark Lord, it was the Knights of the Golden Cross who refused to follow their creator into damnation. Standing alongside ancient dwarven heroes from [[Dwarvenhearth]] and champions of light, the Knights fought against their fallen master, ultimately securing the adamantine gates and hiding the runic keys across the continent so that the horrors Danar collected could never be unleashed.

Centuries later during the [[Ghul Wars]], the Knights rode again, clashing with the necro-mechanical legions of the Half-God Ghul. 

In modern Ptolus, the Order survives not as a glittering army, but as a dedicated, clandestine brotherhood. Operating from concealed undercity chapterhouses, the Knights recognize what the secular politicians and greedy delvers do not: that the earth beneath Ptolus is pregnant with ancient nightmares, and that the recent breaches into the Banewarrens represent an existential threat to all mortal life.

1. **The Inviolability of the Vault:** The Knights do not seek glory, gold, or political power. Their primary oath is to seal the Banewarrens. They view anyone attempting to retrieve artifacts from Danar's vaults—whether chaotic zealots like **[[Shigmaa Wuntad]]** or imperial theocrats like **[[Rehobath Ylestos]]**—as existential adversaries.
2. **The Golden Cross Signet:** True members bear an enchanted golden signet ring capable of detecting the presence of chaotic relics and identifying fellow knights through secret warding pulses.
3. **Alliance with Castle Shard:** Recognizing that **[[Lord Zavere]]** and **[[Lady Rael]]** seek containment rather than plunder, the Golden Cross maintains a discreet intelligence pact with Castle Shard.

## 🧭 Story Directives for the Party

1. **The Meeting at the Crypt:** Lord Zavere provides the party with a token of introduction to meet **High Preceptor Kalis Dru** in a forgotten crypt beneath the [[Temple District]], where the Knights can inspect the **Chaos Spirit Key** and provide them with warding amulets against the Banewarren miasma.
2. **Preventing the Church Incursion:** The Knights warn the party that Holy Emperor Rehobath’s elite inquisitors have located a secondary conduit into the outer vaults, urging the adventurers to reach the inner seal before imperial legionnaires trigger a catastrophic curse-flare.
3. **The Cleansing Rite:** The Golden Cross offers to aid the party in performing a sacred purification ritual to prevent Wuntad from tracking the chaotic resonances of their runic birthmarks.
