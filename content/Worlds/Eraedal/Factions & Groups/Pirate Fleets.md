## Overview

A diverse collection of freebooters and corsairs operating throughout Eraedal, primarily within the Floenry Islands and preying upon the vital Strait of Relar. These groups vary wildly in motivation, ranging from bloodthirsty cutthroats and covert agents to unprincipled crews, classic rogues, and those driven by vengeance or malevolent influence. Pirate activity significantly impacts global trade, causing catastrophic merchant losses, though sovereign kingdoms are often reluctant to fund naval interventions. Periodical naval crusades temporarily suppress piracy, but it inevitably resurges in a 15-25 year cycle. The Western Empire remains uniquely unaffected, hinting at covert geopolitical arrangements. The Floenry Islands provide ideal hideouts, concealing an estimated 150 active pirate vessels targeting specific nations, corporate lines, or acting out of desperation.

## Membership

- **Leader:** Varied, from individual captains to highly skilled Level 8-12 leaders (Pirate Federation), Level 6 Troll arcane spellcasters (Vunoon's Raiders), Level 5 shapechanging infiltrators (Valicar's Raiders), and Level 7 divine spellcasters (Shrr-Ta Jungle Raiders).
- **Notable members:** Experienced crews (Level 5-8 for Federation), specialized magical officer corps (Vunoon's), shapechangers, master infiltrators, primal spellcasters, young intelligent dragons (Valicar's), catfolk (Tezcat), ogre hunters, human/orc outcasts (Shrr-Ta), sea devils, merfolk scouts, aquatic monsters (Submarine League).
- **Membership type:** Diverse: loose alliances (Pirate Federation), disciplined crews (Vunoon's Raiders), elite forces (Valicar's Raiders), tribal contingents (Shrr-Ta Jungle Raiders), and specialized non-human alliances (Submarine League).

## Goals & Influence

- **Goals:** Plunder, terrorize coastal trade routes, seek dominion or vengeance, fulfill black-market contracts for wealthy clients (including the Western Empire), acquire specific goods, target wealthy passengers, or secure high-value cargos for ransom. Some are driven by desperation (low morale, damaged ships, need for plunder).
- **Rivalries:** The Pirate Federation is known to attack rival pirates.
- **Influence:** Severely disrupt global maritime trade, forcing merchant houses into bankruptcy and impacting prices. Their activity indirectly influences kingdoms' decisions regarding naval spending and military intervention. They also serve as deniable assets for covert geopolitical operations (e.g., Western Empire's unaffected trade).

## Locations

- **Headquarters:** Floenry Island hideouts (currently ~150 vessels), Island of Totugo (heavily fortified sanctuary), potentially extradimensional safe havens (Vunoon's Raiders).
- **Strongholds:** Secret bases across the Floenry Islands, potentially in areas like Osto (sheltered bays).
- **Associated locations:**
  - **Strait of Relar:** Primary maritime trade lane.
  - **Sulanok (Pendaltor peninsula):** Free port with black-market fences for stolen cargo.
  - **Ramsbottom (near Sulanok):** "Deadeye Suli's" tavern, a recruiting station for the Pirate Federation.
  - **Oen, Lyrd, South Bay, Grimbor Isle, Dragon's Roost, Serpent's Tooth, The Rock, Osto, The Finger, West Mnn:** Various Floenry Islands used for hiding, resupply, or avoidance.
  - **Cyclone:** Neutral merchant port that welcomes pirate crews for resupply.

## Plot Hooks

- **Naval Crusade:** PCs are hired to participate in or evade a kingdom's naval campaign against pirates.
- **High-Stakes Contract:** PCs are offered a black-market contract to recover specific goods or capture a target for a wealthy client, potentially working for or against a pirate faction.
- **Deniable Operations:** PCs uncover evidence of the Western Empire's covert involvement with privateers or their elusive "Black Ships" and must decide how to act.
- **Smuggler's Run:** PCs need to smuggle goods through pirate-infested waters or use pirate connections to bypass legitimate trade routes.
- **Pirate Lord's Gambit:** PCs are tasked with infiltrating a pirate faction (e.g., the Federation, Vunoon's Raiders) to gather intelligence, rescue a captive, or disrupt their operations.
- **Arena Combat:** Non-human PCs are targeted for recruitment or kidnapping for the gladiatorial arena in the Free Port of West Mnn, or must intervene to save others.
- **Island Investigation:** PCs investigate rumors about dangerous islands like the "Swarm of South Bay" or the "Insanity of Enry," facing unique ecological or psychological threats.
- **Dragon's Tribute:** PCs encounter draconic or monstrous pirates (sphinxes, dragons, harpies) demanding tribute for safe passage.
- **Justice in West Mnn:** PCs commit a crime in West Mnn and must navigate its corrupt legal system, pay steep fines, or face the arena.
