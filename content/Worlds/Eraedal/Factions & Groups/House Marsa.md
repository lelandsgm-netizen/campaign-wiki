### 🌊 Geopolitical Profile & Identity

House Marsa is a proud, salt-crusted lineage of frontier marshals and naval engineers who rule the northernmost tier of the plains. While the flashy cavalry barons of central [[Arcadia]] focus entirely on open grasslands and breeding horses, the Marsa nobles are defined by cold iron, heavy stone walls, and the turbulent waves of the **Straits of Thunder**.

They view themselves as the ultimate unsung heroes of the [[Tarldet Plains]]—the first line of defense against seaborne barbarian raiders from the [[Old Kingdom]] and corsairs operating across the northern waters. Because of their continuous combat exposure against naval anomalies, their fighting edge has atrophied significantly less than the landlocked interior militias.

### 🤝 The Relationship Matrix

#### 1. The Friction with House Wenglid

- **The Economic Drain:** House Marsa relies on heavy architectural labor to maintain their massive sea-walls and keep their shipwright foundries running hot. They are privately furious with [[House Wenglid]] for unilaterally changing the regional charter to emancipate fighting-age slaves.

- **The Conscription Squeeze:** Marsa is complying with the 5-year military conscription directive, but they aren't happy about it. They have modified the policy locally: instead of handing liberated Orcs and Goblins pikes for the western front lines, Marsa is pressing them into brutal, high-mortality rowing service in the northern defensive galleys or forcing them to work the high-heat coal foundries.

#### 2. Outlook on the Border War

- **The Vulnerable Shoreline:** House Marsa views the civil war with the Middle Kingdoms with profound anxiety. While Arcadia looks west toward glory, Marsa looks east and north. They know that as Imperial forces are drawn inward to fight the rebel kingdoms, the northern maritime defenses are dangerously thinned out.

- **Fear of Coastal Exploitation:** They live in constant fear that naval factions aligned with the Middle Kingdoms—or opportunistic monster hordes from the Old Kingdom Frontier—will recognize that the Straits of Thunder are lightly guarded and initiate a full-scale amphibious invasion of the Tarldet northern flank.

#### 3. Stance on Holy Emperor Rehobath Ylestos

- **Absolute Tactical Devotion:** Culturally, the Marsa line has zero tolerance for the secular, fluid politics of [[Caer Itom]]. Because their ancestral bloodline suffered heavily during historic religious expulsions, they are fanatically loyal to the [[Church of Light]].

- **The [[Ptolus]] Anchor:** House Marsa openly and aggressively supports **[[Rehobath Ylestos | The Holy Emperor Rehobath]]**. They view his presence in nearby [[Ptolus]] as a divine insurance policy. Their strategy is simple: if the [[Western Empire]]'s core lines collapse due to the civil war, House Marsa intends to pivot entirely, using their fleet to form a unified coastal iron wall alongside the Commissar's Janissaries to defend [[Ptolus]]

### 👥 Prominent Characters of Note

#### [[Margrave Vaelen Marsa]]

- **Pathfinder 2e Registry:** `Level 8 Human Fighter (Aquatic Combatant / Shield Marshal)`

- **Description:** A scarred, gravel-voiced veteran wearing sea-salt encrusted full-plate armor, sporting a closely cropped gray beard and cold, unblinking eyes. He rarely visits the pleasure arenas of Arcadia, preferring the damp wind of his coastal watchtowers.

- **Temporal Status & Outlook:** Vaelen is completely focused on fortifying the Straits of Thunder before the Middle Kingdoms fully exploit the empire's fractured lines. He treats the newly freed Orcs and Goblins under the Wenglid Conscription contract with absolute ruthlessness, viewing them strictly as organic fuel to keep his war galleys moving. He has a direct line of hidden communication with the military commanders in [[Ptolus]], preparing for the moment he must declare total independence from the western interior.

#### [[High Forgemaster Thrum]]

- **Pathfinder 2e Registry:** `Level 6 Hobgoblin Inventor / Cleric (Torag)`

- **Description:** A massive, soot-stained Hobgoblin whose skin is heavily patterned with ritual brandings of molten metal. Unlike the mistreated non-humans of the western interior, Thrum’s family line was integrated into the Marsa foundries centuries ago, earning him an elite rank within the province's military engineering corps.

- **Temporal Status & Outlook:** Thrum oversees the heavy armories of the northern coast, translating the divine blueprints of **[[Torag]]** into reinforced iron plates for Marsa’s defensive structures and war ships. He is fiercely loyal to the Marsa family line but is secretly using the chaos of the Wenglid Emancipation Charter to quietly ensure that the newly formed non-human conscript battalions are given superior, heavy-grade armor and weapons—quietly preparing them to survive the brutal meat-grinder to come.
