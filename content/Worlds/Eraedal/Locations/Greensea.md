## 📍 Overview
***Greensea Province** (Managed by [[House Amberhall]]) is a rugged northern territory with a long history of seafaring that serves as a fiercely loyal vassal state to [[House Ne'Klosh]]’s imperial claim. Governed by [[House Amberhall]]—who remain deeply indebted to [[House Ne'Klosh]] for saving them during the Civil Wars—the province has rebounded from wartime devastation to become a noteworthy shipbuilding powerhouse and a primary launch point for perilous voyages into the [[Sea of Despair]] or colony fleets bound for the [[northern frontiers]]. The territory is defended by the celebrated Amberhall Marines, a highly efficient amphibious unit engineered to combat aggressive sea monsters and coastal pirates, whose veterans are frequently promoted to elite Imperial Janissaries. At the center of this maritime frontier sits the provincial capital of **[[Greensea]]**, a heavily fortified shoreline metropolis of 30,000 residents who must constantly endure brutal ocean storms, sea beast migrations, and severe isolation. This harsh environment has bred a tough, deeply conservative, and xenophobic populace—composed primarily of humans and rugged dwarves—who fiercely reject outside influences, openly banning elves from entering their borders and reacting with explosive, violent frenzy at the mere whisper of a changeling or shapeshifter infiltration.*
