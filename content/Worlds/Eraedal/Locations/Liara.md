## 📍 Overview
***[[Liara]] Province** (commonly known by its capital, **[[Liara]]**) is a vital inland agricultural territory defined by its rolling crop fields, expansive orchards, and high-yield grape distilleries, but politically estranged from its coastal neighbors due to a bubbling, well-organized separatist movement. While the region’s agricultural wealth is critical to [[Upper Kighfalton]]'s supply chain, its populace feels a deep cultural kinship with the rival territory of [[West Kighfalton]], actively organizing a quiet but militant underground network that views a bloody civil rebellion as an inevitable price for secession. Governed by **[[House Zeitch]]**, the territory suffers under a leadership widely dismissed by rival noble houses as a politically inept, uninspired dynasty. Composed of an massive extended lineage of over 350 members renowned for an extraordinary genetic anomalies of identical twins and triplets, the house remains completely asleep at the wheel regarding the insurrection gathering right outside their palace gates. At the center of this impending conflict lies the provincial capital of **[[Liara]]**, a tightly packed city of 45,000 residents where traditional imperial human supremacy has dramatically eroded. Bonded by a shared separatist ideology, the diverse populace openly unites across ancestral lines—welcoming humans, Goblins, Wolfen, and Trolls alike into the revolutionary fold—maintaining a deceptive, quiet peace on the city streets that masks an imminent transition into a blood-soaked guerrilla war zone.*

## 🗺️ Points of Interest
- **[[Rolling Orchards]]**
- The vast grape and fruit fields that dominate the interior landscape, hiding cell structures of active separatist rebels.
    
- **[[Zeitch Ancestral Manor]]**
- The massive, labyrinthine palace crowded with the bloated, politically oblivious members of House Zeitch.
    
- **[[Underground Alliance Cells]]**
- The secret meetings places inside Liara where humans, Wolfen, and Goblins construct weapons and plan the coming insurgence.
