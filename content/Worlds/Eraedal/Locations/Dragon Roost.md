## Overview

Dragon Roost is an island universally avoided by sailors in the region, known for being a dangerous tropical paradise where mighty dragons roam. Despite the immense value of Horned Dragon bones, teeth, and scales, all expeditions to hunt these creatures have met with disaster. The island is teeming with primordial creatures, making it one of the most hazardous locations in the Floenry chain.

## Geography

The island is covered with thick, lush tropical forest. Its north and west shores are rimmed by low cliffs (80 to 120 feet tall) from which jagged rocks rise out of deep coastal waters, making ship landings extremely dangerous. Only the eastern tip of the island offers a safe approach for ships. The interior is characterized by a series of hills arranged in a "V" shape pointing to the west, with the shadowy valleys at the point of the "V" serving as prime habitat for the powerful Horned Dragons.

## Governance

Dragon Roost possesses no civilized governance. The island's harsh environment and its dominant, territorial primeval inhabitants, particularly the Horned Dragons, effectively rule the land. Any who venture there are subject to the dangers of its ecology rather than any established authority.

## Points of Interest

- **V-shaped Valleys (Horned Dragon Lairs)**
  - The deepest jungles within the V-shaped hills serve as the primary roaming grounds and languishing spots for the mighty Horned Dragons, where they can find shade and lakes.
- **Rocky North & West Coasts**
  - The jagged coastlines along the north and west are home to a large tribe of sea devils living beneath the waves.
- **Eastern Tip**
  - The only safe location for ships to approach and land on the island.

## Notes

### Inhabitants

Dragon Roost is home to a diverse and dangerous array of primordial creatures:

- **Horned Dragons:** Mighty Primal-tradition dragons, typically found in groups of up to two dozen adults and a handful of young, roaming the deepest jungle valleys. They are terrifying, localized apex predators, characterized by raw physical brutality, tracking abilities, and the capacity to impale victims on their massive horns. Their magic ties directly to nature, weather, and the elements. They are known for being extremely territorial, mean-tempered, and aggressive.
- **Harpies:** Frequently seen circling the hills, preying on the young and weak among the island's creatures, possibly including young dragons.
- **Drakes, Basilisks, Wyverns, Chimeras, and Sphinx:** Other dangerous creatures that have been reported by survivors of past expeditions.
- **Sea Devils:** A large tribe resides under the waves along the rocky north coast.
- **Deep-sea Serpents:** Commonly sighted around the south, north, and eastern shores, potentially preying on the local sea devil population.

### Danger Level

The island is considered extremely dangerous. Merchant ships give it a wide berth of approximately 50 miles, and will hesitate to aid any ships in distress if they are within 30 miles of the island due to the pervasive threats. All previous expeditions seeking to hunt Horned Dragons for their valuable parts have ended in disaster.
