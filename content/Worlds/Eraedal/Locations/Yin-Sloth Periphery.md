## 🌿 Overview: The Green Wall

The **Yin-Sloth Periphery** (canonically chronicled as the **Northern Freelands**) is a four-hundred-mile wilderness buffer zone separating the decadent civilizations of the north from the sovereign realms of the deep southern hemisphere. Stretching unbroken from the arid margins of the **[[Baalgor Wastelands]]** and the savannahs of the **[[Land of the South Winds]]** to the jagged ramparts of the **[[Dragon's Gate|Dragon's Gate Mountains]]**, this territory has defied foreign conquerors for over two millennia.

To the **[[Western Empire]]**, the Periphery is an untamed resource frontier: a source of lucrative natural rubber, exotic beasts for Caer Itom's arenas, and captive slaves. But to those who dwell beneath its suffocating three-hundred-foot canopy, it is a realm of fierce, uncompromising sanctuary. Untouched by roads, cities, or imperial law, the Periphery is defended by reclusive indigenous tribes, territorial **[[Tezcat]]** pride-packs, and hardened communities of escaped slaves who would sooner die fighting than return to the iron neck-collars of northern plantations.

## 🗺️ Geography & Survival

Navigating the Periphery is a harrowing journey through dense, multi-tiered rainforest:

```
          [ LAND OF THE SOUTH WINDS & BAALGOR WASTELANDS ]
                                 |
              [ THE DRIFTING FOOTHILLS & SCRUB BORDER ]
          (Slaver Outposts | Fortified Rubber-Camps)
                                 |
                 [ THE HIGH CANOPY OF WEAPONS ]
        (300-Foot Ironwood Trees, Vine-Highways, Avian Eyries)
                                 |
                 [ THE RUNAWAY HIDEOUTS & CAVES ]
        (Fortified Stockades, Pit-Traps, Free Communities)
                                 |
              [ THE RIVER DELTAS: RUNOFF OF BLOOD ]
        (Crocodilian Nesting Grounds, Krel Ambush Creeks)
                                 |
            [ DRAGON'S GATE MOUNTAINS / VALLEY OF SKULLS ]
```

1. **The Three-Tiered Canopy**: Giant mahogany, ironwood, and kapok trees soar hundreds of feet above the jungle floor. Sunlight rarely touches the earth, creating a perpetual emerald gloom. Upper branches are bound together by colossal lianas and strangler figs, forming natural aerial roads traveled by **Avians** and catfolk.
2. **The Slaver's Gauntlet**: Along the northern border, illegal logging camps, rubber-smelting kilns, and fortified slaver stockades probe into the tree line. Armed mercenary bands from the Western Empire venture south on brutal "safaris" to capture peaceful **[[Grimbor]]** and jungle natives.
3. **The Runaway Redoubts**: Hidden in deep limestone ravines and behind thundering waterfalls lie self-sufficient settlements of escaped non-human slaves. These fugitives—Orcs, Goblins, Ogres, and humans—have fortified their villages with lethal punji stick pits, deadfall log traps, and horn poison blowguns. Anyone carrying forged metal weapons or armor is treated as an imperial slaver and executed without parley.
4. **Predatory Megafauna**: Beyond humanoid conflicts, the Periphery harbors primal terrors found nowhere else: colossal *Tomb Worms*, camouflaged *Acid Lizards*, venomous *Tatzelwurms*, and the vicious arboreal ambushers known as the **Krel**.

## 📜 The Tri-System Synthesis: Frontier Lore

### 1. The Physical Shell (Palladium Canon)
- Sourced from *Palladium Fantasy RPG Book 07: Yin-Sloth Jungles (2E)* (pages 121–123).
- Accurately details the **Northern Freelands** as the unconquered buffer zone between the human kingdoms and the Conquered Lands / Orcish Empire, detailing the escaped slave populations and the ferocity of the native tribes.

### 2. The Ancient Bones (Praemal / Ptolus Lore)
- Before the canopy grew, this land hosted the outer suburbs and agricultural terraced canals of the pre-cataclysmic civilization destroyed during the **Battle of Gods** (90,000 years ago).
- Moss-covered statues of forgotten celestial entities sit half-buried in the mud. Beneath several large sinkholes lie sealed crystalline chambers resonant with ancient eldritch energies, guarding primeval artifacts designed to pacify the chaotic dreaming of the **Galchutt**.

### 3. The Active Pulse (Pathfinder 2e Remaster)
- **Pathfinder Survival Engine**: The jungle tests explorers with high-tier PF2e environmental hazards: tropical heat, malaria and swamp-rot (Level 4-6 diseases), quicksand bogs, and treacherous climbing DCs (+18 to +24 for mossy ironwood branches).
- **Faith**:
  - **[[Gozreh]]**: The Wind and the Waves, reverenced as the Living Green and monsoon fury.
  - **Primal Animism**: Worshipped by native witchdoctors who summon primal nature spirits and jungle elementals.
  - **[[Pharasma]]**: Honored by fugitive elders who bury their dead with simple stone rites to prevent them from rising as jungle ghouls.

## 🎯 Campaign & Adventure Hooks

1. **The Slaver Convoy Interception**: A notorious slaver captain from the [[Land of the South Winds]] has captured a family of six peaceful [[Grimbor]] giants and is marching them north toward Sulanok. A desperate runaway guide begs the party to stage a lightning ambush along the River of Blood before the giants reach the open savannah.
2. **The Sunken Observatory**: Severe monsoon rains wash away a riverbank, exposing the obsidian lintel of a 90,000-year-old temple. A scholar from [[Old Timiro]] offers a royal sum for an escort, but a local pride of [[Tezcat]] considers the ruin a holy taboo and marks the expedition for death.
3. **The Escape Pipeline**: Fugitives from the slave markets of [[Aracho]] and [[Credia]] use a secret underground railroad leading through Pendaltor and across into the Periphery. The party is hired to protect a shipment of stolen dwarven tools and seeds bound for the runaway haven of *Freedom's Glen*.
