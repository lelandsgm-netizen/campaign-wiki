## 👑 Overview

The **Timro Kingdom** (canonically known as the *Timiro Kingdom*) is the second-oldest human civilization upon the continent of [[Eraedal]], surpassed in antiquity only by the decadent **[[Western Empire]]**. Forged centuries ago in the bitter aftermath of the Elf-Dwarf War and the cleansing of the wilderness, Timro arose as a realm of righteous knights, sworn paladins, stone citadels, and absolute devotion to the code of chivalry.

Today, the kingdom stands at the absolute zenith of its worldly prosperity—an era historians call its **Golden Age**. Its merchant fleets dominate the southern sea lanes; its fertile eastern heartlands yield boundless harvests; its aristocratic courts languish in imported Western silks, rare spices, and vintage wines; and its armadas of armored warships rule the waves without equal.

Yet beneath this gleaming mantle of martial pride and high chivalry lies an empire rotting from within. A creeping decadence has softened the warrior spirit of its noble houses; its youthful lords duel over vain courtly insults; and, most perilous of all, its colossal agricultural and mining economy has become utterly dependent upon the brutal subjugation of tens of thousands of **enslaved non-humans** (Orcs, Goblins, and Ogres). As unrest spreads across the eastern plantations and colonial insurrections erupt abroad in the **[[Floenry Isles]]**, the seeds of a bloody reckoning have already taken deep root.

- **The Northern Frontier**: Guarded by the imposing chain of the **Old Kingdom Mountains**. A string of stone fortress-outposts (such as [[Tanis]]) and watchtowers hold back the ravenous non-human tribes, rogue monsters, and ancient terrors lurking within the ruins of the fallen [[Old Kingdom]].
- **The Southern Seaboard**: Extensive coastlines rimming the **Sea of Despair** and the **Strait of Relar**. Dotted with bustling fishing havens, commercial shipyards, and deep-water naval ports like [[Credia]] and [[Aracho]].

- **The Eastern Heartlands**: Expansive, rolling plains and river basins clear-cut into sprawling grain fields, vineyards, and sugarcane plantations, bordering the frontier lands of the **[[Eastern Territories]]**.

## 🏛️ Government, Crown & Society

### The Crown of House Gedro
Timro is ruled from the marble spires of [[Credia]] by **King Gedro** and the High Royal Council. The crown maintains nominal absolute authority, though it must constantly balance the competing ambitions of powerful dukes, wealthy guild masters, and the supreme hierarchy of the Church. The succession remains volatile: younger royals, such as the ambitious eighteen-year-old Prince **Bartholmew Gedro** (stationed in the provincial castle of Aria), actively plot to enhance their prestige and secure the throne.

### The Chivalric Aristocracy & Decadence
Feudal titles—Dukes, Barons, Counts, and sworn Knights—are jealously guarded by human and elven aristocratic dynasties. While ancient laws celebrate personal honor, piety, and the protection of the weak, the contemporary nobility has largely succumbed to luxurious indolence. Young lords maintain private retinues of dueling masters, squander fortunes on gladiatorial games, and treat affairs of state as theatrical diversions, leaving mundane governance in the hands of bureaucratic magistrates and mercenary captains.

### The Guild Council
In major cities like Credia and Aracho, over **forty recognized craft and merchant guilds**—ranging from the *Alchemists' Guild*, *Armorers' Guild*, and *Shipbuilders' Guild* to the *Scribes'* and *Physicians' Guilds*—wield immense economic leverage under the aegis of **[[Abadar]]**. The Guild Council effectively nominates the mayors of imperial cities and controls municipal tariffs, port taxes, and trade monopolies.

### The Ticking Calamity: Institutional Slavery
The dark foundation supporting Timro's golden prosperity is **chattel slavery**:
- In the western provinces, enslaved non-humans account for roughly **25%** of the population.
- In the eastern agricultural boomlands and port cities (such as [[Aracho]] and Partha), enslaved laborers comprise an alarming **40% or more** of the total populace.
- **Racial Divide**: Under Timro imperial law, only **Humans**, **Elves**, and **Dwarves** are recognized as sovereign persons endowed with civil rights. All other ancestries—specifically **Orcs**, **Goblins**, and **Ogres**—are legally classified as subhuman chattel, subjected to hard labor in stone quarries, iron mines, agricultural plantations, and the rowing benches of royal war galleys.
- **The Looming Uprising**: Decades of systematic brutality have stoked a smoldering fury among the slave quarters. Escaped slaves flee into the mountains to join free tribal warbands or seek refuge with outlawed insurrectionist cells like the **[[Free Slave Coalition]]**. This powder keg extends directly into Timro's overseas colonies: the anti-colonial uprising currently engulfing [[Oen]]—led by Orc Warlord **[[Drogon Deathbringer]]** and the **[[Order of the Indomitable Shield]]**—draws its burning hatred directly from the horrors of Timro's slave markets.

## ⚔️ The Royal Military

Timro boasts one of the most formidable and well-organized military structures in Eraedal, organized into distinct branches:

### 1. The Sentinels (Elite Cavalry)
The most prestigious and revered branch of the Royal Army, restricted strictly to warriors of proven **noble human or elven lineage**. Clad in fine-honed armor and mounted on purebred war chargers, the Sentinels operate as heavy strike lancers along the hostile northern border. They are renowned for their discipline, martial perfection, and strict adherence to the ancient knightly code.

### 2. The Light Cavalry & Royal Infantry
- **Light Cavalry**: Manned primarily by the younger sons of the gentry and wealthy merchant families; tasked with rapid reconnaissance, patrolling coastal highways, and responding to banditry.
- **Royal Foot Soldiers**: The sturdy backbone of the army. Tens of thousands of disciplined infantry armed with short spears, broadswords, shields, and leather brigandines, stationed across municipal garrisons and border bastions.

### 3. The Mercenary Auxiliaries
Because Timro nobles loathe long, miserable winter postings along the monster-infested northern frontier, the Crown employs vast contingents of foreign mercenaries. These hired companies garrison the freezing watchtowers of the Old Kingdom border, bearing the brunt of Orcish mountain raids and wild beast attacks.

### 4. The Timro Royal Navy
The supreme jewel of the kingdom's military might is the **[[Timro Royal Navy]]**. 
- **Fleet Composition**: Hundreds of war vessels—from swift scouting schooners and heavy sailing frigates to massive, two-hundred-foot war galleys and legendary **Armored Dragon Slayers** (reinforced floating fortresses designed specifically to harpoon and slay colossal deep-sea serpents).
- **Warlock / Kineticist Officers**: Every naval squadron is supported by commissioned **Air and Water Warlocks** (elemental spellcasters and kineticists) who manipulate oceanic winds, quell squalls, conjure blinding mists, and extinguish enemy fires during fleet engagements.
- **Colonial Arm**: It was the Royal Navy that seized the port of **[[Borlon]]** on [[Oen]] twenty years ago, establishing Timro's permanent military beachhead in the [[Floenry Isles]].

In accordance with Eraedal's **[[lore#The Tri-System Synthesis|Tri-System Synthesis]]**, the spiritual and metaphysical reality of Timro reflects three interwoven layers:


### The State Faith: The Order of Light
The dominant ecclesiastical power is the **[[Order of Light]]**, centered in the majestic Cathedral of Light in [[Old Timiro]]. Led by **Cardinal Palance Medoan**—a prelate whose political influence throughout the realm is rivaled only by the King himself—the Church commands massive landholdings, monasteries, and hospitals.
- **[[Sarenrae]]**: Revered as the Dawnflower, patron of the sun, healing, and chivalric redemption.
- **[[Iomedae]]**: Revered as the Inheritor, patron of valor, righteous justice, military honor, and the Sentinel cavalry.
- **[[Erastil]]**: Revered as Old Deadeye, patron of agrarian harvests, family, and rural communities.
- **The Whispered Schism**: Despite the Church's unyielding dogmatism, disturbing theological heresy has leaked inward from the Floenry colony of [[Enry]]. Following the proclamation of the mysterious arch-mage Noah a decade past, radical schismatics whisper that Erastil's ancient rites are secretly siphoning spiritual energy to awaken the **[[lore#The Tri-System Synthesis|Galchutt / Old Ones]]** slumbering beneath the earth. In Timro, voicing such ideas is classified as high treason and heresy, punishable by public execution.

### Other Revered Deities
- **[[Abadar]]**: The Master of the First Vault governs the merchant guilds, judicial contracts, gold coinage, and legal courts of Credia and Aracho.
- **[[Asmodeus]]**: Quietly venerated by hardline magistrate courts, slave auctioneers, and imperial jurists who enforce the letter of binding servitude contracts.
- **[[Pharasma]]**: Honored in the ancient ossuaries, catacombs, and crematoriums of Old Timiro and Credia to shepherd souls and ward against necromancy.
- **[[Torag]]**: Worshiped as the Father of Creation by the sovereign dwarves of [[Acoroc]].

### Primordial Horrors Beneath the Bedrock
While Timro's cities gleam with marble fountains, the ancient earth beneath them is riddled with pre-cataclysmic vaults:
- **The Star Plaza Pentangle**: Beneath the cobblestones of Credia's grand plaza lies an archaic runic circle binding five elder fiends and elemental demon lords (*Rebob*, *Grombeer*, *Aprek-Nar*, *Lemerhop*, *Wsitrab*).
- **The Curse of Manaqua (The Red Death)**: Historical annals record the horrific magical plague unleashed centuries ago by the summoner Manaqua, which drove victims into paranoid homicidal frenzies before consuming them with bloody sores. Quarantined ruins and plague vaults still sit sealed along the southern sewers of Credia.
- **The Slumbering Galchutt**: Vast subterranean tunnels stretching beneath the Old Kingdom mountains and southern plains connect to primordial vaults where remnants of the Old Ones dream in stone, occasionally leaking nightmare ichor and madness into the deep undercities.

## 🏙️ Major Cities & Strongholds

### 1. [[Credia]] (The Capital City)
The crown jewel and administrative heartbeat of the realm, home to **170,000 souls** (118,000 in Central Credia, 30,000 rural farmers, 10,000 soldiers of the Royal Garrison, and 12,000 enslaved laborers). Famous for its multi-tiered stone palaces, royal naval docks, aqueducts, sewage canals, public gladiatorial arenas, and the bustling Star Plaza.

### 2. [[Old Timiro]] (The Ancestral Holy City)
The ancient birthplace of the kingdom and the seat of its religious authority. Here stands the colossal Cathedral of Light, home to **Cardinal Palance Medoan** and the grand council of the Order of Light. The city is adorned with magnificent stone monuments, fountains, and royal tombs honoring founding monarchs such as Gregory the Just, Belgerphon, and Queen Steffay.

### 3. [[Aracho]] (The Great Southern Seaport)
A heavily fortified commercial metropolis on the southern coast, ruled by the King's cousin, the **Duke of Aracho**. As the primary maritime springboard to the [[Floenry Isles]] and the southern oceans, Aracho features vast dry docks, expansive flea markets, and the notorious Cadger and Larwhon slave markets where thousands of non-human captives are auctioned annually.

### 4. [[Acoroc]] (The Sovereign Dwarven Ironhold)
A unique independent dwarven sovereignty nestled within Timro's borders. Established two centuries ago by the historic **Treaty of King Acorok IV**, the underground **City of Iron** is home to over **24,000 dwarves** who govern themselves by their own laws, mining iron ore and forging the finest masterwork weapons and armor in the kingdom. Unknown to human authorities, Acoroc is connected via a secret 350-mile subterranean highway directly to the deep mountain realm of the kobolds.

### 5. Frontier Fortresses: [[Tanis]], [[Tomoro]], & [[Rankin]]
Heavily fortified bastions anchoring the kingdom's borders. Tanis stands vigilant against the monstrous warbands of the Old Kingdom mountains, while Rankin serves as a heavily walled trading city funneling raw timber and ore into the central markets.

## ⚓ Role in the *Prophecy and Balance* Campaign

For the heroes embarking upon the [[Prophecy and Balance]] campaign in the [[Floenry Isles]], the Timro Kingdom is an inescapable, multifaceted power:

1. **The Iron Fist in Borlon**:
   - Twenty years ago, the [[Timro Royal Navy]] seized the northern port city of **[[Borlon]]** on [[Oen]] to secure a strategic naval bastion. Governed by the severe **Constable [[Anya Volkov]]**, Borlon's shipyards repair Timro warships and enforce colonial taxes across the northern waters.
   - Timro spymaster **[[Ornric Broadman]]** and royal auditor **Captain [[Marcus Renault]]** operate from Borlon's lighthouse, directing covert operators (the Black Regiment) to investigate missing ironwood supplies and root out corruption.
2. **The Colonial Rebellion**:
   - Timro's centuries of ruthless non-human slavery have directly ignited the fury of Orc Warlord **[[Drogon Deathbringer]]** and his **[[Order of the Indomitable Shield]]**, who have seized [[Port Oen]] and are waging an all-out war of liberation across the island's plantations.
3. **The Holy War on Enry**:
   - Timro claims the island of **[[Enry]]** as a sovereign overseas parish founded by Cardinal Beliphont. As the religious schism between the Order of Light and the Anti-Erastil faction threatens to erupt into civil war—manipulated by the false-flag horrors of the Cult of the Devouring Void—the Timro Crown is under immense pressure to deploy its battlefleet to Miro Harbor.
4. **The Hunters of Dolan Stormcutter**:
   - As arcane hunters from the Northern Wilderness close in on **[[Dolan Stormcutter]]**, agents with forged Timro naval commissions or covert imperial warrants may attempt to infiltrate [[Vernazza]], forcing Dolan, [[Beau LeFer]], [[Don Keydic]], and [[Rooster Van Damage]] to navigate the razor's edge between imperial law, colonial rebellion, and cosmic destiny.
