## 🌊 Overview: The Crimson Deep

The **Sea of Scarlet Waters** is one of the most striking and dangerous bodies of water in all of [[Eraedal]]. Wedged into the south-central underbelly of the continent, this expansive maritime basin is cradled by the forbidding precipices of the **[[Scarlet Mountains]]** and the open grasslands of the **[[Tarldet Plains]]** to the north and west, while its southern shores lap against the scorched volcanic ash of **[[Mount Nimro]]** and the parched flats of the **[[Baalgor Wastelands]]**.

The sea takes its ominous name from its uncanny, rust-red coloration. When the morning sun crests the eastern peaks, the entire expanse gleams like fresh arterial blood. This crimson hue is born of a volatile synthesis: rich iron-oxide runoff pouring down from the mineral-heavy Scarlet Mountains, hyper-dense seasonal blooms of bioluminescent red algae, and hundreds of sub-aquatic thermal chimneys belching sulfur, steam, and volcanic minerals into the deep trenches from the magma chambers of Mount Nimro.

For the **[[Western Empire]]**, the Sea of Scarlet Waters is both an indispensable strategic highway and a treacherous frontier. Through its northern straits pass heavily escorted imperial convoys carrying iron, grain, and mined minerals between the industrial heartland and the eastern coastal ports like **[[Ptolus]]**. Yet its southern reaches are an active warzone, where imperial galleys clash with the colossal war catamarans of the **[[Mount Nimro|Kingdom of Giants]]** and dodge the boiling currents of volcanic upwellings.

The waters of the Scarlet Sea present unique nautical challenges:

1. **The Crimson Blooms**: During the hot summer months, microscopic flagellate organisms multiply into immense, shimmering slicks of red algae. While harmless to ship hulls, these blooms deplete the water's oxygen, suffocating ordinary fish and causing hundreds of silver-scaled sea serpents and reef sharks to surface in ravenous, agitated feeding frenzies.
2. **Sub-Aquatic Fumaroles & Boiling Slicks**: In the southern quadrants near Mount Nimro, the seabed is fractured by volcanic rifts. Entire square miles of the sea can suddenly begin to boil without warning, churning with superheated steam that strips paint from hulls, warps timber joints, and scalds unwary sailors who fall overboard.
3. **The Strait of Blood-Mist**: The northern neck of the sea, where it funnels past the southern ridges of the Tarldet Plains toward the approaches of the **[[Bay of Ptolus]]**. Dense banks of red-tinged marine fog hang perpetually over the water, cutting visibility to twenty paces and providing ideal cover for privateers and corsairs.
4. **The Iron Cliffs**: The western shore along the [[Scarlet Mountains]] is lined with sheer, blood-streaked rock faces rising five hundred feet out of the waves. Possessing no natural beaches or landing coves, ships crippled in storms are smashed to splinters against the jagged red stone.

## ⚔️ Naval Dominance & Imperial Cold War

### The Southern Imperial Flotilla
The Western Empire maintains a formidable naval squadron stationed at **The Anchorage of Cinders**—a heavily fortified naval base built upon an artificial breakwater of quarried basalt:
- **Iron-Banded War Galleys**: Unlike standard northern timber ketches, imperial warships operating in the Scarlet Waters are equipped with flame-retardant treated hull timbers, reinforced iron rams, and deck-mounted fire siphons capable of spraying volatile alchemical naphtha.
- **The Black Ship Sightings**: Rumors along the waterfront insist that the legendary, warded **Black Demon Ships** of the Western Empire occasionally anchor in deep, fog-draped coves of the Scarlet Sea, discharging cloaked arcanists on secret imperial commissions.

### The Giant Catamarans of Mount Nimro
In recent years, the rising power of **[[Mount Nimro|Sunder Blackrock's Kingdom of Giants]]** has extended directly onto the waves:
- Giant shipwrights, utilizing massive ironwood logs hauled from the northern fringes of the [[Yin-Sloth Jungles]], construct immense double-hulled catamarans sixty feet wide.
- Powered by banks of thirty-foot oars rowed by Jotan and Gigantes warriors, these colossal vessels mount giant boulder-catapults that can shatter an imperial galley in two salvos. They aggressively patrol the southern coastline, sinking any imperial slaver ship that approaches the giant kingdom's borders.

## 📜 The Tri-System Synthesis: Scarlet Waters Canon

### 1. The Physical Shell (Palladium Canon)
- Sourced from *Palladium Fantasy RPG Book 08: The Western Empire (2E)*, *Book 10: Mount Nimro*, and the *Main Rule Book (2E)* (pages 286, 288).
- Preserves the canonical position of the Sea of Scarlet Waters bordered by the Western Empire peninsula, the Scarlet Mountains, and the volcanic domain of Mount Nimro.

### 2. The Ancient Bones (Praemal / Ptolus Lore)
- **The Monument of Xy**: In the sacred *Tristine Chronicles*, Mount Nimrod is described as *"the burning mountain before the Scarlet Waters"* raised during the Age of Chaos to mark the passing of the Chaos Lords.
- Deep within the ocean abyss beneath the scarlet algae lies the **Sunken Caldera of Xy**—a pre-cataclysmic basalt rift where ancient entities bound during the imprisonment of the **Galchutt / Old Ones** slumber in cold magma. The psychic radiation of these elder aberrations leaks upward, causing the algae to mutate and occasionally granting telepathic madness to deep-sea divers.

### 3. The Active Pulse (Pathfinder 2e Remaster)
- **Aquatic Combat Engine**: Operates under PF2e Remaster aquatic rules (swim checks, underwater weapon restrictions, breathing hazards from boiling steam).
- **The Faiths**:
  - **[[Gozreh]]**: Prayed to as the Blood-Tide; sailors offer flagons of vintage wine to the waves to placate the red waters.
  - **[[Pharasma]]**: Worshipped at coastal beacons where the souls of sailors lost in the boiling vents are commended to the Lady of Graves.
  - **[[Asmoddeus]]**: Invoked by imperial admirals to bind marine press-gangs with unbreakable legal oaths.

## 🎯 Role in Regional Politics & Campaign Hooks

1. **The Conduit to Ptolus**: Merchant convoys departing the Western Empire's southern granaries use the Sea of Scarlet Waters to bypass the war-torn interior roads of the [[Three-way Civil War]], sailing north into the **[[Bay of Ptolus]]** to supply Holy Emperor Rehoboth's court.
2. **The Sunken Vault of Iron**: An imperial iron transport was sunk by giant catamarans near the Scarlet Mountains. Its cargo included three warded crates of masterwork dwarven armor destined for the City Watch in Ptolus.
3. **The Scalding Siphon**: Volcanic shifts under Mount Nimro have caused a major steam vent to open directly along the primary shipping channel, cutting off maritime trade. Imperial admirals offer a heavy bounty to any party of adventurers capable of diving into the vent to neutralize the elemental catalyst driving the eruption.
