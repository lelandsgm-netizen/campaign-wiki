## 🌊 Overview

The **Peninsula of Pendaltor** is the strategic, jagged landspit that forms the southernmost reach of the **[[Land of the South Winds]]**. Thrusting nearly one hundred miles out into the **Strait of Relar**, this finger of limestone bluffs, tidal flats, and suffocating mangrove marshes sits directly opposite the northern islands of the **[[Floenry Isles]]** archipelago.

While on standard imperial nautical charts the peninsula appears as an inhospitable stretch of mosquito-ridden wetlands, in geopolitical reality it is one of the most commercially critical and lawless maritime choke-points on the face of [[Eraedal]]. Every merchant vessel sailing from the **[[Western Empire]]** bound for the **[[Timro Kingdom]]**, the **[[Eastern Territories]]**, or the southern jungle ports must traverse the narrow waters flanking Pendaltor’s shores.

Immense riches pass through this corridor: bullion chests, raw silver ingots, warded grimoires, fine silks, vintage spirits, and holds packed with enslaved non-humans. Consequently, the Peninsula of Pendaltor has evolved into the Megaverse's greatest crossroads of piracy, smuggling, and illicit maritime commerce.

## 🗺️ Geography & Hazards

Pendaltor’s terrain is a treacherous labyrinth of land and water that confounds foreign captains and deep-draft naval armadas:

```
                  [ MAINLAND SOUTH WINDS SAVANNAH ]
                                 |
              [ THE NECK: MARSHES OF DESPOND ]
          (Black-Water Bogs, Leech Beds, Corpse Mud)
                                 |
  [ WEST COAST: THE DEAD REEDS ] |   [ EAST COAST: SMUGGLER'S BAY ]
  - Shrr-Ta Raider Inlets        |   - Ramsbottom ("Deadeye Suli's")
  - Mangrove Labyrinth           |   - Deep-water anchorages
  - Hidden trimaran slipways     |   - Smuggler sea-caves
                                 |
                        [ SULANOK ]
                 (The Grand Fence Metropolis)
                                 |
                       [ CAPE DRAGON ]
              (Limestone Cliffs, Signal Spire,
                Devil's Gut Reef & Shipwrecks)
                                 |
                 [ THE STRAIT OF RELAR ]
             (100 Miles to the Floenry Chain)
```

1. **The Limestone Spine**: A low ridge of jagged, weathered karst limestone forms the central axis of the peninsula. Honeycombed with subterranean sea-caves and sinkholes, this ridge shields the eastern leeward bays from the furious seasonal monsoons blowing in from the southwest.
2. **The Dead Mangroves**: Stretching along the western shoreline, colossal mangrove forests with twenty-foot aerial root tangles create a trackless swamp maze. These brackish channels are utterly impassable for standard warships but provide ideal hideouts for shallow-draft **Trimarans** and **Twin Canoes**.
3. **The Devil's Gut Shoals**: Surrounding the southern tip at Cape Dragon, razor-sharp coral reefs and shifting sandbars lie just beneath the waterline. Unwary vessels caught in the fierce rip-tides are regularly torn open, their shattered hulks picked clean by local wreckers and scavengers.
4. **The Shrr-Ta Raiding Waters**: The western estuaries are the prowling grounds of the **Shrr-Ta Jungle Raiders**—a ferocious coalition of Tezcat warriors, jungle ogres, and mad swamp cultists who dart from the reeds in outrigger canoes to swarm stranded merchantmen.

## ⚓ Key Settlements & Strongholds

### 1. [[Sulanok]] (The Grand Fence Port)
Perched on a natural sheltered harbor near the tip of the peninsula, [[Sulanok]] is the crown jewel of southern black-market trade. While officially flying the yellow-and-red dragon banner of the South Winds Crown, Sulanok is operated by a consortium of corrupt admirals, merchant factors, and corsair brokers. Here, stolen cargoes from the Strait of Relar are unloaded, relabeled with falsified imperial manifests, and sold openly in bustling bazaars.

### 2. Ramsbottom & "Deadeye Suli's"
Located fifteen miles north of Sulanok along a sheltered, rocky cove, **Ramsbottom** is a ramshackle coastal village of four hundred fishermen, pearl divers, and cutthroats. It is internationally notorious for a single establishment: **Deadeye Suli’s Inn**.
- **The Recruitment Den**: Deadeye Suli’s is the premier recruitment station for the cutthroat **Pirate Federation** and independent corsair crews. Fugitive sailors, disgraced mercenaries, black-market alchemists, and renegade warlocks come here seeking billets aboard pirate vessels.
- **The Atmosphere**: The inn is built from the ribs of scuttled galleys, reeking of sour mash rum and pipe-weed. Duels and tavern brawls are constant, but anyone who draws steel against Deadeye Suli himself—a retired 8th-level cyclops harpooner with an enchanted glass eye—finds themselves nailed to the wharf pilings.

### 3. Cape Dragon Spire
Crowning the towering white limestone bluffs at the very point of the peninsula, Cape Dragon Spire is a fortified royal bastion. Built from cyclopean basalt blocks quarried during the kingdom's founding two millennia ago, the fortress features a blazing alchemical lighthouse visible for thirty miles across the Strait of Relar. A garrison of sixty royal marines and three heavy, enchanted ballistae mount watch over the channel—primarily to signal allied privateers when rich merchant convoys are approaching from the west.

## 📜 The Tri-System Synthesis: Regional Lore

### 1. The Physical Shell (Palladium Canon)
- Direct adaptation of the **Peninsula of Pendaltor** as detailed in *Palladium Fantasy RPG Book III: Adventures on the High Seas* (pages 170–172).
- Primary home base of the **multi-hulled** shallow-draft fleets: outrigger Trimarans and Twin Canoes that define South Winds maritime life.

### 2. The Ancient Bones (Praemal / Ptolus Lore)
- The karst caves beneath Pendaltor predate mortal memory, carved out during the primordial cataclysms when the **Galchutt / Old Ones** were bound within the subterranean depths.
- In several deep caverns accessible only at low tide, ancient murals carved in weeping green stone depict multi-tentacled cosmic horrors rising from the ocean trench. Sailors who camp in these sea-caves report waking nightmares, bleeding ears, and spontaneous hallucinations.

### 3. The Active Pulse (Pathfinder 2e Remaster)
- **Law vs. Anarchy**: Pendaltor exists in a fragile equilibrium between the cold, tyrannical order of **[[Asmoddeus]]** (upheld by the royal garrison and slave masters) and the deceitful criminal network of **[[Norgorber]]** (worshipped by Sulanok’s fence syndicates).
- **Encounter Hazards**:
  - *Mire Drake* (Creature 7) stalking coastal tidal flats.
  - *Sulanok Wreckers* (Creature 2-3 rogues utilizing false signal lanterns).
  - *Galchutt Chaos-Ichor Ooze* (Creature 5 aberration lurking in deep sea-caverns).

## 🎯 Strategic Role in "Prophecy and Balance"

- **Logistical Springboard**: Pendaltor is the primary assembly staging ground for the South Winds royal expedition force preparing to strike south across the strait to attempt the reconquest of **[[Oen]]**.
- **The Intelligence Gateway**: Informants, smugglers, and shipwrights in Sulanok and Ramsbottom possess up-to-the-minute intelligence regarding naval movements in the Floenry chain, including the movements of the [[Timro Royal Navy]] out of [[Borlon]] and the insurgent defensive lines held by [[Drogon Deathbringer]].
- **Privateer Targets**: Crews operating from Pendaltor regularly clash with **[[Captain Lebrac]]**, whose anti-slavery raiders intercept South Winds slave transports just outside Pendaltor's coastal battery range.
