## Overview

Enry Island, with an estimated population of 38,000 (80% Human, 12% Other, 8% Orc slaves), is the largest island in its chain and an unofficial territory of the Timiro Kingdom. Historically, it was a peaceful and prosperous land, founded by Cardinal Beliphont, and almost entirely devout to the Order of Light (Iomedae, Seranae, and Erastil). However, a decade ago, an extraordinary encounter transformed it into a hotbed of civil unrest and a major political embarrassment for Timiro, making an outright, bloody civil war seem inevitable. The island community is now segregated, with the Order of Light unofficially ruling, controlling the main port, imports/exports, militia, government, immigration, and the keepers of the one true church.

## Geography

The island features a diverse landscape, with the coast and interior dotted by fishing and farming villages, primarily cultivating sugar cane for molasses and rum, and rice. The east is entirely jungle, though less dangerous than neighboring islands due to the elimination of most predators like the Grimbor. In the center of this jungle lies an ancient, pyramid-like ruin, possibly Grimbor in origin, adopted by the Devouring Void cult. This stone pyramid is situated at the nexus junction of three ley lines. The main port, Miro, located on the coast, serves as the safest point for visitors.

## Governance

The chief ruling body of Enry Island is affiliated with the Order of Light. The Order exerts significant control over the island's affairs, acting as unofficial rulers. Speaking against or disrespecting the church, its priests, or its deities (Iomedae, Seranae, and especially Erastil) is considered blasphemy, punishable by fines, public ridicule, or imprisonment. Openly claiming Erastil is a Galchutt or that the church serves a Galchutt is the ultimate heresy, leading to severe consequences including riots, imprisonment, torture, and even public execution. Conversely, those who punish public heresy are celebrated as heroes of the Order of Light, fostering anarchy and discontent.

## Points of Interest

- **Miro (Main Port)**
  - A small, no-frills harbor town serving as a crucial rest and supply stop for merchant ships from the west. It features numerous docks, piers, warehouses, a half-dozen inns, taverns, and brothels. Shops deal in common tools, utensils, clothes, food, and drink. Basic weapons and poor-quality armor can be purchased, but magic items are unavailable. Healing and mystic services are exclusively provided by the Order of Light, marking those who use them as aligned with "Erastil the Galchutt" by the opposing factions.

- **Ancient Pyramid-like Structure**
  - An ancient ruin deep within the jungle, believed to be a former Grimbor site situated on a nexus of three ley lines. Use today is unknown if any.

## Notes

### The Catalyst: Noah's Proclamation

A decade ago, a mage named Noah, claiming to seek retreat after battling Galchutts in the Old Kingdom, arrived without warning. His tales of epic adventures, particularly one where he claimed Erastil was a Galchutt, infuriated a priest of the Order of Light. This sparked a feud that escalated into a magical battle involving over a dozen priests and Noah's companions. Noah and his group triumphed, after which he declared Erastil a Galchutt and warned islanders that worship would awaken ancient evils, thereby condemning them as "pathetic pawns." Noah vanished into the jungle, never to be seen again. His true identity remains a mystery, with some believing him a demon, others an angel, but his actions irrevocably shattered Enry's serenity.

### Factions and Conflict

The island is now tragically divided into two primary factions:

- **The Order of Light (60% of population)**
  - Remains the majority, still devoutly worshipping Iomedae, Seranae, and Erastil. They hold unofficial control over the island's infrastructure and maintain strict religious laws, persecuting those who defy their beliefs, especially regarding Erastil.

- **The Anti-Erastil Cult (40% of population)**
  - Believes Erastil is a Galchutt and that his continued worship will lead to the world's end. Forsaking the Pantheon of Order, they now worship elemental forces and jungle gods. Persecuted by the Order of Light, they've formed secret societies and meeting places. Their initial peaceful movement has devolved into frustration and violence, with an obsession to eradicate Erastil's followers. Temples, shrines, and worshippers of Erastil are constant targets of vandalism, theft, fires, murder, kidnapping, magic attacks, riots, and armed assaults. Many members, though not inherently evil, view their violent actions as self-preservation and enlightenment.

### Traveling on Enry Island

Enry Island is highly dangerous for visitors regardless of their alignment. Expressing strong sentiments or even casual comments can provoke hostile reactions from either faction. The Cult of Devouring Void also exploits unwitting visitors, using them as pawns to stir trouble or framing them for their atrocities. The main port of Miro offers the safest haven for travelers, but venturing into the interior or expressing opinions can quickly lead to deadly confrontations.
