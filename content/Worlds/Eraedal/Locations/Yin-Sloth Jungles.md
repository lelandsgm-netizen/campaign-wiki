## 🌿 Overview: The Primeval Cradle

The **Yin-Sloth Jungles** represent the vastest, most untamed expanse on the continent of [[Eraedal]], blanketing much of the southern hemisphere in an unbroken sea of colossal green canopies, winding river deltas, and suffocating steam baths. For northern mortals in the **[[Western Empire]]** and **[[Timro|Timro Kingdom]]**, the Yin-Sloth is synonymous with nightmare: a fever-haunted wilderness of venomous predators, archaic dragon religions, cannibal headhunters, and prehistoric monstrosities.

Yet beneath this reputation as an uninhabitable green hell lies a land of astonishing antiquity, immense geopolitical complexity, and staggering wealth. The Yin-Sloth is the legendary **cradle of mortal origin**—where humankind first rose and fell nearly ninety millennia ago during the mythic **Battle of Gods**, tens of thousands of years before the rise of elves or dwarves.

In the contemporary era, the northern fringes of the jungle have become the front line of civilization. South of the towering **[[Dragon's Gate|Dragon's Gate Mountains]]**, two powerful rival nations have carved permanent domains out of the wilderness: the human-dominated **Commonwealth of Yin-Sloth Kingdoms**, and the surprisingly disciplined, powerful **Orcish Empire** established by the legendary Ogre Emperor Doragon.

The Yin-Sloth is partitioned into several distinct ecological and geopolitical zones:

1. **The Northern Freelands**: Spanning the territory between the southern borders of the Western Empire and the [[Dragon's Gate|Dragon's Gate Mountains]]. This is virgin, unconquered rainforest inhabited by primitive nomadic clans, reclusive beastfolk, and runaway slaves who fiercely defend their freedom against imperial slave safaris.
2. **The [[Dragon's Gate|Dragon's Gate Mountains]]**: A dramatic mountain chain cutting diagonally across the northeastern jungle. Renowned as the home of sovereign dragons dwelling in mountaintop cities like **Dragonedara**, and the allied Kobold iron-hold of **Kadera**.
3. **The Conquered Lands & The Commonwealth**: Situated south of Dragon's Gate. Over the past three centuries, human settlers from Timro and the Old Kingdom have clear-cut river valleys to establish the **Commonwealth of Yin-Sloth Kingdoms**, centered around the prosperous agricultural metropolis of **Mishala** and the fortified mountain-hub of **Terellia**.
4. **The Orcish Empire**: Established in the fertile eastern river plains by Ogre Chieftain **Doragon Ak Doragon**. Doragon organized barbarian orcs, ogres, and kobolds into a civilized, mercantile power. Their magnificent coastal capital, **Caer Honogath Ak Doragon**, is built upon cyclopean pre-cataclysmic ruins and rivals human seaports in size and commerce.
5. **The Great Fire Bog**: A volcanic swamp in the interior where peat bogs burn with eternal, subterranean sulfur flames, home to Fire Sprites, lava salamanders, and bizarre mutated insectoids.

## 🏛️ Cultures, Inhabitants & The Ancient Battle

### The Tragic Plight of the Grimbor
The gentle, ape-like giants known as the **[[Grimbor]]** are native to the Yin-Sloth and the nearby **[[Floenry Isles]]**. Scholars consider them the tragic descendants of Eraedal's earliest pre-cataclysmic human civilization, cursed and de-evolved during the ancient Battle of Gods. Over the past century, brutal slave expeditions from the **[[Western Empire]]** and the **[[Land of the South Winds]]** have slaughtered or captured over five million Grimbor for gladiatorial slaughter and plantation labor, pushing this noble race toward extinction.

### The Tezcat (Jaguar-Folk)
The primeval interior belongs to the **Tezcat**—a fierce, proud ancestry of feline hunters standing seven feet tall, with spotted golden coats and predatory grace. Wielding glass-sharp obsidian macuahuitls and armor of hardened alligator hide, Tezcat tribes revere primal nature and jaguar spirits, ruthlessly repelling foreign loggers, rubber tappers, and imperial expeditions.

### The Faith of Dragonwright
Unlike the northern realms where human pantheons dominate, religious life across the Yin-Sloth is heavily shaped by **Dragonwright**—the ancient religion honoring the dragon-gods:
- **Kym-nark-myr**: The golden dragon of light, justice, and nobility.
- **Styphon the Black**: The ruthless dragon of darkness, vengeance, and power.
- **Zandragal & Ketanna**: The dragon lords of elemental fury and earth-magic.
- Temples of Dragonwright in cities like Mishala and Dragonedara feature soaring stone pillars, draconic glyphs, and priest-scholars who maintain direct communion with elder dragons.

## 📜 The Tri-System Synthesis: Eraedal Canon

### 1. The Physical Shell (Palladium Canon)
- Sourced directly from *Palladium Fantasy RPG Book 07: Yin-Sloth Jungles (2E)* and *Book 01: Dragons & Gods (2E)*.
- Faithfully preserves the geopolitical tension between the human Commonwealth (Mishala, Terellia) and Emperor Doragon's Orcish Empire, the geography of Dragon's Gate, and unique fauna (Krel, Hytrils, Dogres, Fire Sprites).

### 2. The Ancient Bones (Praemal / Ptolus Lore)
- **The Battle of Gods (90,000 Years Ago)**: Long before elves, dwarves, or the Western Empire existed, the earliest mortal civilization thrived here. When the primordial **Galchutt / Old Ones** whispered corruption to Styphon the Black, rival pantheons clashed in a cosmic civil war that scarred the jungle forever, releasing reality-warping chaos.
- **The Slumbering Horrors**: Deep beneath the Great Bog lies **Kirgi's Maw**, a bottomless subterranean abyss that descends directly into the sealed prisons of the Galchutt. The psychic rot leaking upward spawns mindless Corpse Creatures, weeping idols, and chaos-cults that perform human sacrifice under the jungle canopy.

### 3. The Active Pulse (Pathfinder 2e Remaster)
- **Rules Mechanics**: PF2e Remaster rules govern jungle exploration (disease, trackless difficult terrain, canopy heights, and swampland maneuvers).
- **The Divine Pantheon**:
  - **Dragonwright Gods**: Handled under PF2e divine domains (Wyrmkin, Destruction, Protection, Zeal).
  - **[[Gozreh]]**: Prayed to by river navigators and coastal outrigger crews.
  - **[[Lamashtu]]**: Revered in the darkest interior by mutated goblin tribes and degenerate ogre clans.
  - **[[Pharasma]]**: Honored by monks of the *Commune of Misery*, who train elite Undead-Hunters to purge primordial wraiths from ancient jungle tombs.

## 🎯 Strategic Role & Campaign Hooks

1. **The Rubber Trade Routes**: The world's natural rubber flows north along the jungle riverways toward the [[Land of the South Winds]] and the [[Western Empire]]. Expeditions seeking to bypass Sulanok's high tariffs attempt daring river runs into the interior.
2. **The Orcish Diplomacy**: Emperor Doragon’s ambassadors seek quiet trade pacts with independent ports like [[Vernazza]] in the [[Floenry Isles]], seeking naval timber and iron in exchange for gold minted in Kadera.
3. **The Grimbor Rescue**: PCs allied with the **[[Free Slave Coalition]]** or **[[Captain Lebrac]]** discover a slave convoy carrying captured Grimbor from the Yin-Sloth border toward the arenas of Caer Itom, staging a desperate jungle ambush.
4. **The Ruins of the First Men**: Deep in the southern interior, delvers locate an untouched obsidian ziggurat from the primordial civilization that preceded the Battle of Gods, containing pre-cataclysmic relics that both the Inverted Pyramid and the Church of Light desperately seek.
