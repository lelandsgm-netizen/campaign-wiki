## Island Overview

**Oen Island** stands as a sprawling tropical territory defining the southwestern quadrant of the Floenry chain. Home to an estimated total population of 43,000 residents, the island’s geography is characterized by wide stretches of tall grass, thin jungles with sparse natural predators, and massive sugar cane plantations or fishing villages scattered across its coastlines.

The climate is profoundly warm, holding a coastal average of 90°F that is cooled by sweeping oceanic winds. Stepping away from the shoreline into the interior rain forests, however, drops operators into a suffocating steam bath of trapped humidity fluctuating between 95°F and 100°F. While the west coast is rimmed by sandy beaches allowing easy access for small tactical vessels, the surrounding waters require larger warships to navigate carefully to avoid running aground in shallow channels.

Historically managed as a highly successful plantation province by the [[Land of the South Winds]], Oen has fractured into an all-out civil war. The native populations have rallied under Orc Warlord **[[Drogon Deathbringer]]** and his martial faction, the **[[Order of the Indomitable Shield]]**. Staging from the historical capital, they have systematically overwhelmed foreign garrisons, forcing a chaotic, multi-front military deadlock across the island's unique coastal settlements.

## Major Inhabited Hubs & Staging Points

Operations on Oen are entirely shaped by its vertical, cliffside topography and heavily contested maritime networks. The island's primary settlements are split between entrenched colonial footholds and fiercely independent coastal strongholds.

### 1. [[Manarola]] (The Black Regiment Base of Operations)

* **Status:** Independent / Unofficial Black Regiment Safehouse
* **Topography:** High Vertical Vibe
* **Defensive Force:** Small Constabulary Guard (6), Light Local Militia (<24)
* **Summary:** Built directly into the sheer, jagged cliffs of the southern coast, Manarola boasts a breathtaking, intensely vertical architecture where colorful stone houses seem stacked directly on top of one another above a churning sea. This independent cliffside town serves as the primary covert staging ground and insertion base for your elite Black Regiment squad. Because the town is politically unaligned, it maintains an incredibly small civic guard and a minimal domestic militia. Its precipitous paths and hidden alleyways provide perfect vertical defilade for keeping a deniable special forces unit hidden from both Timro high command and insurgent scouts.

### 2. [[Port Oen]] (The Insurgent Capital)

* **Status:** Rebel Stronghold / Order Headquarters 
* **Defensive Force:** Fanatical Indigenous Army ([[Order of the Indomitable Shield]]) 
* **Summary:** Historically the most civilized, built-up city on the island where royal administrators and tax collectors managed the archipelago's massive sugar cane warehouses and central shipping docks. Port Oen has been entirely seized by [[Drogon Deathbringer]]'s forces. The former Royal Army outpost has been utterly crushed, and the city’s docks now serve as the primary naval staging ground for the rebellion. The local crafters and blacksmiths have been integrated into the insurgent war machine, forging armor and martial weapons to supply the front lines.



### 3. [[Borlon]] (The Timro Juggernaut)

* **Status:** Secure [[Timro]] Sovereign Port
* **Defensive Force:** Heavy [[Timro]] Royal Garrison & Colonial Fleet Artillery
* **Summary:** Located on the bustling northern coast, Borlon is a sprawling, heavily fortified colonial port city flying the proud banner of the [[Timro]] Kingdom. Serving as the primary gateway for corporate and military imports arriving from the mainland, [[Borlon]] is an economic juggernaut filled with deep-water berths, sprawling trade markets, and strict military checkpoints. It represents the largest concentration of [[Timro]]'s official wealth and colonial soldiers on the island—and stands as the primary target for the Order's impending northern offensive.

### 4. [[Vernazza]] (The Defiant Shield)

* **Status:** Independent Sovereignty
* **Defensive Force:** Standing Professional Army (~100), Local Coastal Navy
* **Summary:** Mirroring the dramatic coastal beauty of the south, [[Vernazza]] is a rugged, proudly independent maritime town that has successfully resisted falling into the grasp of [[Timro]] corporate interests or South Winds expansion. Nestled against defensive coastal cliffs, Vernazza protects its sovereignty with a highly disciplined standing army of nearly one hundred elite soldiers and a small, highly aggressive localized navy. They maintain a fragile neutrality, viewing both the greedy foreign colonists and the fanatical insurgents with extreme suspicion.

### 5. [[La Spezia]] (The Aligned Haven)

* **Status:** Independent Municipal Sovereignty / Politically Aligned to [[Timro]]
* **Defensive Force:** Local Municipal Guard backed by [[Timro]] Naval Patrols
* **Summary:** A larger independent coastal port town that has chosen a path of strategic diplomacy. Recognizing the overwhelming military might of the [[Timro]] Kingdom, [[La Spezia]]'s leadership maintains its official independence while aligning its trade, political treaties, and harbor access firmly with Timro command. It functions as a vital logistical rest-stop and naval coordination hub, enjoying [[Timro]] protection from pirate fleets while attempting to insulate its civilian population from the escalating land war tearing through the interior plantations.
