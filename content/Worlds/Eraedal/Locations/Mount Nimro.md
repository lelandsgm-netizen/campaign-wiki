## 🌋 Overview: The Mountains of Fire

Rising like blackened fangs above the cracked earth of south-central [[Eraedal]], **Mount Nimro** and its ancient sister peak, **Mount Nimrod**, form the catastrophic volcanic heart of the **Land of the Twin Furies**. From more than one hundred leagues away across the [[Baalgor Wastelands]] or the [[Old Kingdom]], travelers can see colossal plumes of dark ash and sulfur billowing into an ash-choked sky, painting the sun in shades of rust and bruised violet.

For millennia, human cartographers dismissed this hellish expanse of lava fields, basalt ridges, and toxic fumaroles as an unlivable nightmare. Yet out of the smoke and molten rock has arisen one of the most formidable and terrifying powers of the modern era: **The Kingdom of Giants** (also known as the *Federation of Giant-Kind*).

United under the ruthless, visionary intellect of Fire Giant Warlord **Sunder Blackrock**, diverse giant clans—long fractured into bickering, nomadic brutes—have forged a centralized military empire. Carving subterranean fortress-cities directly into living magma conduits, mining mountains of black iron, and fielding legions of twenty-foot armored warriors, the Kingdom of Giants threatens to upend the fragile balance of power between the **[[Western Empire]]** and the southern human kingdoms.

The terrain surrounding the volcanoes is a brutal gauntlet of tectonic upheaval:

1. **Mount Nimrod (The Elder Spire)**: The older of the two peaks, Nimrod is a dormant giant whose blackened crater has vented continuous, steady smoke and ash since the dawn of time. Ancient legends declare it was raised during the Age of Chaos as a permanent rift to the Elemental Plane of Fire.
2. **Mount Nimro (The Red Fury)**: A violently active super-volcano standing over 14,000 feet high. Rivers of red-hot magma cascade down its tiered basalt slopes, cooling into ever-expanding shelves of black obsidian. Its earth-shattering tremors can be felt across the Baalgor basin.
3. **The Ash Flats**: Downwind of the volcanoes stretch vast plains smothered under feet of grey volcanic ash. Toxic sulfuric gases pool in low ravines, requiring travelers to wear filtered respirators or carry water-soaked scarves.
4. **The Great Canyon Highway**: A colossal roadway blasted and chiseled through solid mountain granite by giant sappers. Towering vertical canyon walls two hundred feet high ensure that any invading army must march through an inescapable deathtrap monitored by giant boulder-catapults and fire ballistae.

## 👑 The Kingdom of Giants: Society & Dominion

### Warlord Sunder Blackrock
Unlike ordinary fire giants who succumb to mindless savagery, **Sunder Blackrock** possesses the tactical brilliance of an imperial general and the arcane vision of an archmage. Recognizing that the mortal human realms systematically hunt, enslave, and exterminate giantkind, Sunder launched a decade-long crusade across the Baalgor Wastelands and Old Kingdom to unite the disparate giant races under a single imperial banner:
- **The Council of Low-Lords**: Sunder rules alongside a council representing the major giant bloodlines: **Jotan** (mighty stone and frost masters), **Gigantes** (ferocious 20-foot cyclopean siege troops), **Algor** (frost giant wanderers exiled from the north), and allied clans of **Trolls**, **Ogres**, and **Dogres**.
- **The Forge City of Nimeret**: Built directly into the hollowed, magma-warmed interior of Mount Nimro, Nimeret is an architectural marvel. Enormous stone corridors fifty feet wide accommodate titan-sized citizens. Massive geothermal bellows power dwarven-style blast furnaces where giant armorers forge full plate armor for twenty-foot warriors and cast iron-spiked siege boulders.

### The Endless Blood-Feud: The Gromek War
The greatest domestic conflict consuming the Kingdom of Giants is their total war against the **[[Gromek]]**:
- **The Sky-Terror**: The Gromek are an indigenous race of winged, gargoyle-like reptilians who have roosted in the high mountain crags of Mount Nimro since antiquity. Possessing leathery wings, razor talons, and stony hide, the Gromek launch diving ambushes against giant patrols, dropping heavy stone boulders from high altitude.
- **No Quarter**: Neither side offers mercy. Giants mount heavy repeating scorpion ballistae upon their citadel ramparts to shoot Gromek from the sky, while Gromek hunting swarms slaughter isolated giant drovers and desecrate their mountain shrines.

## 📜 The Tri-System Synthesis: Eraedal Canon

### 1. The Physical Shell (Palladium Canon)
- Sourced directly from *Palladium Fantasy RPG Book 10: Mount Nimro, Kingdom of Giants (2E)* and the *Main Rule Book (2E)* (page 288).
- Preserves the geography of the Twin Furies overlooking the Sea of Scarlet Waters, the specific giant races (Nimro, Jotan, Gigantes, Algor, Trolls), and the brutal guerrilla war against the Gromek.

### 2. The Ancient Bones (Praemal / Ptolus Lore)
- **The Monument to Chaos**: In the *Tristine Chronicles*, Mount Nimrod is recorded as the monument raised to mark the passing of the Chaos Lords during the primordial cataclysm.
- When the **Galchutt / Old Ones** were driven into the deep mantle, the cosmic impact cracked the tectonic plates, unleashing the eternal fires of Mount Nimrod. Deep beneath Nimeret's deepest magma shafts lie cyclopean seals binding fire-tainted chaotic horrors that whisper madness to the giant shamans who delve too near the core.

### 3. The Active Pulse (Pathfinder 2e Remaster)
- **The Divine Flame & Ruin**:
  - **[[Rovagug]]**: Revering the Rough Beast as the primordial force slumbering within volcanic earth, giant berserkers paint their iron armor with gaping maw iconography.
  - **[[Lamashtu]]**: Veneration of the Mother of Monsters is common among ogre and troll auxiliaries who pray for monstrous progeny.
  - **Primal Fire Animism**: Giant shamans channel high-level primal spells, commanding fire elementals and lava surges.
- **PF2e Mechanics**: Built around Pathfinder 2e Remaster size mechanics (Large and Huge creatures), reach advantages, and forced movement.

## 🎯 Strategic Role & Campaign Hooks

1. **The Imperial Threat**: In Caer Itom, the Imperial Senate monitors Mount Nimro with mounting dread. If Sunder Blackrock completes the consolidation of the giant clans and marches north across the Old Kingdom or west through the Baalgor Wastelands, no single human army could withstand their charge.
2. **The Black Iron Trade**: Smugglers from the [[Land of the South Winds]] and corrupt factors from [[Timro]] brave the fringes of Mount Nimro to trade grain, wine, and steel tools in exchange for high-grade obsidian and enchanted volcanic ore.
3. **The Gromek Alliance Hook**: Adventurers venturing into the mountains may be approached by desperate Gromek aerie-matrons seeking outside mercenaries to infiltrate Nimeret’s foundry vaults and assassinate Sunder’s siege engineers.
4. **The Old One's Vent**: Arcane scholars believe that deep within Mount Nimrod’s crater lies the *Furnace of Xy*—a primordial artifact capable of forging or unmaking god-forged weapons, guarded by magma elementals and bound chaos fiends.
