## 📍 Overview

*The Rallian Province (officially known in antiquity as **Decurance Province**) has been transformed from a sun-drenched sanctuary of luxury into a brutal, highly contested war zone. Once celebrated as the premiere inland engine for the empire's finest champagnes, liquors, and automated fruit greenhouses, the province has been violently fractured by the explosive advance of the **[[Free Slave Coalition]]**.

In a desperate bid to prevent the rebellion from completely enveloping the northern coast, the heavily fortified military might of [[Upper Kighfalton]]—championed by **[[House Paaslaan]]** and directly bankrolled by **[[House Ne'Klosh]]**—has drawn a line of blood across this territory. The sprawling vineyards and private distilleries that once populated the rolling hills are now scarred by deep trench-works, burning orchards, and fortified staging camps. Rallian stands as a pivotal bottleneck on the grand map; if the Coalition breaks the iron defense of the Upper Kighfalton legions here, the gateway to the northern seacoast will be blown wide open.*

## 🗺️ Points of Interest

### 1. [[Distiller's Row]] (The Rebel Supply Nexus)

- **Immersive Description:** A massive, industrial district of stone-walled breweries, sprawling processing distilleries, and copper vat houses that dominates a major sector of the capital city.

- **Current Functional Utility:** Formerly the tightly regulated production hub for the empire's global alcohol trade, this sector has been commandeered as a **Logistics Engine and Rations Foundry** for the Free Slave Coalition. The powerful winery and distillery guilds have aligned completely with the liberators, repurposing their mass-production apparatuses to churn out high-calorie field rations, medicinal grain alcohol, and combustible fluids to supply the Coalition's front-line war machine.

### 2. The [[Iron Vines]] (The Battlefront Borderlands)

- **Immersive Description:** A vast, rolling countryside consisting of thousands of acres of premier grape vineyards, punctuated by scorched earth and makeshift wooden palisades.

- **Current Functional Utility:** This territory now serves as a **No-Man's-Land and Guerrilla War Zone**. [[House Paaslaan]]’s heavy shock troops and auxiliary mercenaries wage a grinding war of attrition through the valleys, actively burning crops and dynamic supply lines to starve out the rebel garrisons, while Coalition skirmishers use the dense, unharvested canopies to launch deadly nocturnal ambushes.

### 3. The [[Glass Spires]] (The City Greenhouses)

- **Immersive Description:** A striking network of monumental glass greenhouses and domestic agricultural domes rising above the city's wide open streets and plazas.

- **Current Functional Utility:** Once the proud playthings of wealthy "city farmers" cultivating exotic fruits, these climate-controlled structures have been locked down as **Strategic Bio-Agricultural Zones**. The occupying Coalition forces utilize the elementally fortified micro-climates within the spires to rapidly grow medicinal herbs, alchemical reagents, and emergency food supplies to withstand the impending siege from the north.

### 4. [[Fort Paaslaan]] Staging Grounds (The Imperial Line)

- **Immersive Description:** A sprawling, hastily erected fortress network of deep-stone earthworks, timber barricades, and heavy ballista emplacements cutting directly across the northern provincial border.

- **Current Functional Utility:** This heavily militarized choke point is utilized as the **Primary Vanguard Command Post** for House Paaslaan and the forces of Upper Kighfalton. From this iron bastion, imperial commanders orchestrate defensive lines and launch counter-offensives into Rallian, utilizing heavy warlock-reinforced fortifications to keep the Coalition forces bottlenecks within the southern valleys.
