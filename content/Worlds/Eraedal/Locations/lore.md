> [!info] The Tri-System Synthesis
> **Eraedal** is a synthesized reality woven from three legendary traditions:
> - **The Physical Shell**: Geography and factions drawn from the **Palladium Fantasy World** (preserved in `Library/`).
> - **The Ancient Bones**: Primordial history and metaphysics drawn from Monte Cook’s **Praemal / Ptolus**.
> - **The Active Pulse**: Mechanics, character options, and divine pantheons drawn from **Pathfinder 2e Remaster**.

---

# The Living Lore of Eraedal

To walk the roads of [[Eraedal]] is to witness mortal kingdoms thriving upon the sealed roof of a cosmic tomb. Behind every imperial edict, trade caravan, and delve into subterranean vaults lies the confluence of three distinct forces that govern the cosmos.

---

## 1. The Physical Shell: Palladium Geography & Factions

The visible world—its continents, mountains, rivers, kingdoms, and sovereign powers—derives directly from the world maps and regional sourcebooks of **Palladium Fantasy** (found within `Library/`).

### The Great Realms
- **The Western Empire (`Library/Palladium - Book 08 - The Western Empire`):** The beating, decadent heart of mortal power. Ruled by ancient aristocratic dynasties and Emperor-appointed prefects, it spans regions such as **[[West Kighfalton]]**, the monumental metropolis of **[[Caer Itom]]**, and rival noble houses (**Itomas, Paaslaan, Milaszc**). Paved Romanesque roadways, competitive colosseums, strict caste distinctions, and sprawling sewer catacombs define its civilized core.
- **The Coast & Oceanside:** Along the eastern edge of the Western Empire lies the **[[Inland Sea]]** and the **[[Bay of Ptolus]]**, where the frontier port of **[[Ptolus]]** nestles below the towering cliffs of the **[[Spire]]**.
- **The Old Kingdom & Frontier (`Library/Palladium - Book 02 - Old Ones`):** The ruined birthplace of human and elven empires, now an overgrown wilderness littered with ancient tombs, monoliths, and wandering monstrosities.
- **The Northern Wilderness & The Wolfen Empire (`Library/Palladium - Book 20 - Wolfen Empire`, `Books 04 & 05`):** A cold expanse where the disciplined, honorable Wolfen tribes forge an emerging empire that challenges human supremacy.
- **The Baalgor Wastelands & Mount Nimro (`Library/PFRPG_09_The_Baalgor_Wastelands`, `Book 10`):** Harsh volcanic plains and mountain strongholds dominated by giant-clans, nomadic tribes, and scorched ruins.
- **[[Timro|The Timro Kingdom]] & Eastern Territories (`Library/Palladium - Book 02 - Old Ones`, `Book 11`):** The second-oldest human realm, renowned for chivalric knights, imperial navies, and the sovereign dwarven freehold of [[Acoroc]], but shadowed by massive chattel slavery and the ancient fiends sealed beneath its capital [[Credia]].
- **The Land of the Damned (`Library/Palladium - Book 20 - Land Of The Damned`):** An isolated, frozen nightmare realm surrounded by mountains of ice and madness, quarantined from the rest of mortal civilization.

## 2. The Ancient Bones: Praemal Metaphysics & Ancient History

While mortal eyes see the borders of the Western Empire, the **deep metaphysical structure and ancient history** belong to Monte Cook’s **Praemal** (the world of *Ptolus: City by the Spire*), preserved within `Library/Ptolus/`.

> [!important] Mechanics Exemption
> The volumes in `Library/Ptolus/` (*City by The Spire.pdf*, *Players-Guide-To-Ptolus.pdf*, *MP005_Banewarrens.pdf*, *NOD.pdf*, *Ptolus_Adventures.pdf*) originate from legacy d20/D&D 3.5e systems. **Their game mechanics are strictly disregarded.** They serve as our authoritative canon for lore, history, factions, district cartography, NPCs, and subterranean hazards—all translated into **Pathfinder 2e Remaster** rules when implemented.

### The Galchutt & The Old Ones Syncretized
In Eraedal's primordial dawn, cosmic entities of chaotic entropy known as the **Galchutt** (syncretized with the **Old Ones** of Palladium lore) descended upon the cosmos. Rather than ruling from distant planes, they plunged into the physical mantle of Eraedal itself.
- Unable to be utterly slain by mortal or divine forces, they were **sealed in the deep bedrock** through ancient sacrifice.
- They slumber in nightmares beneath the continental crust. Their toxic, psychic ichor leaks upward, spawning aboleths, grotesque chaos cults, and mutagenic abominations in the lowest subterranean reaches.

### The Spire & Ancient Monuments
- **The [[Spire]]:** Rising 3,000 feet straight out of the earth beside the [[Bay of Ptolus]], the Spire is an impossible pillar of blackened rock. Rooted in Praemal lore, it was raised in antiquity as an anchor of dark power, once capped by the dread fortress of **Jabel Shammar**.
- **The Moons of Eraedal:** The skies are watched by the familiar pale moon **Lunas** and the dark, ill-omened moon **Vallis**, whose passage marks surges in chaotic sorcery and planar rifts.
- **The Banewarrens & The Subterranean Undercity:** Beneath Ptolus lie endless strata of ancient ruins—from the dwarven fortress of [[Dwarvenhearth]] to sealed vaults housing reality-corrupting banes (*Library/Ptolus/MP005_Banewarrens.pdf*).
- **Ancient Historical Epochs:**
  1. **The Primordial Cataclysm & The Binding:** When the Old Ones / Galchutt were bound into the world's core.
  2. **The Golden Age of Dwarvenhearth:** The great dwarven civilization that carved vast underground citadels beneath the Spire.
  3. **The Ghul Wars:** An apocalyptic struggle where half-fiend conquerors and necromantic hordes battered the mortal realms before being driven back into the dark.
  4. **The Imperial Frontier Era:** The modern age, wherein the Western Empire established **[[Dalenguard]]**, appointed the Commissar, and allowed **[[Ptolus]]** to become a boomtown for adventurers delving into ancient subterranean vaults.
- **The Inverted Pyramid:** A secretive cabal of high arcanists whose extradimensional sanctum floats unseen in the skies above the Spire, guarding cosmic secrets while remaining aloof from mundane imperial politics.

## 3. The Active Pulse: Pathfinder 2e Remaster Mechanics & Deities

When a mortal draws steel, weaves an incantation, or calls upon divine providence, **Pathfinder 2e Remaster** governs the laws of reality.

### The Divine Pantheon (Golarion Syncretism)
The gods of Golarion are the true arbiters of divine power across Eraedal, organized into regional temples and imperial cults:
- **[[Abadar]] (Master of the First Vault):** The patron of civic order, the Imperial Mint, commerce, and legal agreements. His golden scales are prominent throughout Western Empire banking houses and the [[Council of Coin]].
- **[[Iomedae]] (The Inheritor):** Revered by paladins, knightly orders, and provincial crusaders who uphold justice, honor, and martial discipline.
- **[[Asmodeus]] (Prince of Darkness):** Openly or discretely venerated within the aristocratic upper tiers of the Western Empire to legitimize totalitarian rule, unbreakable contracts, and imperial slave markets.
- **[[Pharasma]] (The Lady of Graves):** Feared and respected across all civilized lands; her clergy oversees the rites of passing and wages relentless war against the undead rising from catacombs like the [[Necropolis]].
- **[[Torag]] (The Father of Creation):** Championed by the dwarven clans who trace their lineage to [[Dwarvenhearth]] and the forge-temples of the mountains.
- **[[Desna]], [[Cayden Cailean]], & [[Sarenrae]]:** The beloved patrons of free travelers, tavern brawlers, and common folk resisting the suffocating boot of imperial bureaucracy.
- **[[Lamashtu]] & [[Rovagug]]:** The dark horrors whispered in undercity alleyways, venerated by mutant broods, beastfolk cults, and dwellers of the deep vaults.

### Mechanical Rules
- **Tactical Engine:** Every combat encounter, martial maneuver, skill action, and condition follows **PF2e Remaster** rules (3-action economy, 4 degrees of success, proficiency scaling).
- **Character Ancestries:** Humans, elves, dwarves, and gnomes exist alongside custom and adapted ancestries:
  - **Wolfen:** Modeled using custom ancestry traits or beastkin/gnoll frameworks with cold-resilient and lupine heritage feats.
  - **Ratlings & Goblins:** Thriving in the undercity burrows and sewer labyrinths under standard PF2e ancestry structures.
- **Magic Traditions:** Spells operate under the four divine, arcane, occult, and primal traditions. Ancient pre-cataclysmic relics are handled as high-level PF2e artifacts and reliquaries.
