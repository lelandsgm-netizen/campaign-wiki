## 🌊 Overview

The **Bay of Ptolus** is a deep, naturally sheltered oceanic embayment located along the southeastern seaboard of the **[[Tarldet Plains]]** in the **[[Western Empire]]**. Positioned where the eastern waters of the **[[Inner Sea|Inland Sea]]** meet the coastal currents sweeping up from the **[[Sea of Scarlet Waters]]**, the bay serves as the vital maritime lung of the great boomtown of **[[Ptolus]]**.

Guarded to the north by the towering, impossible black needle of the **[[Spire]]** and framed to the west by the two-hundred-foot coastal bluffs supporting the city's Oldtown and Dalenguard districts, the Bay of Ptolus is one of the busiest, most chaotic anchorages in the Megaverse. On any given afternoon, hundreds of vessels crowd its glistening waters—ranging from gold-laden Western Empire galleons and armored Timro trade ketches to battered privateer caravels transporting eager fortune-seekers arriving to plumb the depths of ancient Dwarvenhearth.

1. **The Deep-Water Basin**: The center of the bay plunges into a deep oceanic trench, allowing even the largest four-masted Western Empire war galleys to set anchor without risk of grounding.
2. **The Sinking Sands**: Unlike traditional rocky or packed-sand harbors, the western shore beneath Ptolus's cliffs is composed of notoriously unstable, water-logged mud and sinking sands. The entire **[[Docks]]** district—warehouses, fish markets, taverns, and crane piers—is constructed atop colossal forest-grown pilings driven deep into the bedrock. Constant construction is required as older piers slowly subside into the brine.
3. **The Sea-Monster Patrols**: Because ancient sewer outfalls and subterranean aqueducts from the deep [[Dungeon]] empty directly into the bay, bizarre predators—scavenging chana-sharks, giant morays, and sewer krakens—frequently lurk around the pilings, keeping the Harbour Watch on high alert.

## 🏛️ Geopolitical Role in the Western Empire

Within the ongoing **[[Three-way Civil War]]** tearing the Western Empire apart, the Bay of Ptolus is an indispensable lifeline:
- **Holy Emperor Rehoboth's Sea Road**: With land routes across the Middle Kingdoms overrun by barbarian Coyles and monstrous legions, the court of Holy Emperor Rehoboth in Ptolus relies almost entirely on sea convoys entering the bay from the south to maintain food and weapons supplies.
- **The Tariff Wars**: Both the Imperial Commissar, **[[Igor Urnst]]**, and the aristocratic merchant consortium known as the **[[Council of Coin]]** maintain competing customs checkpoints along the docks, extracting port dues from arriving merchantmen to finance their respective private guards.

## 📜 The Tri-System Synthesis: Bay Canon

### 1. The Physical Shell (Palladium Canon)
- Fits seamlessly into the coastal geography of the Western Empire along the Inland Sea and Tarldet Plains.

### 2. The Ancient Bones (Praemal / Ptolus Lore)
- Directly adapted from Monte Cook's *Ptolus: City by the Spire* (the Docks, the bay cartography, and harbor encounters).
- Submerged beneath the bay’s northern waters lie drowned basalt structures predating mortal history—the foundation ruins of ancient sea-temples raised during the primordial epoch of the **Galchutt / Old Ones**.

### 3. The Active Pulse (Pathfinder 2e Remaster)
- Operates under PF2e Remaster maritime and harbor mechanics.
- Shrines along the wharves honor **[[Gozreh]]** (The Wind and Waves) and **[[Abadar]]** (Harbor Tariffs and Safe Voyages).
