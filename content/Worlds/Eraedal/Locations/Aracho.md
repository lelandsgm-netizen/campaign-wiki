## ⚓ Overview

**Aracho** is the beating maritime heart and commercial gateway of the **[[Timro|Timro Kingdom]]**. Situated along the sheltered southern coastline overlooking the **Sea of Despair**, Aracho serves as the primary naval springboard connecting mainland Timro to the southern waters, the **Strait of Relar**, and the volatile **[[Floenry Isles]]**.

Ruled with an iron grip by the **Duke of Aracho**—the wealthy, aristocratic cousin of **King Gedro**—the city is a sprawling, heavily fortified commercial juggernaut. Fleets of merchant caravels, grain haulers, and armored warships of the [[Timro Royal Navy]] crowd its deep-water docks. Within its stone districts, vast open-air bazaars and flea markets trade everything from imported Western spices and Elven gossamer silks to dwarven steel from [[Acoroc]].

Yet Aracho is equally infamous across Eraedal as the kingdom's primary **slave trading depot**. Here, the Cadger and Larwhon slave markets process thousands of captured Orcs, Goblins, Ogres, and indentured debtors every month, shipping chained laborers southward to the sugar plantations of [[Oen]] or inland to eastern agricultural latifundia. This immense concentration of oppressed labor makes Aracho a ticking powder keg of seething resentment and abolitionist conspiracy.

### 1. The Ducal Palace & Sovereign Court
Perched upon the high cliffs guarding the harbor entrance, the palace is a massive stone fortress commanding panoramic views of the sea lanes. The Duke of Aracho holds court here, backed by private retinues of heavy cavalry and royal magistrates. By crown decree, only King Gedro himself can overrule the Duke’s municipal mandates.

### 2. The Commercial Docks & Naval Basin
The economic engine of southern Timro:
- **Commercial Piers**: Able to berth dozens of ocean-going vessels simultaneously, handling cargo transfers of grain, rum, timber, and wine destined for foreign kingdoms.
- **Naval Outfitting Yards**: Maintained under high security by the [[Timro Royal Navy]], these dry docks refit heavy frigates and the famous **Armored Dragon Slayers** before they deploy into the southern straits.
- **The Borlon Supply Conduit**: It is from these docks that the military convoys depart to resupply the colonial garrison at **[[Borlon]]** on [[Oen]], transporting royal soldiers, iron armaments, and naval supplies.

### 3. The Slave Markets of Aracho
The darkest and most lucrative sector of the city:
- **The Cadger Slave Market**: Deals exclusively in non-human chattel—primarily **Orcs**, **Goblins**, and **Ogres** captured along the Old Kingdom frontier or bred on state work farms. These captives are branded, bound in heavy chains, and sold in bulk to plantation owners and naval galley captains.
- **Larwhon’s Market**: Specializes in indentured debt slaves and political prisoners, contracting them out for fixed terms of hard service.
- **The Underground Resistance**: Despite constant patrols by the city watch, agents of the **[[Free Slave Coalition]]** and covert spies from the **[[Order of the Indomitable Shield]]** operate safehouses along the harbor alleys, facilitating the escape of captives onto privateer vessels bound for [[Tortugo]].

### 4. Bazaars & Flea Markets
Aracho is renowned for having some of the largest open-air flea markets in Eraedal, where commoners, travelers, and sailors buy and sell goods. Notable establishments include *Chests 'N' Boxes* (selling warded storage trunks), *Spectrum Dyes*, *Norman the Tooler*, and the *Guiding Light* candle manufactory.

## ⚔️ The Strategic Connection to *Prophecy and Balance*

In the active campaign theater of the [[Floenry Isles]], Aracho is the direct mainland puppet-master of events on [[Oen]]:

1. **The Source of Borlon's Requisitions**:
   - The missing **ironwood supplies** currently being investigated by the Black Regiment in [[Borlon]] were originally logged and dispatched from the shipwright depots of Aracho. Any deeper audit leads investigators directly to corrupt shipping magnates in the Duke's inner circle.
2. **Drogon Deathbringer's Blood Feud**:
   - Orc Warlord **[[Drogon Deathbringer]]**, leader of the [[Order of the Indomitable Shield]], spent six years as a branded galley slave chained to an Aracho war caravel before leading a bloody mutiny and fleeing south to liberate the Oen plantations. He has sworn an oath to one day sail an armada into Aracho’s bay and burn its slave markets to the waterline.
3. **Colonial Transports**:
   - Whenever Constable **[[Anya Volkov]]** requests military reinforcements from the mainland, the troop galleons are marshaled and dispatched directly from Aracho's naval basin.

## 🎯 Adventure Hooks in Aracho

1. **The Slaver's Manifest**: A covert operative of the Order of the Indomitable Shield tasks the party with infiltrating the Cadger Market to steal a shipping manifest detailing an upcoming transport of three hundred Orc slaves bound for the Borlon fortifications.
2. **The Missing Ironwood Nexus**: Following clues from Borlon, the party tracks an illicit shipment of enchanted ironwood to the private warehouse of a merchant lord in Aracho, uncovering evidence of high-level treason involving naval officers.
3. **The Escaped Galley Slave**: A high-ranking fugitive bearing secret military codes regarding Timro's southern naval dispositions seeks passage aboard a smuggler's ship in Aracho harbor. The party must extract him past the Duke's inquisitors and deliver him safely to [[Captain Lebrac]] or [[Vernazza]].
