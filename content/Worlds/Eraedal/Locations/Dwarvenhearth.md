## 🏛️ Overview: The Lost City Beneath the Cobblestones

**Dwarvenhearth** is the greatest subterranean ruin on [[Eraedal]]—a gargantuan, multi-tiered dwarven city-state carved out of the solid bedrock beneath the cliffs of **[[Ptolus]]** and the roots of the colossal **[[Spire]]**. Built millennia ago when the dwarven race stood at the pinnacle of stone-shaping mastery, Dwarvenhearth was a civilization of grand vaulted halls, magma-heated aqueducts, mile-long colonnades, and legendary mithral foundries.

During the apocalyptic **[[Ghul Wars]]**, the Half-God Ghul besieged the underground metropolis with legions of mutated abominations, necro-mechanical engines, and chittering burrowing horrors. Faced with annihilation, the High Thanes executed a devastating ritual: they sealed the colossal adamantine blast gates with permanent runic locks, broke the subterranean highway causeways, and abandoned their ancestral jewel to the deep dark.

For centuries, Dwarvenhearth was remembered only in songs, until **713 IA**, when an expedition of daring explorers discovered a subterranean fault line and breached its sealed northern perimeter. The treasures, enchanted weapons, and lost metalcraft recovered from those initial delves triggered an unprecedented gold rush. Thousands of mercenaries, delvers, arcanists, and scoundrels from across the [[Western Empire]] flooded into Ptolus, transforming the sleepy coastal fortress city into the adventuring boom-town it is today.

1. **The Great Adamantine Gate:** The primary historical entrance connecting the upper Ghulvorn labyrinths to Dwarvenhearth proper. The gates are forged from foot-thick slabs of runed adamantine, standing forty feet high, heavily scarred by siege weaponry from the Ghul Wars.
2. **The Colonnade of Thanes:** A monumental avenue flanked by fifty-foot statues of ancient dwarven high kings carved directly from black obsidian. Now home to ethereal dwarven wraiths that attack any who desecrate their ancestral tombs without uttering the ancient passwords of the clans.
3. **The Great Forge Ward:** The industrial nexus of the metropolis. Cold magma channels crisscross the floor, connecting towering basalt blast furnaces where legendary runic smiths once forged arms for the armies of light. Active clockwork automata and clockwork sentries continue to patrol the foundry floors on automated routines.
4. **The Magma Cisterns:** Deep subterranean reservoirs tapping geothermal energy from deep within Eraedal's mantle. The searing heat from these chambers radiates into the deep caverns, creating lethal ambient temperatures for unprepared delvers.
5. **The Deep Chaos Fissures:** Beneath the lowest residential tiers lie ancient fault lines plunging toward the bedrock core. Here, the vile nightmare-ichor of the imprisoned **[[Galchutt]]** (Old Ones) oozes through the rock, mutating native cave vermin into chimeric terrors and attracting heretical chaos cultists from Ptolus.

## 🧭 Significance to the Western Empire

- **The Relic Economy:** The flood of ancient weapons, cold-forged adamantine armor, and high-potency staves coming out of Dwarvenhearth has revolutionized trade in the **[[Western Empire]]**. The **[[Council of Coin]]** collects hefty export duties on delver haul shipped through the **[[Bay of Ptolus]]**, while the **[[Delver's Guild]]** insures expeditions descending into the depths.
- **The Arms Race in the Civil War:** All three factions in the **[[Three-way Civil War]]** crave the lost technologies of the dwarven kings. Envoys from Empress Lasha Itomas, Emperor Skluud Ne'Klosh, and Holy Emperor **[[Rehobath Ylestos]]** offer astronomical bounties for intact siege schematics and runic armaments salvaged from the Forge Ward.
- **The Cosmic Hazard:** Every seal broken in Dwarvenhearth brings mortal delvers closer to the deep containment vaults sealing the cosmic Old Ones. As Commissar **[[Igor Urnst]]** frequently warns, a single careless delving company could crack open the ancient wards and unleash the end of Eraedal upon the surface world.
