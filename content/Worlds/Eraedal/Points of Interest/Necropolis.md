## 🪦 Overview: The Walled City of the Dead

The **Necropolis** is the vast, fog-shrouded cemetery district sprawling across the rolling northern ridges above **[[Ptolus]]**. Enclosed behind massive, forty-foot-tall granite walls sheathed with sheets of warding lead, the district houses the tombs, crypts, and mausoleums of over seven centuries of imperial and native dead.

Unlike ordinary mortal resting grounds, the Necropolis is an active, volatile containment zone. The ancient metaphysical scars left by the **Spire**, the primordial Galchutt slumbering deep in the crust, and subterranean planar leaks from the **[[Banewarrens]]** have worn the barrier between life and death paper-thin. Unsanctified corpses spontaneously rise as shambling skeletons, ravenous ghouls, or vengeful wights.

Because of this constant menace, the Necropolis is heavily fortified. Common citizens are only permitted entry through the **[[Gates of Lead]]** during broad daylight under heavily armed escort to pay respects at family crypts. When the great lead-weighted curfew bell tolls at sundown, the iron portcullises slam shut. Behind the walls, the living retreat, leaving the mist-choked monuments to the nocturnal rule of undead lords and shadowy cabals.

1. **The [[Gates of Lead]]:** The sole official portal into the Necropolis. Fortified like a fortress barbican, manned around the clock by the [[City Watch]] and grim inquisitor-clerics under **[[Brother Fabien]]**.
2. **The [[Dark Reliquary]]:** A towering, monolithic cathedral of jagged black volcanic stone perched upon the highest hill. It serves as the sovereign citadel for **The [[Forsaken]]** (intelligent undead) and **The [[Fallen]]** (fiend-worshipers), openly mocking the holy spires of the city below.
3. **The [[Ossuary]]:** A subterranean labyrinth of stone vaults where millions of bones from the city's poor and plague victims have been stacked for centuries. An epicenter of necrotic pollution and hauntings.
4. **The [[Death's Head Tavern]]:** A grim, lantern-lit tavern built directly against the outer gatehouse wall. It serves as the gathering place for gravediggers, morticians, and bounty-hunting delvers seeking payment for destroyed ghouls.

## ⚖️ Faction Powers: The Dual Sovereignty of Shadow

Behind the iron gates, power is divided between two sinister cabals operating out of the [[Dark Reliquary]]:

*   **The [[Forsaken]]:** A syndicate of intelligent undead—vampires, ancient wights, mummies, and living necromancers led by **[[Vamal Shard]]**. They view the Necropolis as their lawful nocturnal sovereign domain and maintain a cold, sinister truce with city authorities: so long as their feeding remains discreet and the dead stay behind the lead walls, total war is avoided.
*   **The [[Fallen]]:** A fanatical cult of demonologists, tiefling warlocks, and chaos priests who revere fiendish princes and the primordial terror of the Galchutt. Unlike the practical Forsaken, the Fallen actively seek to tear open planar rifts to flood Ptolus with abyssal fire.

## ⚔️ Pathfinder 2e Remaster Mechanics

### Necropolis Environmental Traits & Hazards
*   **Persistent Void Resonance:** The entire district bears the **Void** and **Death** environmental traits. Spontaneous undead creation occurs: any humanoid corpse left unburied and unconsecrated for more than 24 hours rises as a Zombie Shambler or Ghoul at midnight.
*   **Consecration Resistance:** Undead within the Necropolis gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against *Vitality* spells and attempts to Turn Undead.
*   **The Fog of Styx:** Between midnight and dawn, dense grey mist blankets the hills, reducing visibility to 20 feet and imparting the *Concealed* condition to all creatures beyond 10 feet.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Midnight Curfew Trap:** While investigating a stolen ancestral relic inside a noble crypt, the party is delayed by a ghoul ambush. The curfew bell tolls, the [[Gates of Lead]] slam shut, and the party must survive the night against packs of prowling wights.
2. **The Soul-Jar Heist:** A grieving merchant prince from the [[Nobles' Quarter]] hires the party to infiltrate the subterranean crypts of the [[Dark Reliquary]] to recover the trapped soul of his assassinated heir before **[[Vamal Shard]]** converts the youth into a vampire spawn.
3. **The Cleansing of the Ossuary:** A massive planar tremor linked to the **[[Banewarrens]]** ruptures the lower catacombs of the [[Ossuary]], causing hundreds of skeletons to merge into an enormous *Skeletal Titan*. [[Brother Fabien]] calls upon veteran delvers to enter the catacombs and destroy the anomaly.
