## 🍷 Overview: The Quiet Alcove of the Arcanists

While the nearby [[Ghostly Minstrel]] roars with the boisterous laughter of drunken warriors, clattering tankards, and bardic lute songs, **Danbury's Tavern** is a sanctuary of profound, warded stillness. Situated just off the cobblestones of **[[Delver's Square]]**, Danbury’s is marked not by a creaking painted tavern sign, but by an understated brass plaque depicting an open book illuminated by a single silver flame.

The tavern is owned and managed by **Danbury**, a retired wizard of advanced years with impeccably groomed silver hair, elegant dark velvet robes, and a reputation for absolute discretion. Having survived the turbulent years following the repeal of the Edict of Deviltry, Danbury engineered his establishment specifically for spellcasters, sages, and planners who require an environment free of brawling sellswords and intrusive eavesdroppers.

Inside, the tavern is divided into private mahogany booths partitioned by heavy velvet drapes. Every booth is enchanted with permanent low-grade *Silence* and *Private Sanctum* abjurations: inside the booth, conversation is crystal-clear to occupants, but completely inaudible to anyone standing outside. The scent of pipe-tobacco, cedar smoke, and spiced tea fills the air, while silent unseen servants glide between tables delivering vintage wines and delicate pastries.

## 🌙 The Gateway to the Dreaming Apothecary

Danbury's Tavern is famous across Eraedal for one paramount secret: **it is the primary physical liaison for the [[Dreaming Apothecary]]**.

Because the master crafters of the Dreaming Apothecary never set foot in the waking world, prospective clients seeking bespoke magic items must come through Danbury:
1. **The Inquest:** A client approaches Danbury at his private corner counter and whispers the traditional inquiry: *"I seek an audience with the weavers of the twilight veil."*
2. **The Fee & Draught:** Danbury assesses the client's demeanor, accepts the mandatory 50 gp consultation retainer, and provides a lead-sealed phial of *Somnambulist Draught* alongside instructions for preparing the dream-lockbox.
3. **The Guarantee:** Danbury guarantees the integrity of the contact. He takes no cut of the final magic item commission, acting purely as an honored mortal custodian of the dreamlands compact.

## 🍽️ Fare & Special Accommodations

Unlike rough taverns serving salted mutton and cloudy ale, Danbury’s caters to refined palates:

*   **Violet Lotus Mead (5 sp / glass):** Brewed with imported honey and night-blooming lotus petals. Induces a mild, pleasant euphoria that grants a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against anxiety and fear effects for 1 hour.
*   **The Scribe's Board (8 sp):** Sliced smoked duck breast, goat’s milk cheese steeped in rosemary, fresh crusty sourdough bread, and spiced pear compote.
*   **Private Research Rooms:** For 5 gp a day, wizards can rent upper-floor study rooms equipped with drafting desks, alchemical braziers, ink-pots, and reference scrolls on planar theory.

## ⚖️ Pathfinder 2e Remaster Rules of Conduct

Danbury maintains zero tolerance for violent outbursts or aggressive spellcasting:

*   **Aura of Ward-Suppression:** The tavern is protected by a permanent 8th-rank *Dispel Magic* contingency. If a patron casts a hostile spell carrying the evocation, attack, or damage traits within the building, the spell is automatically countered (counteract modifier +22), and two **Clockwork Guardians** (Level 8) step from concealed wall panels to escort the offender to the street.
*   **Anti-Scrying Screen:** Divination sensors and scrying eyes cannot pierce the tavern’s lead-lined outer walls (DC 35 counteract check required to view the interior).

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Intercepted Draught:** When a party member attempts to purchase the slumber-draught to commission an emergency ward from the [[Dreaming Apothecary]], Danbury discovers that his latest batch was adulterated with *Id Insinuation* poison. He suspects an agent of [[House Sadar]] is attempting to assassinate prospective clients.
2. **The Neutral Parley:** Archmage [[Inverted Pyramid | Jevicca Nor]] of the Inverted Pyramid invites the party to meet her in Booth Seven of Danbury's to discuss the recent tremors shaking the Spire, choosing the tavern because its wards shield them from Grand Inquisitor [[Frederick Vane]]'s diviners.
3. **The Dreamer's Debt:** A panicked merchant barricades himself in Danbury’s back room, having deposited counterfeit gold for an Apothecary delivery. With Nightmare Stalkers circling his mind every time he blinks, he begs the party to venture into the undercity to retrieve genuine platinum bullion before the dreamers claim his life.
