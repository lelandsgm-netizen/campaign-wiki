The **Temple District** is the spiritual heart and religious epicenter of [[Ptolus]]. It is a district of sweeping architectural contrast, where towering, immaculate marble cathedrals sit shoulder-to-shoulder with humble stone shrines, fortified priories, and hidden, secretive chapels.

The geography of the district is bisected by the **[[Street of a Million Gods]]**, a massive, cobblestone boulevard teeming with pilgrims, mendicants, and divine champions. In [[Eraedal]], the district serves as a profound theological crucible: here, the widespread, beloved worship of the global **[[Order of Light - Pantheon | Order of Light]]** confronts the hardline, authoritarian dogma of the Western Empire's **[[Imperial Rite - Pantheon | Imperial Rite]]**, transported east following the catastrophic fall of [[Caer Itom]] in 702 IA. The air is perpetually thick with the scent of frankincense and burning myrrh, the resonant chiming of sanctified bells, and the chanting of litanies against the dark.

### Prominent Locations

- **The [[Street of a Million Gods]]:** A magnificent, crowded avenue lined on both sides with shrines, temples, and altars dedicated to virtually every recognized deity in the cosmos. Here, the soaring marble facades of **[[Iomedae]]** and the gilded banking-sanctuaries of **[[Abadar]]** dominate the skyline, standing alongside prominent holy sites dedicated to **[[Sarenrae]]**, **[[Nethys]]**, **[[Pharasma]]**, and hundreds of minor localized gods and planar patrons.

- **The [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]:** A colossal, imposing white-marble fortress-cathedral serving as the supreme ecclesiastical seat of the **[[Order of Light - Pantheon | Order of Light]]** in eastern Eraedal. Its soaring stained-glass rose windows depict the glorious sunburst of [[Sarenrae]] and the righteous longsword of [[Iomedae]]. Heavily armored champions of the Order of the Dawn guard its relic vaults, while Prince [[Kirian Ylestos]] conducts high litanies from the radiant chancel.

- **The Holy Palace:** The lavish, heavily guarded residence and administrative court of Holy Emperor [[Rehobath Ylestos]]. Consecrated following his flight from [[Caer Itom]] in 702 IA, the palace serves as the headquarters of the **[[Imperial Rite - Pantheon | Imperial Rite]]** in exile, where high prelates channel the lingering sparks of the Slumbering Architect [[Aroden]] and marshal funds to reclaim the Lion-Guarded Throne.

- **The [[Priory of Introspection]]:** A vast, austere monastic compound built from pale dressed stone that serves as the supreme redoubt of the **[[Sisterhood of Silence]]**. This quiet sanctuary features walled meditation gardens and stone training courtyards where vow-bound female monks hone their formidable nonlethal martial disciplines.

- **The Iron Tower:** A grim, slate-gray fortress-citadel adjacent to the grand cathedrals, serving as the barracks, armory, and chapterhouse for the militant paladins of the Order of the Dawn and inquisitors tasked with rooting out subterranean chaos cults.

- **The Sanctuary of the Sun:** A luminous, open-air marble temple dedicated specifically to **[[Sarenrae]]**. Featuring a massive polished brass sun-disk that illuminates the avenue at dawn, it serves as the city's primary haven for miraculous healing, free infirmaries, and public soup kitchens.

### Prominent People

- **[[Kirian Ylestos|Prince Kirian Ylestos]]:** The shrewd, aristocratic Prince of the Church and High Prelate of the [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]]. Holding a permanent seat on the [[Council of Coin]], Kirian has managed Ptolus's holy institutions since 682 IA. While outwardly supporting his father Rehobath's claims, Kirian is a seasoned pragmatist who carefully shields the beloved, global [[Order of Light - Pantheon | Order of Light]] from being consumed by his father's obsessive, dead-god [[Imperial Rite - Pantheon | Imperial Rite]] crusade. Mechanically, he is a high-level **Cleric** (Cloistered) who wields diplomacy and holy authority in equal measure.

- **Mother Superior Solenna (or Orelia Kael):** The silent, deeply revered leader of the **Sisterhood of Silence** (operating out of the Priory of Introspection). She holds a permanent seat on the city's ruling council, though she rarely attends in person, choosing instead to send her votes via a high-ranking sister and a dedicated male eunuch translator. Mechanically, she is a high-level **Monk** (utilizing defensive and non-lethal subduing stances) who approaches city security with absolute, unwavering discipline.

### PF2e Mechanical Identity for Vault Tagging

Behind the GM screen, use these Pathfinder 2e baselines to flavor Temple District encounters, divine spellcasting, and holy factions:

- **Common Ancestries:** Human (predominantly traditional Prustan heritages), alongside highly devout **Dwarves** (worshiping Torag) and **Elves** or **Half-Elves** serving as keepers of the arcane/divine balance. **Nephilim (Aasimar)** are highly visible here, viewed as living blessings by the local temples.

- **Prevalent Classes:** **Clerics** (both Warpriests and Cloistered), **Champions** (enforcing holy causes), and **Monks** operating within the silent priories.

- **Signature Magic:** Divine spellcasters heavily utilize the **Vitality (Positive)**, **Holy**, and **Spirit** traits. Spells like _heal_, _divine wrath_, _sanctuary_, and _dispel magic_ define the defensive and cleansing nature of the district.

- **Common Associations:** Strongly aligned with **The Commissar (Igor Urnst)** and the orthodox traditionalists. They maintain a warm, charitable alliance with **House Nagel**, look upon the shadow-wielding **House Sadar** with extreme suspicion, and actively hunt the dark cults of the Necropolis.
