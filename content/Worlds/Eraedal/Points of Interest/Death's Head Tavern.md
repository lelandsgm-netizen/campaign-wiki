## 💀 Overview: The Taproom on the Threshold

The **Death’s Head Tavern** is the infamous, grim tavern built directly into the exterior granite retaining wall of the **[[Necropolis]]**. While the taverns of [[Delver's Square]] like the [[Ghostly Minstrel]] and [[Danbury's Tavern]] echo with lively bardic songs, high-stakes mercantile deals, and bustling cheer, the Death's Head is a quiet, shadowed refuge of low murmurs, clinking pewter mugs, and the heavy smell of clove tobacco and vinegar wash.

The tavern caters to those whose livelihood is inextricably bound to the dead: gravediggers who dig trenches in the mist, embalmers from the mortuary guild, silent couriers from **The [[Forsaken]]**, and battle-hardened adventuring mercenaries who specialize in hunting vampires, ghouls, and wights.

The interior decor reflects its morbid neighborhood: tables are fashioned from weathered granite sarcophagus lids salvaged from collapsed tombs, the chandeliers are constructed from iron-reinforced deer antlers and animal skulls, and behind the heavy oak bar hangs an array of silver stakes, consecrated garrotes, and vial racks filled with holy water and anti-plague elixirs.

## 🍺 Offerings & Black-Market Reagents

Proprietor **Garrick Morvath** runs the establishment with dry, unshakeable calm. Beyond pouring bitter, pitch-black stouts, Garrick provides services tailored to the grim realities of the Necropolis:

*   **The Undead Bounty Ledger:** Garrick acts as an authorized collection broker for **[[Brother Fabien]]** and the [[Order of Light]]. Delvers who bring in verified trophies from dangerous rogue undead can cash them in directly over the bar counter.
*   **Alchemical Reagents:** Under the counter, Garrick buys and sells rare components harvested from the Necropolis hills—luminescent graveyard moss, preserved mummy dust, and distilled embalming fluid used by alchemists and necromancers alike.
*   **Neutral Ground:** By ancient tradition respected by both the **[[City Watch]]** and **The [[Forsaken]]**, the taproom of the Death's Head is considered strictly neutral territory. Drawing weapons or casting offensive spells inside results in a permanent bounty and immediate blacklisting by every grave-worker in Ptolus.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Missing Gravediggers:** A crew of four veteran gravediggers failed to report back to the tavern after opening a forgotten family vault in the lower ridges. Garrick hires the party to enter the vault through a sewer entrance behind the tavern to find out what swallowed them.
2. **The Midnight Messenger:** While the party is having drinks, a pale, cloaked figure approaches their table and leaves a lead cylinder sealed with black wax—a message from **[[Vamal Shard]]** offering a private meeting in the **[[Dark Reliquary]]**.
3. **The Poisoned Keg:** A cell of the **[[Fallen]]** poisons the tavern's cellar kegs with a distilled strain of *Ghoul Fever*, hoping to turn the tavern patrons into a ravenous horde on the eve of the **[[Night of Dissolution]]**. The party must identify the contaminant and locate the saboteur before midnight.
