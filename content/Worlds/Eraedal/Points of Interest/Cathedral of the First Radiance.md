## 🏛️ Overview: The Crown of the Radiance

The **Cathedral of the First Radiance** is the architectural crown, spiritual heart, and premier holy sanctuary of **[[Ptolus]]**. Standing at the eastern crest of the [[Temple District]] where the upper bluffs drop toward the [[Bay of Ptolus]], the cathedral’s towering white-marble spires and gold-leafed cupolas catch the first rays of the dawn sun as it rises over the [[Inland Sea]].

Serving as the supreme regional basilica of the global **[[Order of Light - Pantheon | Order of Light]]**, the cathedral stands as the spiritual counterweight to the dark, necrotic miasma radiating from the [[Necropolis]] and the cosmic abominations slumbering in the deep subterranean vaults beneath the city.

Within its sweeping vaulted naves, thousands of pilgrims, wounded delvers, and devout citizens gather daily. Unlike the nearby, heavily fortified Holy Palace—where exiled Holy Emperor [[Rehobath Ylestos]] maintains his authoritarian court and preaches the Western Empire's dead-god [[Imperial Rite - Pantheon | Imperial Rite]]—the Cathedral of the First Radiance is open to all who seek shelter, healing, and divine guidance under the four benevolent sovereigns of light.

## 🗺️ Architectural Sanctuaries & Altars

The cathedral is laid out in the shape of an immense, radiant four-pointed star, with each arm dedicated to one of the living pillars of the Order of Light:

```
                                │   (Sarenrae — The Sovereign)   │

         ┌──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                      ▼                                      ▼
┌───────────────────────────────┐     ┌───────────────────┐     ┌───────────────────────────────┐
│     The Sword-Transept        │     │  The Great Dome   │     │    The Vault-Hall of Scales   │
│ (Iomedae — Crusades & Shield) │◄───►│ (Perpetual Daylight│◄───►│ (Abadar — Contracts & Registries│
└───────────────────────────────┘     └─────────┬─────────┘     └───────────────────────────────┘
                                                │

                                │   The Pillar-Chapel of Peace   │
                                │  (Erastil — Community & Hearth)│
                                └────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The High Sun-Chancel of [[Sarenrae]]:** 
   The eastern apex of the cathedral. Dominated by a colossal, fifty-foot circular rose-glass window entitled *The Breaking of the First Dawn*. Below it sits the high marble altar, upon which an enchanted golden font continuously wells with sanctified, restorative holy water. It is here that Prince [[Kirian Ylestos]] delivers the High Liturgies of the Radiance.
2. **The Sword-Transept of [[Iomedae]]:**
   The northern wing, constructed with austere granite colonnades and hung with the battle-standards of ancient knightly crusades. The transept houses the vigil-chapels where paladins of the Order of the Dawn kneel in silent prayer before taking up watch on the walls of Dalenguard or plunging into the horrors of the undercity.
3. **The Vault-Hall of [[Abadar]]:**
   The southern wing, managed by cleric-auditors and jurists. Here, citizens register birth covenants, sanctify commercial pacts, and pay voluntary tithes that fund the cathedral’s expansive public charities, free clinics, and soup kitchens.
4. **The Pillar-Chapel of [[Erastil]]:**
   The western entrance hall, flanked by heavy oak timbers brought from the [[Tarldet Plains]]. It is dedicated to rural travelers, communal weddings, and thanksgiving offerings for the harvests that feed the bustling city.
5. **The Deep Consecrated Reliquary:**
   Carved deep into the limestone bedrock beneath the central altar lies the cathedral's fortified crypt-complex. Protected by permanent *consecrate* circles and warding runes, these vaults hold holy relics salvaged across centuries and guard the subterranean sewer approaches against ghoul tunnels burrowing out of the Necropolis.

## ⚖️ The Theological Standoff

The Cathedral exists in an intricate, uneasy dynamic with the other powers of Ptolus:

- **The Holy Palace (Imperial Rite):** Holy Emperor [[Rehobath Ylestos]] frequently seeks to hold high state masses within the Cathedral, demanding that the clergy proclaim his divine mandate derived from [[Aroden]]. Prince Kirian continually maneuvers to keep the Cathedral centered on the living gods of the Order of Light, preventing his father's controversial dead-god dogma from alienating the city's independent flock.
- **The Secular Bastion ([[Dalenguard]]):** Commissar [[Igor Urnst]] maintains an open-door policy with the Cathedral, regularly funding Iomedaean crusader detachments to reinforce Watch garrisons near the subterranean gates.
- **The Underworld Threat:** Both [[House Balacazar]] and the [[Killraven Crime League]] treat the cathedral’s charity funds with predatory interest, frequently attempting to launder gold through church donations or infiltrate the relic vaults.

## ⚔️ Pathfinder 2e Remaster Mechanics & Services

The Cathedral of the First Radiance serves as the primary safe haven and resurrection hub for adventuring parties operating in Ptolus:

- **Sanctuary of Healing:** Characters resting within the Cathedral’s cloister recover from drained, doomed, and wounded conditions in half the normal time.
- **Divine Spellcasting Services:** 
  - *Heal* (up to 7th rank)
  - *Cleanse Affliction* (Curses, diseases, and virulent poisons from the undercity)
  - *Raise Dead* / *Resurrect* (Requires high-level ecclesiastical dispensation from Prince Kirian or senior prelates, alongside customary diamond tithes)
  - *Restore Senses* and *Sound Mind*
- **Ward of Perpetual Day:** The interior of the main nave is under the permanent effect of an 8th-rank *Holy Light* aura. Undead creatures and fiends carrying the unholy trait cannot enter without taking 8d6 spirit damage each round (DC 36 basic Will save).

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Creeping Crypt-Blight:** Father Lamriel reports that the lowest subterranean crypts have grown abnormally cold, and the holy ward stones guarding the deep foundations are cracking. A stealthy delving party is needed to cleanse whatever foul horror is burrowing up from the deep sewer conduits.
2. **The Stolen Sun-Relic:** A fanatical cell of the [[Cult of the Ebon Hand]] has stolen the *Tears of the Dawnflower*—a sacred vial of solar essence—from the high chancel. Prince Kirian needs the relic recovered quietly before his father uses the theft as an excuse to impose martial law in the Temple District.
3. **The Heretic's Asylum:** An escaped scholar from the Western Empire, bearing evidence that imperial prelates in Caer Itom are siphoning energy from forbidden planar faults, seeks sanctuary at Sarenrae's altar while imperial inquisitors surround the plaza.
