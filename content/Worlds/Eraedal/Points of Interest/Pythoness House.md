## 🏚️ Overview: The House of the Twisted Serpent

**Pythoness House** is a brooding, multi-story limestone manor house situated in a quiet, decaying cul-de-sac of **[[Oldtown]]**. Designed over a century ago by the infamous architect **Cobras Baen**, the manor is an architectural monstrosity of distorted perspectives, hidden false corridors, secret gravity-defying stairwells, and concealed subterranean chambers designed specifically to evade the historical Inquisitions of the [[Western Empire]].

Beneath its decaying plaster ceilings and dust-choked silk draperies, the house harbors a sordid, bloody history. Between **716 IA and 718 IA**, Pythoness House operated as an upscale brothel, opium den, and fortune-telling salon run by **Maquent Dellisaria** (a disgraced seer cast out of the Fate Weavers) and her lover, **Radanna Scalth**. Unknown to the wealthy patricians who frequented its lounges, Radanna was a fanatical devotee of chaos who transformed the lower cellars into an active ceremonial temple for the **Crimson Coil** and the **[[Cult of the Ebon Hand]]**.

The house eventually met a violent end when internal betrayal and demonic summonings resulted in a massacre, leaving the manor locked, condemned, and fiercely haunted by the tormented spirits of those murdered within its walls.

1. **The Upper Attics & Taunell’s Haunt:** The top floor is perpetually cold. It was the prison and death chamber of **Taunell**, a tragic servant tortured and murdered by the cultists. His restless, moaning ghost haunted the rafters, desperately seeking resolution and proper burial rites.
2. **The Faded Salons:** The ground and second floors retain eerie vestiges of high luxury: rot-eaten velvet sofas, broken harpsichords, and shattered mirrors. Phantom footsteps and the faint scent of stale violet perfume drift through the drafty corridors.
3. **The Subterranean Sump:** Carved directly into Oldtown's bedrock, the cellars contain the blood-stained altars of the Crimson Coil. Here, hidden behind a loose flagstone, lay **Maquent's Journal**, detailing two years of illicit transactions, noble clients, and early warnings of the coming "Night of Dissolution."
4. **The Vault of the Chaosomoton:** The deepest chamber of Pythoness House, accessible only through a hidden shaft lined with serrated stone teeth. Guarded by the **Chaosomoton**—a terrifying, multi-limbed clockwork and chaositech automaton powered by liquid corruption—the vault held the ancient chaotic relics assembled by the cultists.

## 🩸 The Climax & The Wuntad Ambush

Pythoness House was the site of the traumatic cliffhanger that closed the adventurers' previous chapter:

1. **Defeating the Chaosomoton:** Pushing their Level 5 capabilities to the absolute brink, the adventuring party fought a desperate, tactical battle in the lower vault, destroying the lethal construct and securing the room.
2. **The Ambush:** Before the party could catch their breath or bind their bleeding wounds, **[[Shigmaa Wuntad]]**, **Ibard**, and **Saavia** ambushed them. Incapacitated by Ibard's ringing bell and Wuntad's overwhelming shadow-magics, the party was thoroughly defeated.
3. **The Stolen Relics:** Wuntad ripped away their hard-won treasures: the *Skull of Chaos*, the *Anarchic Longsword*, and jars of alchemical chaos dust.
4. **The Heroic Sleight of Hand:** In the seconds before unconsciousness took him, **[[Calebrimbor Algarvran | Calebrimbor]]** desperately concealed the **Chaos Spirit Key** within his *Sleeves of Holding*, preserving the single most critical relic needed to unlock the [[Banewarrens]].
5. **The Extraction:** Leaving the party for dead as "marked livestock," Wuntad's cultists vanished. Minutes later, scouts from **[[Castle Shard]]** slipped through the breached front doors, carrying the broken heroes up to the Spire to be healed by **[[Lord Zavere]]** and **[[Lady Rael]]**.

## 🧭 Ongoing Campaign Significance

Although the manor has been cleared of immediate threats, Pythoness House remains a vital geographic and historical link:
*   **The Spirit Key's Origin:** Arcane analysis reveals that the Chaos Spirit Key was never meant to remain in the vault; it was brought to Pythoness House to be handed off to a courier bound for the sewer entrance of the **[[Banewarrens]]**.
*   **The Watch Investigation:** Commissar [[Igor Urnst]]’s [[City Watch]] has officially boarded up Pythoness House following reports of arcane explosions and screams, but agents of the **[[Cult of the Ebon Hand]]** are already attempting to break back in to search for the missing Key.
