## 🏰 Overview: The Black Bastion of Righteous Steel

**The Iron Tower** is a grim, monumental fortress of polished black basalt rising like an obsidian monolith above the white marble colonnades of the **[[Temple District]]**. Built three centuries ago to serve as the permanent coastal bastion for the **[[Order of the Dawn]]**, the fortress is an imposing physical reminder that faith in [[Eraedal]] is defended by forged steel, disciplined legions, and uncompromising martial strength.

Surrounded by a dry moat and thirty-foot-thick curtain walls pierced with arrow slits and enchanted murder-holes, the Iron Tower houses the full operational apparatus of Ptolus's holy warriors: extensive underground stables for heavy armored warhorses, deep forge-works where church smiths consecrate arms in holy water, and the grand **Hall of Sunburst Shields**, where the heraldic devices of fallen paladins are preserved in perpetual honor.

While the neighboring [[Cathedral of the First Radiance]] serves as the public sanctuary of peace, mercy, and prayer, the Iron Tower is the church's iron fist—the assembly point from which mounted crusades ride out into the [[Necropolis]], reinforce the city gates, or descend into the subterranean depths beneath [[Oldtown]].

1. **The Hall of Sunburst Shields:** The vaulted central nave of the fortress. Flanked by massive statues of ancient knights, the hall features a 60-foot dais where the Order conducts formal courts of honor, knightings, and war councils.
2. **The Divided Quarters:** The living quarters reflect the Order's growing ideological fracture. The Western wing is occupied by the battle-hardened veterans of Caer Itom who fly the lion-and-sunburst standards of the **[[Imperial Rite - Pantheon | Imperial Rite]]**, while the Eastern wing houses the Ptolus-born crusaders dedicated to the **[[Order of Light - Pantheon | Order of Light]]**.
3. **The Sanctified Forges:** Run by master armorers, these subterranean smithies produce masterwork plate armor, dawnsilver blades, and holy water fonts used by crusader strike teams.
4. **The Subterranean Inquest & Iron Cells:** Carved deep into the limestone foundation lies the tower's warded dungeon level. Here, prisoners accused of demonic pacts, chaotic corruption, or unholy crimes are kept behind lead-lined bars. In recent months, Grand Inquisitor [[Frederick Vane]] has requisitioned several lower cell blocks to interrogate suspects regarding the **[[Banewarrens]]**.

## ⚖️ Pathfinder 2e Remaster Environmental Wards

The Iron Tower is one of the most heavily fortified defensive structures on the continent:

*   **Aura of Sanctification:** The entire superstructure is bathed in a permanent 7th-rank *Consecrate* and *Sanctified Ground* aura aligned with the Holy trait. Fiends, undead, and creatures carrying the Unholy trait take a -2 status penalty to attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws within the walls.
*   **The Beacon of Dawnsilver:** Atop the tower sits an enchanted brass brazier fueled with sanctified cedar and powdered silver. When lit in times of crisis, its light cuts through magical darkness for 3 miles and alerts the [[City Watch]] at [[Dalenguard]] to initiate district lockdown protocols.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Inquest Extraction:** A young knight belonging to the "New Dawn" faction has been detained in the lower cells by Inquisitor Vane after refusing to surrender an ancient map fragment of the Banewarrens. His allies reach out to the party to orchestrate a discreet extraction before Vane subjects him to inquisitorial branding.
2. **The Stolen Sunburst Lance:** A ceremonial lance blessed by Iomedae has been stolen from the Hall of Shields by an infiltrator bearing the brand of the [[Cult of the Ebon Hand]]. The Order offers a massive bounty and official Church safe-conduct papers to anyone who tracks the thief into the sewers.
3. **The Schism Mediation:** Prince Kirian hires the party to act as neutral bodyguards during a high-stakes closed-door summit within the Iron Tower between Knight-Commander Tallhart and Dame Shannon, fearing that Imperial Rite hotheads are planning to assassinate the New Dawn leadership.
