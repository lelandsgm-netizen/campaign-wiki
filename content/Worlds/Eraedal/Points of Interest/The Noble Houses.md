## 👑 Overview: The Ten Dynasties of Ptolus

The **Ten Noble Houses of Ptolus** represent the pinnacle of political prestige, dynastic wealth, and cultural authority in the city. Granted hereditary estates and judicial privileges centuries ago by imperial decrees from the [[Western Empire]], these houses have weathered barbarian sieges, the Ghul Wars, and the recent fracturing of the imperial crown in the [[Three-way Civil War]].

While the common citizens of [[Midtown]] and the [[Warrens]] live day-to-day on trade or dungeon salvage, the Noble Houses inhabit a rarefied world of manicured cliffside estates, marble galas, and private armies. Each house possesses a distinct cultural heritage, commercial empire, and metaphysical specialty:

## 🏛️ Directory of the Ten Houses

### 1. [[House Dallimothan]] (The House of the Dragon)
*   **Patriarch:** Lord **[[Kirstol Dallimothan]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Draconic chivalry, aerial dragon-drake cavalry, eastern bluffs citadel. Staunch allies of secular law and Commissar [[Igor Urnst]].

### 2. [[House Vladaam]] (The House of Wickedness)
*   **Patriarch:** Lord **[[Iristul Vladaam]]** (Absent on dark pilgrimage; estate managed by [[Navrus Vladaam]], [[Alisanna Vladaam]], and [[Godrick Vladaam]]).
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Ancient tiefling nobility, dark alchemy, forbidden relics, assassins, and a bitter generational feud against House Nagel.

### 3. [[House Sadar]] (The House of Shadows)
*   **Patriarch:** Lord **[[Renn Sadar]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Shadow magic, umbral conduits, volcanic basalt palace beneath the Spire. Allied with the [[Inverted Pyramid]]; estranged from [[Lady Rael]] of [[Castle Shard]].

### 4. [[House Abanar]] (The House of Coin)
*   **Patriarch:** Lord **[[Dered Abanar]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Banking, mortgages, currency exchange, the [[Abanar Exchange]], dominant bloc on the [[Council of Coin]].

### 5. [[House Kath]] (The House of the Muses)
*   **Matriarch:** Lady **[[Devina Kath]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** High-society galas, opera houses, fine arts, patron of playwrights, wielders of social blackmail.

### 6. [[House Khatru]] (The House of the Sword)
*   **Patriarch:** Lord **[[Dorant Khatru]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Traditional martial tournament champion, heavy cavalry armories, unyielding military honor.

### 7. [[House Erthuo]] (The House of Lore)
*   **Matriarch:** Lady **[[Peliope Erthuo]]** & Scholar **[[Alistair Erthuo]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Elven ancient antiquities, historical excavation sponsorship, deep libraries, and historical artifact translation.

### 8. [[House Rau]] (The House of the Sea)
*   **Matriarch:** Lady **[[Martha Rau]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Merchant fleets, maritime commerce across the [[Bay of Ptolus]], harbor warehouses, naval privateers.

### 9. [[House Shever]] (The House of Machinists)
*   **Patriarch:** Lord **[[Thollos Shever]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Industrial foundries, clockwork mechanics, technomancy, close industrial ties with the **[[Shuul]]**.

### 10. [[House Nagel]] (The House of Fallen Grace)
*   **Matriarch:** Lady **[[Fransin Nagel]]**
*   **Domain & Specialty:** Impoverished, noble house with celestial aasimar ancestry; stripped of wealth but fiercely honorable, defending the poor of Ptolus.

## 🧭 Story Hooks for Adventurers

1. **The Great Council Gala:** The [[Council of Coin]] hosts an annual grand summit in the [[Nobles' Quarter]]. The party is hired as covert bodyguards by Lord [[Kirstol Dallimothan]] to prevent an anticipated assassination attempt orchestrated by [[House Vladaam]].
2. **The Inter-House Excavation Race:** When a new vault entrance beneath the **[[Banewarrens]]** is discovered, [[House Abanar]], [[House Erthuo]], and [[House Sadar]] each hire rival adventuring companies to retrieve the primary artifact before the others.
3. **The Disputed Succession:** An ancient will discovered in an imperial catacomb reveals that an impoverished orphan in the Warrens is the true legal heir to a massive estate claimed by [[House Rau]], triggering a desperate race to keep the heir alive against hitmen.
