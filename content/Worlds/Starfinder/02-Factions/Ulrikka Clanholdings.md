## Overview

**Ulrikka Clanholdings** is one of the most venerable, wealthy, and technologically proficient industrial powers in the [[Pact Worlds]]. Founded centuries ago by a coalition of traditionalist dwarven clans who pooled their ancestral wealth following the end of [[The Gap]], the conglomerate bridges the ancient, solemn traditions of dwarven stonecraft with modern, cutthroat corporate enterprise.

Operating from a gleaming, high-security corporate suite in Bluerise Tower on **[[Absalom Station]]** and commanding the heavily fortified **[[Star Citadel Theodrane]]** in the heart of **[[The Diaspora]]**, Ulrikka Clanholdings is responsible for extracting, refining, and distributing a massive portion of the heavy industrial metals, rare minerals, and adamantine that construct the starships and orbital stations of the system.

## Corporate Culture & Leadership

Unlike human megacorporations like **[[AbadarCorp]]**, which often prioritize quarterly profit margins above all else, Ulrikka Clanholdings is guided by the sacred dwarven tenet of **Honor in the Stone**:
* Every corporate action is weighed against the clan's multi-century reputation. Contracts signed by Ulrikka executives are ironclad, and their word is considered as durable as reinforced adamantine.
* While they are ruthless in defending their legal asteroid claims against rival claim-jumpers and pirates, they treat their employees, contractors, and miners with fair wages, top-tier hazardous environmental gear, and unyielding clan loyalty.

### Key Figures
* **Belenze Shadraz:** Executive Vice President of Asteroidal Acquisition. A sharp, brilliant dwarven strategist who manages corporate contracts, hiring independent contractor teams for high-risk, sensitive missions in the belt.
* **Rabech Shadraz:** Belenze’s husband and Chief Voidmaster. A grizzled veteran miner with decades of experience navigating the deadly debris currents of the Diaspora, possessing an encyclopedic knowledge of asteroid geology.
* **Whatzon Whatz-Gurz:** A meticulously dressed, fiercely intelligent orc attorney-at-law who serves as Ulrikka’s lead legal counsel, ensuring all corporate salvage claims withstand the scrutiny of the [[Pact Council]].

## The Mysterious Beacon: The Call to Adventure

> [!important] The Diaspora Expedition (*Guilt of the Grave World*)
> In recent days, Ulrikka seismic listening posts at **[[Star Citadel Theodrane]]** detected an inexplicable, rhythmic technomagical power surge emanating from a hollowed asteroid tumbling through an uncharted sector of **[[The Diaspora]]**.
> 
> Recognizing that the signal's energy frequencies resemble long-dormant pre-Gap engineering rather than natural tectonic shifts, the leadership of Ulrikka Clanholdings has mobilized discreet corporate resources from their suite in Bluerise Tower. 
> 
> Seeking capable, unaligned freelance contractors to bypass corporate bureaucracy and claim-jumpers, they have dispatched recruitment inquiries across [[Absalom Station]] to assemble a specialist team to board the drifting rock and unlock the secrets of **[[Iovan Station Alpha]]**.
