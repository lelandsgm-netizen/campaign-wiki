#### Overview: The Keepers of the Cosmic Record

The **Dataphiles** are the Starfinder Society's premier intelligence network, digital archivists, and cybernetic infiltration specialists. In a galaxy fractured by **[[The Gap]]**—where an entire millennium of recorded galactic history was physically and digitally erased from existence—the Dataphiles consider uncorrupted data to be the most precious resource in the universe. To a Dataphile, flesh is frail and steel decays, but information encoded in immutable silicon or quantum crystalline drives can endure until the heat death of the cosmos.

Operating from isolated, liquid-helium-cooled server sanctums deep within **[[The Lorespire Complex]]**, the Dataphiles intercept starship sensor telemetry, decrypt forgotten alien databanks, slice corporate firewalls, and monitor the vast infosphere relays running through **[[Absalom Station]]**. Whether an archaeological team returns from **The Vast** with a broken data-pad or an encrypted crystalline storage lattice, it is the Dataphiles who scrape away the digital corrosion and rebuild the lost truth.

#### Leadership & Organization: The Logic of Historia-7

##### Historia-7 and the Neural Hive
The architect and guiding intellect of the faction is **[[Historia-7]]**, a pale, silver-haired [[Android]] technomancer of cold precision. In order to process the unimaginable tidal wave of data streaming into the Society from across the cosmos, Historia-7 underwent radical cybernetic surgery, excising non-essential emotional subroutines in favor of expanded analytical coprocessors. Speaking in measured, emotionless cadences, Historia-7 views personal identity as fluid and subservient to the preservation of truth.

The internal structure of the Dataphiles operates in tight, compartmentalized cells:
1. **The Slicers & Infiltrators:** Covert hackers who breach rival research networks, intercept [[Corpse Fleet]] transmissions, and liberate proprietary planetary surveys from unscrupulous megacorporations.
2. **The Deep Archivists:** Digital preservationists who work alongside the digital entity **[[Guidance]]** in the central core of [[The Lorespire Complex]], transcribing alien languages into standardized Pact databases.
3. **The Electronic Countermeasure Teams:** Defensive cyber-warfare specialists who protect the Society's confidential research from counter-attacks by rival factions, the Aspis Consortium, and predatory infosphere viruses.

#### Headquarters & Presence on Absalom Station

The physical footprint of the Dataphiles reflects their obsession with computational power and secure infrastructure:

- **The Sub-Spire Vaults:** Located far beneath the public galleries of **[[The Lorespire Complex]]** in **[[The Ring]]**, where rows of crystalline server obelisks hum inside sub-zero containment fields. Only vetted Dataphiles bearing cryptographic key-implants are granted physical access.
- **The Eye Relay Shrines:** Dataphile monitoring suites situated within the pinnacle comms spires of **[[The Eye]]**, skimming ambient subspace transmissions as ships arrive via the Drift.
- **The Infosphere Trenches:** Secret safehouses and proxy server banks hidden across **[[Downlow]]** and the upper levels of **[[The Spike]]**, providing untraceable network bridges into the wider station infosphere.

#### Inter-Faction Dynamics

- **[[The Starfinder Society]] Core:** The Dataphiles are considered indispensable by **[[Luwazi Elsebo]]** and the Forum; without their digital cartography and intelligence briefings, deep-space expeditions would fly blind into disaster.
- **[[Acquisitives]]:** A relationship characterized by constant friction. Dataphiles fiercely resent when Acquisitives attempt to patent alien tech or sell proprietary data caches for personal profit.
- **[[Exo-Guardians]]:** Dataphiles provide real-time battlefield telemetry, weak-point scans, and electronic warfare support to Exo-Guardian strike teams, making them formidable tactical partners.
- **[[Wayfinders]]:** Close collaborators. Wayfinders supply the raw sensor logs and star-charts from the uncharted edges of the galaxy, which Dataphiles catalog and map.

#### GM Plot Hooks & Operational Scenarios

1. **The Ghost In The Mainframe:** A rogue AI fragment recovered from a derelict pre-Gap orbital station begins rewriting the historical archives in [[The Lorespire Complex]]. Historia-7 tasks the PCs with executing a digital dive into the core server banks to excise the corrupted entity before it erases the records.
2. **The Black-Site Infiltration:** The Dataphiles discover that an unscrupulous division of [[AbadarCorp]] is illegally hoarding a recovered alien star-chart showing an intact precursor world. The PCs are recruited to execute a covert data heist on an orbital satellite in [[The Armada]].
3. **The Glitch Transmission:** An anonymous packet of encrypted telemetry arrives through the station’s comms arrays, originating from the edge of [[Aucturn]]. Decrypting the file reveals an active distress beacon from a Starfinder exploratory vessel listed as missing for two centuries.
