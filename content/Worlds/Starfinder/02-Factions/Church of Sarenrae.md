#### Overview: The Sun's Mercy in the Void

Among the ancient religions of pre-Gap antiquity that survived the historical blackout of **[[The Gap]]**, few burn with the widespread reverence and diplomatic authority of the **Church of Sarenrae**. Dedicated to the radiant goddess of healing, redemption, the sun, and cleansing honesty, the church is one of the moral anchors of the modern [[Pact Worlds]].

The physical and spiritual center of the faith is not situated on a planet or station, but within the blazing heart of the star **[[Mataras]]**. From the breathtaking, gold-leafed spires of the *Radiant Cathedral* in **[[Dawnshore]]**—the capital bubble-city of **[[The Burning Archipelago]]**—the High Curate oversees a vast network of solar hospitals, charitable clinics, redemption sanctuaries, and military knightly chapters spanning from the core worlds to the furthest edges of The Vast.

To spacers traversing the cold dark, a starship bearing the soaring gold-and-bronze angel-wings of Sarenrae’s holy crest represents salvation: free medical aid, fair arbitration, and fierce protection against void-corsairs and undead horrors.

#### Core Tenets & Space-Age Liturgy

The theology of Sarenrae in the Starfinder era translates her ancient Golarion tenets into the realities of faster-than-light spaceflight:

##### 1. The Light of Creation
The church teaches that every star in the universe is a physical manifestation of Sarenrae’s loving warmth, while [[Mataras]] is her personal crown within the Pact Worlds. Solar energy is regarded as a sacred, purifying force:
* Sarenite starships often feature large solar collectors that power both propulsion engines and high-intensity therapeutic healing lamps.
* Sarenite technomancers specialize in photomorphic energy shields, plasma-cauterization medical lasers, and searing solar magic.

##### 2. The Doctrine of Redemption (The Offered Hand)
In keeping with the Dawnflower’s compassionate heart, the church holds that no mortal soul is inherently beyond saving:
* **The Sanctuary Right:** Any being—be it an outlaw from [[Broken Rock]], an indebted corporate wage-slave, or a repentant pirate—who enters a Sarenite temple seeking genuine redemption is granted unconditional legal sanctuary while their case is arbitrated.
* **The Second Chance:** The church funds prisoner rehabilitation facilities on [[Verces]] and [[Akiton]], offering vocational training in engineering and agriculture to those seeking to escape syndicates and crime rings.

##### 3. The Cleansing Scimitar (The Iron Response)
While Sarenrae preaches mercy, her patience is not infinite. When faced with entities that revel in cruelty, enslave sentient minds, or propagate the unholy abomination of undeath, the church strikes with terrifying, righteous fury:
* **Anti-Undead Crusade:** The church maintains an unyielding theological and military war against the **[[Corpse Fleet]]** and rogue necromancers from **[[Eox]]**. Sarenite paladins will burn an undead dreadnought to cinders without hesitation.
* **Slavery Abolition:** Sarenites are staunch opponents of chattel slavery, actively funding underground railroads in **[[Corona]]** to liberate captives smuggled by the **[[Efreet]]** from the [[Plane of Fire]].

#### The Dawnguard: Champions of the Flame

The martial wing of the Church is the **Dawnguard**, an elite knightly order of paladins, solarians, combat medics, and void-fighter pilots:
* **Coronal Peacekeeping:** Inside [[The Burning Archipelago]], the Dawnguard serves as the primary municipal police force, patrolling linecrawlers, safeguarding the energy tethers, and neutralizing deep-sun threats emerging from the solar mantle.
* **The Sun-Cutters:** The Dawnguard operates a specialized fleet of armored, gold-plated patrol corvettes called *Sun-Cutters*. Engineered to survive solar temperatures exceeding $15,000^\circ\text{C}$, these vessels escort civilian pilgrim transports through the Solar Transit Corridor and intercept hostile incursions from The Crucible.
* **Armament:** Dawnguard paladins are famous for wielding *solar scimitars*—curved phase-swords integrated with plasma conduits that ignite into blinding arcs of coherent solar flame when drawn.

#### Inter-Faction Dynamics

- **[[The Stewards]]:** A rock-solid, harmonious partnership. The Stewards respect the Dawnguard's discipline and ethical integrity, frequently cross-deputizing Sarenite paladins as joint peacekeepers during interplanetary disputes.
- **[[Xenowardens]]:** Strong spiritual alliance. Both faiths share a profound reverence for natural life and the preservation of biospheres, collaborating closely in the agricultural gardens of **[[Verdeon]]**.
- **[[Bone Sages]] of [[Eox]]:** Deep, irreconcilable ideological tension. While the Eoxian government is legally protected under the Absalom Pact, Sarenite clergy openly preach that unlife is an unholy curse, monitoring Eoxian vessels for illegal necromantic bio-weaponry.
- **[[AbadarCorp]]:** Practical cooperation paired with philosophical friction. While the church relies on AbadarCorp for banking and supply shipping, Sarenite curates frequently criticize corporate loan-sharking and wage-slavery as antithetical to mortal dignity.

#### Campaign Hooks

* **The Stolen Sun-Scimitar:** The ceremonial scimitar of Sister Novaheart—the historic founder of the Burning Archipelago—is stolen from the reliquary of the Radiant Cathedral. The High Curate hires the PCs to track the thieves to the lawless markets of [[Corona]] before the sacred relic is sold to an extraplanar collector.
* **Redemption of a Corsair:** A notorious [[Free Captains]] raider docks at [[Dawnshore]], surrendering his weapons and claiming the Right of Sanctuary after refusing an order to slaughter an unarmed colony transport. With pirate bounty hunters encircling the cathedral, the PCs are tasked by the Dawnguard with escorting the repentant pirate to a secure safehouse on [[Absalom Station]].
* **The Corpse Fleet Incursion:** An unholy scout corvette belonging to the [[Corpse Fleet]] slips into the solar corona, deploying experimental gravimetric mines intended to sever the energy tethers between Dawnshore and [[Verdeon]]. The party must join a Dawnguard sun-cutter squadron to intercept and neutralize the death-ship.
