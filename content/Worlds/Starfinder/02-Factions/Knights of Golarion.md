#### Overview: The Sunburst Across the Void

Among the myriad fleets and factions crisscrossing the **[[Pact Worlds]]**, few inspire as much awe, comfort, or terror as the **Knights of Golarion**. Flying within magnificent, stained-glass-adorned cathedral-ships whose hulls are emblazoned with the blazing golden sunburst and longsword of **[[Iomedae]]**, the Knights represent the living embodiment of righteous chivalry, unyielding martial honor, and cosmic justice.

Where **[[The Stewards]]** are bound by legal treaties, political compromises, and planetary sovereignty boundaries, the Knights of Golarion answer to a higher divine mandate. They are holy crusaders who took up the sword in the dark centuries following **[[The Gap]]**, swearing an eternal oath that even if their ancestral cradle-world of Golarion had vanished into the unknown, its noble spirit, moral laws, and heroic virtues would never perish from the stars.

Operating as an independent military ally of the **[[Pact Council]]**, the Knights patrol the dangerous fringes of the [[Mataras System]], hunting down pirate corsairs in **[[The Diaspora]]**, defending outer rim colonies from alien marauders, and waging an unceasing, zero-tolerance holy war against the undead warships of the **[[Corpse Fleet]]**.

#### Historical Foundations: The Lost World's Living Shield

The order’s origins are inextricably bound to the tragedy of the **Gap** and the divine ascension of their patron goddess:

```

|                           [[Absalom Station]] adrift in the void.           |
| [ORDER RECONSTITUTED]===> Paladins unite under the sword of the Inheritor to|
|                           defend survivors and honor their lost cradle.     |
| [VESK & SWARM WARS]  ===> Holy cathedral-ships break enemy front lines.     |
| [THE ETERNAL CRUSADE]===> Present: Vanguard against the [[Corpse Fleet]].   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Displaced Champions
When mortal memory returned at the end of the Gap, the faithful of Iomedae found themselves stranded upon **[[Absalom Station]]** and scattered orbital vessels, looking down upon an empty orbital coordinate where the green continents and blue oceans of Golarion ought to have rested. In their grief, rather than succumbing to despair, the surviving paladins, priests, and holy knights gathered beneath the radiant light of the Starstone.

They proclaimed that Golarion was not dead, but merely hidden by the gods, and that the greatest monument they could erect to their lost mother-world was to become its righteous shield across the cosmos. Reorganizing the ancient chivalric traditions into a modern void-faring military brotherhood, they founded the **Knights of Golarion**.

##### 2. The Crucible of the Vesk & Swarm Incursions
During the bloody border wars with the **[[Veskarium]]** in the late 280s AG, the Knights deployed their heavily armored battlecruisers to protect civilian evacuation convoys, fighting to the last breath against overwhelming reptilian dreadnoughts. Later, during the apocalyptic invasion of the extragalactic Swarm in 319 AG, the Knights stood side-by-side with Vesk warriors, casting divine radiant auras that seared through chitinous hive swarms and earning the eternal respect of even their bitterest former foes.

##### 1. The Grand Marshal
The supreme military and spiritual leader of the order is the **Grand Marshal**, chosen by the High Council of Justiciars for unblemished righteousness, strategic mastery, and martial prowess. The current Grand Marshal is **Helena Sagesparrow**, a veteran human paladin celebrated for her iron composure, compassionate defense of refugee populations, and fearless naval engagements against Corpse Fleet dreadnoughts.

##### 2. Ranks and Specializations
* **Knight-Commanders:** Experienced starship captains who command the crusader frigates, battleships, and mobile repair tenders of the Radiant Armada.
* **Knight-Justiciars:** Master duelsmen and void warriors clad in blessed powered armor, trained to breach hostile starships and challenge enemy warlords in single combat.
* **Knight-Inquisitors:** Somber detectives and occult specialists tasked with identifying internal corruption, tracking hidden cults of the **[[Elder Mythos]]** across civilized stations, and ensuring demonic or necrotic contamination does not compromise the fleet.
* **Knights-Errant:** Newly anointed knights dispatched across the stars on independent quests to right wrongs, protect isolated settlements, and bring honor to the Inheritor.

##### 1. The Flagship: *The Inheritor's Wrath*
The spiritual and military beating heart of the order is ***The Inheritor's Wrath***, a colossal three-mile-long battle-station and dreadnought. Its interior features vaulted stone halls, crystalline rose windows that function as optical sensor arrays, monastic barracks, and a soaring grand basilica where thousands of knights kneel in prayer before embarking on war patrols.

##### 2. Naval Tactical Doctrine
Unlike the containment and non-lethal suppression tactics favored by **[[The Stewards]]**, the Knights engage cosmic evil with overwhelming holy fury:
* **Spinal Solar Lances:** Starship-grade energy cannons that focus concentrated ionizing radiation infused with positive divine energy, specifically tuned to incinerate undead hulls and necrotic flesh.
* **Adamantine Ramming Prows:** Reinforcing forward shields and accelerating into enemy flagships to deploy teleportation shock-troops directly onto hostile bridges.

#### Sociopolitical Friction: The Eoxian Stumbling Block

While the Knights of Golarion are revered as heroic protectors across most civilized worlds, their rigid moral compass frequently creates sharp political crises within the **[[Pact Council]]**:

##### 1. The Eoxian Disgust
The single greatest source of bitter resentment for the Knights is the **[[Absalom Pact]]**'s formal inclusion of the undead world of **[[Eox]]**. To a paladin of Iomedae, undead existence is an unnatural abomination that violates cosmic order and mocks the gift of life.
* While the Knights reluctantly honor the treaty by not staging unprovoked invasions of sovereign Eoxian airspace, they openly despise the [[Bone Sages]].
* They maintain a permanent battle group patrolling the shipping lanes near Eox, aggressively intercepting any undead vessels that stray even a kilometer outside authorized trade corridors.

##### 2. The Corpse Fleet Blood Feud
Because the official government of Eox legally banished the **[[Corpse Fleet]]** following the signing of the Pact, the Knights possess full legal and moral freedom to hunt these rogue undead warlords without restraint. Skirmishes between the Radiant Armada and Corpse Fleet bone-cruisers across the dark expanse of [[The Diaspora]] are ruthless, quarterless engagements fought until complete destruction.

##### 3. Independent Moral Enforcement
Because the Knights are a religious order rather than a government agency, they answer to no mortal legislature. If an open-and-shut crime or demonic incursion occurs in international space, the Knights will not wait for bureaucratic extradition papers from the Directorate—they will strike immediately, executing divine judgment and creating thorny diplomatic headaches for the diplomats in the Plenara.

#### Key Bastions & Holy Sanctuaries

* **The Citadel of Light ([[The Ring]], [[Absalom Station]]):** A radiant white marble fortress overlooking the boulevards of the Ring. It serves as the order's primary diplomatic embassy, recruitment hall, and wounded veterans' sanctuary.
* **The Commandery of the Sunburst ([[Castrovel]]):** An expansive orbital space station and terrestrial training ground in southern Asana, where knights practice extreme-condition jungle survival and mounted combat atop airborne beasts.
* **Sanctuary of the Blazing Sword ([[The Diaspora]]):** A hollowed-out, holy-water-pressurized asteroid bastion hidden within the pirate belt, serving as an advanced listening post and strike base against corsairs and cultists.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Stolen Relic of the Inheritor:** A pre-Gap holy avenger—the *Blade of the First Light*, reputedly carried by an ancient champion of Golarion—is unearthed from an uncharted asteroid ruin by unscrupulous **Aspis Consortium** scavengers. The Knights hire the player characters as independent field agents to infiltrate an exclusive, high-security black-market auction on [[Absalom Station]] and secure the relic before it is bought by an Eoxian necromancer seeking to defile it.
* **Ambush at the Diaspora Veil:** While escorting an unarmed civilian convoy of sun-worshiper pilgrims traveling toward [[Mataras]], the PCs’ ship is ambushed by a stealth wolfpack of [[Corpse Fleet]] necro-cutters. Outgunned and suffering hull breaches, the party must fight a desperate delaying action until a squadron of Knight-Justiciars aboard a *Crusader*-class battlecruiser can drop out of the Drift to turn the tide.
* **The Zealot's Blockade:** A fanatical Knight-Inquisitor, convinced that a remote mining outpost on an outer asteroid has been completely compromised by a mind-warping cult of [[Aucturn]], establishes an unauthorized military blockade, threatening to vaporize any ship attempting to flee. The desperate, starving colonists hire the PCs to slip past the Knights' sensor cordon, land on the asteroid, uncover the truth of the cult's presence, and defuse the explosive standoff before holy orbital bombardment begins.
