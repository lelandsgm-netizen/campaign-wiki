#### Overview: Loyalists of the Grand Mandate

Unlike the other internal factions of the **[[Starfinder Society]]**, which pursue perpetual institutional goals such as profit, data hoarding, or martial readiness, the **Second Seekers** are defined by an evolving, personal covenant. They exist to realize the specific, grand vision of whoever holds the elected office of **First Seeker**.

Every First Seeker is chosen by the composite digital intelligence **[[Guidance]]** and ratified by the Forum based on an ambitious operational platform—a single driving goal that will define their tenure. The Second Seekers serve as the field marshals, primary investigators, and personal troubleshooters dedicated to seeing that mandate carried to completion. When a First Seeker’s term ends, their specific chapter of Second Seekers passes into history as an honored alumni fellowship, while a new generation of Second Seekers rallies around their successor.

#### The Era of Luwazi Elsebo: Rebirth from the Ashes

##### The Current Mandate
In the modern era, the Second Seekers are galvanized under the banner of **[[Luwazi Elsebo]]**, the visionary human executive who guided the Society through its darkest hour. Following the catastrophic **Scoured Stars Incident**—where prior First Seeker Jadnura led roughly eighty percent of the Society’s fleet into an ancient alien trap behind an impenetrable energy shield—the Starfinder Society was on the brink of complete institutional collapse.

Luwazi Elsebo stepped into the vacuum, establishing a clear and urgent mandate:
1. **Rebuild the Ranks:** Recruit, train, and deploy an entirely new generation of field agents to restore operational capacity across the [[Pact Worlds]].
2. **Restore Public Trust:** Re-establish shattered credibility with the [[Pact Council]] and counter the opportunistic predatory maneuvers of the Aspis Consortium.
3. **The Scoured Stars Salvation:** Unravel the ancient precursor technology of the shield, breach the barrier, and mount a desperate rescue mission to retrieve Jadnura and the thousands of lost Starfinders.

Starfinders wearing the badge of Second Seeker (Luwazi Elsebo) are direct agents of this revival. They investigate anomalies linked to the Scoured Stars, hunt down precursor keys, and secure the vital political and economic alliances necessary to launch the greatest rescue fleet in Pact history.

#### Historical Chapters of Note

Over the centuries since **[[The Gap]]**, the Second Seekers have taken many forms:

- **Second Seekers (Jadnura):** The followers of the previous Kasatha First Seeker, who focused on the enigmatic *Tear* artifact recovered in the Drift and launched the ill-fated Scoured Stars expedition.
- **Second Seekers (Roheste):** Dedicated to the tenure of First Seeker Roheste decades prior, whose historical expeditions mapped vast expanses of the Diaspora and established several permanent Deep Space Lodges.
- **Second Seekers (Ehu Hadif):** Emerging in the aftermath of the Scoured Stars rescue, following the contemplative Kasatha traditionalist Ehu Hadif, who champions institutional healing, ethics, and decentralization.

#### Headquarters & Presence on Absalom Station

The Second Seekers occupy the corridors of power within the Starfinder hierarchy:

- **The First Seeker’s Sanctum (Lorespire Complex):** The executive offices and planning chambers located on the upper levels of **[[The Lorespire Complex]]** in **[[The Ring]]**, where high-priority missions are briefed directly to trusted agents.
- **The Archives of Precedent:** Restricted historical cloisters where records of previous First Seekers' expeditions are analyzed for clues to solve current interstellar crises.
- **The Forum Gallery:** Seats within the governing assembly of the Society, where Second Seeker representatives champion the First Seeker's policy proposals.

#### Inter-Faction Dynamics

- **[[The Starfinder Society]] Leadership:** As the direct embodiment of the First Seeker’s will, Second Seekers work in seamless coordination with Luwazi Elsebo and the ruling Forum.
- **[[Wayfinders]]:** Shared passion for the frontier, though Second Seekers often redirect Wayfinder scouts toward worlds bearing clues relevant to the active mandate.
- **[[Exo-Guardians]]:** Indispensable allies. Luwazi Elsebo's plan to breach the Scoured Stars relied heavily on Exo-Guardian shock-troops, and the two factions share deep mutual respect.
- **[[Acquisitives]]:** Pragmatic business ties. While Second Seekers occasionally sigh at Radaszam's commercial opportunism, Luwazi Elsebo relies on Acquisitive capital to finance her rebuilding efforts.

#### GM Plot Hooks & Operational Scenarios

1. **The Precursor Cipher:** Luwazi Elsebo summons the PCs to a private briefing in [[The Lorespire Complex]]. A newly recovered precursor tablet holds the harmonic frequency needed to weaken a section of the Scoured Stars barrier, but an elite squad of mercenary saboteurs is hunting for the tablet’s twin half.
2. **Political Inquest at The Plenara:** A hostile delegate in **[[The Plenara]]** accuses the Starfinder Society of concealing dangerous void-pathogens brought back from the frontier. The PCs are assigned to represent the First Seeker, investigating the origin of the false rumor and exposing the conspiracy in the Senate.
3. **Ghost of a Past First Seeker:** A sealed vault belonging to a pre-Gap First Seeker suddenly unlocks in the lower catacombs of the Lorespire, broadcasting an encrypted challenge intended only for true Second Seekers capable of passing an ancient trial of intellect and courage.
