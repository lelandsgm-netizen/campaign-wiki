#### Overview: The Conscience of Exploration

The **Advocates** represent the moral anchor and humanitarian vanguard of the **[[Starfinder Society]]**. While the thrill of discovering ancient alien ruins or amassing vast wealth drives many adventurers, the Advocates ask the hard questions that others overlook: *What gives the Society the right to take relics from their native resting places? What happens to the vulnerable inhabitants of a frontier world when corporate mega-freighters arrive in the wake of Starfinder star-charts?*

Formed in response to centuries of imperialistic excess and reckless tomb-raiding, the Advocates fight to ensure that the Society operates as a force for universal good rather than an engine of exploitation. They champion strict codes of archaeological ethics, advocate for the sovereignty and self-determination of newly discovered civilizations, provide free medical and disaster relief to marginalized communities on **[[Absalom Station]]**, and ensure that the voice of the powerless is heard in the highest halls of **[[The Plenara]]**.

#### Organization & Grassroots Leadership

##### The Mediation Council
Unlike factions dominated by a single charismatic commander, the Advocates operate through an egalitarian, consultative council of senior anthropologists, field medics, philosophers, and community organizers. Among their most respected voices is **Ambassador Royo**, a soft-spoken Kasatha diplomat renowned for negotiating non-violent settlements between planetary mining syndicates and indigenous populations.

The faction organizes its field operations into three major branches:
1. **The Cultural Preservation Division:** Oversees the ethical review of artifacts brought into [[The Lorespire Complex]]. If an item is determined to be sacred or stolen from living cultures, the Advocates organize its respectful repatriation.
2. **The Community Relief Outreach:** Operates free medical clinics, nutrient distribution kitchens, and legal aid clinics in the neglected under-decks of **[[Downlow]]** and **[[Fogtown]]**, ensuring that Absalom Station's poorest residents benefit from the Society’s resources.
3. **The First Contact Ombudsman Office:** Deploys trained observers alongside [[Wayfinders]] expeditions to monitor the sociological impact of first-contact scenarios, preventing accidental contamination or predatory trade agreements.

#### Headquarters & Base of Operations on Absalom Station

The Advocates choose to maintain their presence where the need is greatest:

- **The Harmony Pavilions (Lorespire Complex):** Open-air courtyards surrounded by calming gardens in [[The Ring]], designed for neutral-ground dispute resolution, cultural dialogue, and philosophical debates.
- **The Lowside Relief Dispensaries:** Permanent community centers situated in **[[Downlow]]** and **[[Fogtown]]**, providing emergency medical care, clean water filtration, and refugee shelter to station underclasses.
- **Liaison Offices near The Plenara:** A modest suite of offices located near **[[The Plenara]]**, where Advocate lobbyists pressure the Pact Council to pass protective legislation for frontier worlds.

#### Inter-Faction Dynamics

- **[[The Starfinder Society]] Leadership:** First Seeker **[[Luwazi Elsebo]]** frequently calls upon the Advocates to smooth over diplomatic crises and repair the Society's public image when rogue agents cause scandals.
- **[[Acquisitives]]:** Continuous and vehement ideological conflict. The Advocates view Radaszam’s commercialization as a stain on the Society’s honor, while Acquisitives dismiss the Advocates as naive bleeding-hearts.
- **[[Wayfinders]]:** A collaborative bond. Advocates accompany Wayfinder expeditions to ensure ethical first-contact protocols, earning deep mutual respect.
- **[[Exo-Guardians]]:** Complementary protectors. While Exo-Guardians wield physical weapons, Advocates wield diplomacy and medical supplies; together, they protect both the lives and the dignity of those caught in galactic conflicts.

#### GM Plot Hooks & Operational Scenarios

1. **The Repatriation Heist:** An unscrupulous collector in [[The Eye]] is auctioning an irreplaceable funerary crown stolen from a pre-Gap tomb on an isolated world. The Advocates hire the PCs to legally—or covertly—recover the crown so it can be returned to its ancestral temple.
2. **Plague in Fogtown:** An unknown alien spore accidentally brought back on a cargo shuttle from The Vast breaks out in the dense industrial alleys of **[[Fogtown]]**. The PCs must work with Advocate medics to contain the outbreak and track down the patient zero before station authorities impose a lethal purge quarantine.
3. **The Frontier Treaty Crisis:** A predatory resource-extraction conglomerate plans to strip-mine an inhabited moon in The Vast, claiming the local inhabitants are non-sentient animals. The Advocates task the PCs with traveling to the moon, establishing conclusive proof of the species’ sapience, and rushing the evidence back to [[The Plenara]] before the orbital bombardment begins.
