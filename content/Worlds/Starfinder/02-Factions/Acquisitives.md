#### Overview: The Business of Discovery

The **Acquisitives** represent the pragmatic, media-savvy, and financially aggressive arm of the **[[Starfinder Society]]**. While traditionalists in the Society argue that archaeological expeditions should be pure, untainted academic endeavors, the Acquisitives operate on an incontrovertible truth of interstellar logistics: *ships do not fly on curiosity alone.* Fueling colony starships, retrofitting Drift engines, and provisioning deep-space expeditions across **The Vast** demands staggering sums of credits.

Led by the charismatic, gold-crested Vesk operative **[[Radaszam]]**, the Acquisitives view every Society agent as an elite brand and every unmapped ruin as an investment opportunity. They secure lucrative corporate sponsorships, broker exclusive rights for infosphere streaming documentaries, negotiate arms leases with [[AbadarCorp]], and leverage the prestige of the Starfinder badge to amass personal fortunes and organizational independence.

To an Acquisitive, true success is surviving the horrors of an alien vault, retrieving the ancient relic, and walking away with a holo-deal, a custom plasma rifle, and enough credits to buy a private villa on [[Castrovel]].

#### Leadership & Organization: The Dealmaker's Desk

##### Radaszam the Dealmaker
The public face and undisputed architect of the Acquisitives is **[[Radaszam]]**, a sharp-dressed [[Vesk]] with gold-leaf inlays across his crest scales. Unusually suave for his martial species, Radaszam maintains dual allegiance as a high-ranking manager within the **Obsidian Consortium**—a powerful interstellar private military contractor and security conglomerate. His unique position allows him to shuttle high-paying mercenary contracts and cutting-edge military surplus directly to Starfinder agents who align with his faction.

Under Radaszam’s leadership, the faction operates with corporate efficiency:
1. **The Sponsorship Bureau:** Arranges gear-endorsement deals for field agents. High-tier Starfinders often wear custom armor plastered with the logos of weapon manufacturers, biotech labs, and luxury synth-ration brands.
2. **The Media Production Wing:** Deploys embedded camera-drones on high-risk expeditions to broadcast thrilling, edited combat and exploration footage across Pact Worlds infospheres.
3. **The Salvage & Liquidation Office:** Appraises, cleans, and markets recovered non-historical alien artifacts to private collectors, universities, and corporate research labs on [[Absalom Station]].

#### Headquarters & Presence on Absalom Station

While the Acquisitives maintain their primary administration offices within the gleaming ivory spires of **[[The Lorespire Complex]]** in **[[The Ring]]**, their agents are rarely desk-bound. They are prominent figures across [[Absalom Station]]:

- **Executive Lounge (Lorespire Complex):** A luxurious penthouse suite outfitted with plush leather seating, chilled champagne dispensers, and secure comms links to the galactic stock exchanges.
- **The Arms Concourses:** Faction fixers maintain permanent booths in the private starship hangars of **[[The Arms]]**, meeting incoming explorer vessels at customs to broker immediate cargo sales before port fees accumulate.
- **The Trade Floors of The Freemarket:** Acquisitive appraisers operate out of **[[The Freemarket]]**, bidding against independent brokers to corner the market on strange alien materials and relics brought back from the frontier.

#### Faction Benefits & Agent Capabilities

Starfinders who pledge allegiance to the Acquisitives gain access to institutional perks that prioritize survival through superior equipment and financial leverage:

| Operational Specialty | Mechanical & Narrative Advantage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Corporate Sponsorship** | Discounted procurement on weapons, armor upgrades, and starship components through AbadarCorp and Obsidian Consortium accounts. |
| **Media Celebrity** | Agents cultivate public fame; holovid exposure grants social advantages and easier access to high-society galas, corporate elites, and Pact officials. |
| **Hazardous Salvage Rights** | Legal contracts ensuring agents retain personal ownership and resale cuts of salvage discovered during Society expeditions. |
| **Mercenary Reinforcements** | Direct emergency lines to hire vetted security contractors and firepower when planetary explorations turn hostile. |

#### Inter-Faction Dynamics

- **[[The Starfinder Society]] Core:** The broader Society leadership appreciates the Acquisitives' cash infusions, especially during the rebuilding following the Scoured Stars disaster, but continually polices them to prevent reckless plundering.
- **[[Dataphiles]]:** Cold friction exists between the two. The Dataphiles believe all knowledge should be freely indexed and encrypted for the archives, whereas the Acquisitives seek to patent and monetize actionable discoveries.
- **[[Exo-Guardians]]:** A symbiotic partnership. The Exo-Guardians provide the sheer combat prowess to secure lethal ruins, while the Acquisitives provide the heavy artillery, armored plating, and medical insurance to keep them alive.
- **[[Advocates]]:** Heated ideological adversaries. The Advocates accuse the Acquisitives of turning archaeological exploration into a vulgar commercial circus, while the Acquisitives counter that idealism without bank accounts results in dead Starfinders.

#### GM Plot Hooks & Operational Scenarios

1. **The Holo-Contract:** An Acquisitive producer hires the PCs for an expedition to an uncharted asteroid, but with a strict condition: every member must wear sensory-recorder feeds, and the contract includes bonus payouts for dramatic, near-death combat sequences.
2. **The Stolen Relic Dispute:** A wealthy collector on [[Absalom Station]] claims an ancient pre-Gap artifact recovered by the PCs was promised under an exclusive forward contract arranged by Radaszam, while an indigenous species delegation from the frontier demands its repatriation.
3. **Corporate Espionage in The Arms:** The PCs are tasked with guarding an ultra-rare bio-sample retrieved from the Vast during its handover in [[The Arms]], but rival operatives from the Aspis Consortium ambush the docking bay to steal the prize before the Acquisitives can register the patent.
