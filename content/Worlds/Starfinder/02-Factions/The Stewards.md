#### Overview: The Shield of the System

The **Stewards** are the primary peacekeepers, federal investigators, and tactical defense military of the **[[Pact Worlds]]**. Instantly recognizable across the system by their tailored white-and-gold void uniforms, polished crest badges, and immaculate starships, the Stewards represent the thin line holding a fragile interplanetary civilization together against lawlessness, foreign conquest, and cosmic horror.

Unlike planetary militias or corporate security corps whose loyalties belong to a single species, CEO, or homeworld, the Stewards swear an inviolable constitutional oath to the **[[Absalom Pact]]** itself. Their mandate is neither conquest nor tyranny, but the preservation of stability, the enforcement of universal treaties, and the guarantee that any sentient traveler may traverse the space lanes without being preyed upon by corsairs, slavers, or warmongering oligarchs.

Headquartered within the immense void citadel known as **[[The Bastion]]** on **[[Absalom Station]]**, the Stewards command a versatile starfleet, forensic detective bureaus, and elite special-operations tactical units capable of deploying anywhere in the [[Mataras System]] within hours via Drift travel.

The lineage of the Stewards stretches back into the shadowy centuries before **[[The Gap]]**, born not from interplanetary ambition, but from the pragmatic necessity of planetary survival.

##### 1. The Stewards of the Ring (Verces)
On the tidally locked world of **[[Verces]]**, civilization flourishes along the narrow, temperate twilight band known as **[[The Ring of Nations]]**. For centuries, twenty-seven sovereign nation-states shared this narrow strip of habitable land, perpetually divided by border disputes, resource wars, and economic friction. 

To prevent mutually assured destruction, the Ring nations established the *Stewards of the Ring*—a fiercely neutral, incorruptible peacekeeping constabulary recruited from every nation. Stripped of national flags and granted sweeping cross-border authority, the Stewards protected the transit highways, arbitrated diplomatic summits, and suppressed rogue warlords. Their profound impartiality earned them the universal trust of the Vercite populace.

##### 2. The Absalom Pact (291 AG)
When the expansionist armada of the **[[Veskarium]]** assaulted the outer rim of the Mataras system in the late 280s AG, the scattered worlds realized that uncoordinated planetary militias were doomed to fall one by one. 

When delegates convened on [[Absalom Station]] to sign the **[[Absalom Pact]]** in **291 AG**, the primary stumbling block was military command: neither [[Castrovel]], [[Akiton]], nor [[Eox]] would consent to place their defense in the hands of another world's armed forces. Verces offered the solution: expand the time-tested, neutral framework of the Stewards into a federal interplanetary corps. 

The proposal was ratified unanimously. The Stewards were re-commissioned as the military and law enforcement arm of the newly formed **[[Pact Council]]**, taking up primary residence in [[The Bastion]].

##### 1. The High Command (The Bastion)
Supreme authority over the Stewards rests with the **Director-General**, a veteran commander chosen by the **[[Pact Council]]**'s Directorate. The current Director-General is **Lin Cammar**, a thoughtful, iron-willed human tactician renowned for his uncompromising resistance to corporate lobbying and his deft handling of post-Swarm military balance. 

Under the Director-General serves the **Council of Marshals**, each overseeing a broad theater of operations (e.g., Inner System Command, Rim Patrol, Station Security, and Intelligence).

##### 2. The Constabulary (Field Agents & Investigators)
The civilian face of the organization consists of **Constables** and **Special Agents**:
* **Beat Constables:** Stationed in federal sectors like the Arms and Ring of [[Absalom Station]], as well as treaty ports across the worlds. They maintain public order, manage customs check-in points, and handle civilian crises.
* **Special Agents:** Autonomous, highly trained detectives and investigators tasked with pursuing interplanetary crimes: corporate espionage, cross-border homicides, arms smuggling from [[Apostae]], and trafficking in prohibited necro-weapons.
* **Deputy Marshals:** Trusted troubleshooters deployed to remote frontier stations or backwater mining outposts in [[The Diaspora]], empowered to deputize local citizens during emergencies.

##### 3. Tactical Divisions (Special Operations)
When diplomacy and investigation fail, the Stewards deploy their elite military strike units:
* **The Bastion Marines:** Vacuum-hardened shock troops trained in ship-to-ship boarding actions, orbital drop-pod assaults, and zero-gravity breach-and-clear maneuvers.
* **The Tactical Response Unit (TRU):** Counter-terrorist teams armed with customized suppression shields, hardlight barriers, and heavy precision sniper rifles, routinely deployed against barricaded cultists or hostile strike teams.

##### 4. The Systemic Starfleet
The naval arm of the Stewards operates dozens of starship squadrons patrolling the primary shipping lanes between [[Absalom Station]], [[Castrovel]], [[Verces]], and the outer gas giants. These crews specialize in deep-space interdiction, planetary orbital blockades, and anti-piracy sweeps.

#### Jurisdiction: Federal Mandate vs. Planetary Sovereignty

The greatest challenge facing the Stewards is navigating the razor-thin legal boundary established by the **[[Absalom Pact]]**:

| Operational Theater | Legal Authority | Description & Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Interplanetary Void** | **Absolute** | The Stewards hold complete jurisdiction across the vacuum between planetary orbits. Any starship in open space is subject to search, seizure, and customs inspection. |
| **Federal Enclaves** | **Absolute** | Complete police and military control over [[The Bastion]], the Plenara, federal customs docks, and official Pact embassies. |
| **Universal Crimes** | **Universal** | Unconditional authority to pursue and neutralize interplanetary piracy, planet-killing superweapons, forbidden bioweapons, and genocidal cults. |
| **Sovereign Soil** | **Strictly Limited** | Stewards **cannot** enforce laws or deploy troops upon the soil of a member world without explicit invitation from that world's sovereign government or a declared state of system emergency. |

##### The Frustrations of Sovereignty
This jurisdictional barrier frequently binds the hands of field agents:
* **The Eoxian Wall:** When rogue death-dealers or [[Corpse Fleet]] sympathizers flee into the sovereign space of [[Eox]], Stewards patrol cruisers must halt at the orbital frontier. Unless the [[Bone Sages]] grant formal extradition, the suspects remain out of federal reach.
* **Corporate Sovereignty:** On worlds like [[Akiton]] or [[Triaxus]], powerful conglomerates exploit local legal shields. A corporate mercenary team that commits murder on Absalom Station can often escape prosecution simply by returning to a corporate-owned citadel on Triaxus where local dragon laws protect them.

##### 1. The Ringworks Partnership
Nearly all starships flown by the Stewards are commissioned through **[[Ringworks]]**, the orbital shipyards of [[Verces]]. Built with sleek, aerodyne frames, reinforced ceramic-composite hulls, and gleaming white-and-gold enamel, these vessels are designed to inspire hope in lawful travelers and terror in outlaws:
* ***Peacekeeper*-class Cutters:** Agile, heavily shielded starships equipped with high-yield tractor beams, interdiction pulse cannons, and forward boarding pods designed to disable pirate vessels without venting their hulls.
* ***Vigilance*-class Cruisers:** The workhorses of the fleet, measuring several hundred meters in length. These ships serve as mobile command bases, equipped with medical bays, prisoner holding cells, and fighter bays.

##### 2. The Oath of Escalation
Stewards recruits at the Bastion Academy are drilled rigorously in the **Doctrine of Proportional Response**. Officers carry weapons capable of instant switching between lethal force and non-lethal incapacitation (such as zero-charge stun pulses and sonic disruption). Deadly force is legally permissible only when civilian life or systemic security is under immediate threat.

#### Key Outposts & Strategic Commands

While anchored at [[Absalom Station]], the Stewards maintain critical strategic bases across the system:

* **[[The Bastion]] ([[Absalom Station]]):** A city-sized fortress attached to the outer hull of the Station's Arms. The Bastion features fortified dry docks, cadet training grounds, federal crime laboratories, and high-security containment vaults housing dangerous extraterrestrial artifacts and political prisoners.
* **The Skydock Command ([[Verces]]):** The primary ancestral training academy and repair station for the Stewards' naval armada, connected directly to the planet's surface via the ancient space elevators of [[Verces]].
* **The Diaspora Patrol Base (Asteroid Belt):** A series of hollowed-out, heavily armored asteroids scattered along the edge of [[The Diaspora]], serving as intercept points against the [[Free Captains]] and smuggler routes.
* **The Aucturn Quarantine Flotilla:** A permanent naval cordon maintaining a 24-hour sensor net around the nightmare world of [[Aucturn]], tasked with intercepting deranged cultist pilgrim ships and preventing mutagenic biological contagions from reaching the inner system.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Stolen Ringworks Prototype:** A cutting-edge *Peacekeeper* interdiction frigate undergoes sea trials near [[Verces]], only to be hijacked by deep-cover agents of the [[Corpse Fleet]]. An undercover Stewards marshal contacts the PCs—independent contractors operating outside formal military channels—to board the vessel, bypass its electronic counter-measures, and neutralize the infiltrators before the warship makes a Drift jump into the uncharted void.
* **The Sovereign Deadlock:** A prominent serial assassin sought for murders on [[Castrovel]] and [[Absalom Station]] takes refuge inside an offshore sovereign drilling rig on [[Akiton]], protected by a corrupt regional warlord. Legally barred from launching an armed assault, the Stewards hire the player characters as civilian bounty hunters to infiltrate the rig, abduct the fugitive, and extract them across international borders.
* **Mutiny at the Aucturn Cordon:** Deep-space communications from *The Vigilant Sentinel*, a Stewards cruiser enforcing the blockade of [[Aucturn]], suddenly go dead. When the PCs arrive to investigate, they discover the ship drifting in orbit, its crew driven violently mad by an ancient psychic transmission beaming from the Black Citadel below. The party must restore ship systems and purge the spreading madness before the vessel crashes into the amniotic swamps.
