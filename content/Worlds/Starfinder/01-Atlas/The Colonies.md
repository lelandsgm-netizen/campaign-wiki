#### Overview: The Subterranean Empire

Occupying the rolling southern landmass of **[[Castrovel]]**, **The Colonies** is the sovereign ancestral continent of the insectoid **[[Formians]]**. While the surface features craggy mountain ridges, winding rivers, and grassy savannahs, ninety percent of the continent's population lives underground.

Carved deep into the planetary bedrock lies a colossal, continental-scale labyrinth of interconnected tunnels, fungal bio-reactors, subterranean larval nurseries, and crystalline telepathic relays. From the soaring three-mile-high chitin spire of **[[Queensrock]]**, the **Overqueen** synchronizes the collective consciousness of the Hive Web, directing planetary agriculture, foreign trade, and joint bio-technological research.

Following millennia of brutal warfare against the Lashunta of [[Asana]], The Colonies has transformed into a progressive, trade-forward realm. Former battlefields have been converted into joint research installations and cultural free zones, with thousands of Formian workers collaborating with off-world engineers to build self-repairing starships.
