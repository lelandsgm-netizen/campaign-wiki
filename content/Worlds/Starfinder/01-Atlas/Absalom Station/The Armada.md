#### Astrogation & Orbital Structure: The Unanchored City

Adrift in the silent void surrounding **[[Absalom Station]]** in a loose, shifting orbital cloud is **The Armada**—a sprawling, unanchored flotilla of thousands of independent void-craft. Comprising rugged asteroid freighters from **[[The Diaspora]]**, multi-generation nomad arks, freelance mercenary gunboats, and salvage platforms, the Armada functions as a living, breathing shadow-city orbiting the heart of the [[Pact Worlds]].

The origins of the Armada are rooted in economic pragmatism and fiercely defended spacer independence. By maintaining orbit outside the physical docking berths of **[[The Arms]]**, vessel captains avoid Absalom Station’s heavy municipal docking fees, commercial tariffs, and invasive customs inspections. Over the centuries since **[[The Gap]]**, this floating collective has evolved its own proud maritime culture, legal traditions, and mutual-defense pacts.

#### Macro-Geography & Life in the Flotilla

##### 1. "Rafts" & Tethered Clusters
While many vessels maintain solo orbits, ships frequently moor together into temporary floating agglomerations called "rafts." Using magnetic clamps, carbon-nanotube cables, and pressurized flexible umbilical tunnels, dozens of freighters and passenger shuttles interconnect. Residents walk between hulls in shirtsleeves, visiting neighbors, trading supplies, and patronizing zero-g hydroponic diners. These rafts form and disperse organically based on trade winds, fuel supplies, and clan alliances.

##### 2. The Concourse (The Void Bazaar)
The economic heart of the Armada is **The Concourse**, a semi-permanent cluster of thirty massive, decommissioned bulk freighters tethered together in a ring. Within the pressurized interior holds of the Concourse lies a bustling zero-g trade fair where off-world belters, smugglers, and independent merchants barter goods without municipal oversight or AbadarCorp processing surcharges.

##### 3. *The Master of Stars*
The crown jewel of the Armada is the historic flagship of the **[[Starfinder Society]]**, *The Master of Stars*. A gargantuan, heavily customized pre-Gap colony carrier measuring nearly a mile in length, this legendary vessel serves as a mobile academy, research shipyard, and deep-space staging platform. Anchored within the outer ring of the Armada, it can house thousands of field agents, starships, and archaeological relics recovered from The Vast.

##### 4. The Welder's Cluster
A specialized industrial flotilla of mobile fuel refineries, plasma scrap-rigs, and zero-g welding gantries. Operated largely by [[Ysoki]] grease-monkeys and dwarven void-smiths, the Welder's Cluster offers fast, no-questions-asked starship armor repairs and black-market transponder alterations for vessels damaged in skirmishes across the outer rim.

#### Politics, Sovereignty & Systemic Defense

##### The Captains' Moot & The Armada Council
Civil authority in the Armada is purely consensual. Each registered ship is legally regarded as a sovereign enclave governed by its captain. Major disputes, orbital traffic routing, and collective defense are arbitrated by the **Armada Council**, an elected assembly of veteran commodores and clan leaders who convene in the central dome of the Concourse.

##### The Auxiliary Fleet
While municipal authorities on Absalom Station view the Armada with suspicion—often labeling it a haven for deserters, tax-evaders, and smugglers—the relationship is symbiotic. In times of existential threat, the Armada represents an indispensable military asset. During the apocalyptic invasion of **[[The Swarm War]]**, the improvised gunboats, Q-ships, and armed freighters of the Armada rallied alongside **[[The Stewards]]**, providing a tenacious auxiliary screen that prevented alien hive-pods from reaching the station's hull.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Ghost Raft:** A cluster of five interconnected transport vessels goes dark on communication channels and begins drifting out of designated orbital lanes, on a collision course with Docking Arm 17. The party is scrambled by [[The Stewards]] to board the silent raft in zero gravity, restart the station-keeping thrusters, and discover why the entire crew has undergone violent, spontaneous bio-mutation.
* **The Smuggler's Runaway:** A high-value informant carrying decryption keys to an illicit [[Corpse Fleet]] cache flees into the Concourse. As rival squads of corporate bounty hunters from [[AbadarCorp]] and mercenary squads scour the pressurized tunnels, the PCs must locate the informant and extract them across the void via thruster packs before the raft is raided.
* **Sabotage at the Moot:** The Armada Council gathers aboard *The Master of Stars* to vote on an interplanetary defense treaty with [[The Stewards]]. Moments before the vote, an explosive decompression breach rocks the assembly hall. The party must seal the breach, restore life support, and unmask the saboteur hiding among the assembled captains.
