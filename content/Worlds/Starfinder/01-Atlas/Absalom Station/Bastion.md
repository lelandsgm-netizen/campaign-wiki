#### Astrogation & Structural Overview: The Shield of the Pact Worlds

Anchored directly into the dense, reinforced structural skeleton of **[[The Ring]]**, **Bastion** is the colossal military citadel serving as the galactic headquarters of **[[The Stewards]]**. Forged from layers of matte-gray adamantine alloy and gleaming white ceramic plating accented with gold trim, the fortress stands as the physical embodiment of the rule of law, mutual defense, and systemic order established under the **[[The Absalom Pact]]**.

While the delegates of the **[[Pact Council]]** debate policy in the crystalline heights of **[[The Plenara]]**, Bastion is where that policy is enforced across the void. From within this heavily shielded complex, peacekeeper marshals coordinate interplanetary anti-piracy patrols across **[[The Diaspora]]**, enforce the orbital quarantine blockade around **[[Aucturn]]**, monitor cold war tensions with the **[[Corpse Fleet]]**, and scramble rapid-response starship squadrons to protect member worlds.

#### Macro-Structure & Tactical Facilities

##### 1. The War Room (Systemic Operations Center)
The beating heart of Stewards military intelligence. Located in the deeply recessed interior core of Bastion, the War Room is a vast, sunken amphitheater dominated by a forty-foot holographic display projecting real-time orbital telemetry, Drift transit vectors, and planetary tracking feeds across the fourteen worlds of the [[Mataras System]]. Tiered operator stations staffed by veteran analysts track known pirate corsairs from [[Broken Rock]], monitor seismic anomalies on frontier outposts, and maintain encrypted quantum comms links with Stewards forward garrisons on [[Verces]] and [[Castrovel]].

##### 2. The Ringworks Launch Gantries
Unlike commercial vessels that must navigate the crowded civilian berths of **[[The Arms]]**, Bastion possesses its own dedicated starship bays. Forged directly into the exterior hull of The Ring, these heavy blast gates open directly into the vacuum of space. Using internal magnetic catapults, Stewards patrol cutters, *Vigilance*-class void interceptors, and armored boarding gunboats can scramble into high station orbit in under ninety seconds during a red-alert mobilization.

##### 3. Detention Block 9
Deep within the bedrock-like structural keel of the complex lies **Detention Block 9**—the highest-security penitentiary facility in the Pact Worlds. Equipped with cryogenic stasis pods, dimensional anchors that neutralize teleportation spells, and localized null-magic fields, Block 9 holds high-value prisoners awaiting trial before the Pact Council. Inmates include captured Corpse Fleet naval officers, Azlanti spies, rogue technomancers, and interplanetary syndicate bosses.

##### 4. The Marshalate Tower & The Bureau of Investigation
Rising above the main citadel roof, this fortified spire houses the offices of Director-General **[[Lin Cammar]]** and the Chief Marshals. Here, the Special Operations Directorate oversees deep-cover undercover agents, anti-corruption investigations into station syndicates, and high-threat counter-terrorism taskforces.

#### Jurisdictional Tensions & Corporate Pressure

##### The Planetary Sovereignty Barrier
Bastion's primary operational dilemma is the constitutional limit of Stewards authority. Under Article V of the Absalom Pact, peacekeepers have absolute jurisdiction in interplanetary space, on trade lanes, and within federal installations like Absalom Station. However, once an offender reaches sovereign planetary soil—such as the drow vaults of [[Apostae]] or the corporate zones of [[Triaxus]]—the Stewards cannot pursue without formal diplomatic extraditions, creating endless jurisdictional friction with corrupt regional planetary magistrates.

##### Resisting Corporate Privatization
Megacorporations, led by **[[AbadarCorp]]**, continuously lobby the Pact Council to outsource local security patrols and convoy escorts to private military contractors. Director-General [[Lin Cammar]] has fiercely resisted these initiatives from Bastion, arguing that privatizing systemic peacekeeping would reduce justice to an auctioned commodity.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Mole in the War Room:** A covert intelligence file detailing peacekeeper decoy routes across [[The Diaspora]] is leaked to the [[Free Captains]], leading to the ambush of an armored relief convoy. Suspecting an insider within the War Room’s communications staff, Director-General Lin Cammar circumvents standard protocols and hires the player characters as independent investigators to identify the traitor.
* **The Breakout at Block 9:** A mercenary extraction team disguised as station maintenance workers deploys a localized antimatter pulse, disabling the null-magic fields in Detention Block 9. Dangerous prisoners break out of their cryogenic cells and take the lower gantry engineers hostage. Trapped inside the complex on official business, the party must fight through mutinous cellblocks to restore power before the prisoners reach the launch gantries.
* **The Ghost Gunboat:** A decommissioned Stewards interceptor with erased military serial numbers docks without permission at an exterior gantry of Bastion. The interior is smeared with necrotic frost and the bridge console is locked into a repeating distress signal broadcasting from an uncharted sector of [[The Ghost Levels]].
