#### Astrogation & Structural Overview: The Cloud in the Keel

Situated in the damp, echoing middle decks of **[[The Spike]]**, **Fogtown** is one of the most atmospheric and inhospitable shantytowns aboard [[Absalom Station]]. Positioned directly beneath the colossal exhaust arrays of **Atmospheric Scrubber Hub 4** and the thermal cooling manifolds of the station’s lower power grid, the entire district is permanently shrouded in a dense, warm blanket of chemical vapor, steam runoff, and oily condensation.

In Fogtown, the concepts of night and day do not exist. Amber and sodium-vapor emergency lights glow like sickly halos through the drifting gray fog, while warm water constantly rains down from ceiling grates onto makeshift corrugated-tin roofs and elevated wooden boardwalks. The air smells strongly of engine lubricant, ozone, and wet rust, creating an environment that is physically suffocating to unaugmented lungs yet magnetically attractive to those who wish to disappear.

#### Geography & Life in the Mist

##### 1. The Drip Alleyways
The streets of Fogtown are not solid decks, but rather a precarious network of iron catwalks, wooden pallets, and scaffolding erected over deep drainage sumps. Because thousands of gallons of condensed water fall from upper machinery every hour, the floor grates are slick with fluorescent moss and engine sludge. Citizens navigate the catwalks wearing heavy rubberized cloaks, oilskin duster coats, and filtration masks to protect against the ever-present drizzle.

##### 2. The Steam Cathedral
The spiritual and communal shelter of the district, constructed inside a cavernous, forty-foot-tall former geothermal radiator vault. The massive, inactive copper heat-exchange coils have been hollowed out into multi-room communal dwellings, illuminated by blue luminescent lichen. The cathedral is widely known across the Spike as a safe sanctuary for runaway androids, indentured debt-laborers fleeing corporate contracts, and deserters seeking to start new lives under false identities.

##### 3. Scrubber Exhaust Array 4
The monstrous industrial engine that creates Fogtown’s unique climate. Four immense turbine flues—each thirty feet across—vent processed exhaust air from the station’s central recycling systems into the district. The deafening roar of the turbines masks gunfire and shouting, while the heat generates tropical micro-climates where illegal botanical ingredients for synthetic narcotics are cultivated.

##### 4. The Condensate Siphon
A sprawling labyrinth of open copper pipes, settling vats, and distillation filters managed by the **Valve Wardens**. The Wardens siphon warm condensate runoff before it drains into the sewage works, distilling the cleaner water into potable supplies for Downlow and brewing "Spike Gin"—a potent, rough-edged moonshine favored by dockers across the station.

#### The Haven of the Hunted

The thick, moisture-heavy fog of Fogtown is not merely an environmental hazard; it is an invaluable tactical defense. The dense water droplets and localized heat plumes effectively blind modern surveillance technology:
* **Sensor Baffles:** Thermal scopes, infrared optics, and facial recognition cameras deployed by **Absalom Station Security** or bounty hunter drones suffer severe scattering in the mist, reducing effective targeting range to knife-fighting distance.
* **The Valve Wardens' Network:** This informal syndicate of rogue hydraulic mechanics and pipe-fitters controls the manual pressure valves throughout the sector. When corporate extraction teams or mercenary hit squads enter Fogtown, the Wardens open steam valves to create blinding thermal walls, allowing targeted individuals to slip through unmapped drainage flumes into **[[Downlow]]** or down toward **[[The Ghost Levels]]**.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Caustic Inversion:** Saboteurs tamper with the chemical intake injectors on Scrubber Array 4, causing the mist in Fogtown to turn into a stinging, acidic aerosol that begins corroding metal bulkheads and blinding citizens. The local Valve Wardens hire the PCs to race through the fog in environmental hazard suits, fight off armed saboteurs guarding the control catwalks, and manually vent the system before hundreds of squatter families suffocate.
* **The Synthetic Asset:** An elite bounty hunter team representing a prominent tech syndicate on [[Verces]] corners an escaped android prototype inside The Steam Cathedral. The android’s neural core contains encrypted schematics of an illegal corporate bioweapon. The party must decide whether to aid the android in fighting off the corporate squad through the mist-shrouded boardwalks or collect the staggering bounty from the corporation.
* **The Lurker in the Drips:** A series of gruesome disappearances plagues the lowest catwalks of Fogtown. Victims are dragged into the dark drainage flumes beneath the grates, leaving behind only acid burns and severed cybernetic limbs. The community pools its scrap-credits to hire the party to wade through the flooded drainage reservoirs and hunt down whatever subterranean horror has crawled up from the boundary of [[The Ghost Levels]].
