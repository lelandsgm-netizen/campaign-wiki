#### Astrogation Dossier: The Hollow World

**Apostae**—frequently whispered across spacer taprooms as **The Maiden** or **The Derelict World**—is the twelfth major celestial body of the [[Mataras System]], orbiting in the desolate outer rim approximately thirteen astronomical units from [[Mataras]]. To incoming astro-navigators, Apostae appears as a barren, jagged, airless lump of carbonaceous stone and impact-shattered basalt, tumbling through the void on a strange, highly inclined orbital plane.

Yet beneath its scarred, lifeless rock crust lies one of the most chilling secrets in galactic cosmology: **Apostae is not a natural planet.**

Centuries of subterranean drilling and mining operations revealed that the stone exterior is merely a miles-thick shell of space dust, slag, and fossilized ice accumulated over millions of years. Beneath this rocky mantle lies the gargantuan, ribbed hull of a titanic **alien generation ship or planetary ark**, forged by an unknown precursor civilization eons before the dawn of mortal history. Honeycombed with thousands of miles of sealed bulkheads, zero-gravity maintenance shafts, forgotten alien laboratories, and dormant xenotech horrors, Apostae is a fortress of mysteries.

Today, this ancient derelict is ruled with an iron fist by the matriarchal noble houses of the **[[Drow]]**—dark elves who have traded ancestral cavern-skulking for cutthroat megacorporate capitalism, turning Apostae into the foremost military arms factory and mercenary depot in the **[[Pact Worlds]]**.

#### The Social Hierarchy of Apostae

Apostae’s society is a ruthless corporate dystopia built upon cold legalism, racial supremacy, and brutal labor exploitation.

##### 1. The Drow Noble Houses
The dark elves of Apostae restructured their ancient noble bloodlines into modern, diversified **arms-manufacturing megacorporations**:
* **House Zeizerer:** The undisputed sovereign of Apostae. Specializing in capital starship weaponry, plasma torpedoes, planetary defense batteries, and munitions logistics, House Zeizerer commands the planetary government and controls the world’s most prized relic: the Portal of Nightarch.
* **House Vaelis:** The masters of biological warfare, genetic tampering, and cyber-espionage. House Vaelis operates vast underground flesh-vats, synthesizing combat stimulants, cybernetic implants, and grotesque fleshwarped beasts.
* **House Rasheen & House Xicton:** Secondary corporate houses specializing in vacuum mining, kinetic weapon foundries, and private security contracting.

##### 2. The Indentured [[Orcs]]
The economic foundation of Apostae is built upon the backs of millions of **Orcs** and half-orcs:
* Orcs are legally held in permanent corporate debt-bondage, bound by complex, generational contracts that skirt the anti-slavery laws of the [[Pact Worlds]].
* Orcs toil in the airless exterior quarries, labor in boiling chemical vats, and serve as the frontline shock-troop infantry for Drow private military contractors. While isolated rebel cells (such as the underground *Bloodied Hand*) launch hit-and-run guerrilla sabotage, the Drow suppress dissent with shock collars, cybernetic tracking beacons, and merciless executions.

##### 1. [[Nightarch]] (The Obsidian Capital)
Constructed inside a colossal impact crater on the planetoid’s exterior, **Nightarch** is the capital and only major city on the surface of Apostae.
* **Atmospheric Force Domes:** Shimmering energy barriers enclose the crater, maintaining an artificial, filtered atmosphere and low-intensity lighting tailored for dark-adapted Drow eyes.
* **Architecture of Domination:** The skyline is a forest of jagged obsidian spires, razor-edged corporate arcologies, and industrial smokestacks. Corporate enforcers in powered armor patrol the neon-lit plazas, while subterranean cargo elevators hoist millions of tons of heavy armaments to surface landing pads.
* **The Black Market:** Nightarch is the premiere arms depot of the outer system. Here, mercenary fleets, corporate buyers, and even corsairs from [[The Diaspora]] purchase high-grade laser rifles, anti-matter warheads, and illegal combat cybernetics without background checks or questions asked.

##### 2. The Portal of Nightarch (The Arch)
Rising hundreds of feet above the center of Nightarch stands the city’s namesake: an immense, indestructible horseshoe arch crafted from an iridescent, seamless alien alloy that resists all scanning and damage.
* **The Wormhole Gateway:** A relic of the precursor race that built Apostae, the Arch can be calibrated through complex arcane-technological keystones to open instantaneous two-way spatial portals directly to distant star systems across the galaxy, bypassing the need for [[The Drift]].
* **The Monopoly of Zeizerer:** House Zeizerer maintains absolute military control over the Arch, charging exorbitant transit fees to corporate trade caravans, exploratory fleets, and military expeditions seeking instant transit to the uncharted fringes of the Vast.

##### 3. The Deep Vaults & The Under-Hulls
Beneath the surface crust, Apostae is partitioned into hundreds of numbered "Decks" spanning thousands of miles toward the core.
* **Corporate Delving:** The upper fifty decks have been cleared, pressurized, and converted into corporate munitions factories, hydroponic slave warrens, and executive penthouses.
* **The Uncharted Darkness:** Below Deck 50 lies a pitch-black nightmare of alien architecture. Sealed blast doors, non-Euclidean corridors, gravity anomalies, and rogue automated defense constructs protect stasis vaults containing alien bio-weapons and precursor artifacts. Delver teams hired by rival Drow houses descend into these vaults on suicide salvage missions; less than half return alive.

#### Politics & The Pact Worlds

Apostae’s relationship with the rest of the **[[Pact Worlds]]** is characterized by mutual cynicism, disgust, and cold dependency.

##### The Absalom Pact Signatory
Apostae was admitted as a sovereign member of the Pact in **291 AG**. 
* While high-minded worlds like [[Castrovel]] and the paladins of the **[[Knights of Golarion]]** fiercely protested the admission of a demon-worshiping, slave-holding autocracy, realpolitik prevailed: the Pact desperately needed Apostae’s massive munitions factories and hardened orc legions to survive the brutal invasion fleets of the **[[Veskarium]]**.
* **The Corporate Loophole:** When challenged on Pact anti-slavery statutes, House Zeizerer’s elite legal teams argue that orcs are not property, but "contractually bound lifetime associates" who owe debt for their passage and life-support—a legal fiction the Pact Council reluctantly tolerates to preserve access to Apostae's weapons.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Deck 84 Outbreak:** An exploratory delve funded by House Vaelis breaches a sealed stasis vault on Deck 84, awakening an ancient swarm of microscopic alien nanites that reanimate dead biological tissue into bio-mechanical horrors. With the lower lifts sealed by corporate quarantine, the PCs are trapped in the under-decks and must fight through zombie-hordes and alien security turrets to override the blast doors.
* **Incursion Through the Arch:** The great alien portal in the heart of [[Nightarch]] activates without warning, vomiting forth an armada of xenophobic extra-galactic strike craft that begins bombarding the city's force domes. The party must defend the landing pads, breach the portal control sanctum, and decipher the precursor sequence to sever the wormhole.
* **The Debt Ledger Heist:** A charismatic half-orc revolutionary backed by abolitionist sympathizers on [[Absalom Station]] hires the heroes for an audacious corporate heist: infiltrate the high-security quantum datavault of House Zeizerer, extract the encrypted financial master-ledger that binds two million orcs in indenture, and escape to a waiting stealth transport on the airless surface.
