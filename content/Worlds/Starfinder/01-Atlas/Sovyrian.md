#### Overview: The Cloistered Paradise

Covering an entire continent in the eastern hemisphere of **[[Castrovel]]**, **Sovyrian** is an ancient, enigmatic realm of primordial forests, pristine crystalline coastlines, and silent, canal-threaded stone cities. It is the sovereign ancestral sanctuary of the **[[Elves]]**, who crossed the void via ancient teleportation portals from lost Golarion in deep antiquity to escape planetary extinction.

Unlike the bustling, high-tech spaceports of **[[Qabarat]]** or the subterranean industry of **[[Queensrock]]**, Sovyrian appears frozen in timeless, pre-industrial grace. Foreign starships approaching the continent find their navigational scanners blinded and their communications jammed by the *Aegis of Sovyrian*—an immense orbital defense web combining ancient arcane runes with automated kinetic satellite batteries. 

Unauthorized vessels entering Sovyrian airspace are fired upon without warning. Within its borders, modern mass-market electronics, internal combustion engines, and industrial factories are strictly outlawed; the continent relies entirely on ancient ley-line power grids, clean solar batteries, and high-order elven magic.

#### Landmarks & The Capital of El

##### 1. El (The City of Canals)
The capital and crown jewel of Sovyrian:
* Constructed upon the tranquil waters of an inland delta, El possesses no roads or wheeled vehicles; transit is conducted entirely via silent, solar-powered gondolas gliding along turquoise canals.
* Buildings are sculpted from living darkwood trees grown into cathedral-like spires, integrated seamlessly with towering obelisks of enchanted rose-quartz and white marble.
* Houses the **Sovereign Court**, where the High Monarch and the Council of Mages deliberate on the affairs of the realm, surrounded by unbroken libraries whose records date back to before **[[The Gap]]**.

##### 2. The Great Aiudara Nexus
Hidden within a secluded, warded forest grove outside El:
* A concentric ring of ancient stone arches known as the **[[Aiudara]]** (Elf Gates).
* These permanent planar conduits connect Sovyrian to sister arches in Qabarat, deep-space outposts, and the ruined monuments of other worlds in the [[Mataras System]].

##### 3. The Forest of Whispers
A vast, primordial temperate rainforest covering the interior:
* The trees are infused with ancient magic, acting as acoustic and psychic conduits that alert elven rangers to the presence of any un-permitted trespassers within minutes of entry.

#### Society & Foreign Policy

Sovyrian society is aristocratic, deeply traditionalist, and famously xenophobic:
* **The Concept of Chyrs:** Non-elves are classified under elven law as *chyrs* (literally "brief-lives" or "outsiders"). Even half-elves born outside the continent face profound social prejudice, viewed as tragic symbols of diluted bloodlines.
* **The Forlorn Rift:** Sovyrian society looks down upon the "Forlorn"—elves who leave the continent to dwell among mortal races in the wider galaxy—treating them as culturally compromised wanderers.
* **Reluctant Pact Membership:** Sovyrian signed the **Absalom Pact** in 291 AG only under immense geopolitical pressure to maintain a united front against external threats like the **[[Veskarium]]**. They maintain a small diplomatic embassy on [[Absalom Station]], but rarely participate in standard Pact Council debates unless elven sovereignty is threatened.

#### Campaign Hooks

* **The Royal Heist:** An elite team of infilitrators breaches the Sovereign Archives in [[El]], stealing an ancient pre-Gap astronomical star-chart rumored to reveal the exact coordinates where Golarion vanished during [[The Gap]]. The Sovereign Court covertly hires the PCs to track the thieves before the data is sold to the **[[Starfinder Society]]**.
* **The Crash Beyond the Aegis:** A civilian passenger shuttle carrying a high-ranking diplomat from [[Qabarat]] suffers engine failure and crash-lands inside Sovyrian’s forbidden northern forests. With elven border-wardens mobilizing to eliminate the "intruders," the PCs must mount a covert rescue mission to extract the survivors.
