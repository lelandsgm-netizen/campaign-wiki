## Overview

Anchored within a remarkably stable orbital pocket amidst the tumbling chaos of **[[The Diaspora]]**, **Star Citadel Theodrane** is the crowning engineering jewel of **[[Ulrikka Clanholdings]]**. 

Originally a solid, hyper-dense metallic planetoid rich in platinum and titanium, Theodrane was systematically excavated across two centuries of disciplined dwarven stonecraft. Today, it stands as an impregnable subterranean bastion: a fully enclosed, self-sustaining hollow-asteroid city capable of withstanding planetary orbital bombardment and housing thousands of miners, forge-smiths, and starship crews.

The citadel functions as the primary logistics hub and sovereign fortress for dwarven operations in the belt. Here, deep-range survey cutters are fitted with heavy drilling lasers, damaged ore haulers are repaired in zero-gravity drydocks, and raw belt asteroid ore is transmuted into the finest armor alloys in the [[Pact Worlds]].

## Key Facilities & Districts

### 1. The Lower Cradles & Tow Bays
The primary airlocks and magnetic landing slips of Theodrane can berth up to four corporate frigates and dozens of smaller mining craft simultaneously.
* **Emergency Tow Fleet:** Theodrane maintains a fleet of heavy-displacement tractor-tugs on standby around the clock. Spacers whose engines stall in the belt or whose hulls are battered by asteroid collisions can broadcast an emergency beacon to Theodrane for extraction and repair.

### 2. The Toragine Hearths (Solar Forges)
Using massive parabolic solar-mirror arrays deployed outside the asteroid's shadow, dwarven smiths channel concentrated starlight directly into deep vacuum crucibles.
* Operating in hard vacuum prevents atmospheric oxidation, allowing the artisans of Clanholdings to forge flawless sheets of adamantine alloy, cold iron ship plating, and kinetic artillery rounds.

### 3. Deep Sensor Arrays & The Silent Watch
Because of its position along the mid-belt transit routes, Theodrane houses some of the most sensitive sub-space listening posts and gravimetric acoustic sensors in the Diaspora.
* Dwarven seismologists monitor the drift of millions of tumbling asteroids, tracking rogue pirate armadas from **[[Broken Rock]]**, detecting corporate survey teams from **[[AbadarCorp]]**, and cataloging unexplained electromagnetic anomalies throughout the sector.

## The Mysterious Signal: The Hook from *Guilt of the Grave World*

> [!warning] Tactical Dispatch: Sector 7 Anomaly
> Recently, long-range seismic and sub-space sensors at Star Citadel Theodrane registered an unprecedented, repeating technomagical energy spike originating from an uncharted asteroid tumbling through the Diaspora. 
> 
> The acoustic signature did not match any known geological movement or contemporary transponder code: it bore the mathematical cadence of ancient pre-Gap elebrian circuitry, pulsing outward like a rhythmic beacon from within the stone. 
> 
> Determined to secure the site before rival prospectors or the rogue **[[Corpse Fleet]]** catch wind of the signal, **[[Ulrikka Clanholdings]]** leadership at Theodrane has begun hiring trusted, discreet freelance contractors to investigate the mysterious beacon at **[[Iovan Station Alpha]]**.
