#### Overview: The Powerhouse of the Inner Void

Positioned at the eastern terminus of the **Sun-Gutter**—the massive, equator-spanning solar collection trench that cuts across **[[Aballon]]**—lies **Endeavor**, the undisputed energy heart of the machine world.

While other megaplexes forge armor or debate philosophy, Endeavor has a single, colossal function: capturing, converting, and distributing the staggering thermonuclear energy radiating from nearby **[[Mataras]]**. Through millions of square miles of photovoltaic collector fields, liquid-sodium heat exchangers, and particle accelerators, Endeavor channels petawatts of power into Aballon's subterranean industrial grids.

Furthermore, Endeavor operates massive orbital power-masers that transmit tightly focused, wireless microwave energy beams to orbital relay satellites, solar refineries in **[[The Burning Archipelago]]**, and interplanetary freight stations throughout the inner system.

---

#### Campaign Hooks

* **The Grid Inversion:** A massive solar flare from Mataras overloads Endeavor's primary capacitor banks, triggering an emergency power cascade that threatens to vaporize the Sun-Gutter Terminus. The PCs must brave lethal radiation fields to manually trip the emergency superconducting breakers.
* **The Energy Piracy:** Orbital sensors detect clandestine spaceborn siphon-probes tapping into Endeavor's wireless power beams, diverting millions of gigawatts of energy to an unmapped asteroid base in **[[The Diaspora]]**. The Central Grid Authority hires the PCs to trace and sever the illegal siphon.
