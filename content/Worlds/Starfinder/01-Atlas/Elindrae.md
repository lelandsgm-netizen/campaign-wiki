#### Overview: The Industrial Crucible of the Green Mother

While the surface of **[[Castrovel]]** is preserved as a pristine ecological paradise of lush jungles, coral cities, and untamed wildlife preserves, the planet’s single natural moon—**Elindrae**—bears the full, unyielding weight of heavy industrial civilization.

Airless, heavily cratered, and bathed in cosmic radiation, Elindrae functions under planetary law as Castrovel's "sacrificial basin." Because strict environmental treaties co-signed by the Lashunta, Formians, and Xenowardens ban all toxic chemical smelting, heavy metal refining, and capital starship construction on the planet's surface, all hazardous industrial operations are shunted to the moon.

Today, Elindrae is a dazzling, hyper-active industrial titan. Concentrated inside massive pressurized domes and deep subterranean craters, thousands of factories, smelting crucibles, and zero-G drydocks ring the moon's equator, producing the commercial freighters and military warships that defend Castrovel across the [[Pact Worlds]].

#### The Kevolari Collective

The crown jewel of Elindrae is the **Kevolari Collective**, a massive, historic industrial partnership formed between the **[[Lashunta]]** and the **[[Formians]]** following the peace treaty of 287 AG:
* **Hybrid Biomechanical Engineering:** The Collective is famous across the galaxy for pioneering a revolutionary starship manufacturing technique that fuses Lashunta technomancy and sensor arrays with Formian biological chitin-weaving.
* **The Living Ships:** Vessels produced by Kevolari possess self-repairing organic hull plates that regenerate from kinetic punctures over time, integrated with cutting-edge electronics and heavy plasma weapon arrays.
* **Symbol of Unity:** The shipyards serve as the living proof of peace: Lashunta flight engineers work side-by-side with Formian worker-castes and android fabricators in zero-G gantries.

#### Campaign Hooks

* **The Antimatter Containment Breach:** Sabotage by an eco-terrorist splinter cell disables the primary cooling grid of an antimatter refinery on Elindrae, threatening to trigger a detonation that would shower Castrovel's upper atmosphere with radioactive fallout. The PCs must execute a zero-g assault to recapture the control center.
* **The Stolen Prototype Cruiser:** A newly completed, military-grade Kevolari biomechanical stealth corvette is hijacked from Drydock 7 by rogue mercenaries. The PCs are hired by the Collective Directorate to intercept the vessel before it reaches the jump-points of **[[The Drift]]**.
