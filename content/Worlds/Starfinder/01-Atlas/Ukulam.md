#### Overview: The Primordial Sanctuary

Stretching across the northern expanse of **[[Castrovel]]**, **Ukulam** is an untamed, primeval wilderness of staggering biological ferocity. While civilization flourishes across [[Asana]], [[The Colonies]], and [[Sovyrian]], Ukulam has been preserved by international planetary treaty as an unharvested ecological sanctuary.

The continent is choked with colossal thousand-foot ironwood trees, predatory carnivorous vines, toxic moldstorms, and gargantuan saurian megafauna. It is also the native homeland of the **[[Khizars]]**, an indigenous species of eyeless, photosynthetic plant-folk who dwell in aerial root-canopies and live in harmonious communion with the forest.

Overseen by cloaked watchposts maintained by the **[[Xenowardens]]**, Ukulam serves as a living laboratory for rapid evolutionary cycles. Deep beneath its trackless green canopy lie ancient, overgrown cyclopean ruins and dormant **[[Aiudara]]** (Elf Gates) from civilizations that walked Castrovel before mortal history was recorded.
