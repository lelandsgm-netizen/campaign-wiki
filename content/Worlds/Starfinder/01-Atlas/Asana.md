#### Overview: The Realm of the Telepathic Spires

**Asana** is the largest, most densely populated, and politically influential continent on **[[Castrovel]]**. Spanning the equatorial latitudes of the Green Mother, Asana is a vibrant, rolling tapestry of towering ironwood rainforests, fertile river deltas, and savanna plateaus.

It is the ancient ancestral cradle of the **[[Lashunta]]**, who dwell within independent, matriarchal city-states connected by trade pacts, telepathic councils, and prehistoric **[[Aiudara]]** (Elf Gates). Rather than conquering or clear-cutting their environment, the Lashunta designed their arcologies to blend into the landscape: fossil-shell walls repel aggressive saurian predators, while aerodynamic crystalline towers harness solar and wind energy.

From its primary coastal spaceport of **[[Qabarat]]**, Asana serves as the primary diplomatic and commercial bridge connecting the biosphere of Castrovel to the wider [[Pact Worlds]].
