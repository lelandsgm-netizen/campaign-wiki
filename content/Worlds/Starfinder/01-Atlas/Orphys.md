# Orphys (The City of Domes)

> [!info] Quick Facts
> **Planet & System:** [[Eox]] / [[Mataras System]] (The [[Pact Worlds]])
> **Location:** The Gorgon Rift (Equatorial canyon complex spanning over 80 miles)
> **Settlement Type:** Planetary Capital / Subterranean & Force-Domed Necropolis
> **Surface Gravity:** Low Terrestrial ($\times 0.9\text{ G}$)
> **Atmosphere:** Controlled Nitrogen-Oxygen Mix beneath force domes; toxic radioactive vacuum beyond the perimeter
> **Population:** ~4,200,000 (Undead [[Elebrians]], Necrovites, Corpsefolk, Vampires, Ghouls, Skeletons; roughly 8% living off-worlders and resident Elebrians)
> **Ruling Governance:** The Council of **[[Bone Sages]]** (Meeting in the Hall of Sages)
> **Primary Districts:** The Living Enclaves, The Necro-Arcade, The Spire of Sages, The Low Bone Warrens
> **Dominant Factions:** **[[Bone Sages]]**, **[[AbadarCorp]]** Consular Mission, **The Ministry of Undeath**, **[[The Stewards]]** Legation
> **Primary Deities:** **[[Urgathoa]]** (The Pallid Princess), **[[Zon-Kuthon]]** (The Midnight Lord), **[[Abadar]]** (Commerce)

## Overview & Architecture

Constructed deep within the sheer, basalt-walled depths of the Gorgon Rift along Eox’s equator, **Orphys**—frequently commemorated as **The City of Domes** or the **Necropolis of Glass and Bone**—is the undisputed political, cultural, and commercial capital of [[Eox]].

Unlike the rest of the blasted, airless planet, Orphys is designed to accommodate the biological realities of interplanetary diplomacy and commerce. Sealed beneath shimmering, kilometers-wide magnetic containment force fields, the canyon traps a warm, pressurized atmosphere that shields living beings from the lethal cosmic radiation, solar flares, and toxic dust of the exterior surface.

Beneath the domes, soaring spires carved from black volcanic obsidian and reinforced with calcified bone-composites rise into the artificial sky. Bridges of glowing emerald force-energy leap between canyon walls, while magnetic grav-rails ferry immortal oligarchs, corporate merchants, and off-world tourists between luxurious enclaves and shadowy necromantic laboratories.

## Major Districts

### 1. The Embassy Terraces & Living Enclaves
Perched along the upper western walls of the canyon, the **Embassy Terraces** cater exclusively to biological visitors:
* **The Living Shield:** Here, atmospheric scrubbers maintain standard terrestrial humidity and oxygen levels, while luxury hotels, synthetic botanical gardens, and high-end dining clubs serve biological food imported from [[Castrovel]] and [[Verces]].
* **The Diplomatic Core:** Houses the official consulates of [[The Stewards]], [[AbadarCorp]], the [[Veskarium]], and sovereign delegations from [[Absalom Station]]. Armed Steward peacekeepers patrol these boulevards to ensure off-world delegates are shielded from predatory undead citizens.

### 2. The Hall of Sages (The Seat of Power)
Dominating the center of the rift is the colossal palace of the **[[Bone Sages]]**:
* Fabricated from interlocking blocks of vitrified obsidian and ribbed with ancient adamantine plating, the Hall is the judicial and legislative heart of Eox.
* In the central amphitheater—the *Circle of Inanimatums*—immortal necrovite lords convene to debate trade tariffs, allocate mineral extraction quotas across the planet's vast basalt flats, and vote on systemic policy before the [[Pact Council]].

### 3. The Necro-Arcade (The Broadcast Spire)
Eox is the reality-television and extreme bloodsports capital of the Pact Worlds, and the Necro-Arcade is its glittering, garish nervous system:
* Massive multi-tiered holographic broadcasting towers beam live high-definition feeds of deadly obstacle races, gladiator matches, and macabre comedy specials out through orbital Drift antennas to billions of paying subscribers across the galaxy.
* Production studios employ thousands of undead and living writers, stunt performers, holographic editors, and celebrity hosts.

### 4. The Low Bone Warrens (The Crypt-Undercity)
Carved directly into the subterranean bedrock below the canyon floor lies the industrial undercity of Orphys:
* **Automated Skeletal Foundries:** Thousands of mindless skeletons and zombies, unhindered by fatigue, toxic fumes, or heat, refine ores brought from surface quarries and assemble starship components.
* **Necrovitae Synthesis Refineries:** Immense biochemical vats produce *necrovitae*—the specialized nutrient fluid used to sustain intelligent undead and maintain biological tissue grafts.

## Culture & Daily Life

Life in Orphys is a strange, chilling study in contrast:

1. **The Silence of the Dead:** Outside of the commercial markets and broadcast studios, the residential spires of the undead are eerily quiet. With citizens who have no need to breathe, sleep, or eat, residential corridors lack kitchens, air vents, or standard heating systems, bathed only in dim violet or pale green running lights.
2. **Cold Hospitality:** Undead Elebrians treat living tourists and business partners with polite, clinical courtesy. While off-worlders often shudder at the hollow eye-sockets and calcified grins of their hosts, violence against registered living guests within the city is strictly forbidden under the decree of the Bone Sages—carrying harsh penalties up to and including the permanent dissolution of a citizen’s phylactery.
3. **The Flesh-Bazaars:** In specialized markets, licensed cosmetic surgeons offer living and undead clients cutting-edge **necro-grafts**: preserved muscle fibers, cybernetically augmented ocular implants, and cosmetic skin treatments that bridge the gap between living flesh and eternal preservation.

## Security & Defense

Orphys is one of the most heavily fortified cities in the solar system:
* **The Kinetic Canopy:** The magnetic containment domes are capable of polarizing into dense, deflecting kinetic shields in the event of an orbital bombardment.
* **The Boneguard Garrison:** Internal order is maintained by the Boneguard—elite skeletal soldiers and cybernetically augmented corpsefolk officers armed with cryogenic suppression rifles and paralyzing neuro-shock batons.
* **Vacuum Purge Protocol:** In the event of a catastrophic civil insurrection or foreign invasion, the Bone Sages possess emergency overrides capable of venting the atmospheric domes into the planetary vacuum, instantly suffocating any biological invader within minutes while leaving the undead population completely unaffected.

## Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Stolen Broadcast:** A rogue entertainment producer in the Necro-Arcade has intercepted classified military surveillance feeds from [[The Stewards]] and plans to air them as an interactive true-crime special. The party is hired by station authorities to infiltrate the studio and recover the data core before the broadcast goes live across the Pact Worlds.
* **Murder in the Living Enclave:** A prominent [[AbadarCorp]] trade negotiator is found assassinated inside their penthouse in the Embassy Terraces, their body completely drained of blood. With the local Boneguard threatening to frame a living activist group, the PCs must investigate the high-society vampire clans to uncover the true killer before a diplomatic crisis erupts.
* **The Siphon Incident:** An unknown hacker has breached the environmental grid of the Living Enclaves, slowly venting oxygen into the canyon and replacing it with radioactive surface dust. The party must descend into the hazardous low-level ventilation ducts to purge the virus and restore life-support before thousands of biological residents perish.
