# Nisis

> [!info] Quick Facts
> **System & Region:** [[Mataras System]] / Outer [[The Diaspora]] (trailing toward [[Eox]])
> **Celestial Classification:** Cryo-Volcanic Dwarf Planetoid
> **Diameter:** ~1,400 miles (2,250 km)
> **Surface Gravity:** Low ($0.35\text{ G}$)
> **Atmosphere:** Thin, poisonous methane-nitrogen haze (Toxic & unbreathable; average surface temp $-280^\circ\text{F}$ / $-173^\circ\text{C}$)
> **Crust:** 5 to 20 miles of compressed methane, ammonia, and water ice
> **Subsurface Hydrosphere:** Global abyssal ocean of supercooled, high-salinity liquid brine, heated by core tidal flexing and volcanic sulfur chimneys
> **Population:** ~35,000 (Submersible refinery workers, deep-sea researchers, corporate security, outlaws, and native marine cryptids)
> **Settlements:** [[Grovak's Bore]], **Deepwater Station Alpha**, **The Sunless Warrens**
> **Dominant Factions:** **[[AbadarCorp]]** (Cryogenic & mineral extraction), **Independent Drilling Guilds**, **The [[Free Captains]]** (Deep-ice smuggler caches)
> **Native Apex Predator:** **The Kalyama** (Giant, telepathic bioluminescent deep-sea terrors)
> **Primary Deities:** **[[Torag]]** (Mining & deep excavation), **[[Abadar]]** (Corporate enterprise), **[[Besmara]]** (Submersible piracy)

## Astrogation Dossier: The Ice Fortress

Drifting along the cold, dim outer periphery of **[[The Diaspora]]** near the orbital track of [[Eox]], **Nisis** is a bleak, unforgiving world of frozen gloom. Unlike the jagged nickel-iron asteroids that dominate the belt, Nisis is one of the few bodies massive enough to have pulled itself into hydrostatic equilibrium, forming a gleaming white-and-azure sphere coated in jagged sheets of methane frost, ammonia glaciers, and jagged mountain ridges of diamond-hard water ice.

Beneath this frozen planetary armor lies one of the greatest natural resources—and deadliest ecological environments—in the [[Pact Worlds]]: a boundless, lightless liquid ocean hundreds of miles deep. Warmed by core geothermal vents and gravitational friction from neighboring planetoids, Nisis's interior seas are rich in heavy hydrocarbons, noble gases, and exotic prebiotic chemical compounds that drive an insatiable multi-billion-credit extraction industry.

## The Surface & The Bores

The exterior of Nisis is an inhospitable, wind-scoured ice desert where temperatures plunge low enough to freeze atmospheric moisture into razor-sharp needle-blizzards. Only heavy automated refineries, thermal exhaust towers, and armored landing cradles break the smooth expanse of the ice plains.

### 1. Grovak’s Bore
The primary surface settlement and extraction hub is **Grovak’s Bore**, established by an ambitious dwarven mining clan and heavily co-funded by **[[AbadarCorp]]**.
* **The Thermal Shaft:** A cylindrical shaft half a mile wide blasted directly through twelve miles of solid ice using massive geothermal laser rigs and continuous chemical heating coils.
* **The Elevator Grid:** Heavy industrial elevators and pressurized descent pods travel down the shaft on superconducting magnetic rails, ferrying mining crews, heavy submarine hulls, and extracted liquid fuels between the surface spaceport and the sub-ocean.

### 2. The Smugglers' Crevasses
Because planetary radar and systemic sensor sweeps cannot penetrate through miles of hyper-dense ice, the outer fractures of Nisis are legendary among the **[[Free Captains]]**.
* Pirates carve camouflaged drydock hollows into the sides of deep glacial crevasses, parking stealth starships in sub-surface ice hangars to evade patrols by [[The Stewards]].

## The Sunless Sea (The Subsurface Abyss)

Passing through the bottom of the ice crust reveals the boundless, pitch-black ocean of Nisis. With no sunlight to penetrate the miles of overhead ice, the waters are completely dark, illuminated only by the artificial floodlights of submarines and the eerie, pulsing hues of deep-sea bioluminescence.

### 1. Environmental Hazards
* **Crushing Hydrostatic Pressure:** Diving into Nisis requires specially reinforced deep-sea submersibles or hyper-dense power armor. A hull breach at depths exceeding two miles results in instantaneous implosion.
* **Thermal Chasm Vents:** Immense basalt chimneys erupting from the planetoid's seabed spew superheated mineral slurry and sulfur compounds, creating boiling vertical currents surrounded by freezing brine.

### 2. Deepwater Station Alpha
Suspended beneath the ice ceiling by massive tension tethers anchored to the bedrock below, **Deepwater Station Alpha** is the primary sub-ocean research and processing outpost.
* Fabricated from interlocking spheres of reinforced polycarbonate and titanium, the station houses three thousand marine miners, corporate oceanographers, and mercenary divers.
* It serves as a staging ground for bathyscaphes harvesting cryogenic fuels and mapping ancient mineral spires along the ocean floor.

## The Kalyama: Terror of the Abyss

The greatest peril of Nisis is not the crushing cold or pressure, but its native apex predator: the **Kalyama**.

* **Biology & Hunting Methods:** Kalyama are colossal, cephalopod-like horrors growing up to ninety feet in length. Their flesh is rubbery, translucent, and adapted to withstand oceanic pressure that would crumple a starship bulkhead.
* **The Telepathic Lure:** Along their dorsal crests, Kalyama possess organs that emit mesmerizing patterns of shifting azure and violet light. Simultaneously, they project telepathic psychic hallucinations into the minds of intelligent prey—mimicking the voices of loved ones, distressed crewmates, or feelings of euphoric safety.
* **The Feeding Frenzy:** Unsuspecting divers or submersible pilots mesmerized by the lure drift directly into the monster's tentacles, where razor-sharp chitinous mandibles crush armored cockpits like eggshells. Even inside reinforced stations, workers frequently report hearing cold, aquatic whispers echoing through the plumbing and hull plating.

## Economy & Corporate Exploitation

Despite the lethal fauna, Nisis represents a commercial goldmine for systemic industry:

1. **Cryo-Chemicals & Hydrocarbons:** The deep seas contain pure reservoirs of liquid methane and rare ammonia compounds essential for starship coolant loops, terraforming atmospheric cocktails, and industrial chemical synthesis.
2. **Sub-Surface Mineral Spires:** Hydrothermal vents on the seabed deposit massive crystalline pillars of refined copper, cobalt, and platinum, easily harvested by remote-operated mining dredges.
3. **The Ice-Diving Black Market:** Adventurous Free Captains smuggle rare, frozen Kalyama venom and bioluminescent glandular fluid to luxury syndicates on [[Absalom Station]], where it commands astronomical prices as an ingredient for high-end optics and exotic combat stimulants.

## Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Silent Rig:** All communications from *Deepwater Station Alpha* abruptly cease. [[AbadarCorp]] hires the PCs to take a deep-sea submersible down Grovak’s Bore to investigate. Arriving at the flooded facility, they find the exterior floodlights smashed, the crew trapped in psychic catatonia, and a gargantuan Kalyama stalking the station's submerged glass corridors.
* **The Sunken Pre-Gap Cache:** Sonar surveys near a volcanic sea-trench detect the hull of an ancient, pre-Gap starship that crashed through the ice millions of years ago when Damiar was shattered. The party must dive the wreck in high-pressure suits to retrieve its data core before a Free Captain salvage sub cuts through the hull.
* **Prisoners of the Ice:** A notorious hacker fleeing [[The Stewards]] has retreated to an illegal pirate enclave hidden inside a glacial cavern deep within Nisis. The party must traverse the howling methane surface storms, locate the hidden borehole, and infiltrate the ice bunker before the target sells military encryption keys to the [[Corpse Fleet]].
