> [!info] Quick Facts
> **World & Continent:** **[[Castrovel]]** (The Green Mother), southern coast of **[[Asana]]** (~0.7 AU)
> **Settlement Classification:** Planetary Capital / Spaceport Metropolis / Academic Arcology
> **Population:** ~1,250,000 permanent residents (Plus hundreds of thousands of off-world students, merchants, and tourists)
> **Demographics:** 65% **[[Lashunta]]** (Both Damaya scholars and Korasha warriors), 14% [[Humans]], 8% [[Formians]], 5% [[Elves]], 4% [[Androids]], 4% Other
> **Ruling Authority:** The Bright Matriarch of Qabarat & The High Council of Scholars
> **Key Landmarks:** *The Shell Walls*, *Shipyard of the Sun (Spaceport)*, *Qabarat University of Xenoarchaeology*, *The Battlemarket*, *The Forum of the Mind*
> **Planetary Gateway:** Houses the sovereign terminus of an ancient **[[Aiudara]]** (Elf Gate)

#### Overview: The Jewel on the Sparkling Sea

Perched atop towering sea-cliffs of ancient, petrified iridescent fossil-shell along the warm waters of the Sparkling Sea stands **Qabarat**, the undisputed cultural capital, intellectual epicenter, and primary commercial spaceport of **[[Castrovel]]**.

Revered as *The Jewel of Asana*, Qabarat is an architectural triumph that blends cutting-edge planetary technology with organic harmony. Behind colossal pre-Gap defensive curtain walls carved from solid mother-of-pearl, the city rises in concentric terraces of aerodynamic solar glass, open-air amphitheaters, and hanging botanical gardens. In the streets below, telepathic **[[Lashunta]]** scholars debate philosophy with **[[Formians|Formian]]** diplomats, while off-world starships from [[Absalom Station]] and [[Verces]] descend in silent plumes of ionic exhaust toward the coastal landing pads.

Qabarat is also the primary point of immigration and customs for all off-worlders visiting Castrovel. Due to planetary conservation laws that strictly ban toxic industrial emissions, all starships entering the atmosphere must clear stringent environmental scans at the *Shipyard of the Sun* before passenger disembarkation.

#### Landmarks & Academic Centers

##### 1. The Shipyard of the Sun (Spaceport)
Extending outward over the azure waters of the Sparkling Sea on massive pylons of white coral and titanium:
* Handles all commercial freighters, passenger liners, and diplomatic shuttles arriving from orbit.
* Houses advanced biological de-contamination scrubbers to prevent invasive off-world spores or pathogens from reaching Castrovel's pristine rainforests.

##### 2. Qabarat University of Xenoarchaeology
The most prestigious academic institution on Castrovel and a close institutional partner to the **[[Starfinder Society]]**:
* Renowned across the Pact Worlds for recovering, preserving, and translating pre-Gap artifacts discovered in the jungles of [[Ukulam]] and deep-space ruins.
* Features massive holographic lecture amphitheatres and zero-gravity research vaults where telepathic scholars decode ancient alien scripts.

##### 3. The Battlemarket
The vibrant, bustling merchant and entertainment heart of Qabarat:
* In the center of a sprawling bazaar of exotic jungle fruits, bio-enhancements, and tailored silks sit the historic sand-pits of the Battlemarket.
* Here, *Korasha* warriors and off-world gladiators engage in non-lethal, high-dexterity martial duels to earn military prestige, resolve legal disputes, and test new weapons before cheering crowds.

##### 4. The Aiudara Arch of Qabarat
Concealed within a secured botanical park near the university stands a pristine, active **[[Aiudara]]** (Elf Gate):
* This soaring arch of ancient stone links Qabarat directly to sister arches in **[[Sovyrian]]** and remote sanctuaries across the planet.
* Heavily guarded by an elite detachment of Damaya battle-mystics to prevent unauthorized transit.

#### Politics & The Modern Peace

Qabarat was the birthplace of the historic diplomatic movement that brought an end to the thousand-year war between the Lashunta and the Formians:
* **The Emissary Quarter:** Established following the 287 AG Treaty of Queensrock, this district houses a permanent diplomatic enclave of **[[Formians]]**, who trade bio-architectural schematics and fungal agricultural techniques with lashunta universities.
* **The Bright Matriarch:** Governance is overseen by an elected Damaya statesman who balances the fiery martial impulses of the Korasha defensive garrisons with the progressive, trade-focused agenda of the merchant councils.

#### Campaign Hooks

* **The Contaminated Airlock:** A cargo freighter arriving from [[Akiton]] bypasses environmental quarantine at the Shipyard of the Sun, inadvertently releasing a swarm of predatory parasites into Qabarat's shell-districts. The PCs are deputized by port security to track and exterminate the nest before it spreads into the surrounding jungle.
* **The University Vault Breach:** An uncataloged pre-Gap relic stolen from the University of Xenoarchaeology is linked to a clandestine network of **[[Drow]]** agents operating out of an illicit warehouse along the sea-cliffs. The party must recover the artifact before it is smuggled through an off-world courier.
* **The Arena Sabotage:** During the championship bout of the Battlemarket, a gladiator's plasma blade is sabotaged to detonate upon contact, threatening to ignite an international incident between visiting Vesk diplomats and the Lashunta Council. The PCs must intervene mid-duel to prevent catastrophe.
