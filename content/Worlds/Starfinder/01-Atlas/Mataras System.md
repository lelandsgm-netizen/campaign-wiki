#### Theater Overview: The Crucible of the Galaxy

The **Mataras System**—widely referred to throughout the galaxy simply as the **[[Pact Worlds]]**—is a densely populated, politically complex solar system orbiting the yellow dwarf star **[[Mataras]]**. Despite the disappearance of its historical cradle world during **[[The Gap]]**, the system remains the undisputed crossroads of known space, primarily because the **[[Starstone]]** aboard **[[Absalom Station]]** acts as the galaxy's most powerful navigation beacon inside **[[The Drift]]**.

For nearly three centuries following **[[The Gap]]**, the system's worlds operated as rival, independent powers, fighting localized resource wars and fending off raids from the undead navy of **[[Eox]]** and pirate syndicates in **[[The Diaspora]]**. That fractured era ended in **291 AG** when external aggression from the neighboring reptilian empire of the **[[Veskarium]]** forced the system's diverse civilizations to sign the **Absalom Pact**. Today, the system is governed by representative democracy on [[Absalom Station]], policed by the elite **[[The Stewards|Stewards]]**, and interconnected by constant interplanetary trade.

##### The Pre-Gap Mystery
Every world in the [[Mataras System]] bears scars of pre-Gap history that modern science cannot fully explain. Ancient, solar-shielded cities hang tethered inside the plasma of [[Mataras]], subterranean alien vaults riddle the interior of [[Apostae]], and ancient Drift lanes converge on the void where a forgotten planet once orbited.

#### Manifest of the 14 Celestial Bodies

The following astrogation intelligence reports map out the primary inhabited worlds, stations, and orbital regions of the [[Mataras System]], ordered outward from the sun.

##### 1. [[Mataras]] (The Burning Sun)
* **Status:** Pact Protectorate / Deep-Solar Energy Hub
* **Summary:** The yellow dwarf star at the center of the system is far from uninhabited. Anchored within its roaring solar filaments are the **[[Burning Archipelago]]**—a chain of enchanted, force-shielded bubble cities connected by fireproof conduits. Originally built by pre-Gap planar travelers, these habitats (led by the port city of **[[Dawnshore]]**) are populated by [[Suli]], fire elementals, and [[Efreet]], serving as the system's premier center for plasma mining and stellar research.

##### 2. [[Aballon]] (The Horse)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Construct Technocracy
* **Summary:** The innermost planet of the system is a scorched, cratered, airless rock rich in heavy metals and bathed in intense solar radiation. [[Aballon]] is ruled entirely by the **[[Anacites]]**, a race of self-replicating sentient machines left behind by vanished pre-Gap creators. From towering industrial complexes like **[[First City]]**, the Anacites endlessly harvest solar power and manufacture advanced technology, philosophically split between *Those Who Wait* (for their creators' return) and *Those Who Become* (who seek independent evolution).

##### 3. [[Castrovel]] (The Green Mother)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Multi-State Coalition
* **Summary:** A hot, humid world of steaming primordial jungles, inland seas, and colossal megafauna. [[Castrovel]] is divided among three distinct powers: the telepathic, scholarly **[[Lashunta]]** who govern from sparkling arcology-cities like **[[Qabarat]]**; the insectoid **[[Formians]]**, who recently ended a millennia-long war with the Lashunta; and the isolationist **[[Elves]]** of the continent of **[[Sovyrian]]**, who guard their ancient pre-Gap magical traditions from outsiders.

##### 4. [[Absalom Station]] (The Nexus at the Void)
* **Status:** System Capital / Seat of the [[Pact Council]]
* **Summary:** Orbiting third from the sun in the exact spatial coordinates where the lost world of Golarion vanished during **[[The Gap]]**, [[Absalom Station]] is a five-mile-wide starbase housing millions of citizens from every known species. Powered by the divine **[[Starstone]]** reactor at its core, the station serves as the headquarters for the **[[Pact Council]]**, **[[The Stewards]]**, and the **[[Starfinder Society]]**. Its neighborhoods range from the wealthy corporate towers of the **Eye** to the lawless, gang-controlled lower decks of the **Spike**.

##### 5. [[Akiton]] (The Red Planet)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Post-Industrial Frontier
* **Summary:** Once the wealthy mining powerhouse of the system due to its massive deposits of *thasteron* starship fuel, [[Akiton]] suffered catastrophic economic collapse when Drift engines rendered thasteron obsolete. Today, its rust-red deserts and freezing canyons are a lawless frontier of abandoned corporate ghost towns, nomadic **[[Shobhads]]** (four-armed giants), clever **[[Ysoki]]** salvagers, and bloodthirsty gladiatorial arenas in hubs like **[[Arl]]** and **[[Maro]]**.

##### 6. [[Verces]] (The Ring of Nations)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Cybernetic Confederacy
* **Summary:** [[Verces]] is tidally locked to the sun: one hemisphere is a blistered desert of eternal noon (**Fullbright**), and the other is a frozen wasteland of eternal midnight (**Darkside**). Virtually all civilization is concentrated within the **[[Ring of Nations]]**, a narrow temperate twilight zone encircling the globe. Known as the birthplace of **[[The Stewards]]** and the center of the **[[Augmented]]** movement, [[Verces]] leads the system in cybernetics, biotech, and spacefleet engineering.

##### 7. [[The Diaspora]] (The Shattered Belt)
* **Status:** Represented Territory / Lawless Asteroid Expanse
* **Summary:** Millions of rocky asteroids orbiting between [[Verces]] and [[Eox]], representing the pulverized remains of two twin planets (**Damiar** and **Iovo**) destroyed in antiquity. While **[[Dwarves]]** operate massive hollowed-out mining citadels and the void-adapted **[[Sarcesians]]** string sniper-communes between paired rocks, the belt is notorious as the stronghold of the **[[Free Captains]]**—a ruthless coalition of space pirates ruling from the hidden asteroid haven of **[[Broken Rock]]**.

##### 8. [[The Idari]] (The Eternal Colony Ship)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Sovereign Generation Ship
* **Summary:** A colossal, multi-mile-long cylinder starship permanently parked in an orbit just beyond [[The Diaspora]]. Built ages ago by the four-armed, tradition-bound **[[Kasathas]]** for a sub-light voyage from their dying home star, [[The Idari]] arrived in the [[Mataras System]] only to discover its worlds were already claimed. Its rotating interior drums hold artificial deserts, factories, and ancient temples where Kasatha clans balance ancestral customs with modern galactic politics.

##### 9. [[Eox]] (The Dead World)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Necromantic Oligarchy
* **Summary:** Prior to **[[The Gap]]**, the inhabitants of [[Eox]] destroyed the twin worlds of [[The Diaspora]] with a planet-cracking superweapon, only for the backlash to incinerate their own atmosphere. To survive, the planetary elite—the **[[Bone Sages]]**—transformed themselves into undead liches and necrovites. Today, [[Eox]] is a blasted, radioactive wasteland of bone-spires, necromantic factories, and cruel reality-television bloodsports, shadowed by rumors that its rulers secretly fund the exiled **[[Corpse Fleet]]**.

##### 10. [[Triaxus]] (The Wanderer)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Draconic-Corporate Feudalism
* **Summary:** Due to its wildly eccentric orbital path, [[Triaxus]] experiences seasons that last over a century each. Currently transitioning into its generations-long **Great Winter**, the planet is home to the dimorphic **[[Ryphorians]]** and powerful **True Dragons**. Rather than sleeping on piles of gold, the dragons of the **[[Dragonkin]]** and **[[Allied Territories]]** run cutthroat interplanetary megacorporations, facing off against the militaristic **[[Skyfire Legion]]**.

##### 11. [[Liavara]] (The Dreamer)
* **Status:** Brethedan Protectorate / Ecological Preserve
* **Summary:** A serene, peach-and-gold ringed gas giant protected under strict environmental laws by its larger sister world, [[Bretheda]]. Its upper atmosphere is populated by drifting, ascetic **[[Barathu]]** "Dreamers" whose songs help stabilize the planet's storms, while its numerous moons—such as the resort moon of **[[Roselight]]** and the harsh mining moon of **[[Nchak]]**—host bustling off-world settlements.

##### 12. [[Bretheda]] (The Cradle)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Bio-Technological Collective
* **Summary:** The largest planet in the system is a turbulent blue-green gas giant inhabited by the **[[Barathu]]**—amorphous floating aliens capable of rewriting their own genetic code and merging into massive corporate super-beings. Operating from living, buoyant sky-cities like **[[Sophiar]]**, [[Bretheda]] dominates the system's biological engineering market, growing living starships and administering dozens of inhabited moons across the outer system.

##### 13. [[Apostae]] (The Maiden)
* **Status:** Full Pact Member / Megacorporate Drow Autocracy
* **Summary:** An airless, barren planetoid near the edge of the system that is actually a titanic, millennia-old alien derelict spacecraft captured by [[Mataras]]'s gravity. The surface and upper caverns are ruled by ruthless **[[Drow]]** noble houses who operate as arms-dealing megacorporations from the dark metropolis of **[[Nightarch]]**. Using indentured **[[Orcs]]** and half-orcs as shock troops, the Drow harvest grotesque fleshwarping technology and xenotech from the sealed alien vaults deep inside the planetoid's core.

##### 14. [[Aucturn]] (The Stranger)
* **Status:** Non-Member World / Quarantine Hazard
* **Summary:** The outermost planet of the [[Mataras System]] is a swollen, sickly yellow world swathed in toxic, hallucinogenic mists. Shunned by the [[Pact Council]], [[Aucturn]] is widely believed by occult scholars to be a living, embryonic **Great Old One** slowly gestating at the edge of the system. Its shifting, organic continents are ruled by the enigmatic **[[Carcinoi]]**, Cults of the **[[Elder Mythos]]**, and the nightmare-worshipping oracle **[[Casandalee]]** / **[[Nyarlathotep]]** sects out of **[[Citadel of the Black]]**.
