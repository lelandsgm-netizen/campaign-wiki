#### Overview: The Great Solar Bazaar

If Dawnshore is the spiritual soul of **[[The Burning Archipelago]]**, **Corona** is its roaring economic furnace. Floating on the western arc of the coronal cluster inside **[[Mataras]]**, Corona is the premier interplanar free-port of the [[Pact Worlds]], serving as the gateway where the mortal technology of material civilization meets the exotic sorceries of the **[[Plane of Fire]]**.

Unlike the dignified white marble and tranquil cloisters of Dawnshore, Corona is loud, pungent, chaotic, and relentlessly mercenary. Streets paved in heat-treated volcanic basalt are lined with brass-domed shops, shimmering silks woven from fire-drake scales, and bubbling hookahs venting cinnamon-scented vapor. Over head, the filtered solar sky gleams like molten bronze, reflecting off the polished armor of **[[Efreet]]** trade-barons, salamander artificers, corporate executives from **[[AbadarCorp]]**, and grinning **[[Ysoki]]** scrap-merchants.

##### 1. The Brass Bazaar
The beating heart of Corona. The Brass Bazaar is a colossal, multi-tiered marketplace that occupies the lower half of the bubble:
* **Planar Wares:** Thousands of canopied stalls trade in goods found nowhere else in the Material Plane: crystallized solar flares, bottled plasma-elementals, pure planar brass, magma-diamonds, and rings woven from living flame.
* **Corporate Enclaves:** Megacorporations like **[[AbadarCorp]]** maintain massive glass pavilions where currency brokers exchange Pact credits for planar fire-gems, gold bullion, and soul-signed promissory notes.

##### 2. The Opal Spire (Planar Embassy)
A towering, iridescent needle carved from a single petrified elemental geode:
* Serves as the official diplomatic consulate for the Grand Sultanate of the City of Brass.
* Efreet diplomats negotiate trade tariffs, planar transit permits for the Far Portal, and treaties governing the treatment of extraplanar merchants within Pact territory.

##### 3. The Salamander Smelters
Located near the lower airlocks where raw solar heat can be channeled into specialized crucible-chambers:
* Master salamander smiths forge legendary armor, plasma blades, and heat-resistant hull plating that can withstand the crushing pressures of the solar mantle.
* Weapons forged here bear unique elemental seals that prevent them from degrading in extreme environments.

##### 4. The Ash-Quarter
The shadow-side of Corona. Beneath the glittering bazaar lies a maze of narrow alleyways and steam-pipes where smugglers, fence-bosses, and corsairs from **[[Broken Rock]]** trade in illicit plasma-mining rigs, untraceable data-slates, and contraband dredged up from the Deep Sun.

#### Politics & The Coronal Accord

Corona operates under the **Coronal Accord**, a complex legal treaty co-signed by the Council of Bubble Ministers, **[[The Stewards]]**, and the Ambassador of the City of Brass:
* **The Slavery Ban:** While the City of Brass on the Plane of Fire relies heavily on chattel slavery, the Accord strictly forbids the ownership, sale, or transit of sentient slaves within Corona's force dome. The Dawnguard and Stewards enforce this law ruthlessly, freeing any planar slaves who cross the threshold into the city.
* **Elemental Immunities:** Because dozens of merchant species are composed of living flame or radiate lethal heat, municipal bylaws mandate that all public spaces, linecrawlers, and dining halls provide fire-suppression wards and heat-dampened furniture.

#### Campaign Hooks

* **The Stolen Fire-Gem:** An efreeti noble's prize possession—a flawless ruby containing the trapped essence of a planar noble—is stolen from a vault in the Opal Spire. The noble hires the PCs to find the thief before their personal guard burns the Ash-Quarter to the ground.
* **The Slaver's Smuggling Ring:** An undercover Steward agent goes missing while investigating rumors that an extraplanar cartel is smuggling captive [[Lashunta]] psychics through Corona’s lower cargo iris to be sold into servitude on the Plane of Fire. The PCs are contracted to infiltrate the warehouse and bust the ring.
* **Contract of the Salamander:** A renegade salamander artificer offers the party a set of experimental heat-shielding schematics that would allow a starship to dive deeper into the Deep Sun than ever before. However, hit-squads from an **[[AbadarCorp]]** subsidiary and rival efreet assassins are already closing in to silence him.
