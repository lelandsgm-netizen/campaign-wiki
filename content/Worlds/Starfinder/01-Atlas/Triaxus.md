#### Astrogation Dossier: The Elliptical Nomad

**Triaxus**—universally designated by astronomers as **The Wanderer**—is the ninth planet outward from [[Mataras]]. Unlike its terrestrial peers whose orbits trace near-perfect circular paths, Triaxus travels along an extraordinarily eccentric, elongated elliptical trajectory that spans 317 standard years. At perihelion, the planet draws near the inner warm bands of the system; at aphelion, it swings out into the freezing black void far beyond the orbit of the gas giants.

This extreme celestial mechanic subjects Triaxus to planetary seasons that last well over a century each: the lush, tropical **Great Summer** and the brutal, glacial **Great Winter**. Currently, the planet has transitioned into the early decades of the **Great Winter**: tropical rainforests have withered into frost-shrouded tundra, once-bustling shipping oceans have frozen beneath sheets of pack ice hundreds of feet thick, and civilization has retreated into geothermal arcologies and reinforced mountain bastions. 

Despite this harsh climate, Triaxus is a major geopolitical power within the **[[Pact Worlds]]**, boasting a wealthy corporate economy dominated by boardroom-savvy **True Dragons**, the legendary aerial strike force of **[[The Skyfire Legion]]**, and the biologically adaptable **[[Ryphorians]]**.

#### The Seasons of The Wanderer

The 317-year orbit dictates every facet of Triaxian ecology, biology, and historical architecture:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                           THE TRIAXIAN CENTURY CYCLE                        |
+=============================================================================+
|  [THE GREAT SUMMER]     =====> Warm, humid, tropical; global rainforests,   |
|  (~158 Standard Years)         open oceans, Summerborn Ryphorians dominate. |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                       ||
                                       v
|  [THE GREAT WINTER]     =====> Planetary ice age; glaciers advance from     |
|  (~159 Standard Years)         the poles, oceans freeze, Winterborn rise.   |
|                                (CURRENT PLANETARY EPOCH)                    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Great Summer
During the warm half of the orbital cycle, Triaxus basks in solar heat. Glaciers retreat, exposing vast fertile river valleys, sprawling rainforests, and warm archipelagos. Agriculture booms, shipping lanes span the open waters, and planetary culture adopts an expansive, exuberant outlook.

##### 2. The Great Winter (Current Epoch)
As Triaxus swings toward deep space, solar radiation plummets. Over several decades of autumn, temperatures collapse, driving ferocious planetary blizzards and sheets of ice across the continents. 
* **The Glacial Freeze:** Today, the northern continent of **Ayling** and the southern **Drakelands** are locked in a relentless planetary winter. 
* **Technological Mitigation:** In ancient pre-Gap eras, the onset of the Great Winter triggered horrific societal collapse, mass starvation, and savage migration wars. In the modern era, high technology has fundamentally transformed the winter: massive geothermal tap-plants, atmospheric thermal-retention domes, nuclear-heated sub-ice transit tunnels, and planetary import lines from [[Castrovel]] allow civilization to thrive in sub-zero conditions.

#### Native Inhabitants & Biology

Triaxus is home to two prominent sentient native species whose histories and destinies are inextricably intertwined.

##### The [[Ryphorians]] & Environmental Dimorphism
The dominant humanoids of Triaxus are the **Ryphorians**: an elven-proportioned species featuring pointed ears and a remarkable, genetically hardcoded adaptive trait known as *seasonal dimorphism*.
* **Winterborn Ryphorians:** Born during or leading into the Great Winter, their bodies develop a thick, insulating coat of stark white fur, feathered ear tufts, dark heat-absorbing skin pigmentation around the eyes, and specialized circulatory systems that prevent frostbite in temperatures dropping to $-50^\circ\text{F}$.
* **Summerborn Ryphorians:** Born during the warm epoch, their bodies are hairless with sleek, golden or olive skin, wide heat-dissipating ears, and dense sweat glands adapted to tropical humidity.
* **Modern Dimorphism:** With the rise of indoor climate control, off-world travel, and gene-therapy clinics, the strict seasonal divide has blurred. Winterborn and Summerborn now live side by side year-round, and Ryphorians can alter their seasonal traits via safe cosmetic bio-treatments.

##### The [[Dragonkin]] & The Soul-Bond
Sharing the skies of Triaxus are the **Dragonkin**: large, intelligent, bipedal draconic beings standing 8 to 10 feet tall with powerful leathery wings and fiery breath weapons.
* **The Bond:** Dragonkin possess a sacred spiritual and telepathic ability known simply as *the Bond*. A Dragonkin may choose to form a permanent, lifelong psychological union with a trusted humanoid partner (most frequently a Ryphorian). Bonded partners share emotional states, spatial awareness, and unspoken tactical combat intuition, operating in battle as a single, lethal organism.

#### Geopolitics: Democracies, Dragon CEOs & The Skyfire Legion

Triaxus’s mortal politics are divided between two diametrically opposed continental philosophies:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             GEOPOLITICS OF TRIAXUS                          |
+=============================================================================+
|  [THE ALLIED TERRITORIES]      |  [THE DRAKELANDS]                          |
|  Democratic, constitutional    |  Feudal-corporate empires ruled overtly    |
|  parliamentary coalition;      |  by True Dragon CEOs and venture banks;    |
|  high-tech financial hubs.     |  ruthless corporate capitalism.            |
+--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
|                       [THE SKYFIRE LEGION]                                  |
|  Elite mercenary air cavalry; bonded Dragonkin & Ryphorian knights;        |
|  impartial defenders of peace and planetary sovereignty.                    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. [[The Allied Territories]] (The Free Republics)
Occupying the temperate equatorial regions of the northern continent of **Ayling**, the Allied Territories is a wealthy, democratic federation of sovereign nation-states.
* **[[Kamiat]]:** The cosmopolitan financial capital of Triaxus. Built inside towering, climate-controlled spire-arcologies, Kamiat is the seat of the Allied Parliament and home to the headquarters of Triaxian aerospace manufacturers and tech developers.
* **Ideology:** Committed to individual rights, open commerce, and constitutional law, the Allied Territories serve as the primary democratic voice representing Triaxus on the [[Pact Council]] on [[Absalom Station]].

##### 2. [[The Drakelands]] (The Corporate Feudalists)
In stark contrast to the democracies of Ayling lies the southern continent of the **Drakelands**.
* **Boardroom Dragons:** In the ancient past, Triaxian **True Dragons** ruled mortal kingdoms through fire and terror. Over millennia, the dragons recognized that hoarding coins in mountain caves was primitive. In the modern era, they took over the corporate sector: dragons incorporated themselves into multinational conglomerates, trading breath weapons for corporate mergers, shareholder buyouts, and high-frequency financial trading.
* **Draconic Conglomerates:** Today, ancient chromatic and metallic dragons serve as chief executive officers, managing vast industrial monopolies, mining concessions, and manufacturing mills. Mortal workers sign binding lifelong employment covenants, trading loyalty for warm housing and security within draconic arcologies.

##### 3. [[The Skyfire Legion]]
Based in the fortified mountain aerie-citadel of **Chuton**, the **Skyfire Legion** is one of the most celebrated and formidable martial institutions in the Pact Worlds.
* **Airborne Cavalry & Space Aces:** The Legion comprises bonded pairs of Ryphorian aerial knights and their Dragonkin partners. They train from youth to fight as unified strike teams, piloting custom starfighters, boarding dropships, or conducting atmospheric drop-attacks armed with heavy laser-cannons and plasma lances.
* **Neutral Guardians:** While headquartered in the Allied Territories, the Skyfire Legion remains an independent, highly honorable mercenary organization. They contract their elite battalions across the solar system to defend commercial shipping lines against pirates from [[The Diaspora]] and reinforce the border garrisons of [[The Stewards]].

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Corporate Dragon Hostile Takeover:** A ruthless Blue Dragon CEO of a massive Drakelands industrial conglomerate has orchestrated a violent, under-the-table cyber-attack against a rival aerospace shipyard in [[Kamiat]]. The PCs are hired by an Allied Territories executive to retrieve the encrypted intrusion logs from a high-security penthouse vault before dragonkin enforcers sanitize the crime scene.
* **Skyfire Bond Breach:** A decorated Dragonkin officer of [[The Skyfire Legion]] has fallen into a catatonic coma following the mysterious assassination of their bonded Ryphorian partner during a blizzard patrol in the Parapet Mountains. The party is retained by the Legion commander to track the assassin’s snow-crawler tracks into the deep glaciers to uncover an off-world plot funded by the [[Corpse Fleet]].
* **The Frozen Leviathan of the Deep Ice:** The creeping glaciers of the Great Winter have crushed the hull of an ancient, pre-Gap research outpost buried beneath the ice sheet of the Frozen Sea. An SOS ping alerts the player characters to a stasis vault containing pristine pre-Gap weapon schematics—leading to a desperate subterranean race against rival dwarven salvagers and apex frost worms.
