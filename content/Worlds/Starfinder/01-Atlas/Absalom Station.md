#### Astrogation Dossier: The Crossroads of the Galaxy

**Absalom Station**—universally referred to across spacer cant simply as **The Station** or **The Nexus at the Void**—is the undisputed political, cultural, and navigational capital of the [[Pact Worlds]]. Orbiting exactly third from the sun where the vanished cradle world of Golarion turned before **[[The Gap]]**, this five-mile-wide artificial starbase is home to millions of sentients representing every recognized species in the galaxy.

Absalom Station's preeminence does not stem merely from its central position or diplomatic assembly. Deep within the station’s heavily armored ventral keel burns the **[[Starstone]]**—an ancient, divine relic that powers the entire station's life support and shields while simultaneously projecting a psychic hyper-beacon across the planar dimensions. Because every Drift Engine in the galaxy can calibrate directly to the Starstone, starships traveling through **[[The Drift]]** can reach Absalom Station from any corner of known space in a mere $1\text{d}6$ days, cementing the station as the supreme hyper-spatial hub of the cosmos.

#### Macro-Structure & Station Geography

Viewed from approaching void-craft, Absalom Station resembles a glittering, multifaceted jewel or a stylized metallic sunburst. The station is engineered in four distinct vertical and radial macro-sectors:

##### 1. [[The Eye]] (The Pinnacle of Power & Wealth)
Capping the apex of the central spindle, The Eye is a transparent, dome-shaped super-sector sheltered beneath meters of diamond-hard force fields. Bathed in artificial daylight and pristine starry nights, the Eye is the domain of corporate executives, planetary ambassadors, and elite academia.
* **The Plenara:** The monumental parliamentary amphitheater where the delegates of the **[[Pact Council]]** debate system policy, allocate defense fleets, and negotiate interplanetary treaties.
* **The Lorespire Complex:** The sprawling headquarters of the **[[Starfinder Society]]**. Centered around a skyward-thrusting spire of green glass, the complex houses the legendary *Archives*, the *Hall of Discovery*, the technology laboratories, and the private offices of the First Seeker and the advisory AI known as *Guidance*.
* **Jatembe Park:** A lush, five-square-mile botanical wonderland of ancient imported trees, rolling emerald lawns, and tranquil lagoons, dedicated to the legendary pre-Gap archmage Old-Mage Jatembe.

##### 2. [[The Ring]] (The Beating Commercial Heart)
The vast, disc-shaped deck sector encircling the base of the Eye. The Ring houses the bulk of the station’s middle-class citizens, bustling shopping districts, entertainment hubs, and corporate outposts.
* **The Freemarket:** A chaotic, multi-level bazaar occupying several square blocks where spacers, merchants, and smugglers buy and sell everything from Vercesian cybernetics to Akitonian thasteron crystals.
* **The Arcanamirium:** The premier university of the Pact Worlds, dedicated to blending traditional spellcasting with modern computer science, technomancy, and planar mechanics.
* **Bastion:** The monolithic, heavily armored fortress-citadel serving as the galactic headquarters of **[[The Stewards]]**. From Bastion, Director-Generals deploy peacekeeper squads, dispatch gunships, and coordinate interplanetary intelligence.
* **Cultural Enclaves:** Neighborhoods such as *Little Akiton* (populated by [[Ysoki]] warrens and [[Shobhads]]), *The Neon Trench* (cybernetic clinics and night clubs), and *The Vesk Quarter* (martial shrines and reptilian eateries).

##### 3. [[The Arms]] (The Ports of the Galaxy)
Extending outward from the outer rim of the Ring like the spokes of an immense wheel are dozens of radial docking pylons known collectively as The Arms.
* **Starship Berths & Customs:** Thousands of starships—from swift exploratory couriers to colossal bulkhead freighters—dock along the Arms daily. Cargo gantries, pressurized boarding tubes, fuel siphons, and customs checkpoints hum with round-the-clock activity.
* **Dockside Culture:** The Arms are legendary for rough spacer bars, bunkhouse motels, black-market pawn shops, and mercenary hiring halls, anchored by watering holes like the *Moons of Sleep* and *The Tooth & Nail*.

##### 4. [[The Spike]] (Downside & The Underbelly)
Stretching downward beneath the radial plane like an enormous inverted cone or dagger, The Spike is the dark, neglected industrial spine of Absalom Station.
* **Industrial Levels:** Dense, noise-filled sublevels housing water purification plants, atmospheric scrubbers, geothermal cooling towers, and hydroponic nutrient vats.
* **Downlow & Fogtown:** Cramped, impoverished districts shrouded in leaking coolant steam, flickering holograms, and condensation runoff. Dominated by street gangs like the *Level 21 Crew* and private security enforcers working for corrupt mining combines.

##### 5. The Ghost Levels & The [[Starstone]] Core
Beneath the lowest inhabited decks of the Spike lie the **Ghost Levels**—hundreds of sealed, dark sublevels unmapped by modern station blueprints.
* **The Ghost Levels:** Derelict corridors, abandoned pre-Gap research vaults, and airless chambers infested with feral vermin, mutant horrors, and rogue combat drones. Explorers who descend into the Ghost Levels either emerge with fortune in pre-Gap tech or never return.
* **The Starstone Reactor:** Anchored at the absolute physical center of the Spike's lowest point. Enclosed within meters of impenetrable alien shielding, the divine Starstone generates clean, limitless energy for the entire station and emits the beacon that guides the galaxy through [[The Drift]]. Access to the core is strictly forbidden, guarded by elite autonomous defensive systems and the devout warrior-clerics of [[Iomedae]].

#### The Orbital Cloud: [[The Armada]]

Surrounding Absalom Station in a loose, shifting orbital cloud is **The Armada**—a sprawling, unanchored flotilla of thousands of independent starships.

Composed of nomadic spacers, belter miners from [[The Diaspora]], freelance traders, and corporate vessels that refuse to pay station docking fees, the Armada functions as an autonomous, floating shadow-city. While technically beyond the jurisdiction of Station Security, the Armada is loosely regulated by spacer councils and can be rallied by [[The Stewards]] to form a formidable auxiliary defense fleet in times of system crisis.

#### History & The Mystery of 0 AG

When mortal memory and historical records abruptly flickered back to life at the end of **[[The Gap]]** in **0 AG**, the citizens of the system found Golarion missing. In its exact coordinates floated Absalom Station, fully crewed, powered, and operational. 

No individual aboard could remember building the station, nor could divine divination reveal who had laid its titanium bulkheads. The presence of pre-Gap monuments like Jatembe Park, the Arcanamirium, and the Starstone itself proved that the station was intimately tied to vanished Golarion, prompting the belief that the gods or ancient masters constructed the starbase as an ark to preserve mortal civilization before Golarion was hidden away.

#### Governance & Dual Sovereignty

Absalom Station maintains a unique, overlapping system of municipal and systemic authority:
* **Municipal Administration:** Station civil affairs, policing, and municipal taxation are overseen by the **Prime Executive** (currently Kumara Melacruz) alongside the **Council of Syndics**, an elected representative body drawn from the station’s various districts, guilds, and corporate factions.
* **The Systemic Directorate:** As the host world for the **[[Pact Council]]**, the station enjoys autonomous protectorate status under the Absalom Pact. System-wide legislation, interplanetary trade law, and foreign military diplomacy with powers like the [[Veskarium]] are adjudicated directly in the Plenara.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Ghost Level Breach:** A maintenance conduit in the upper Spike ruptures, releasing strange, necrotic cyber-constructs originating from the unexplored Ghost Levels. Station Security hires the party to descend into the unmapped depths to weld the bulkhead shut and locate the source of the breach.
* **Espionage in the Plenara:** A delegate representing the mining syndicates of [[The Diaspora]] is assassinated inside a high-security embassy suite in the Eye hours before a critical vote on corporation tax tariffs. [[The Stewards]] recruit the crew as neutral outside investigators to uncover the assassin before riots break out.
* **Smuggler Run in the Armada:** The party is hired to deliver an unregistered pre-Gap datapad to an eccentric kasatha collector living aboard a modified generation-freighter in the Armada. When corporate gunboats from [[AbadarCorp]] move in to impound the vessel, a zero-g firefight erupts through the docking rings.
