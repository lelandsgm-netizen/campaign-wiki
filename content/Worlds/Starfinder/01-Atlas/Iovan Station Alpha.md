# Iovan Station Alpha (The Mysterious Beacon)

> [!info] Site Dossier
> **Location:** Uncharted Sector, **[[The Diaspora]]** (Drifting orbit)
> **Site Type:** Hollow Asteroid Bastion / Pre-Gap Military Research Installation
> **Origin:** Ancient Pre-Gap Elebrian (Constructed before the cataclysm of Eox and the shattering of Damiar)
> **Current Status:** Recently Awakened / Broadcasting Encrypted Beacon
> **Contracting Patron:** **[[Ulrikka Clanholdings]]** (Operating from **[[Star Citadel Theodrane]]**)
> **Atmosphere:** Degraded / Vacuum in breached bays; restored life-support in sealed central compartments
> **Gravity:** Variable Microgravity ($0.1\text{ to }0.3\text{ G}$)
> **Known Hazards:** Derelict defense grids, automated security constructs, vacuum decompression, dormant scrap vermin

## Overview & The Awakening

To external long-range starship sensors, **Iovan Station Alpha** appears to be nothing more than one among millions of silent, craggy nickel-iron rocks tumbling aimlessly through the cold void of **[[The Diaspora]]**. 

Beneath its jagged, crater-pocked stone exterior, however, lies an ancient pre-Gap elebrian military outpost. Constructed millennia ago during the interplanetary wars that preceded the apocalypse of [[Eox]] and the destruction of the sister worlds Damiar and Iovo, the station was designed to survive direct planetary bombardment, functioning as a high-security research and observation enclave embedded deep within hollowed bedrock.

For centuries, the installation drifted in absolute dormancy, its power cores dead, its corridors sealed, and its historical records lost to [[The Gap]]. Recently, however, an incomprehensible cosmic ripple jolted its ancient technomagical capacitors back to life. status monitors flared, long-dormant conduits began drawing residual solar radiation, and the station began broadcasting an **encrypted repeating beacon** across the void.

## The Race for the Beacon

The activation of the station's beacon has triggered a quiet, high-stakes covert struggle across the Diaspora:

### 1. The Dwarven Initiative ([[Ulrikka Clanholdings]])
Deep-space acoustic sensors at **[[Star Citadel Theodrane]]** were the first to isolate the beacon's rhythm. Recognizing the mathematical markers of pre-Gap engineering, clan leadership mobilized quickly:
* Rather than risking corporate survey ships that might alert competitors, Ulrikka Vice President Belenze Shadraz has sought out discrete, capable freelance contractors on [[Absalom Station]] to locate the drifting asteroid, dock in secret, and extract the station's core logs.

### 2. The Shadow of the Corpse Fleet
Unfortunately, the dwarves are not the only ones listening to the void:
* Long-range listening pickets belonging to the rogue **[[Corpse Fleet]]** have also detected the anomalous transmission. 
* Believing the installation may hold classified naval codes, ancient weapon schematics, or tactical coordinates from Eox's lost military empire, black-hulled Corpse Fleet cruisers have begun altering their patrol trajectories through the belt, racing toward the coordinates of the beacon.

## What Lies Within

Explorers who breach the station's outer docking airlock step into a time capsule frozen since the dawn of the system:
* **The Living Quarters:** Ancient pre-Gap artifacts, mummified remains, and personal journals chronicling the terrifying final hours before the ancient catastrophe.
* **The Arcanatech Laboratories:** Experimental chambers holding sapient energy spheres, strange holographic projections, and forgotten planetary telemetry.
* **The Command Sanctum:** The heart of the installation, where an ancient solid-state terminal continues to transmit the encrypted signal—guarding a secret that could alter the political and metaphysical landscape of the entire [[Pact Worlds]].
