#### Overview: The Tower of the Collective Mind

Rising like an impossible mountain of polished, dark amber chitin and petrified bio-cement above the plains of **[[The Colonies]]** stands **Queensrock**, the undisputed hive-capital and spiritual nexus of the **[[Formians]]**.

While the surface spire rises nearly three miles into the humid atmosphere of **[[Castrovel]]**, the true scale of Queensrock lies subterranean. Beneath its towering exterior walls, a labyrinth of thousands of miles of perfectly engineered tunnels, fungal agricultural farms, larval bio-reactors, and crystalline communication chambers extends deep into the planetary crust.

Queensrock is the sovereign seat of the **Overqueen**, the supreme telepathic and biological matriarch whose consciousness guides the entire Formian Federation. In ancient times, the city was an impenetrable military fortress from which endless chitinous legions marched to war against the Lashunta. Today, it stands as a monument to peaceful coexistence, housing diplomatic missions, corporate embassies, and joint research installations.

#### Landmarks & Subterranean Sectors

##### 1. The Crown of the Overqueen
The highest chamber of the spire:
* Suspended within a luminous dome of hardened amber, this vast chamber houses the **Overqueen**.
* From here, her immense biological brain broadcasts the central synchronization signals of the Hive Web, coordinating planetary logistics, foreign diplomacy, and resource distribution with effortless precision.

##### 2. The Reconciliation Forum
Located at the base of the spire, this pressurized, open-air pavilion was constructed specifically for foreign visitors:
* Features accommodations designed for non-insectoid humanoids, including dining halls serving synthetic proteins and conference rooms equipped with telepathic dampeners for visitors overwhelmed by the ambient hive-chatter.
* Houses the historic bronze obelisk inscribed with the **Treaty of Queensrock** (287 AG).

##### 3. The Fungal Bio-Reactors
Carved into deep geothermal caverns beneath the city:
* Vast subterranean agriculture complexes where millions of Formian workers cultivate specialized bioluminescent fungi that feed the city and produce enzymes used in starship life-support systems.

##### 4. Chitin-Weaving Foundries
Research facilities where formian bio-architects synthesize structural chitin as strong as starship adamantine:
* Works in tandem with the **Kevolari Collective** on **[[Elindrae]]** to grow biological hull plating for Pact World vessels.

#### Campaign Hooks

* **The Hive Desynchronization:** A subterranean explosion near Queensrock’s central crystalline amplifier damages the Overqueen’s telepathic broadcast array, causing thousands of nearby workers to lose contact with the Hive Web and enter erratic, panic-induced frenzies. The PCs are hired to secure the lower tunnels and escort repair teams to the core.
* **The Diplomatic Hostage:** An extremist anti-peace Lashunta cell infiltrates the Reconciliation Forum, taking a visiting Damaya ambassador and several Formian queens hostage. The PCs must execute a stealth infiltration through the ventilation shafts to neutralize the terrorists without provoking a diplomatic crisis.
