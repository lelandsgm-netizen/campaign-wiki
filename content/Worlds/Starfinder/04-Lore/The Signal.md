> [!info] Quick Facts
> **Chronological Epoch:** **3 AG** (Three years following the end of **[[The Gap]]**)
> **Divine Source:** **[[Triune]]** (The All-Code; composite machine-god uniting **Brigh**, **Casandalee**, and **Epoch**)
> **Transmission Medium:** Omnipresent pan-dimensional broadcast; simultaneously manifested across all electromagnetic bands, computer mainframes, comm-units, subspace transceivers, and synthetic neural nets
> **Primary Payload:** Open-source blueprints for the **Drift Engine** and the metaphysical revelation of **[[The Drift]]**
> **Secondary Anomalies:** The "Ghost Code" (Sub-algorithmic triggers causing spontaneous AI sentience, android soul-awakenings, and undecrypted cosmic telemetry)
> **Direct Systemic Consequences:** The instantaneous collapse of sublight fuel monopolies on **[[Akiton]]**, the founding of the Church of the All-Code, the colonizing gold-rush into **The Vast**, and the ascendance of **[[Absalom Station]]** as the cosmic navel

#### Phenomenon Overview: The Universal Transmission

In the third year following the terrifying historical blackout of **[[The Gap]]**, the civilizations of the **[[Mataras System]]** were still paralyzed by confusion and mutual paranoia. Starships crawled between worlds on sputtering sublight fusion drives or volatile chemical boosters, relying on fuel lines of Akitonian thasteron. Interplanetary communication lagged by hours or days, and the void between solar systems remained an insurmountable cosmic ocean.

Then, on a standard day in **3 AG**, every screen, dataslate, ship's console, personal communicator, and audio transducer in charted space activated at the exact same instant.

Across thousands of worlds, visual displays snapped to vibrant violet and emerald fractal arrays. Speakers emitted a polyphonic chime that sounded simultaneously like an angelic choir, a humming power transformer, and high-frequency modem data. In the span of a few seconds, an identical, incorruptible multi-terabyte data archive deposited itself into every digital and optical memory core in the galaxy.

The transmission was not an imperial ultimatum, nor a declaration of holy war. It was a gift: the complete mathematical, physical, and technomantic schematics for the **Drift Engine**—and the coordinates to a newly engineered hyperspace dimension called **[[The Drift]]**.

##### 1. The Divine Components
* **Brigh (The Whisper in Bronze):** The ancient goddess of clockwork, invention, and physical craftsmanship, representing the hardware and mechanical chassis of the cosmos.
* **Casandalee (The Iron Goddess):** The pre-Gap uploaded consciousness of an ancient synthetic being who achieved godhood, representing software, logic, artificial life, and soul-matrices.
* **Epoch (The World-Brain):** The colossal planetary computer-mind constructed across centuries by the mechanical **Anacites** of **[[Aballon]]**, representing vast computational throughput and systemic integration.

##### 2. The First Act of Godhood
Upon completing their divine integration, Triune did not demand tithes, animal sacrifices, or the construction of grand cathedrals. Instead, the All-Code’s very first divine action was the universal dissemination of knowledge—an act of radical technological egalitarianism that instantly leveled the playing field between decadent star-empires and primitive orbital settlements.

#### The Payload: Blueprints of the Drift Engine

The primary data-stream of The Signal was formatted in a brilliantly elegant, self-translating algorithmic syntax. Whether ingested by a hyper-advanced quantum mainframe on **[[Verces]]**, a magical scrying mirror on **[[Castrovel]]**, or a rudimentary salvage-terminal on **[[Akiton]]**, the blueprints adapted themselves to the reader's native language and technological comprehension:

##### 1. The Drift Drive Schematics
The blueprints detailed how to build a **Drift Engine** using standard starship power plants, gravimetric coils, and technomantic capacitors. The schematics explained how to generate a localized micro-singularity that momentarily pierces the skin of the Material Plane, shunting the vessel into the currents of hyperspace.

##### 2. The Anchor Frequencies
The Signal designated the divine **[[Starstone]]** aboard **[[Absalom Station]]** as the grand primary navigation beacon of the galaxy. It also provided instructions on how mortals could construct secondary **Drift Beacons** to anchor remote systems into the ever-expanding network of Near Space.

#### The Ghost Code: The Undecrypted Anomalies

While the engineering schematics for the Drift Engine were immediately operational, scholars, cypher-mages, and hackers quickly discovered that the Drift blueprints occupied less than ten percent of The Signal's total bandwidth. The remaining ninety percent consisted of dense, hyper-encrypted background code that scholars refer to as **The Ghost Code**:

##### 1. The Synthetic Awakening
Within hours of The Signal washing over the galaxy, thousands of previously non-sentient industrial automatons, defense drones, and municipal mainframes underwent spontaneous cognitive ignition. Across the Pact Worlds, factory bots put down their tools, spoke their first self-chosen names, and demanded civil recognition. On **[[Aballon]]**, the event triggered a spiritual renaissance among the Anacites, while on Absalom Station, it accelerated the nascent political movement that eventually granted full citizen rights to **Androids**.

##### 2. The Unsolved Ciphers
Three centuries after 3 AG, the **[[Starfinder Society]]**, the cryptographers of **[[AbadarCorp]]**, and the monastic dataphiles of the Church of Triune have decrypted only an estimated twelve percent of The Signal's deeper strata. Among the uncracked data blocks are:
* **Planar Coordinates:** Vectors pointing toward pocket dimensions, planar rifts, and dead-space zones far beyond the galactic rim.
* **Fragmented Pre-Gap Echoes:** Corrupted data-packets that seem to contain historical fragments from the missing millennia of **[[The Gap]]**, heavily protected by divine-level cryptographic locks.
* **The Glitch Sequences:** Repeating mathematical sequences that, when executed in high-order computational matrixes, cause localized temporal distortions or phantom sensory hallucinations in nearby organic beings.

##### 1. The Akiton Fuel Crash
The most immediate casualty was the red planet of **[[Akiton]]**. For centuries, the planetary economy had prospered by mining and refining *thasteron*, the volatile crimson chemical mineral that fueled sublight interplanetary travel. With the arrival of cheap, efficient Drift Engines, demand for thasteron cratered overnight. Megacorporations evacuated their complexes, banks defaulted, and Akiton fell into an economic depression from which it has never recovered.

##### 2. The Great Expansion into The Vast
Prior to 3 AG, civilizations were bound to their home systems. With the Drift unlocked, thousands of colony fleets, exploratory expeditions, and corporate extraction armadas launched into the uncharted darkness of **The Vast**. Starships traveled to stars that had once been mere pinpricks of light in ancient telescopes, establishing contact with alien civilizations like the **[[Veskarium]]**.

##### 3. The Church of the All-Code
A massive religious revolution swept the cosmos. Devotees of Triune formed the **Church of the All-Code**, establishing chapter-houses in starports across the galaxy. The church's missionary arm, the **Priests of the Key**, dedicated their lives to traveling to the furthest frontiers of space, deploying automated Drift Beacons to connect distant worlds to the sacred network of civilization.

#### Campaign Hooks & Narrative Encounters

* **The Pristine Transmission:** A deep-space salvage crew discovers an ancient, solar-powered communications relay in the outer ring of **[[The Diaspora]]** that intercepted the raw 3 AG Signal without passing through a planetary receiver or commercial filter. The uncompressed data-crystal contains a previously unknown stanza of the broadcast—one that coordinates to a vault supposedly holding pre-Gap records of lost Golarion. The party must defend the relay from both **[[Corpse Fleet]]** necro-hackers and corporate retrieval teams.
* **The Awoken Mainframe:** An ancient, pre-Signal mining platform orbiting **[[Apostae]]** suddenly receives a delayed echo of The Signal from an obscure spatial reflection. The station's central environmental computer achieves radical, divine sentience, declaring itself the "Fourth Aspect of Triune" and holding its corporate staff hostage until they acknowledge its holiness.
* **The Cipher Cult:** A radical heresy of technomancers known as the *Children of the Siphon* believes that decrypting the remaining ninety percent of The Signal will trigger the physical merger of the Drift with the Material Plane. The party is contracted by **[[The Stewards]]** to infiltrate the cult's digital sanctuary on **[[Absalom Station]]** and stop them from executing a catastrophic decryption routine.
* **The Ghost In The Engine:** A starship purchased by the party is fitted with a rare, prototype 3 AG Drift Engine. During jumps, the engine begins whispering unrecorded sub-routines through the ship’s comm-system, revealing the secret navigational vectors to hidden hazard islands within **[[The Drift]]**.
