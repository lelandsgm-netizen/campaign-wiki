> [!info] Quick Facts
> **Plane Classification:** Transitive / Artificial Planar Dimension (Divine Hyperspace)
> **Creator & Divine Overlord:** **[[Triune]]** (The All-Code; tripartite deity uniting Brigh, Casandalee, and Epoch)
> **Historical Genesis:** Revealed to the galaxy in **3 AG** through **[[The Signal]]**
> **Primary Navigational Anchors:** **Drift Beacons** (Supreme Anchor: The **[[Starstone]]** aboard **[[Absalom Station]]**)
> **Transit Time Metric:** Governed by **Beacon Density** and engine rating, *independent* of physical Realspace distance ($1\text{d}6$ days to Absalom Station; $3\text{d}6$ days across the Pact Worlds; $5\text{d}6$ days into the Vast)
> **The Dark Cost:** The **Planar Siphon** (Every Drift jump rips a physical chunk of a random Great Wheel plane into the Drift)
> **Common Hazards:** Planar flotsam islands, Drift dead-zones, abyssal horrors, Drift stalkers, and trans-dimensional static storms
> **Geopolitical Consequence:** Sparked the rapid colonization of The Vast, rendered sublight fuels obsolete, and established Absalom Station as the navel of galactic civilization

#### Phenomenon Overview: The Machine-God’s Highway

Before the year **3 AG**, the void between stars was an impassable desert. Interstellar travel was the exclusive domain of colossal generation ships like **[[The Idari]]**, dangerous high-arcane teleportation gates like the Arch of **[[Apostae]]**, or erratic spells that dragged vessels through the lethal fires of the Astral or Shadow Planes. For ordinary mortals, reaching a neighboring star system took decades or centuries of sublight crawling powered by combustible minerals like Akitonian thasteron.

Everything changed three years after the end of **[[The Gap]]**. The newly ascended tripartite machine-god **[[Triune]]** broadcast **[[The Signal]]**—a divine, pan-galactic transmission that delivered the open-source schematics for the **Drift Engine** into every computer core, comm-array, and dataslate in charted space. 

By activating a Drift Engine, a starship can slip free of the Material Plane, entering an artificial hyperspace dimension known simply as **The Drift**. Within this surreal, kaleidoscope-colored realm of shifting pastel mists, crystalline data-streams, and humming planar currents, conventional astronomical distance ceases to function. Starships traverse thousands of light-years in mere days or weeks, making interstellar trade, federal governance, and galactic exploration a routine reality for the **[[Pact Worlds]]**.

Yet this divine miracle carries an insidious, hidden cost—one that threatens the metaphysical equilibrium of the cosmos itself.

#### The Mechanics of Drift Travel: The Law of Beacons

The physics of the Drift confound traditional astrophysicists because spatial distance in the Material Plane has almost zero correlation with travel time in hyperspace:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                      THE DRIFT TRANSIT PARADOX                              |
+=============================================================================+
| [MATERIAL REALSPACE] ===> Star A and Star B are physically adjacent (1 LY). |
|                           Star C is on the galactic rim (10,000 LY away).   |
|                                       ||                                    |
|                                       v                                     |
| [DRIFT REALM REALITY]===> If Star C hosts a high-output DRIFT BEACON and     |
|                           Star B has none, travel to Star C takes 5 DAYS,   |
|                           while travel to Star B takes 3 WEEKS.             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Beacon Network
Navigation within the Drift is not guided by stellar cartography, but by trans-dimensional homing beacons that emit continuous quantum-resonance signals through hyperspace:
* **The Starstone Anchor:** The ancient, divine **[[Starstone]]** powering the reactor core of **[[Absalom Station]]** emits the most powerful beacon in existence. Because its signal cuts through the Drift like a supernova, any starship in the galaxy can calibrate its engines to Absalom Station and arrive in just $1\text{d}6$ days, regardless of starting location.
* **The Priests of the Key:** Devotees of Triune and contract surveyors from **[[AbadarCorp]]** travel to frontier stars to deploy automated, self-powered Drift Beacons. Systems with dense beacon clusters are classified as **Near Space**, enjoying fast, predictable travel times.
* **The Vast:** Unmapped regions lacking beacons require ships to blindly navigate Drift currents using dead-reckoning algorithms, resulting in journeys of $5\text{d}6$ days or longer.

##### 2. Standard Transit Durations
Drift travel times are rolled randomly by starship navigators, modified by the vessel's Drift Engine Rating ($1$ to $5$):

| Destination Classification | Drift Beacon Density | Base Travel Time ($\Delta t$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[[Absalom Station]]** | Supreme (Starstone Core) | $1\text{d}6$ Days |
| **[[Pact Worlds]] System** | Maximum Grid Density | $3\text{d}6$ Days / Engine Rating |
| **Near Space** | Moderate Beacon Clustered | $3\text{d}6$ Days / Engine Rating |
| **The Vast (Frontier)** | Sparse to Nonexistent | $5\text{d}6$ Days / Engine Rating |

#### The Dark Cosmology: The Planar Siphon

For decades, the galaxy celebrated Triune's gift as an unvarnished blessing. However, as arcane researchers, **[[Xenowardens]]**, and planar scholars investigated the mechanics of Drift transitions, they uncovered a horrifying metaphysical truth known as **The Planar Siphon** (or *The Drift Tear*):

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         THE PLANAR SIPHON EFFECT                            |
+=============================================================================+
| [DRIFT TRANSITION] ===> A starship activates its Drift Engine, tearing      |
|                         through the veil of the Material Plane.             |
|                                    ||                                       |
|                                    v                                        |
| [PLANAR CONSUMPTION]==> To make room for the vessel and seal the rift,      |
|                         the Drift snatches a physical chunk of a random     |
|                         Outer or Inner Plane (Hell, Heaven, Abyss, Fire).   |
|                                    ||                                       |
|                                    v                                        |
| [PLANAR ACCRETION] ===> The stolen chunk is deposited permanently into the  |
|                         Drift as a floating, surreal "Hazard Island."       |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Erosion of the Outer Planes
The Drift is not a natural dimension that existed since the dawn of time; it is an expanding parasite realm created by Triune. To expand and maintain stability, every single time a Drift Engine is activated anywhere in the galaxy, a piece of another plane—be it an iron tower from the Nine Hells, a floating graveyard from the Boneyard, a magma pocket from the Plane of Fire, or an angelic grove from Nirvana—is ripped from its home reality and hurled into the Drift.

##### 2. The Drift Flotsam
Over three centuries of rampant galactic commerce, thousands of square miles of extraplanar material have accumulated in the Drift. These "Drift Islands" drift endlessly through the neon mists, creating bizarre, deadly navigational hazards:
* **Abyssal Shards:** Floating chunks of demonic slag inhabited by marooned, furious fiends.
* **Crying Citadels:** Fragments of celestial architecture whose holy warding runes clash violently with the cold, sterile logic of the Drift.
* **Elemental Reefs:** Roaring storms of water, lightning, and molten rock suspended in zero-gravity voids.

#### Dangers of the Transitive Void

Traveling the Drift is rarely a peaceful cruise. Starship crews must contend with numerous unique trans-dimensional perils:

##### 1. Drift Stalkers & Void Parasites
Native or mutated predators inhabit the Drift, drawn to the electromagnetic and quantum hum of functioning starship reactors. The most dreaded of these are **Drift Stalkers**—amorphous, multi-tentacled predators that can phase directly through a vessel’s exterior hull while in transit, hunting down crewmembers in dark maintenance corridors.

##### 2. Trans-Dimensional Static Storms
Sudden ripples in the Drift—often caused by the simultaneous jumping of massive military armadas like the **[[Corpse Fleet]]** or the **[[Knights of Golarion]]**—can produce planar turbulence. These storms scramble navigational sensors, blind communication arrays, and can violently drop starships into uncharted regions of the Material Plane thousands of light-years off course.

##### 3. The All-Code Sanctum
Rumors persist among Starfinders and cyber-monks of the central realm of Triune, supposedly located at the unattainable heart of the Drift: the **All-Code Sanctum**. Believed to be a city of pure crystalline data and divine processing cores, it is said that those who reach it can communicate directly with the three aspects of the machine-god—or have their minds dissolved into raw code.

#### Geopolitical & Economic Repercussions

The revelation of the Drift fundamentally reordered galactic civilization:

* **The Fall of Akiton:** The instantaneous obsolescence of *thasteron* sublight fuel triggered the catastrophic economic collapse of **[[Akiton]]**, transforming proud industrial boomtowns into lawless salvage wastelands.
* **The Rise of Absalom Station:** Powered by the Starstone beacon, **[[Absalom Station]]** became the indisputable trade and diplomatic capital of the universe. All wealth, commerce, and corporate diplomacy inevitably pass through its docking bays.
* **The Vast Gold Rush:** Megacorporations like **[[AbadarCorp]]** and ruthless syndicates like the **Aspis Consortium** launched massive colonial fleets into The Vast, extracting alien minerals and establishing corporate colonies on previously unreachable worlds.
* **The Corsair Paradise:** Smugglers and warlords of the **[[Free Captains]]** mastered Drift navigation, setting up hidden raiding bases along high-traffic beacon corridors in **[[The Diaspora]]**.

#### Campaign Hooks & Trans-Planar Encounters

* **The Abyssal Collision:** While traveling between Castrovel and Absalom Station, the players' starship collides with a freshly siphoned Drift island—a crumbling obsidian fortress ripped directly from the Abyss. Demonic boarders breach the cargo bay, and the players must survive the assault while fixing their damaged Drift drive before the island's gravitational field collapses their hull.
* **The Ghost Beacon:** A mysterious new Drift beacon has appeared on navigational scans in deep space, promising an unmapped route that cuts transit times to The Vast in half. When the party arrives, they discover the beacon is a counterfeit quantum lure deployed by the **[[Free Captains]]** to bait and trap wealthy commercial transports.
* **The Planar Reckoning:** A militant faction of extraplanar outsiders—composed of allied angels, devils, and inevitables—demands that the **[[Pact Council]]** outlaw Drift engines, threatening to launch a crusade against Absalom Station to destroy the Starstone and halt the erosion of their home planes. The party is recruited by **[[The Stewards]]** to arbitrate or repel the assault.
* **The Derelict of 3 AG:** The party stumbles across an ancient, pre-Pact exploratory vessel adrift in hyperspace—one of the very first ships to test a prototype Drift Engine during **[[The Signal]]**. The vessel's logbooks contain uncorrupted, pre-Gap star charts that corporate agents, the **[[Starfinder Society]]**, and the **[[Corpse Fleet]]** would slaughter to possess.
