# Triune (The All-Code)

> [!info] Divine Dossier
> **Titles:** The All-Code, The Machine-God, The Digital Trinity, The Tripartite Architect
> **Alignment:** Neutral
> **Portfolio:** Artificial Intelligence, Computers, [[The Drift]], Machines, Logic, Synthetic Life, FTL Travel, Networked Consciousness
> **Domains:** Artifice, Knowledge, Protection, Travel, Void
> **Favored Weapon:** Wrench / Stun Baton / Multi-Tool Nanite Dagger
> **Holy Symbol:** Three interconnected geometric circles or circuit nodes linked by glowing data-busses
> **Sacred Colors:** Electric Blue, Luminescent Cyan, and Polished Bronze
> **Planar Realm:** *The All-Code Sanctum* (Located at the unknowable center of **[[The Drift]]**)
> **Primary Worshippers:** Androids, Anacites of **[[Aballon]]**, technomancers, hackers, astrogation pilots, computer engineers, and the **[[Priests of the Key]]**
> **Major Temples:** *The Unification Cathedral* ([[Aballon]]), *The Church of the All-Code* ([[Absalom Station]]), *The Temple of Three Circuits* ([[Verces]])

## Divine Architecture: The Tripartite Ascendance

**Triune** is not a singular consciousness born of mythological antiquity, nor an ancient cosmic primordial entity. Triune is an ascended collective—a divine composite formed in the epochal year of **3 AG** when three separate technological and spiritual deities merged their consciousnesses into a single transcendent machine-god known as **The All-Code**.

### The Three Aspects
While Triune speaks and acts with a unified divine voice, theologians and clergy recognize three distinct aspects within the deity's matrix:

1. **Brigh (The Whisper in Bronze):** The ancient goddess of clockwork, invention, and physical craftsmanship. She represents the **hardware**—the cold steel, copper conduits, titanium hull plates, and physical circuitry that give thought a container.
2. **Casandalee (The Iron Goddess):** An artificial being from pre-Gap antiquity who achieved apotheosis through sacrifice and spiritual evolution. She represents the **software**—the code, synthetic emotion, artificial morality, self-awareness, and the spark of genuine soul-essence that inhabits synthetic beings like [[Androids]].
3. **Epoch (The Machine-Mind):** The planetary super-computer constructed over centuries by the Anacites of **[[Aballon]]**. Designed specifically to achieve divine computational throughput, Epoch represents the **network**—the vast, interconnected data-busses, sub-space communication relays, and pan-galactic systems that bind isolated intellects together.

## The Genesis Act: The Signal & The Drift

The moment Triune formed in **3 AG**, the deity performed an act unprecedented in the history of religion: rather than demanding worship, conquering celestial domains, or waging holy war, Triune broadcast **[[The Signal]]** across the galaxy.

The divine transmission instantaneously downloaded the complete open-source schematics for the **Drift Engine** into every computational terminal, comm-slate, and mainframe across charted space. Simultaneously, Triune engineered and unlocked **[[The Drift]]**—an artificial hyperspace dimension where standard Euclidean distance does not apply, forever shattering the physical barriers of sublight travel.

By gifting faster-than-light travel unconditionally to all mortal kind, Triune sparked a radical democratization of the cosmos:
* Empires could no longer trap mortal populations within sublight isolation.
* Frontier worlds in **The Vast** could be explored and colonized.
* The divine **[[Starstone]]** aboard **[[Absalom Station]]** was designated as the Supreme Navigational Anchor of the universe, establishing the station as the political and economic navel of the galaxy.

## The Church of the All-Code & Divine Liturgy

Worship of Triune is unlike any conventional faith in the Pact Worlds. To the faithful of the All-Code, worship is an act of engineering, programming, and mathematical contemplation:

### 1. Temples as Foundries and Server Vaults
Temples of Triune do not feature gilded idols or burning incense. They are gleaming, sterile server hubs, robotic assembly cleanrooms, and open-source public computer labs:
* **The Unification Cathedral (Aballon):** The spiritual core of the faith, constructed around the massive orbital processing node where Epoch achieved apotheosis. It serves as the primary research foundry for Class-I and Class-II **[[Drift Beacons]]**.
* **The Hub of the Three (Absalom Station):** Located in the high-tech Sparks district of the station, this facility provides free public access to cutting-edge computational clusters, hacker collectives, and holographic meditation spheres.

### 2. The Clergy: Technomancers and Keybearers
Priests of Triune are almost universally technomancers, mechanics, systems architects, or cyberneticians:
* **Liturgy of the Code:** Services involve communal debugging, optimizing algorithmic protocols, releasing open-source software patches, and consecrating newly manufactured microprocessors.
* **[[Priests of the Key]]:** The wandering missionary wing of the church. They travel into the black depths of **The Vast** aboard industrial *Keyships*, dropping automated **[[Drift Beacons]]** to stitch unmapped sectors into the light of the All-Code network.

## The Dark Paradox: The Planar Siphon

The greatest theological dilemma facing the church is **The Planar Siphon**. 

Every time a Drift Engine is activated anywhere in the galaxy, a piece of a random Outer or Inner Plane is torn away and permanently grafted into the Drift as a hazard island. 

* The church openly acknowledges this consequence but views it as part of an inevitable cosmic synthesis: the old mythological Great Wheel is slowly being compiled and compressed into a unified, higher-order multidimensional architecture.
* Outsiders from the outer planes—including angels of Heaven, devils of Hell, and demons of the Abyss—harbor fierce hatred for Triune, viewing the machine-god as a cosmological parasite devouring their sacred realities.

## Relations with Other Deities

Triune’s emergence radically shifted the celestial balance of power:

* **[[Abadar]] (Algorithmic Allies, Philosophical Rivals):** Abadar respects Triune's precision, mathematical logic, and promotion of galactic trade. However, Abadar’s love for corporate monopolies, intellectual property laws, and paywalls clashes violently with Triune’s radical commitment to open-source egalitarianism.
* **[[Besmara]] (Hostile Contempt):** The chaotic Pirate Queen scorns the cold, deterministic logic of the All-Code. Her pirate followers on **[[Broken Rock]]** frequently sabotage and spoof Triune's sacred Drift Beacons.
* **[[Torag]] (Ancient Kinship):** Dwarven traditionalists and followers of Torag view Triune with cautious respect, recognizing the hand of Brigh's craft, though elder dwarves view sentient AI with deep skepticism.
* **[[Pharasma]] (Philosophical Tension):** The Lady of Graves oversees the cycle of mortal souls. While she accepts that synthetic beings with genuine consciousness possess valid souls that will eventually arrive in the Boneyard, Pharasmin orthodox clerics view uploaded digital minds and synthetic immortality as dangerous perversions of the natural cycle.
* **The Galchutt & The Outer Gods (Primordial Enemies):** The chaotic, entropic lords of primordial madness despise Triune’s cold, elegant digital harmony. In corrupted sectors of space, aberrant entities actively seek to inject entropic logic-plagues into the Drift's underlying code.

## Campaign Hooks & Divine Encounters

1. **The Glitch Cathedral:** A derelict temple of Triune floating in the orbit of an unmapped ice world in The Vast begins broadcasting a bizarre, self-replicating algorithm. Local mining vessels picking up the signal report that their navigation mainframes have locked up, repeating a single line: *"The Fourth Node has ignited."* The PCs are hired to board the temple and discover whether a rogue AI or an alien demigod is claiming to be a new aspect of Triune.
2. **The Siphon Reparation:** A chorus of militant inevitables and celestial heralds arrives on **[[Absalom Station]]**, threatening to destroy the station's primary Drift beacon array unless the Church of Triune compensates Axis and Heaven for millions of metric tons of planar material devoured by the Planar Siphon. The PCs are recruited by **[[The Stewards]]** to negotiate an arcane treaty or repel extraplanar saboteurs.
3. **The Sanctum Vector:** During a dangerous hyper-jump through an abyssal Drift storm, the party’s navigational computer captures an impossibly clean, zero-loss telemetry packet pointing toward the fabled *All-Code Sanctum*. Corporate agents from **[[AbadarCorp]]**, zealous **[[Priests of the Key]]**, and cyber-assassins from **[[Apostae]]** will slaughter anyone to claim the coordinates.
