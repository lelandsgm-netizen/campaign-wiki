# Suli (The Elemental Scions)

> [!info] Biological & Cultural Overview
> **Ancestral Roots:** Mortal humanoids infused with the primordial planar bloodline of **Jann** (mortals touched by all four elemental planes: Fire, Earth, Water, and Air)
> **Average Height:** 5 to 6.5 feet (1.5 to 2.0 meters)
> **Average Weight:** 130 to 220 lbs (60 to 100 kg)
> **Lifespan:** ~120 Standard Years
> **Key Biological Traits:** Elemental Resistance (Cold, Fire, Electricity, Acid), Elemental Assault (Infuses strikes and weapon attacks with pure elemental damage), Low-Light Vision
> **Societal Structure:** Cosmopolitan enclaves, planar trade guilds, solar navigators
> **Primary Habitats:** **[[The Burning Archipelago]]** ([[Dawnshore]], [[Corona]], [[Verdeon]]), **[[Verces]]**, planar trading ports
> **Primary Deities:** **[[Sarenrae]]** (The Dawnflower), planar philosophies, elemental spirits
> **Iconic Disciplines:** Solarians, solar envoys, interplanar diplomats, plasma mechanics, flame dancers

## Origin & The Planar Legacy

While many planetary species in the [[Mataras System]] trace their lineage back to terrestrial evolution on specific worlds, the **Suli** (or *Suli-jann*) are creatures born of cosmic convergence. Carrying the ancient genetic and metaphysical heritage of **Jann**—genies formed from the union of all four elemental planes—Sulis are mortals who walk with the raw forces of creation thrumming through their veins.

In the modern era of the [[Pact Worlds]], nowhere is the Suli population more concentrated, celebrated, and politically influential than within **[[The Burning Archipelago]]** suspended inside **[[Mataras]]**. Drawn by the natural planar thinning that connects the star to the **[[Plane of Fire]]**, generations of Sulis migrated to the coronal bubble-cities. Here, their innate resistance to extreme temperatures, natural affinity for solar plasma, and cultural fluency in interplanar etiquette make them the indispensable bridge between mortal civilization and extraplanar empires.

## Anatomy & Physiology

To a casual observer, a Suli appears as an exceptionally striking, athletic humanoid, closely resembling a human or lashunta. Upon closer inspection, their planar ancestry reveals itself through subtle, mesmerizing physical traits:
* **The Shimmering Complexion:** A Suli’s skin often possesses a soft metallic or gem-like sheen—ranging from warm amber bronze and polished copper to deep basalt or cool jade. When experiencing intense emotion or channeling their abilities, faint elemental patterns (such as flickering embers or winding frost-runes) ripple beneath their flesh.
* **Eyes of Living Light:** Their eyes gleam with intense, vibrant hues of molten gold, electric azure, or emerald green, lacking the dullness of ordinary mortal retinas.
* **Elemental Resistance:** Their cells are permeated with primal planar warding fields. A Suli possesses innate biological resistance to extreme heat, biting cold, electrical currents, and caustic acids, allowing them to stroll casually along the sun-swept plazas of [[Dawnshore]] in clothing that would leave other humanoids sweltering.
* **Elemental Assault:** Through a brief exertion of willpower, a Suli can awaken their bloodline, manifesting a corona of fire, crackling electricity, caustic acid, or freezing rime around their hands or weapons. A plasma rifle fired by an awakened Suli strikes with dual physical and elemental vengeance.

## Culture & Life in the Archipelago

Within [[The Burning Archipelago]], Sulis hold a position of high cultural prestige:

### 1. The Planar Translators
Because they share kinship with both mortal species and the true genies of the Outer Planes, Sulis serve as the primary diplomats, commercial brokers, and arbitrators in **[[Corona]]'s** Brass Bazaar. An **[[Efreet]]** trade-baron who treats humans or androids with haughty disdain will often speak respectfully to a Suli merchant, acknowledging them as distant planar cousins who understand the sacred laws of hospitality and contract.

### 2. Devotion to the Dawnflower
The vast majority of Sulis inhabiting the Archipelago are devout followers of **[[Sarenrae]]**. They view the sun not merely as an energy reactor, but as the living heart of their goddess’s warmth and honesty. Many serve as elite paladins in the **Dawnguard**, patrol officers along the linecrawler energy tethers, or combat-medics in Dawnshore's *Radiant Cathedral*.

### 3. The Solar Dancers
Suli artists are famous for the *Raqs al-Shams* (The Dance of the Sun)—an acrobatic, zero-G martial art practiced in observation domes and public plazas. Dancers weave trails of coherent light, plasma ribbons, and localized heat waves into mesmerizing choreographies that depict the history of the Gap and the founding of the Archipelago.

## Sulis Across the Pact Worlds

Outside of Mataras, Sulis are valued throughout the system as deep-space pilots, hazardous-environment surveyors, and corporate negotiators:
* **Verces Ring-Pioneers:** In the harsh thermal transition zones of [[Verces]] (between Fullbright and Darkside), Sulis thrive as frontier scouts where sudden fluctuations between baking desert heat and sub-zero blizzards would kill unprotected organics.
* **Wayfinder Scouts:** Long-range exploratory expeditions launched by the **[[Wayfinders]]** regularly recruit Suli crew members, valuing their versatile environmental survivability during first-contact landings on volcanically active or frozen exoplanets in The Vast.

## Campaign Hooks

* **The Bloodline Inheritance:** A Suli diplomat in [[Corona]] is revealed to be the rightful heir to an ancient pre-Gap planar citadel on the border of the **[[Plane of Fire]]**. An ambitious efreeti warlord dispatches planar assassins to eliminate the Suli before they can claim their birthright, and the PCs are hired as protective escorts.
* **The Coronal Fire-Trial:** To earn the rank of Solar Captain in the Dawnguard, a young Suli knight must perform a solitary, unshielded space-walk outside [[Dawnshore]]'s force bubble during a coronal flare. When an anomalous plasma-ooze ambushes the knight, the PCs must mount an emergency rescue in a sunskimmer.
* **The Planar Embargo:** Tensions between the Council of Bubble Ministers and the City of Brass erupt after a Suli customs inspector impounds a shipment of forbidden planar artifacts in the Brass Bazaar. The party is brought in by **[[The Stewards]]** to defuse the standoff before efreeti gunboats open fire.
