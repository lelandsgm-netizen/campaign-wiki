# Dwarves (Stoneborn of the Stars)

> [!info] Species & Cultural Dossier
> **Homeworld:** **Lost Golarion** (Vanished during [[The Gap]]; current major sovereign holdings across **[[The Diaspora]]** and [[Absalom Station]])
> **Average Height:** 4 to 4.5 feet (1.2 to 1.4 meters; exceptionally broad and dense)
> **Average Weight:** 150 to 220 lbs (70 to 100 kg)
> **Lifespan:** ~350 to 450 Standard Years
> **Key Biological Traits:** High Physical Density, Darkvision, Innate Tectonic & Metal Intuition, Toxin & Radiation Resistance
> **Societal Structure:** Traditional patriarchal/matriarchal clanholdings, corporate-guild conglomerates (e.g., **[[Ulrikka Clanholdings]]**), master artisan guilds
> **Major Bastions:** **Star Citadel Theodrane**, *Torag’s Anvil*, *Hearthstone* (The Diaspora); Downlow & The Spike ([[Absalom Station]])
> **Primary Deities:** **[[Torag]]** (The Father of Creation; patron of the forge and stone), **[[Angradd]]** (Forge-fire & war), **[[Abadar]]** (Commerce & mining charters)
> **Iconic Disciplines:** Void wildcatters, solar smelters, kinetic heavy weapons specialists, starship armor fabricators

## Origin & The Galactic Quest for Sky

Long before the celestial amnesia of **[[The Gap]]**, the ancestral dwarves of Lost Golarion undertook the legendary **Quest for Sky**—an epic, generational migration that led them up from the radioactive, darkling depths of the Darklands to the surface world under the divine command of their creator god, **[[Torag]]**.

When the Gap receded and Lost Golarion was discovered to have vanished from the solar system, the dwarves did not despair. Viewing the loss of their planet as a divine sign, dwarven clan-elders declared a modern, cosmic continuation of their sacred pilgrimage: **The Second Quest for Sky**. 

Rather than clinging to the crowded corridors of [[Absalom Station]], the most ambitious dwarven clans looked toward the sixth planetary orbit of the [[Mataras System]]—the sprawling, shattered expanse of **[[The Diaspora]]**. To a people who had spent thousands of years carving fortresses out of solid basalt and mining veins of adamantine, an asteroid belt consisting of millions of tumbling, mineral-rich nickel-iron planetoids was not an inhospitable wasteland. It was the ultimate, god-given mountain range.

## Anatomy & Physical Adaptation

Standing just over four feet tall with broad, muscular frames and bone densities nearly double that of a typical human, dwarves are naturally suited to heavy industrial labor and harsh gravimetric conditions:

### 1. High-G & Microgravity Resilience
* Because of their low center of gravity and extraordinarily dense musculature, dwarves adapt seamlessly to erratic asteroid gravities. 
* Their circulatory systems resist the bone-density loss and cardiovascular atrophy that plague other biological humanoids during extended tours in microgravity.

### 2. Toxin & Radiation Tolerance
* Millennia of subterranean life on Golarion and centuries inside asteroid hulls have gifted dwarven physiology with remarkable natural resistances to airborne rock particulate, industrial heavy-metal poisoning, and solar radiation leaks.
* Even when a refinery leak vents caustic gases, a dwarf can often hold their breath, manually dog an airlock hatch, and walk away with minor burns where others would perish.

## Dwarven Culture in the Diaspora: The Star Citadels

While other species see the Diaspora as a lawless frontier of cutthroats and pirates, dwarven clans view it as their sovereign industrial empire. They do not merely drill into asteroids; they **tame** them.

### 1. Hollow Asteroid Bastions
The pride of dwarven engineering in the belt is their network of **Star Citadels**—dense, metallic asteroids that have been carefully excavated from the inside out:
* **Thermal Core Reactors:** Deep within the hollowed center, geothermal and fusion taps generate stable, rotating artificial gravity ($1.0\text{ G}$) along the inner circumference.
* **The High Forges:** Utilizing orbital mirrors and focused solar-radiation arrays, dwarven smiths operate vacuum-smelters capable of liquefying metric tons of raw adamantine, cold iron, and titanium without atmospheric contaminants, producing the purest armor alloys in the [[Pact Worlds]].
* **Defensive Supremacy:** A dwarven Star Citadel is an impregnable naval bastion. Armed with spinal-mount kinetic railguns and layered point-defense grids, even massive fleets of the **[[Free Captains]]** know better than to assault a clanhold.

### 2. The Clanholdings & Corporate Guilds
Dwarven society is organized into ancient clans that function simultaneously as extended bloodline families and publicly traded industrial corporations:
* **[[Ulrikka Clanholdings]]:** The premier dwarven mining conglomerate in the Diaspora. Led by visionary executives like **Belenze Shadraz** and veteran void-masters like **Rabech Shadraz**, Ulrikka Clanholdings commands fleets of deep-range survey ships, automated dredges, and heavy escort frigates that range from the Diaspora all the way into the Vast.
* **The Miner-Corsair Dynamic:** Dwarves in the Diaspora maintain an armed, pragmatic relationship with neighboring **[[Free Captains]]** on **[[Broken Rock]]**. While they fiercely repel raiders attempting to breach corporate ore-convoys, dwarven armorers routinely sell bulk structural plating and engine housings to reputable pirate captains who honor the *Code of Asteroids* and target corporate rivals like **[[AbadarCorp]]**.

## The Diaspora Destination: The Mysterious Beacon

> [!important] The Anomaly in the Stone
> In the modern era, dwarven seismic surveys and deep-core acoustic probes operating from **[[Star Citadel Theodrane]]** in **[[The Diaspora]]** registered an unprecedented discovery: a rhythmic, artificial technomagical pulse echoing from an unmapped, tumbling asteroid in a remote sector of the belt.
>
> The acoustic signature did not match any contemporary transponder or natural geological resonance; it bore the unmistakable mathematical harmonics of pre-Gap engineering. Recognizing the monumental value—and danger—of the discovery, **[[Ulrikka Clanholdings]]** leadership mobilized to secure the site before rival corporations or the rogue **[[Corpse Fleet]]** could intercept the transmission.
>
> Seeking to avoid bureaucratic entanglements, Ulrikka executives dispatched discreet recruitment inquiries across [[Absalom Station]], assembling a team of capable, unaligned freelance contractors to chart a course into the belt, dock with the hollow rock, and unlock the ancient secrets of **[[Iovan Station Alpha]]**.

## Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Runaway Bore:** An automated deep-core asteroid boring rig owned by [[Ulrikka Clanholdings]] suffers a catastrophic logic fault after breaching an unmapped subterranean cavern in the Diaspora. The machine is drilling unchecked toward the civilian residential sector of a nearby mining habitat. The party is hired by Rabech Shadraz to board the rogue drill in EVA suits and disable its primary reactors before impact.
* **The Star Citadel Siege:** A radical splinter cell of the [[Corpse Fleet]] attempts to commandeer a dwarven solar smelter near *Torag's Anvil* to fabricate unholy naval hull plating for a shadow dreadnought. The PCs must infiltrate the molten foundry, ally with dwarven forge-priests, and use the industrial slag vats to incinerate the undead boarding party.
* **The Adamantine Claim:** A group of independent human and ysoki wildcatters discovers a pure adamantine micro-asteroid tumbling toward the territory of **[[Broken Rock]]**. Both a greedy syndicate from [[AbadarCorp]] and an armada of Free Captain corsairs are en route to seize the claim. The miners hire the PCs to escort an Ulrikka heavy tug to haul the asteroid to the safety of *Star Citadel Theodrane*.
