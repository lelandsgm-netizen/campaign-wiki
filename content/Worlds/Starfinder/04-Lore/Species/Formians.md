# Formians (The Chitin-Born of Castrovel)

> [!info] Biological & Cultural Overview
> **Homeworld:** **[[The Colonies]]**, **[[Castrovel]]** (The Green Mother); sovereign presence across the **[[Pact Worlds]]**
> **Creature Type:** Monstrous Humanoid (Insectoid)
> **Average Height:** 4 to 7 feet (1.2 to 2.1 meters; varies significantly by caste)
> **Average Weight:** 140 to 450 lbs (65 to 200 kg; chitinous carapace and dense musculature)
> **Lifespan:** Workers: ~40 Standard Years | Warriors: ~60 Years | Queens: Centuries
> **Key Biological Traits:** Formian Senses (Blindsense [scent] 30 ft, Darkvision 60 ft), Chitinous Carapace, Telepathic Hive Mind, Formian Venom (Acidic stingers on Warriors and Queens)
> **Caste Hierarchy:** Workers (Industrious builders), Warriors (Chitinous shock troops), Queens (Brood-mothers & administrative nodes), and the **Overqueen** (Supreme nexus)
> **Primary Habitats:** **[[The Colonies]]** (**[[Queensrock]]**), **[[Elindrae]]** (Kevolari Collective Shipyards), [[Absalom Station]]
> **Primary Philosophy:** The Shared Harmony (The Hive Mind), civic order, engineering synchronization
> **Iconic Disciplines:** Bio-engineers, heavy ordnance troopers, logistical coordinators, hive diplomats

## Origin & The Thousand-Year War

The **Formians** are the native insectoid civilization of **[[The Colonies]]**, the sprawling southern continent of **[[Castrovel]]**. Evolving in subterranean warrens and volcanic caverns, the formians developed an unshakeable communal intellect: a telepathic hive mind capable of coordinating millions of individuals into a singular, synchronized societal engine.

For millennia before **[[The Gap]]**, formian expansionism brought them into violent, catastrophic conflict with the **[[Lashunta]]** of the northern continent of **[[Asana]]**. To the formians, the individualistic, emotionally volatile lashunta appeared chaotic, dangerous, and inefficient; to the lashunta, the advancing chitinous legions represented an apocalyptic swarm that would devour their independence.

The conflict raged across countless generations in brutal trench warfare, saurian charges, and bio-chemical strikes. However, in **287 AG**, progressive scholars from [[Qabarat]] and the visionary leadership of the newly crowned **Overqueen** achieved a historic breakthrough: the signing of the **Treaty of Queensrock**. Four years later in **291 AG**, formians and lashunta stood together as joint founding signatories of the **Absalom Pact**, permanently cementing their historic peace and opening formian civilization to the wider galaxy.

## Anatomy & Caste Physiology

Formians possess an unmistakable, centaur-like anatomy:
* **The Six-Legged Carriage:** Their lower bodies resemble the abdomen and thorax of a giant ant, supported by six jointed chitinous legs that provide absolute balance across zero-G bulkheads, sheer subterranean walls, and mud-slick jungle floors.
* **The Upright Torso:** Emerging from the anterior thorax is a humanoid-like upper torso with two multi-jointed arms ending in opposable, four-fingered claws. Their heads feature compound faceted eyes, sweeping sensory antennae, and formidable serrated mandibles.
* **The Hive Web (Telepathy):** Every formian is born into the **Hive Web**—an innate, continuous telepathic mesh that links them to their local Queen and, through her, to the Overqueen in **[[Queensrock]]**. This connection does not erase free will, but it provides a profound, calming sense of belonging, instant non-verbal tactical coordination, and seamless data-sharing.

## Culture & The Post-Peace Awakening

Since the 287 AG armistice, formian society has undergone a radical cultural renaissance:
* **The Rise of the Individual:** While ancestral formians were entirely subservient to hive dictates, modern formians have eagerly embraced individual self-expression. Workers and warriors now choose unique personal names, pursue hobbies (such as mechanical customization, painting, or culinary experimentation), and adopt off-world fashions.
* **The Kevolari Shipyards:** Partnering with the Lashunta, formians established the **Kevolari Collective** on Castrovel’s industrial moon, **[[Elindrae]]**. Combining formian bio-architectural chitin-weaving with lashunta technomancy, the shipyards produce some of the most resilient, organically self-repairing starships in the Pact Worlds navy.
* **Formian Diplomats:** Due to their complete lack of personal vanity and flawless mathematical honesty, formian ambassadors are sought after throughout the [[Pact Council]] as unbiased corporate arbitrators and treaty negotiators.

## Campaign Hooks

* **The Severed Hive:** A deep-space mining team of formian workers operating in **[[The Diaspora]]** has their local Queen slain by void-corsairs. Disconnected from the Hive Web, the workers panic and barricade themselves inside an ore refinery. The PCs are hired to deliver a synthetic telepathic relay to calm the survivors before corporate strikebreakers storm the facility.
* **The Hive-Mind Sabotage:** A virulent neuro-toxin developed by anti-treaty lashunta extremists is introduced into a subterranean water duct in [[Queensrock]], threatening to infect the brood chambers and trigger violent madness across the hive. The PCs must track down the terrorists and synthesize an antidote before the peace collapses.
* **The Individualist Rebel:** A formian warrior stationed on [[Absalom Station]] refuses a direct directive from her Queen to return to Castrovel, seeking asylum with the **[[Starfinder Society]]** to pursue a career as a deep-space pilot. The party is caught in the middle of a delicate jurisdictional showdown between formian royal bailiffs and the Society.
