#### Overview: The Four-Armed Titans of the Red Wastes

Towering ten to twelve feet in height with four muscular arms capable of tearing open armored airlocks or firing two heavy anti-materiel rifles simultaneously, the **Shobhads** are the undisputed apex warrior nomads of the **[[Pact Worlds]]**. Native to the dying red planet of **[[Akiton]]**, they spent millennia roaming the windswept badlands, hunting colossal desert predators from the saddles of six-legged shotalashu mounts and clashing in ritualized clan warfare over water wells and thasteron deposits.

When the advent of **[[The Drift]]** and the **[[The Signal|Signal]]** rendered Akiton’s thasteron mining economy obsolete, the planet plunged into catastrophic financial ruin. While their homeworld decayed into a dust-choked rustbelt, the Shobhads adapted with stoic, brutal pragmatism. Taking their ancient traditions of clan honor and martial discipline to the stars, thousands of Shobhads signed on as private security contractors, deep-space bodyguards, and elite heavy shock-troopers across **[[Absalom Station]]** and the wider galaxy.

#### Physiology & Four-Armed Anatomy

##### 1. Colossal Quad-Armed Frame
A typical Shobhad stands between ten and twelve feet tall with a wide, armored torso supported by two dense legs and four fully articulated, independent arms. Their leathery, thick hide ranges from stony slate-gray to dusty brick-red, naturally insulating them against both freezing desert nights and blistering solar heat. Their facial features are severe, marked by heavy brow ridges, deep-set amber or red eyes, and tusklike lower canines.

##### 2. Multi-Weapon Coordination
Unlike species with vestigial secondary limbs, all four of a Shobhad's arms possess equal strength, dexterity, and neural integration. A seasoned Shobhad warrior can brace a heavy machine gun with one pair of arms while drawing a powered doshko and reloading a grenade launcher with the other. This unique coordination makes them terrifying in close-quarters urban combat and boarding skirmishes, where they can lay down suppressing fire and engage in devastating melee simultaneously.

##### 3. Harsh Climate Resilience
Evolving on a dry, low-gravity, thin-atmosphere world like Akiton, Shobhads possess massive lung capacities and efficient water-retention systems. Their thick skin resists abrasions from whipping sandstorms and micro-meteorite impacts, allowing them to endure toxic or vacuum-breach environments longer than frail, single-lunged species.

#### Society, Honor, and The Mercenary Code

Despite their intimidating, monstrous appearance, Shobhads are not senseless brutes. Their society is governed by an intricate, unbending code of honor inherited from centuries of desert clan living:

##### The Sanctity of the Contract
To a Shobhad, a signed contract is not merely legal ink—it is a spiritual covenant sealed in the presence of their ancestors. Once a Shobhad accepts an employer’s retainer and swears an oath of service, they will fight to the death to fulfill their duty. A Shobhad who breaks a sworn contract or flees from battle without orders is branded an outcast (*shobhad-kosh*), stripped of clan heraldry, and hunted as a pariah.

##### Symbiosis with the Ysoki
On Akiton, Shobhads and **[[Ysoki]]** have shared geography for thousands of years. While initially clashing in ancient times, the two species formed an enduring cultural partnership. Ysoki provide the mechanical genius, intricate electronic repairs, and weapons modification that Shobhad giants respect; in exchange, the massive Shobhads provide physical protection and heavy muscle. This alliance remains vibrant aboard **[[Absalom Station]]**, where the two species share neighborhoods and business consortiums in **[[Little Akiton]]**.

#### Shobhads on Absalom Station

Aboard **[[Absalom Station]]**, Shobhads are a formidable, unmistakable presence:

- **[[Little Akiton]]:** The enclave's architecture features soaring, twenty-foot ceilings specifically constructed to accommodate Shobhad families and their heavy equipment. Traditional smokehouses roast desert beasts, while clan lounges ring with deep, bass-heavy warrior songs.
- **Berth Enforcers in [[The Arms]]:** Due to their immense strength and unwavering discipline, port authorities and shipping syndicates frequently hire Shobhads to guard high-security docking locks and bullion vaults against pirate raids.
- **The Proving Grounds of the [[Exo-Guardians]]:** Many Shobhad veterans find a spiritual home in the martial ranks of the Starfinder Society's Exo-Guardians, serving as frontline heavy-weapons gunners alongside their Shirren commander **[[Zigvigix]]**.

#### GM Plot Hooks & Operational Scenarios

1. **The Outcast's Vengeance:** A dishonored Shobhad mercenary outcast arrives on [[Absalom Station]], seeking to assassinate the corrupt corporate executive who framed him for contract betrayal. The PCs are caught in the crossfire between the vengeful titan and corporate kill-drones in **[[The Neon Trench]]**.
2. **The Stolen Clan Relic:** An ancestral thasteron-cannon dating back a thousand years before The Gap is stolen from a Shobhad clan hall in **[[Little Akiton]]** and slated for illegal sale in the black markets of **[[The Ghost Levels]]**. The clan chieftains hire the PCs to track the thief and recover the sacred gun before a clan blood-war erupts.
3. **The Unbreakable Oath:** A dying Shobhad bodyguard in **[[The Arms]]** transfers his personal oath of protection over a defenseless refugee child to the PCs, binding them to an ancient Akitonian blood-pact. Ruthless bounty hunters from the Azlanti Star Empire are closing in, and honoring the fallen giant's vow is the only path forward.
