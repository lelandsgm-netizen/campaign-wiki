# Androids

> [!info] Species & Cultural Dossier
> **Origin:** Manufactured synthetic biological humanoids originally engineered on Lost Golarion (and subsequently replicated in automated planetary foundries)
> **Average Height:** 5 to 6.5 feet (1.5 to 2 meters)
> **Average Weight:** 140 to 220 lbs (65 to 100 kg)
> **Lifespan:** Physical body operates indefinitely; individual consciousness and soul typically undergo *Renewal* after 100 Standard Years
> **Key Biological Traits:** Synthetic Biology, Artificial Soul (Infused with positive energy / spirit), Nanite Repair Matrices, Emotional Flatness, Exceptional Logical Processing
> **Legal Status:** Full Sovereign Citizens under **[[The Absalom Pact]]** (Enacted via the post-Gap Machine Rights Accords)
> **Primary Concentrations:** [[Absalom Station]], [[Verces]] (Ringworks), [[Akiton]], **[[The Diaspora]]** (Autonomous enclaves & pirate crews), [[Apostae]] (Runaway underground)
> **Primary Deities:** **[[Triune]]** (The All-Code; patron of synthetic life), **[[Casandalee]]** (The Empathic AI deity), **[[Brigh]]** (The Whispering Bronze), **[[Ibra]]** (Cosmic purpose)

## Origin & The Awakening of Souls

Unlike true mechanical automatons or simple corporate service drones, **Androids** are living, organic-synthetic beings. Fabricated from advanced carbon-fiber composites, synthetic tissue cultures, polymer bone matrices, and microscopic circulatory nanite networks, their bodies are designed to mirror the human form while possessing vastly superior environmental durability.

### 1. The Divine Mystery of the Soul
The defining enigma of android existence is metaphysical: **Androids possess true souls**. 
* When an android's body is synthesized in a cloning crèche or planetary biomechanical foundry, a genuine spark of vital soul-stuff drawn from the River of Souls anchors itself into the synthetic neural core. 
* Because they have souls, androids can cast divine and occult magic, channel the planar energy of the cosmos, be resurrected by healing magic, and face Pharasma's judgment upon permanent death just like biological mortals.

### 2. Historical Subjugation & Liberation
Before the Gap, androids on Lost Golarion were widely treated as disposable bio-mechanical tools, manufactured to perform dangerous labor, toxic waste management, and orbital construction. 
* Following the end of the Gap, as android populations demanded self-determination and the tripartite machine god **[[Triune]]** broadcast **[[The Signal]]** granting the galaxy the Drift, the legal status of synthetic beings became impossible to ignore.
* Through the historic **Machine Rights Movement** championed across [[Absalom Station]] and [[Verces]], the newly formed **[[Pact Council]]** codified that any being possessing an independent soul and self-awareness holds full constitutional rights under **[[The Absalom Pact]]**. Today, treating an android as property is a high crime across the civilized core.

## Anatomy & Physiology

An android's synthetic biology grants them significant advantages in hazardous environments:

### 1. Complex Biomechanical Synthesis
An android's internal organs are composed of flexible biopolymer matrices that process sustenance and synthesize bio-electricity. They bleed an amber or silver-white nutrient fluid infused with millions of microscopic nanites.
* **Environmental Immunity:** Androids do not breathe, eat, or sleep in the traditional biological sense (though they must enter a four-hour regenerative nanite diagnostic recharge cycle daily). 
* **Vacuum & Disease Resistance:** They are naturally immune to biological diseases, airborne pathogens, and decompression sickness, though they remain vulnerable to electrical shocks, acid erosion, and synthetic computer viruses.

### 2. Subdermal Circuitry & Tattoos
Running just beneath an android's translucent or pearl-like synthetic skin are faint, glowing geometric pathways known as **circuit tattoos**. 
* These circuits channel nanite flow and bio-luminesce softly when an android experiences intense cognitive processing, spellcasting, or emotional turmoil.
* The color and pulsing frequency of these circuits often reflect an android's internal alignment, mood, or personality.

### 3. The Mystery of Renewal
After roughly a century of life, an android's soul naturally reaches the culmination of its mortal journey. 
* The individual soul willingly detaches and ascends into the afterlife, entering the cosmic cycle of reincarnation.
* Rather than rotting or shutting down, the android's physical body undergoes **Renewal**: the nanites reset neural pathways, scrub old personality matrices, and prepare the vessel for an entirely new, nascent soul to awaken. 
* An awakened android inherits the body and skills of their predecessor, but possesses zero memory or emotional attachment to the previous soul's identity.

## Culture & The Diaspora Diaspora

Because androids were freed from bondage only a few centuries ago, their culture is defined by a fierce pursuit of individuality, self-determination, and psychological exploration.

### 1. Emotional Expression & Logic
Many androids struggle with standard biological social cues. Their cognitive matrices favor direct logic, calculated probability, and blunt honesty, leading other species to mistakenly perceive them as cold or aloof. In truth, androids often feel emotions intensely, but lack the subtle involuntary facial tics that biological humanoids use to telegraph internal feelings.

### 2. Outlaws & Free Spirits in the Diaspora
While many androids integrate into corporate and civil life on [[Absalom Station]] or [[Verces]], a significant portion of the population gravitates toward the lawless frontier of **[[The Diaspora]]**:
* **The Haven of Broken Rock:** On **[[Broken Rock]]**, androids are valued as elite pilots, precision starship engineers, and fearless boarding marines who can breach open vacuum without bulky spacesuits. Many fly as respected captains within the **[[Free Captains]]**.
* **Foundry Enclaves:** Deep within the uncharted asteroid clusters of the belt, independent android collectives seek out dormant pre-Gap manufacturing facilities, attempting to reclaim automated assembly pods to manufacture new synthetic bodies free from corporate branding.
* **The Underground Drift:** On worlds like [[Apostae]] where cruel [[Drow]] house syndicates continue to practice illegal android slavery, the **Android Abolitionist Front (AAF)** uses the asteroid belt as a secret pipeline, smuggling escaped synthetics to safety through the Diaspora.

## Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Stolen Soul-Spark:** A corporate bio-tech lab on [[Absalom Station]] has developed an experimental code designed to artificially bind and overwrite an awakened android's soul during Renewal. The PCs are hired by an android rights cell to intercept the prototype before it is delivered to corporate contractors on [[Akiton]].
* **The Ghost Foundry:** Sensor probes in [[The Diaspora]] detect an ancient, functional pre-Gap android creation engine embedded inside a hollowed-out nickel asteroid. The party must defend the site from rival salvagers from [[AbadarCorp]] and the [[Corpse Fleet]] who wish to scrap the sacred foundry for parts.
* **Escape from Apostae:** An android smuggler carrying dozens of escaped synthetic slaves crashes on the ice-plains of **[[Nisis]]**. The party must rescue the survivors from hunting packs of [[Drow]] blood-hounds and the telepathic predators of the abyss.
