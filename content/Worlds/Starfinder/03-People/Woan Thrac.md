#### Overview: The Architect of the Dead Navy

Across the civilized starways of the **[[Pact Worlds]]**, few names evoke such visceral dread as **Grand Admiral Woan Thrac**. An immortal **Necrovite** whose origins trace back to the pre-Gap cataclysms of **[[Eox]]**, Thrac is the supreme warlord, ideological prophet, and founding mutineer of the **[[Corpse Fleet]]**.

Operating from the shadowy, airless bridge of ***The Empire of Bones***—a colossal, multi-mile dreadnought capable of glassing planetary surfaces—Thrac commands hundreds of bone-hulled warships and millions of mindless undead shock troops. To Thrac, the living species of the system are not diplomatic peers, but a disgusting biological infestation. He refers to mortals dismissively as *"breathers"* or *"the cattle"*, viewing their reliance on food, oxygen, warmth, and moral compromise as a pitiful evolutionary failure.

Brilliant, patient, and utterly devoid of mercy, Grand Admiral Thrac has spent over a century orchestrating deep-space ambushes, harvesting civilian starships for raw biological material, and testing horrific necrotic pathogens, all in preparation for the day his fleet returns to crush the living civilizations of the Pact Worlds under iron and bone.

#### The Great Mutiny of 291 AG: The Schism of Orphys

Thrac was not always an outlaw; he was once the most decorated military commander of the sovereign Eoxian navy:

```

|                       THE CHRONOLOGY OF WOAN THRAC                          |
+=============================================================================+
| [PRE-PACT NAVY]     ===> High Admiral of Eox; defended the outer rim during |
|                          the Veskarium invasions with devastating brutality.|
| [291 AG: MUTINY]    ===> Refused to accept the [[Absalom Pact]]; led half   |
|                          the Eoxian starfleet into deep-space exile.        |
| [DIASPORA FORTRESS] ===> Hollowed out the asteroid fortress *Marrow Mound*; |
|                          built black-market supply lines across the rim.    |
| [THE EMPIRE RISES]  ===> Commissioned the super-dreadnought *Empire of Bones*|
|                          to execute total systemic extermination.           |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Crucible of the Vesk War
During the 280s AG, when the imperial armada of the **[[Veskarium]]** assaulted the outer worlds of the Mataras system, then-Admiral Thrac commanded the primary strike flotilla of Eox. Utilizing zero-atmosphere bone-cruisers, radioactive kinetic torpedoes, and zero-G ghoul assault boarding parties, Thrac shattered multiple Vesk battle-lines, earning the respect of even the most battle-hardened reptilian generals.

##### 2. The Refusal of the Pact
When the ruling **[[Bone Sages]]** of Eox traveled to [[Absalom Station]] in **291 AG** to sign the **[[Absalom Pact]]**, Thrac viewed the treaty as an unforgivable act of treason. The pact required Eox to dismantle unauthorized superweapons, submit its naval movements to civilian oversight by **[[The Stewards]]**, and pledge non-aggression toward short-lived, fragile biological mortals.

Enraged that immortal beings should bow before *"insects whose flesh rots within decades"*, Thrac orchestrated a synchronized mutiny across the orbital drydocks of [[Orphys]]. Over half the Eoxian space navy seized their warships, slaughtered living dockworkers, looted naval munitions caches, and made coordinated Drift jumps into uncharted space.

##### 3. The Public Pariah, Private Asset
In the Plenara, the Bone Sages formally condemned Thrac as a traitor and banished the Corpse Fleet forever. In truth, however, Thrac remains in constant contact with hardline, reactionary Bone Sages. These covert sympathizers view Thrac’s armada as a vital insurance policy, secretly laundering multi-million-credit defense contracts and military prototypes to the Corpse Fleet in exchange for deniable black-ops strikes against corporate rivals.

#### The Mind of the Despot: Ideology & The Phylactery

Grand Admiral Thrac’s tactical dominance is amplified by his supernatural undead existence:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         THE NATURE OF THE NECROVITE                         |
+=============================================================================+
| [THE ELECTRO-PHYLACTERY] ===> Thrac's soul matrix is fused directly into    |
|                               the negative-energy reactor of his flagship.  |
| [ABSOLUTE VACUUM]        ===> Commands in zero-atmosphere, freezing vacuum; |
|                               immune to hull breaches or decompression.     |
| [THE URGAHTOAN ZEALOT]   ===> Views undeath as divine perfection; slaughter|
|                               is an ecstatic rite of cosmic purification.   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Fused Soul Core
As a **Necrovite**, Thrac’s consciousness and life force are anchored to an advanced electro-necromantic **phylactery**. According to classified Stewards naval intelligence, Thrac underwent an unholy cybernetic procedure that permanently integrated his phylactery into the armored, radiation-shielded reactor core of ***The Empire of Bones***. 
* Should Thrac’s physical skeletal body be incinerated in combat, his consciousness immediately reconstitutes a new physical form within the flagship’s rejuvenation vaults within days.
* To permanently destroy Grand Admiral Thrac, one must board his multi-mile flagship, fight through legions of elite ghoul marines and necro-technomancers, and detonate the primary negative-energy core.

##### 2. Physical Appearance
Thrac is an imposing, nightmarish figure. His desiccated, leathery skin stretches tight over an ancient Elebrian skull, while malevolent emerald flames blaze within his sunken eye sockets. He wears vacuum-sealed, blackened adamantine plate armor trimmed in tarnished silver filigree and flowing funeral shrouds. Cybernetic conduits pierce his ribcage, funneling necrotic energy from his flagship directly into his withered cardiovascular framework.

#### Tactical Doctrine: The Fleet of Cold Bone

Under Thrac’s despotic command, the Corpse Fleet adheres to a brutal, asymmetrical naval doctrine:

* **Zero Life Support Advantage:** Thrac never installs life support systems aboard his vessels. By eliminating atmospheric scrubbers, gravity generators, food storage, and medical bays, his ships allocate one hundred percent of their power output to kinetic railguns, negative-energy shielding, and thruster velocity.
* **The Harvest Protocol:** When Corpse Fleet warships disable an enemy vessel, they do not accept surrender. Thrac’s ghoul assault marines board the crippled craft, slaughter the crew, and harvest their corpses. The fresh flesh is converted into rations for ghoul shock-troops, while the skeletal remains are wired with cybernetic control collars to become automated labor-thralls.
* **Biological Weaponry:** Thrac’s pet projects are weaponized necrotic pathogens. Operating in deep-space facilities hidden in the dark ravines of **[[Apostae]]**, his lich-chemists synthesize airborne bio-agents designed to exterminate entire planetary populations while leaving the industrial and mineral infrastructure intact for undead occupation.

#### Primary Nemeses & The Galactic War

* **Grand Marshal [[Helena Sagesparrow]] ([[Knights of Golarion]]):** Thrac’s most relentless and dangerous adversary. Sagesparrow’s cathedral-dreadnought ***The Inheritor's Wrath*** has clashed with Thrac’s battle-groups across the Diaspora in three major engagements. Thrac respects her tactical brilliance, but mocks her devotion to justice, vowing to one day mount her skull upon the prow of his flagship.
* **Director-General [[Lin Cammar]] ([[The Stewards]]):** Cammar’s federal interdiction patrols represent the primary obstacle to Thrac’s supply lines. Thrac has placed a ten-million-credit bounty on Cammar’s head, regularly sending high-tier vampire assassins to infiltrate [[The Bastion]].
* **The Living Traitors:** Thrac reserves his deepest, most burning hatred for the diplomats of **[[Eox]]** who sit in the Plenara on [[Absalom Station]]. He considers every undead ambassador who shakes hands with a living politician to be a traitor who has disgraced their dead world.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Phylactery Resonance:** An elite Starfinder deep-recon scout vessel intercepts anomalous quantum tachyon signals in [[The Diaspora]]. The signal is decoded by the PCs, revealing the coordinates to a secondary, redundant backup phylactery that Thrac hid inside an ancient pre-Gap asteroid crypt. Grand Marshal Sagesparrow retains the party to lead a surgical strike team into the crypt to destroy the soul-node before Thrac realizes his secret is compromised.
* **The Flagship Decoy:** Naval intelligence from [[The Stewards]] reports that Grand Admiral Thrac’s command cruiser has dropped out of the Drift near [[Akiton]]. The PCs are hired to pilot an experimental stealth bomber to cripple the vessel’s engines, only to discover upon boarding that the ship is an automated ghost-vessel packed with dirty nuclear warheads, rigged to detonate as an ambush against the Stewards' fleet.
* **The Sage's Extortion:** A prominent member of the [[Bone Sages]] of Eox summons the PCs for a clandestine meeting in a luxury dome on [[Absalom Station]]. The Sage reveals that Thrac is blackmailing him, threatening to release encrypted holo-logs proving the Sage secretly supplied weapons to the Corpse Fleet unless the Sage provides the security override codes to the Station’s Starstone reactor. The PCs must infiltrate Thrac’s hidden listening post on [[Apostae]] to eliminate the blackmail courier.
