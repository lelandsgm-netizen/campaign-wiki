#### Overview: The Iron Constable

If Prime Executive **[[Kumara Melacruz]]** is the silver tongue holding the **[[Pact Worlds]]** together through diplomacy, **Director-General Lin Cammar** is the iron shield that ensures those diplomatic words carry weight. As supreme commander of **[[The Stewards]]**, Cammar directs the vast federal constabulary, tactical starfleet, and counter-terrorist divisions headquartered within **[[The Bastion]]** on **[[Absalom Station]]**.

Cammar is neither an aloof aristocratic admiral nor a slick bureaucratic politician. He is a career lawman who rose through every echelon of the Stewards—from walking dangerous beat patrols in the shadow of [[The Spike]], to commanding void interceptors against pirates in **[[The Diaspora]]**, to leading planetary crisis teams during the Swarm War. Frontline officers revere him as a "constable’s constable" because he has bled in the vacuum himself and refuses to assign his deputies any mission he would not lead in person.

Broad-shouldered, weathered by decades of stellar radiation, and sporting close-cropped steel-gray hair, Cammar is famously blunt, incorruptible, and immune to corporate bribes. In a system teeming with opportunistic megacorporations and sovereign despots, he views the **[[Absalom Pact]]** not as a convenient business treaty, but as a sacred covenant protecting ordinary citizens from cosmic terror and tyrannical exploitation.

#### Career of the Peacekeeper: From the Spike to the Bastion

Cammar's trajectory to high command was forged in the fire of systemic conflict:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         THE TRAJECTORY OF LIN CAMMAR                        |
+=============================================================================+
| [ROOKIE CONSTABLE]  ===> Patrolled customs docks & Spike gangs on Absalom.  |
| [VOID INTERDICTOR]  ===> Commanded a Ringworks cutter hunting Diaspora corsairs.|
| [SPECIAL OPS]       ===> Led Bastion Marine boarding teams during Swarm War.|
| [MARSHAL ELEVATION] ===> Directed the Aucturn Quarantine Flotilla.          |
| [DIRECTOR-GENERAL]  ===> Appointed supreme commander by the Pact Directorate.|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Crucible of the Frontline
Born to Vercite immigrants on Absalom Station, Cammar entered the Bastion Academy at eighteen. His early years as a beat constable in the turbulent docking bays taught him how quickly minor jurisdictional disputes could escalate into riots. Later, as captain of the patrol cutter *Vigilant Ring*, he earned a reputation for tactical brilliance during the **Asteroid Blockade of 308 AG**, outmaneuvering three pirate gunboats in the Diaspora without losing a single crewmember.

##### 2. The Swarm Veteran
During the apocalyptic Swarm invasion of 319 AG, Cammar—then a tactical commander—led zero-G boarding actions to cleanse infested orbital stations and civilian evacuation transports. The horrific carnage of that war left an indelible mark on his soul, solidifying his belief that internal bickering between Pact member worlds is an existential luxury that the system cannot afford.

##### 3. Supreme Command
Following the retirement of his predecessor, the Pact Council’s Directorate appointed Cammar as **Director-General**. His appointment was a relief to field officers who feared a corporate puppet might be installed to soften customs inspections for **[[AbadarCorp]]**.

#### Command Philosophy: The Doctrine of Proportional Force

Director-General Cammar’s leadership of the Stewards is governed by three foundational doctrines:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       THE CAMMAR COMMAND DOCTRINES                          |
+=============================================================================+
| [PROPORTIONAL FORCE]  ===> Exhaust all non-lethal suppression methods first;|
|                            when lethal force is necessary, deliver it with  |
|                            uncompromising, decisive finality.               |
| [SOVEREIGN BOUNDARIES]===> Respect planetary borders, but hold absolute     |
|                            dominion over the interplanetary void.           |
| [CORRUPTION ZERO]     ===> Punish internal misconduct more severely than    |
|                            external crime; an unjust Steward is a traitor.  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### 1. The Shield Before the Sword
Recruits at the Bastion Academy are drilled endlessly in Cammar’s signature maxim: *"We are peacekeepers, not conquerors."* Under his direction, every Steward officer is equipped with dual-mode weaponry (stun pulses, hardlight barriers, and sonic dispersers) and trained in crisis de-escalation. However, Cammar makes it clear to enemies of the Pact that if a hostile force targets civilians or deploys forbidden superweapons, the white-and-gold starfleet of **[[Ringworks]]** cruisers will respond with devastating naval superiority.

##### 2. Standing Up to Corporate Hegemons
Cammar’s most contentious battles are fought not in deep space, but in the conference rooms of the Directorate. He has repeatedly blocked attempts by [[AbadarCorp]] and dragon CEOs from [[Triaxus]] to privatize space lane security or purchase exemptions from federal customs checks. When corporate lobbyists attempt to intimidate him, Cammar is famous for setting his sidearm on the boardroom table and asking if their lawyers have a counter-measure for high-yield arc plasma.

#### Strategic Alliances & Friction Points

Cammar operates at the nexus of the system’s most powerful martial factions:

* **Prime Executive [[Kumara Melacruz]]:** Cammar’s civilian superior and closest confidant. While Cammar occasionally complains about Melacruz’s cautious diplomatic hesitations, he deeply respects the Prime Executive’s integrity. The two men meet privately every week in the Bastion’s war room, coordinating military deployments to reinforce Melacruz’s diplomatic leverage.
* **First Seeker [[Luwazi Elsebo]] ([[Starfinder Society]]):** A close personal friend. When the Scoured Stars disaster struck, Cammar authorized emergency logistical support to protect outlying Starfinder Lodges. In return, Elsebo regularly shares classified telemetry from deep-space expeditions, giving Cammar early warnings of threats brewing in the Vast.
* **Grand Marshal [[Helena Sagesparrow]] ([[Knights of Golarion]]):** Cammar maintains profound respect for Sagesparrow’s military genius. However, their relationship is often strained: the paladins’ religious zeal often leads them to chase suspected heretics across sovereign planetary borders, leaving Cammar to clean up the resulting diplomatic fallout with the Plenara.
* **The Bitter Enemy:** Grand Admiral **[[Woan Thrac]]** and the **[[Corpse Fleet]]**. Cammar considers the rogue undead armada to be the greatest conventional military threat facing the Pact Worlds, maintaining a permanent task force dedicated exclusively to hunting their hidden bases in the Diaspora.

#### Persona & Demeanor

* **The Gruff Guardian:** Cammar does not indulge in social pleasantries. He speaks in crisp, clipped sentences, evaluating everyone he meets through the lens of threat assessment and integrity. 
* **The Common Touch:** Despite holding supreme military rank, Cammar refuses to eat in private executive dining rooms, preferring the communal mess halls of the Bastion where he sits alongside junior technicians and cadets.
* **The Burden of the Void:** Beneath his stoic armor, Cammar carries the psychological scars of centuries of collective conflict. He rarely sleeps more than four hours a night, constantly reviewing real-time sensor feeds from border listening posts and the **[[Aucturn]]** quarantine blockade.

#### Campaign & Adventure Hooks

* **The Bastion Mole:** Director-General Cammar personally summons the player characters to a clandestine briefing in an unmonitored docking bay of [[The Bastion]]. He reveals that classified patrol schedules have been leaked to the **[[Corpse Fleet]]**, resulting in the ambush of two *Peacekeeper* frigates. Because he cannot trust his own internal affairs division, Cammar hires the PCs as independent external investigators to root out the traitor on the High Command Operations Deck.
* **Extradition Beyond the Law:** A notorious corporate assassin responsible for bombing a civilian passenger terminal on [[Castrovel]] flees to a sovereign drilling citadel on [[Akiton]], protected by a corrupt local warlord. Legally barred from sending uniformed Stewards onto sovereign soil, Cammar retains the PCs for a deniable "black bag" extraction operation to abduct the fugitive and deliver him to federal custody in international space.
* **The Aucturn Anomaly:** Telemetry from the picket flotilla blockading the nightmare world of **[[Aucturn]]** goes haywire, reporting massive organic bio-signatures pulsing from the deep amniotic swamps. Suspecting that cultists of the [[Elder Mythos]] have prepared a cosmic summoning ritual, Cammar tasks the PCs with a high-risk reconnaissance mission: slip past the blockade, land on the surface, and destroy the ritual apparatus before he is forced to order a catastrophic orbital bombardment.
