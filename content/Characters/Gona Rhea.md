## 😡 Class Features, Feats & Rage

> [!abstract] Giant Instinct Rage
> Can **Rage** for 10 rounds per use. While raging, Gona gains **9 temporary HP** and deals an additional **+2 damage** with melee strikes.

- **Fighter Combat Actions:**
  - **Reactive Strike (Reaction):** Attack a creature within reach that uses a manipulate/move action, makes a ranged attack, or leaves a square.
  - **Intimidating Strike (2 Actions):** Melee strike that frightens the target.
  - **Slam Down (2 Actions):** Melee strike that automatically trips the target on a hit.
  - **Sudden Charge (2 Actions):** Stride twice and make a melee Strike.
- **Orc Ancestry Reactions:**
  - **Orc Ferocity (1/day):** When reduced to 0 HP, stay at 1 HP instead.
  - **Orc Superstition (Reaction):** Triggered when attempting a saving throw against a spell or magical effect.
  - **Victorious Vigor (Reaction):** Gain 4 temporary HP when bringing a foe to 0 HP.
