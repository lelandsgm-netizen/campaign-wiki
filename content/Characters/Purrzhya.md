### Bard & Archetype Combat Feats

- **Maestro Muse**: Dedication to perfect choreography and composition orchestration loops.
    
- **Swashbuckler Dedication**: Access to Panache, the Battledancer performance focus, and speed acceleration elements.
    
- **Acrobat Dedication**: Automatically scales Acrobatics to Master, providing specialized infiltration avenues.
    
- **Basic Flair & Advanced Flair**: Acquired bonus martial feats (_Focused Fascination_, _Leading Dance_).
    
- **Leading Dance**: Force enemies across the battlefield under theatrical performance loops.
    
- **Tumbling Strike**: High-speed combat displacement that strikes right through enemy perimeters.
    
- **Well-Versed**: Fortified mental baseline provides defensive resilience against illusionary or auditory hazards.
    

### Ancestry & General Feats

- **Cat's Luck (1/day) [Free Action]**: Triggered on a failed Reflex save to completely re-roll the fortune outcome.
    
- **Lucky Break**: Combine your catlike reflexes to overcome structural skill check failures cleanly.
    
- **Fleet**: Baseline structural adjustment optimizing standard traversal speeds.
    
- **Kip Up [Free Action]**: Instantly stand from a prone position without spending actions or drawing reactions.
    
- **Graceful Leaper**: Execute broad jumps or vertical clearings calculated off your master Acrobatics metric instead of Athletics.
    
- **Acrobatic Performer**: Gracefully mask standard performance checks with intricate physical acrobatics.
    
- **Fascinating Performance**: Command the absolute attention of surrounding target observers.
    
- **Nimble Crawl / Steady Balance**: Rapidly traverse tight crawls prone and automatically upgrade standard balancing checks to critical successes.
