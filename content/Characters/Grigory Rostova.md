## Feats & Special Talents

### Ranger & Archetype Combat Feats

- **Beastmaster Dedication**: Form a powerful bond with an animal companion, granting you tactical minion coordination.

- **Snarecrafter Dedication**: Allows the utilization of Survival instead of Crafting for trap mechanics. Can deploy formulas cleanly out of wilderness resources.

- **Initiate Warden**: Unlocks vital focus capabilities, granting access to Warden spells.

- **Snare Crafting**: Allows the rapid formulation and engineering of structural mechanical traps.

- **Surprise Snare (3 Actions)**: Rapidly deploy a single snare formula directly onto an adjacent square during an active combat round.

- **Remote Trigger**: Securely detonate or spring your crafted snares from a safe tactical distance.

- **Snare Hopping (2 Actions - Focus Spell)**: Teleport a snare you constructed to another valid space within a 10-foot range boundary.

### Ancestry & General Feats

- **Natural Ambition**: Human drive allows the acquisition of a bonus 1st-level class feat during initialization.

- **General Training**: Grants an extra general feat slot selection.

- **Additional Lore**: Enhances and autoscales specialized subcategory expertise (Butchering Lore).

- **Magical Crafting**: Provides capabilities to embed, alter, and forge magical properties and runes into equipment.

- **Improvise Tool**: Work efficiently on mechanical installations without incurring penalties from lacking a standard artisan toolkit.

- **Schooled in Secrets**: Decipher underground signals, codes, and hidden community interactions seamlessly.

- **Survey Wildlife**: Spend time studying tracks and environments to discover local fauna populations and threats.

- **Tame Animal / Train Animal**: Befriend wild targets and spend downtime teaching them fundamental baseline commands.
