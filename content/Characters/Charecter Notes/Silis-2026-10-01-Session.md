### ⚔️ Encounters & Hazards

**The Webbing & Trapped Ledge**
Encountered non-threatening spiders in a webbing-filled chamber and took a 3-hour rest to recover resources.
Explored south through the room featuring spider webbing and hanging hammocks, eventually scaling a cliff to reach an upper ledge area.
Detected and dismantled a hidden fire glyph trap, though a secondary magical flame trap was triggered, inflicting temporary blindness on several party members. The magical curse effects were resisted and wore off after a few tense moments.

**Ambush by Alchemists and Kobolds**
Engaged enemy alchemists and dragonborn characters blocking the upper ledge path.
Utilized speed boosts, flying carpets, and tactical positioning to navigate the rough terrain.
The party unleashed precise ranged ice bullets, dual throwing axes, and melee multi-action strikes to eliminate the enemies, including the remaining kobolds and an attacking imp.

**The Transformed Monstrosity**
While moving through the area, a hidden poisonous gas trap was triggered. A surviving kobold was exposed and transformed into a deformed, winged monster of massive size.
The transformed monster fiercely attacked the party, inflicting severe damage.
The party's spellcasters cast deflection and protection spells to enhance defense and toughness while blinded, while the bard provided much-needed inspiration.
The monster was battered with specialized ice and lightning bullets, armor-piercing shots, and powerful smite strikes.
A fire breath spell ignited the undead alchemical creature before it finally collapsed from the coordinated physical and magical assault. (One party member narrowly survived a massive blow by soaking the damage).

**The Crystal Chamber & Spiked Ambush**
Successfully disarmed a trap on the upper ramp leading to a crystal chamber.
Disabled an additional hidden sandstone trap encountered while climbing the stairs.
Navigated past a wooden structural barricade blocking the upper level, only to be ambushed by small spiked creatures suddenly emerging from the stone.
