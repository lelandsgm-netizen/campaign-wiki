### ⚔️ Encounters & Hazards

**The Webbing Room & The Necromancer**
After carefully burning away thick webbing to enter the cavern, we discovered giant, emotive pods. Donoif successfully used his Empathy to calm the entities inside. As we pushed forward, a Necrotic Warlock ambushed us from the ceiling, commanding three spider swarms. However, an enormous giant spider descended and seemingly countered the Warlock's control over the swarms. 

The Warlock blasted the party with a burst of necrotic energy. Adrian and I advanced, dealing heavy damage while Adrian completely shattered the Warlock's armor. Rocky landed the finishing shot to end the threat.
