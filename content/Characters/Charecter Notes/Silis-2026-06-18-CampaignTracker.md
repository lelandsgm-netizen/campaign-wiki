## 1. Biography & Timeline

### Personal History

* **1931 | A Birth in the Spire's Shadow:** Born the same year the **Aetheric Spire (Empire State Building)** activated. Caught between two worlds: the raw, living magic of your elven druid mother (Lyraeniel Starbreeze) vs. the brutal, industrial ambition of your mafia enforcer father (Eddie "The Kraken" Bianacci).
* **The 40s | The Kraken's Prodigy:** Rose through the Undercity's shadow markets. Became your father's top enforcer by the mid-40s, leading crews to shake down aether-smugglers and track rival mages.
* **1951–1955 | A Disappearance and a Fall:** Mother found hanged in an alley in 1951 after spiraling over the Spire's true purpose. In 1955, orchestrated your father's escape to neutral territories via a smuggled airship. Took the fall for him and spent 5 years in the "Cogworks" prison.
* **1960–1965 | A New Path & The Great Steam-Out:** Released in 1960. Sought discipline and joined a hidden dojo teaching the "Way of Focused Ki." During the **Great Steam-Out of '65**, used your ungrid-dependent monk training to protect and help citizens through 13 hours of total darkness.
* **1969 | The Gilded Grimoire Uprising:** Acted as a peaceful shield during the Clockwork Paladins' raid on the non-human sanctuary bar. Used defensive mastery to save dozens of fleeing patrons, earning a quiet, respected reputation.
* **1975–1976 | The Brass Rot & The Ripper:** Refused old mafia contacts trying to pull you back during the economic decay. Became a vigilante neighborhood guardian to protect the innocent from the terrifying **Cogwork Ripper**.
* **1977 | The Night a Team Was Forged:** The Night of Rust and Ruin. Dimensional wards shattered, spiders invaded, and you fought side-by-side with your true crew: a powerhouse wrestler, a fire-slinging gnome, and a bomb-tossing lunatic.

## 2. Martial Arts & Dojo Training

Silis sought refuge in this hidden dojo when he was rudderless, weary of crime, and grieving his parents. Here, he traded his firearms for lethal fist training, learning focus, honor, and discipline.

### Muay Boran Fighting Style

An ancient, brutal Thai martial art of attrition developed to practice lethal combat safely without weapons. It relies on *na-wa arwud* (the nine weapons): head, fists, elbows, knees, and shins. Fighters condition their bodies relentlessly—even breaking palm trees with their shins—and wrap their hands in knotted rope (*kaad chuek*), sometimes embedded with glass or bone. Grappling is strictly limited to close-quarters clinching to deliver rapid-fire knee and elbow strikes.

> **Current Mastery Progress:** Silis has spent nearly 20 years here and is currently **one edge away** from completely mastering the style.

A massive corporate entity executing an aggressive, cash-heavy real estate land grab across New York, completely funded by an anonymous private trust.

They heavily targeted the Vanguard Campus for repossession before the crew took on debt to buy them out. They currently have outstanding aggressive cash offers targeting the crew's old apartments and Papa Twilight's popular club.

Funded entirely by Silis to provide free, clean produce to campus residents and sell surplus items to the public. Managed directly by a volunteer tenant board.

* **South Warehouse:** Future plans target a modern health-conscious Tavern and Bar lounge on the ground floor, featuring specialized intoxicating elixirs developed with help from Mark Hennesy.

* **The Freighter (The "Carlscraft"):** An 80-foot, heavy-duty commercial cargo freighter powered entirely by internal magical lodestone engines. Retained as an operational prize following the successful raid on the Maersk warehouse docks.
