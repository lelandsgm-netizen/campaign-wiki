## Feats & Special Talents

### Rogue & Archetype Combat Feats

- **Swashbuckler Dedication**: Access to Panache, Battledancer performance style, and speed styles.

- **Ranger Dedication**: Grants the Hunt Prey action targeting exploration tracking and hunting sequences.

- **Basic Finisher**: Allows the spending of Panache to execute a high-damage finisher blow.

- **Finishing Precision**: Retain a portion of precision damage even when missing a Finisher strike.

- **Tumble Behind**: Tumbling through an enemy's space automatically renders them off-guard to your attacks.

- **Analyze Weakness**: Study identified targets to maximize weakpoint damage outputs.

- **Flashy Dodge [Reaction]**: Quickly raise defensive values when targeted by a visible attack string.

- **Charmed Life [Reaction]**: Apply a luck-driven circumstance bonus to a saving throw before rolling.

### Ancestry & General Feats

- **Goblin Weapon Familiarity**: Cultural training unlocking complex goblin armaments like the dogslicer.

- **Kneecap (1 Action)**: Strike a foe’s lower limbs to reduce their movement speed capabilities.

- **Assurance (Acrobatics & Thievery)**: Forego d20 fortune rolls to guarantee stable, calculation-based check results (Acrobatics: 21, Thievery: 23).

- **Experienced Smuggler**: Keep items concealed safely from inspections and casual structural searches.

- **Pickpocket**: Steal small objects effortlessly without provoking standard observation checks.

- **Quiet Allies**: Combine stealth footprints with party members during infiltration routines.

- **Shadow Mark**: Follow targets cleanly through urban terrain using specialized tracking angles.

- **Tumbling Theft**: Snatch an item off an opponent seamlessly as part of a Tumble Through motion.

- **Wary Disarmament**: Grants an AC and saving throw bonus (+2 circumstance) vs traps triggered during disarm attempts.
