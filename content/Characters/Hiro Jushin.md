## Feats & Special Talents

### Monk & Archetype Combat Feats

- **Acrobat Dedication**: Automatically scales your Acrobatics skill capability to Master and opens high-mobility evasion angles.
    
- **Qi Spells**: Unlocks access to spiritual monk energy reserves and your initial pool of focus techniques.
    
- **Wild Winds Initiate**: Grants access to the cutting ranged capabilities of the _Wild Winds Stance_.
    
- **Crane Flutter [Reaction]**: Intercept incoming strikes dynamically while fighting defensively in Crane Stance.
    
- **Dodge Away [Reaction]**: Spend a reaction when targeted by a melee attack to safely slip away, shifting your position and increasing your defenses.
    
- **Contortionist**: Squeeze through highly restrictive blockades and narrow structural vents seamlessly.
    
- **Tumbling Strike (1 Action)**: Flourish. Evade straight through a foe's space via an Acrobatics check, hitting them with an off-guard melee strike on a success.
    

### Ancestry & General Feats

- **Climbing Tail / Combat Climber**: Frees your hands up during vertical ascents and keeps you from being caught flat-footed while climbing structures.
    
- **Acrobatic Performer**: Swap performance checks for theatrical physical stunts using your master Acrobatics proficiency.
    
- **Tumbling Theft**: Pickpocket small objects right off an active foe's belt while tumbling cleanly past them.
    
- **Graceful Leaper**: Calculate standard jump distances using your Acrobatics metric instead of Athletics.
    
- **Dirty Trick**: Blind or disorient adjacent targets temporarily via quick sleight-of-hand movements.
    
- **Pickpocket**: Snatch hidden items from guards or targets effortlessly without triggering routine notices.
    
- **Quick Squeeze / Steady Balance**: Speed up tight structural bypasses and automatically upgrade balance successes to critical successes.
    
- **Fascinating Performance**: Draw crowd focus away from team members during perimeter entries.
    
- **Ragdya's Revelry [Reaction]**: A divine/racial trick triggered once per day when an adjacent enemy misses you, forcing an annoying disruption loop onto them.
    
- **Rousing Splash (Innate Cantrip)**: At-Will divine cantrip. Cleanse toxic substances or provide brief mental focus insulation to a target within 60 feet.
